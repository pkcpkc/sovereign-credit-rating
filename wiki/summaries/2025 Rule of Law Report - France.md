# Summary of: 2025 Rule of Law Report - France

## Context Metadata
*   **Source Document:** Commission Staff Working Document (SWD(2025) 910 final)
*   **Date of Issue:** July 8, 2025
*   **Geographic Focus:** France
*   **Subject Matter:** Rule of law, judicial independence, anti-corruption measures, and media freedom.

## Executive Summary
The 2025 Rule of Law Report for France highlights a period of significant structural reform and digitalization within the justice system. While judicial independence remains an average perception among the public and businesses, the Superior Council of Magistracy (CSM) has actively defended the judiciary against personal attacks on magistrates. Significant strides have been made in digitalizing civil and criminal proceedings, though speed of implementation remains a concern. On the anti-corruption front, France is moving toward a new national plan (2024-2029) with increased resources for high-level prosecutions and the reorganization of the Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA). Media freedom updates show progress in ownership transparency through a new national database, though challenges persist regarding the practical right of journalists to access official documents.

## Key Highlights

### I. Justice System & Independence
*   **Magistracy Reforms:** Implementation of the Nov 20, 2023 law, which repealed age limits for admissions and introduced a 360° evaluation process for court presidents.
*   **Judicial Independence:** Public perception of independence slightly decreased in 2025 compared to 2024, despite high-level support from the President of the Republic.
*   **Digitalization Progress:** The *Portalis* tool is expanding to all labor courts, and online access to court decisions has surpassed one million records.
*   **Efficiency Challenges:** The average time to resolve bribery cases remains the highest in the EU (3.8 years). "Flash missions" were launched in late 2024 to address sentencing execution and case scheduling.

### II. Anti-Corruption Framework
*   **National Plan:** The 2024-2029 national anti-corruption plan is expected by summer 2025, focusing on organized drug crime and municipal integrity.
*   **Institutional Strengthening:** The Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA) was reorganized into public and private sector departments, while the HATVP increased human resources to handle a 48% increase in asset declarations.
*   **Lobbying Transparency:** Progress has been made on registering lobbyists, though disclosure requirements for top-executive officials remain a point of contention.
*   **Whistleblower Protection:** Reports to the Defender of Rights doubled in 2024, prompting plans for organizational changes to manage the increased workload.

### III. Media Pluralism and Freedom
*   **Regulatory Expansion:** ARCOM has expanded its scope to include Digital Services Act (DSA) oversight and is preparing for the European Media Freedom Act (EMFA).
*   **Ownership Transparency:** A significant move toward a single national database for media ownership is underway to clarify complex shareholding structures.
*   **Public Broadcasting:** Debate continues over the "France Médias" holding company; while a shift to VAT-based funding was approved, the 2025 budget still includes cuts.
*   **Right to Information:** Despite strong legal frameworks, journalists report "cumbersome" practical hurdles in accessing documents, leading to a new legislative proposal based on the *États généraux de l’information*.

### IV. Institutional Checks and Balances
*   **Legislative Simplification:** Businesses welcome the "simplification of economic life" law, but stakeholders express concern over reduced public consultation.
*   **Investment Protection:** 62% of companies express confidence in the protection of investments by French law and courts.
*   **ECtHR Compliance:** France saw a decrease in pending leading judgments from the European Court of Human Rights as of June 2025, though some cases remain pending for over 15 years.
*   **Artificial Intelligence:** Ongoing studies are evaluating the use of AI in the justice system, with a focus on cybersecurity and citizen rights.
