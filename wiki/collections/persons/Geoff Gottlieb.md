# Geoff Gottlieb

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member (involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Poland)

## Biography & Context

Geoff Gottlieb is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team. He participated in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland, which involved extensive discussions held in Warsaw between October 8 and October 17, 2024. His work contributed to the production of the IMF Staff Report (completed December 16, 2024) regarding Poland's economic recovery, fiscal deficit management under the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP), and structural reforms related to labor migration and climate transition.
