# Krzysztof Krogulski

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member (Article IV Consultation Team)

## Biography & Context

Krzysztof Krogulski is identified as a key participant in the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. He was involved in the staff discussions held in Warsaw between October 8 and October 17, 2024, which culminated in the completion of the IMF Staff Report on December 16, 2024.

His work contributed to the analysis of Poland's economic recovery, focusing on growth projections (3.5% for 2025-2026), the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds, and the country's fiscal position under the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP). The context of his involvement includes evaluating Poland's monetary policy stance, debt sustainability (targeting 60% of GDP), and structural reforms related to labor markets, migration, and the climate transition.
