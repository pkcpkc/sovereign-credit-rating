## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Mr. Mohommad served as an IMF Staff member during the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Austria. His work contributed to the IMF's assessment of Austria's economic and financial stability amid a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by successive recessions in 2023 and 2024. The consultation highlighted significant risks regarding fiscal sustainability, with the public debt rising to 81.2% of GDP and the deficit widening to 4.7% of GDP in 2024. 

Mr. Mohommad's involvement was part of a team that analyzed the necessity for substantial medium-term fiscal adjustment to stabilize the debt ratio against rising pressures from demographic aging, defense spending, and green transition costs. The team also assessed structural reforms aimed at boosting labor supply and productivity, as well as financial sector health, particularly focusing on the risks associated with Commercial Real Estate (CRE) exposures and the expiration of borrower-based mortgage lending measures. The IMF concluded that while Austria's banking sector remains well-capitalized, maintaining prudent lending standards and addressing fiscal imbalances are critical for long-term stability.
