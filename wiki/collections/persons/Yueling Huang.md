# Yueling Huang

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Yueling Huang is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. This work involved assessing Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges, including productivity growth and demographic headwinds.
