# Cheryl Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania.

## Biography & Context

Cheryl Li is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. Her work contributes to the analysis of Romania's macroeconomic landscape, specifically focusing on fiscal consolidation, inflation management, and banking sector resilience. 

During the consultation period (September–November 2025), she was part of the team evaluating Romania's "twin deficits," the impact of VAT rate increases, and the sustainability of public debt. Her context includes monitoring the "sovereign-bank nexus," the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds, and structural reforms aimed at improving labor force participation.
