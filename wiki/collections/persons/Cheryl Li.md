# Cheryl Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Cheryl Li is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) team involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. Her work contributes to the analysis of Romania's macroeconomic stability, focusing on fiscal consolidation, inflationary pressures, and structural reforms. She was involved in the assessment of Romania's twin deficits, the sovereign-bank nexus, and the country's progress in absorbing European Union funds.
