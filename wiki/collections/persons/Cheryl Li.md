## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Cheryl Li serves as staff within the International Monetary Fund (IMF) working on Romania. Her work is situated within the context of the 2025 Article IV Consultation, which examines Romania's macroeconomic stability and sovereign credit profile. The consultation highlights significant fiscal challenges, including a fiscal deficit of 8.7 percent of GDP in 2024, driven by pension reforms and public wage increases. The IMF staff, including Cheryl Li, evaluated the new coalition government's comprehensive fiscal consolidation package adopted in 2025, which aimed to reduce the deficit through VAT rate hikes and other tax measures.

The analysis covers key areas such as debt sustainability, monetary policy, and structural reforms. Key risks identified include the execution of fiscal consolidation, rising public debt-to-GDP ratios, and vulnerabilities in the banking sector, particularly regarding unhedged foreign exchange loans. The IMF mission, led by Joong Shik Kang and including staff members like Cheryl Li, engaged with Romanian government officials, including Prime Minister Ilie Bolojan and Finance Minister Alexandru Nazare, as well as National Bank of Romania Governor Mugur Isarescu. The mission concluded its discussions in September 2025, with the Executive Board concluding the consultation in November 2025. The focus remains on restoring fiscal and macroeconomic sustainability through continued consolidation, prudent monetary policy, and accelerated structural reforms to unlock EU funds and support long-term growth.
