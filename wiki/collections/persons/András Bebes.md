# András Bebes

## Affiliations & Roles

- Official involved in the IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Hungary

## Biography & Context

András Bebes is a key figure involved in the economic and fiscal oversight of Hungary. He participated in the discussions during the IMF staff mission in Budapest (June 2025) regarding the country's "challenging juncture" of stagnant output and persistent inflation. His involvement relates to the high-level coordination required for fiscal adjustment, debt sustainability analysis (DSA), and the implementation of structural reforms aimed at improving firm dynamism and energy security. He is part of the group of Hungarian officials engaged with the IMF to address sovereign-bank linkages, trade policy shocks, and the transition toward a more sustainable primary surplus.
