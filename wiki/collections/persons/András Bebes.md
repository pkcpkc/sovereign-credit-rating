## Biography & Context

András Bebes is identified as a key representative of the Hungarian authorities during the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2025 Article IV Consultation. He participated in the staff mission and discussions regarding Hungary's macroeconomic stability, fiscal adjustment requirements, and structural reforms. His involvement centered on addressing the challenges of stagnant output, high inflation, and the transition toward a more sustainable fiscal position aimed at reducing the headline deficit and managing debt-to-GDP ratios.
