# Carolina Claver

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff/Official involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy.

## Biography & Context

Carolina Claver is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. Her work involved assessing Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges. This included evaluating the country's return to a primary surplus, record-high employment rates, and the implementation progress of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP). Her role encompassed analyzing key macroeconomic factors such as productivity growth, demographic headwinds (aging population), and sovereign debt dynamics to provide policy recommendations for fiscal consolidation and labor market reforms.
