# Carolina Claver

- **Role**: IMF Staff Member
- **Organization**: [[International Monetary Fund]]
- **Countries Covered**: Italy

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Carolina Claver served as a staff member on the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) mission team responsible for Italy's 2025 Article IV Consultation. As part of the IMF staff, Claver contributed to the comprehensive assessment of Italy's economic stability, fiscal policy, and financial sector health. The consultation, concluded in July 2025, evaluated Italy's economic resilience, noting record-high employment and a return to a primary surplus, while addressing significant challenges such as high public debt (135.3% of GDP) and weak productivity growth. Claver's work involved analyzing the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) and assessing the sustainability of Italy's fiscal trajectory under the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP).
