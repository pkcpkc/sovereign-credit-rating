## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Robert Holzmann serves as the Governor of the [[Oesterreichische Nationalbank]] (OeNB), Austria's central bank. In the context of the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation, his institution is cited as a pillar of policy credibility and institutional strength, possessing high credibility in financial stability oversight. The OeNB operates within the independent monetary policy framework of the European Central Bank (ECB) while maintaining a robust supervisory role over the domestic banking sector.

Under Holzmann's tenure, the OeNB has maintained strong institutional frameworks that contribute to Austria's favorable sovereign credit profile. The central bank is recognized for its high data quality and transparency, adhering to SDDS Plus standards, which supports Austria's strong rule of law and institutional stability. Key areas of focus include monitoring financial sector health, particularly regarding rising Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) in the Commercial Real Estate (CRE) sector, and overseeing macroprudential measures such as the Systemic Risk Buffer (SyRB) and the Systemic Real Estate Buffer (SSyRB). The IMF highlighted the importance of the OeNB's vigilance on CRE data gaps and recommended that prudent lending standards, previously enforced via borrower-based measures, be made permanent to mitigate macro-financial risks.
