## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Ms. Pinat served as IMF Staff during the **2025 Article IV Consultation with Austria**. As part of the mission team led by Mission Head [[Mr. Fletcher]], she contributed to the assessment of Austria's macroeconomic stability and sovereign credit risk profile. Her work involved analyzing the country's fiscal consolidation efforts following a widened deficit of 4.7% of GDP in 2024 and rising public debt levels. The consultation, which concluded in July 2025, highlighted challenges such as sluggish productivity growth, demographic aging, and risks within the banking sector, particularly regarding Commercial Real Estate (CRE) exposures. Ms. Pinat's analysis supported the IMF's recommendations for medium-term fiscal adjustment and structural reforms to boost labor supply and productivity, ensuring debt sustainability amidst global trade uncertainties and regional economic headwinds.
