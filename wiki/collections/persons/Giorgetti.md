## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

As Minister of Economy and Finance for Italy, Giorgetti oversaw the country's economic performance leading up to the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation. Under his tenure, Italy demonstrated notable economic resilience despite heightened global uncertainty, with real GDP growing by 0.7 percent in 2024. A significant achievement during his term was the return to a primary surplus of 0.4 percent of GDP in 2024, driven by improved tax compliance mechanisms and strong labor market performance.

The IMF Executive Board commended the fiscal performance but emphasized the need for sustained consolidation to place high public debt (135.3% of GDP) on a clear downward trajectory. Giorgetti's administration focused on a Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan aligned with the EU fiscal framework, aiming to reach a primary surplus of 3 percent of GDP by 2027 through measures such as rationalizing tax expenditures and streamlining pension-related spending.

Key challenges addressed during his ministry included sluggish productivity growth, rapid population aging, and below-EU average female labor force participation. The administration prioritized the full implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), achieving 54 percent of milestones and ranking among top EU performers in fund allocation. The IMF stressed that Giorgetti's continued focus on structural reforms—focusing on productivity, labor supply, and the green transition—is essential to durably lift growth and offset demographic headwinds.
