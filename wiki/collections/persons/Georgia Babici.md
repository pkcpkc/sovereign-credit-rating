# Georgia Babici

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania.

## Biography & Context

Georgia Babici is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. Her work contributes to the analysis of Romania's macroeconomic stability, specifically focusing on fiscal consolidation, inflation management, and the resilience of the banking sector. 

Her involvement encompasses the evaluation of Romania's fiscal reform package, which includes VAT rate adjustments and public sector wage freezes aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit. She is part of the team providing oversight on "twin deficits," sovereign-bank nexus risks, and the structural reforms necessary to accelerate the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds.
