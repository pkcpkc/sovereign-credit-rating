# Mark Puccia

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Mark Puccia is associated with the development and publication of S&P Global Ratings' Sovereign Rating Methodology. His work involves the framework for assessing the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities, specifically focusing on the 2017 methodology which emphasizes net general government debt, current account payments (CAP), and the assessment of contingent liabilities via BICRA scores.
