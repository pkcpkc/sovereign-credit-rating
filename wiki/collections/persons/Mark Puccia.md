## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Mark Puccia serves as a Criteria Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], playing a key role in the development and maintenance of the firm's sovereign rating frameworks. He was identified as a primary contact for the "Sovereign Rating Methodology" published and effective on December 18, 2017. This methodology provides a standardized framework for assessing the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities, integrating five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments.

In his capacity as a Criteria Contact, Puccia was associated with significant updates to S&P's analytical framework, including:
*   **Local vs. Foreign Currency Ratings**: Implementing a stricter cap that limits local-currency rating uplifts to one notch above foreign-currency ratings (reduced from the previous two-notch limit).
*   **Fiscal Assessment Shift**: Transitioning the primary fiscal metric from gross debt to *net* general government debt (gross debt minus liquid financial assets) as a percentage of GDP to better capture true fiscal stance.
*   **External Liquidity Metrics**: Refining external assessment ratios by using Current Account Payments (CAP) instead of Current Account Receipts (CAR) in specific scenarios involving external liquid assets.
*   **Contingent Liabilities**: Integrating the Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA) to analyze banking sector risks and their impact on sovereign debt burdens.

Puccia's work supports the broader institutional assessment of sovereigns, focusing on policymaking effectiveness, transparency, and debt payment culture.
