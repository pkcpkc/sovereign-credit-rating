# Ed Hearne

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Belgium.

## Biography & Context

Ed Hearne is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was part of the staff team involved in the 2025 Article IV consultation for Belgium. His work contributed to the assessment of Belgium's economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and structural challenges. The consultation focused on addressing high public debt (104.1% of GDP), the impacts of automatic wage indexation on labor-cost competitiveness, and the implementation of a seven-year fiscal adjustment period under the new EU economic governance framework.
