# Ed Hearne

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member (associated with the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Belgium)

## Biography & Context

Ed Hearne is identified as a key participant in the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Belgium. His work involved analyzing the Belgian economy's resilience to shocks, structural challenges such as the automatic wage indexation system, and fiscal sustainability measures. He contributed to the assessment of Belgium's 7-year adjustment plan and the evaluation of its public debt trajectory, which is projected to reach 123% of GDP by 2030.
