# Lone Christiansen

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of Staff Team for the IMF Italy Country Report (2025)

## Biography & Context

Lone Christiansen served as the Head of Staff Team for the International Monetary Fund (IMF) during the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. In this capacity, she oversaw the staff discussions and the production of the country report, which analyzed Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges. Her work involved evaluating Italy's progress on the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), its primary surplus targets, and demographic headwinds such as a rising old-age dependency ratio.
