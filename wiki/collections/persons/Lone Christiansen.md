## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Lone Christiansen served as the **IMF Staff Team Head** for the **2025 Article IV Consultation with Italy**, leading the mission that assessed the country's economic performance and policy framework. Under her leadership, the IMF staff highlighted Italy's economic resilience, noting GDP growth of 0.7% in 2024 and record-high employment levels, while identifying significant global uncertainty and structural challenges such as weak productivity growth and population aging.

A central focus of the consultation led by Christiansen was **fiscal sustainability and debt management**. The staff report emphasized the high public debt-to-GDP ratio of 135.3% in 2024 and recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment, targeting a primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027 to ensure a downward debt trajectory. This recommendation contrasted with the Italian authorities' proposal for a more gradual adjustment under the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP).

Additionally, Christiansen's team evaluated **financial sector stability**, noting improved banking sector health with low non-performing loans but flagging vulnerabilities in Less Significant Institutions (LSIs) and the persistent sovereign-bank nexus. The mission also stressed the importance of continuing structural reforms through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) to boost productivity and address demographic headwinds.
