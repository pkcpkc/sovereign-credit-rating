# Lone Christiansen

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of IMF Staff Team at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Lone Christiansen served as the Head of the IMF Staff Team responsible for the 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Italy. In this capacity, Christiansen oversaw the evaluation of Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges. The work involved analyzing Italy's successful return to a primary surplus, the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), and providing policy recommendations to address productivity growth and demographic headwinds.
