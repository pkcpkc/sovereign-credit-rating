## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Yang Yang serves as IMF Staff within the European Department, participating in the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the Republic of Poland. The consultation, conducted in October 2024 with staff reports completed in December 2024 and Executive Board discussions in January 2025, focused on Poland's macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy, and structural reforms.

Yang Yang was part of the mission team that assessed Poland's robust economic recovery, driven by domestic demand and the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. The mission highlighted significant fiscal challenges, including a projected deficit of 5.9% of GDP in 2024 due to public sector wage increases and high defense spending. The IMF recommended frontloading fiscal consolidation to rebuild buffers, aiming for a deficit of 2.9% of GDP by 2028 through measures such as VAT reforms and pension adjustments.

The consultation also addressed monetary policy, with the National Bank of Poland maintaining a tight stance to combat inflation. Yang Yang contributed to the assessment that interest rate cuts should commence only when wage growth decelerates and inflation is firmly declining towards the 2.5% target. Furthermore, the team evaluated the resilience of the financial sector, noting strong capital buffers alongside risks from legacy CHF mortgage litigation. Structural reforms were emphasized as critical for sustaining growth, focusing on labor market integration, innovation, and climate transition.
