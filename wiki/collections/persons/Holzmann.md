# Holzmann

## Affiliations & Roles

- Central Bank official (Interlocutor) at [[Austria]]

## Biography & Context

Holzmann is a key figure in the Austrian economic landscape, serving as a primary interlocutor during the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation. In this capacity, Holzmann engaged with the IMF staff team to discuss Austria's fiscal strategy, monetary policy, and financial stability. The discussions centered on navigating the post-recession recovery, managing sticky inflation, and addressing systemic risks within the commercial real estate sector.
