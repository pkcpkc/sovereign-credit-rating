## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Holzmann serves as the Central Bank Governor of Austria, playing a pivotal role in the nation's macroeconomic stability and financial regulation. He was a key participant in the IMF’s 2025 Article IV Consultation, meeting with the mission team from March 26 to April 8, 2025. During these discussions, he interacted with Minister of Finance Marterbauer and IMF representatives Oya Celasun, Bergljot Barkbu, and mission head Fletcher.

Under his leadership, the Oesterreichische Nationalbank has focused on maintaining financial stability amidst a challenging economic environment characterized by successive years of recession and high inflation. The IMF highlighted the banking sector's health, noting a strong CET1 capital ratio of 17.7% and high profitability, though risks from Commercial Real Estate (CRE) exposures remain a focus of supervisory vigilance. Holzmann’s bank implemented a Sectoral Systemic Risk Buffer (SSyRB) of 1% for CRE assets in July 2025 to mitigate these risks.

Monetary policy under his tenure has addressed persistent inflation, which spiked to 3.3% in early 2025 due to the expiration of electricity price caps and sticky services inflation. The central bank operates within an independent framework targeting convergence toward the Euro Area average of 2% by end-2026. Holzmann’s engagement with international bodies underscores Austria’s commitment to prudent monetary policy and structural reforms aimed at boosting labor supply and productivity.
