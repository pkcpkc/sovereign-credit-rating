# Kotaro Ishi

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of Staff Team for [[Thomas Gade]] at the International Monetary Fund (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Kotaro Ishi serves as the Head of Staff Team for Thomas Gade during the IMF's 2024 Article IV consultation with Malta. In this capacity, he contributed to the development of the IMF Staff Report published in January 2025. The work involved analyzing Malta's economic transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, addressing fiscal consolidation strategies, and evaluating structural reforms such as Corporate Income Tax (CIT) alignment with EU Pillar II and macroprudential policy adjustments for the banking sector.
