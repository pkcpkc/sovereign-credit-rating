Kotaro Ishi is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff involved in the 2025 Malta Country Report. He served as the Head of the staff team for [[Thomas Gade]] during the Article IV Consultation process, which focused on Malta's economic growth, fiscal sustainability, and structural reforms.
