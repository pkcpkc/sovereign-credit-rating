## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Kotaro Ishi served as the Team Leader for the International Monetary Fund (IMF) during the 2024 Article IV Consultation with Malta. His team conducted staff missions in Valletta starting in November 2024, concluding policy discussions by mid-November and finalizing the staff report in December 2024. The consultation was endorsed by the IMF Executive Board in January 2025.

Under Ishi's leadership, the IMF staff assessed Malta’s robust economic performance, characterized by high growth driven by export-oriented service industries such as tourism and online gaming. The assessment highlighted that while growth remains among Europe’s highest, it faces structural constraints including labor shortages and strain on public services due to population density. Key findings emphasized Malta’s strong external position, sound financial system with high capital buffers, and commitment to fiscal consolidation. The staff recommended continuing structural reforms focused on productivity, digitalization, and climate sustainability, while urging vigilance regarding real estate exposures and geopolitical risks.
