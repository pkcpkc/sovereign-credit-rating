## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Rishi Goyal served as a Department Head within the [[IMF]] during the 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[Italy]]. In this capacity, he was a senior member of the IMF staff team responsible for the assessment of Italy’s economic and financial landscape. His work contributed to the staff report which evaluated critical factors including fiscal policy credibility, debt sustainability, and structural reform implementation. The consultation highlighted Italy’s resilient economic performance in 2024, characterized by a return to primary surplus and robust employment, while identifying persistent challenges such as high public debt (135.3% of GDP), weak productivity growth, and population aging. The IMF staff, under the broader leadership of team head Lone Christiansen and department head Helge Berger, recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment to ensure a declining debt path, a stance that contrasted with the Italian authorities' preference for a gradual adjustment. Goyal’s role involved overseeing the analysis of these sovereign risk factors and the broader macroeconomic stability assessments associated with the consultation.
