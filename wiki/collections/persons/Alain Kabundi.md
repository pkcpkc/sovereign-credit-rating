# Alain Kabundi

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Consultant (Involved in 2025 Article IV Consultation - Italy)

## Biography & Context

Alain Kabundi is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. This work involved assessing Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance—specifically the transition to a primary surplus—and structural challenges such as low productivity growth and demographic headwinds. His involvement contributed to the analysis of Italy's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) implementation and the assessment of sovereign risk.
