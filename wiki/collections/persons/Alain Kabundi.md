## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Alain Kabundi served as a staff member for the [[International Monetary Fund]] during the 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[Italy]]. As part of the IMF mission, Kabundi contributed to the assessment of Italy’s macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy, and financial sector health. The consultation, concluded in July 2025, highlighted Italy’s economic resilience with 0.7% GDP growth in 2024 but emphasized significant structural challenges, including weak productivity growth, rapid population aging, and high public debt levels (135.3% of GDP).

Kabundi’s work involved evaluating the implementation of the [[National Recovery and Resilience Plan]] (NRRP) and the [[Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan]] (MTFSP). The IMF staff, including Kabundi, recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment to achieve a primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027 to ensure debt sustainability. The analysis also covered the banking sector’s strength, noting high profitability and low non-performing loans, while identifying vulnerabilities in Less Significant Institutions (LSIs) and the persistent sovereign-bank nexus. The mission engaged with key Italian institutions, including the [[Bank of Italy]] and the [[Ministry of Economy and Finance]], to discuss policy paths for boosting productivity and addressing external vulnerabilities.
