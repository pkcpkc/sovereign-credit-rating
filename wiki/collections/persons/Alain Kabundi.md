# Alain Kabundi

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member

## Biography & Context

Alain Kabundi is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) as part of the staff involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. His work contributed to the assessment of the Italian economy's resilience, fiscal performance, and structural reforms. This includes evaluating Italy's progress on the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), analyzing productivity challenges, demographic headwinds, and sovereign risk dynamics.
