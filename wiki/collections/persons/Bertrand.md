## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Bertrand serves as a State Secretary within the Belgian Federal Government, appointed following the formation of the new federal government in February 2025. This political transition followed protracted coalition negotiations after the June 2024 elections, which had previously stalled policymaking. As part of the new administration, Bertrand is involved in implementing the government's policy agenda, which includes significant structural reforms and a seven-year fiscal adjustment plan endorsed by the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

The IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation highlights the importance of coordinating federal and regional entities to implement these reforms effectively. Bertrand operates within a complex fiscal federalism framework, working alongside Regional Ministers such as Sven Gatz (Brussels-Capital Region) and Ben Weyts (Flemish Region). The government's mandate focuses on durably reducing the deficit below 3 percent of GDP and placing public debt, which stood at 104.1 percent of GDP in 2024, on a solidly downward path. Key areas of policy focus include rationalizing social benefits and the public wage bill, enhancing labor force participation, and addressing aging-related spending pressures.
