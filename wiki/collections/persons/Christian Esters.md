# Christian Esters

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Christian Esters is associated with S&P Global Ratings, specifically contributing to the development and publication of the Sovereign Rating Methodology. His work involves establishing frameworks for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness through multi-pillar assessments including Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary factors. He has been involved in key methodology updates, such as the transition to net general government debt focus and the integration of Current Account Payments (CAP) into external assessment ratios.
