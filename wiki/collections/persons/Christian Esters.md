## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Christian Esters serves as an Analytical Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], playing a key role in the institution's sovereign credit assessment framework. He is listed as a primary contact for the "S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology" updated in December 2017. In this capacity, he is associated with the application and explanation of the five-pillar assessment framework used to determine the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities. This framework evaluates Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments to derive indicative rating levels. His role supports the standardized analysis of sovereign credit risk, including adjustments for local versus foreign currency ratings, fiscal sustainability metrics such as net general government debt, and external liquidity measures.
