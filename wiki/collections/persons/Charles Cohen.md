## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Charles Cohen served as the Head of the Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP) during the [[International Monetary Fund]]'s 2025 Article IV consultation with [[France]]. In this capacity, he led the assessment of the French financial sector's resilience, focusing on capital buffers, liquidity coverage, and risks associated with non-bank financial intermediaries and corporate indebtedness.

His analysis highlighted that while the French banking sector maintained healthy capital ratios (19.8% regulatory capital ratio) and low non-performing loans, profitability remained weak due to net interest margin compression. Cohen's assessment underscored the importance of monitoring cyber and climate risks, as well as the sovereign-financial sector nexus, within the broader context of France's fiscal consolidation efforts and debt sustainability. His work contributed to the IMF's broader recommendation for structural reforms and fiscal prudence to ensure long-term economic stability.
