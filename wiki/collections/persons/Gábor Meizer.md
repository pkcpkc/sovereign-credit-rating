## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Gábor Meizer is an official within the International Monetary Fund's Office of External Relations (OED), engaged in the institution's surveillance activities regarding Hungary. He was identified as a key participant in the IMF’s 2025 Article IV Consultation with Hungary, a process conducted alongside other senior IMF mission leaders including Head of Mission Anke Weber and Department Heads Kristina Kostial and Jacques Miniane.

During this consultation, the IMF assessed Hungary’s economic outlook, highlighting stagnant output, elevated inflation, and significant fiscal challenges, including a public debt-to-GDP ratio of 73.5% in 2024. The mission emphasized the need for additional fiscal adjustment to ensure debt sustainability and warned of risks associated with deepening geoeconomic fragmentation and the suspension of €19 billion in EU funds due to rule of law concerns. Meizer’s involvement underscores the IMF’s ongoing dialogue with Hungarian authorities, represented by officials such as Márton Nagy of the Ministry for National Economy, on critical issues of monetary policy, structural reforms, and institutional strength.
