# Gábor Meizer

## Affiliations & Roles

- Participant in the IMF Article IV Consultation regarding Hungary's economy.

## Biography & Context

Gábor Meizer is identified as a key stakeholder involved in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. His involvement relates to the analysis of Hungary's economic landscape, which is currently facing stagnant output and inflation levels exceeding the 3% target. 

The context of his involvement includes discussions on high-quality fiscal adjustment, debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), and the maintenance of a tight monetary policy stance by the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB). His role intersects with critical structural reform priorities, including energy security, the elimination of state aid distortions, and governance reforms aimed at unlocking EU Cohesion funds.
