# Kelly MacKinnon Mukherjee

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Kelly MacKinnon Mukherjee is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). She was involved in the IMF staff team responsible for the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. Her work contributed to the analysis of Poland's economic recovery, which includes monitoring growth projections (estimated at 3.5% for 2025-2026), inflation trends, and the impact of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. 

Her involvement included evaluating Poland's fiscal position—specifically the widening deficit and the subsequent Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan—as well as assessing financial sector stability, structural reforms for labor supply and decarbonization, and the broader macroeconomic implications of regional migration.
