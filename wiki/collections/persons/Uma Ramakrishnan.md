## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Uma Ramakrishnan serves as the Director of the European Department at the [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]. In this capacity, she provides oversight for the IMF's surveillance and policy advice regarding European economies. Notably, her department was involved in the [[Poland]] 2024 Article IV Consultation, where the IMF assessed the country's robust economic recovery driven by domestic demand and [[Next Generation EU (NGEU)]] fund absorption.

Under her department's supervision, the IMF staff report highlighted significant fiscal pressures in Poland, including a general government deficit of 5.9% of GDP in 2024, which triggered the [[European Union]]'s Excessive Deficit Procedure. The report recommended frontloading fiscal consolidation to support disinflation and create space for monetary easing by the [[National Bank of Poland (NBP)]]. The IMF also emphasized structural reforms needed to address long-term challenges such as population aging, climate transition, and diminishing cost-competitiveness. Ramakrishnan's role involves ensuring that the IMF's policy recommendations align with broader European economic stability goals and sovereign credit sustainability frameworks.
