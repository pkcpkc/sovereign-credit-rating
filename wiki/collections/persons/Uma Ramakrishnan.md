# Uma Ramakrishnan

## Affiliations & Roles

- Approving Official at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Uma Ramakrishnan served as an Approving Official during the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. In this capacity, she oversaw the review of economic assessments regarding Poland's recovery phase, which included analyzing growth projections (estimated at 3.5% for 2025-2026), inflation trends, and the impact of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. Her role involved the oversight of reports concerning Poland's fiscal position, specifically the country's status under the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) and its Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan.
