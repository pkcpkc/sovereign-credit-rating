# Marterbauer

## Affiliations & Roles

- Minister of Finance at [[Austria]]

## Biography & Context

Marterbauer serves as the Minister of Finance for Austria. In the context of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, he acted as a key interlocutor for the IMF mission. He is involved in navigating Austria's economic recovery following a period of recession, specifically overseeing fiscal consolidation strategies aimed at reducing the national deficit below 2% of GDP. His role involves managing structural reforms, addressing demographic headwinds in the labor market, and coordinating with the Central Bank on financial stability and commercial real estate risks.
