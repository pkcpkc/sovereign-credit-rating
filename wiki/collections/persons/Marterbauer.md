## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Marterbauer serves as the Minister of Finance for Austria, playing a central role in the country's fiscal policy and economic management during a period of significant macroeconomic challenge. In 2024, under his ministry's purview, Austria experienced its second successive year of recession, with Real GDP falling by 1.3%. This economic contraction, driven by high energy prices, rapid interest rate hikes, and weak domestic and external demand, led to a widening fiscal deficit of 4.7% of GDP and a rise in public debt to 81% of GDP.

Following the formation of a new coalition government in March 2025, comprising the ÖVP, SPÖ, and NEOS, Marterbauer has been instrumental in prioritizing reforms to boost growth and reduce the fiscal deficit. The IMF mission, led by Fletcher and including staff members Oya Celasun and Bergljot Barkbu, conducted discussions with Marterbauer from March 26 to April 8, 2025. The IMF recommended a bold and well-designed fiscal consolidation package, including pension reforms, limits on public-sector wage increases, and increased reliance on property, inheritance, and excise taxes, to put the debt ratio on a declining path. Marterbauer's administration has committed to frontloaded consolidation measures and structural reforms aimed at boosting labor supply and enhancing long-term growth potential.
