## Biography & Context

Gábor Horváth is identified as a key representative of the Hungarian authorities during the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation. He participated in the official staff mission and subsequent discussions regarding Hungary's macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy adjustments, and structural reforms. His involvement centers on navigating the challenges of stagnant economic growth, high inflation, and the implementation of fiscal adjustments required to manage public debt and sovereign-bank linkages.
