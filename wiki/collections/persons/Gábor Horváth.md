# Gábor Horváth

## Affiliations & Roles

- Participant in the IMF Article IV Consultation for Hungary.

## Biography & Context

Gábor Horváth was involved in the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation regarding Hungary's economic standing. During this period, he was part of the group of stakeholders and officials engaged in discussions regarding Hungary's fiscal adjustment, monetary policy stance, and debt sustainability. The consultation focused on addressing stagnant output, high inflation, and the need for structural reforms to improve firm dynamism and energy security.
