## Biography & Context

Just is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Austria. As part of the mission team, Just contributed to the analysis and reporting on Austria's economic recovery, fiscal consolidation strategies, and financial stability risks during the consultation period of March 26 – April 8, 2025.
