## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Bartosz Swirski serves as the Chair of the National Broadcasting Council (KRRiT) in Poland, a role central to the country's media pluralism and regulatory framework. His tenure is situated within a broader context of significant, yet uneven, progress in addressing rule of law concerns through the government's Action Plan under Prime Minister Donald Tusk.

Swirski's role is critical in the ongoing efforts to reform media governance and ensure the independence of the National Broadcasting Council. The 2025 Rule of Law Report highlights that while politically biased content in public service media has decreased, challenges remain regarding the independence of the media regulator and the transparency of media ownership. The government has proposed a media law reform aimed at addressing these structural issues, with Swirski's leadership being key to implementing these changes.

Despite these positive steps, perceived judicial independence remains very low in Poland, creating a complex environment for regulatory bodies like the National Broadcasting Council. The report notes ongoing tensions with the Constitutional Tribunal, whose legitimacy is contested by the government, and the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ). Swirski operates in this environment where institutional checks and balances are being tested, with the Ombudsperson Waldemar Kowalewski playing a key role in monitoring these dynamics.
