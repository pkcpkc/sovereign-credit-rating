## Biography & Context

Rob Shearman is an analyst at [[Fitch Ratings]], where he contributes to the assessment of sovereign creditworthiness. His work involves utilizing the Sovereign Rating Model (SRM) and Qualitative Overlay (QO) frameworks to determine Issuer Default Ratings (IDRs). His expertise encompasses the analysis of structural features, macroeconomic performance, public finances, and external finances to evaluate sovereign risk.
