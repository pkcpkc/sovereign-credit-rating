## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Rob Shearman serves as an Analyst at [[Fitch Ratings]], contributing to the development and application of the firm's sovereign credit rating methodology. He is associated with the publication of the **Fitch Ratings Sovereign Rating Criteria (Effective 15 September 2025)**, which outlines the framework for assessing sovereign issuers' capacity and willingness to honor debt obligations.

In this capacity, Shearman works alongside colleagues such as [[James Longsdon]], [[Ed Parker]], and [[Carlos Masip]]. His role involves the analysis of key drivers of sovereign creditworthiness, including the **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM)** and the **Qualitative Overlay (QO)**. The methodology he helps uphold places significant weight on Structural Features (53.7%), followed by Public Finances, External Finances, and Macroeconomic Performance. The 2025 criteria also introduces new analytical components, such as **Sovereign Climate Vulnerability Signals (Climate.VS)** and enhanced monitoring of macro-prudential risks via the Macro-Prudential Indicator (MPI) and Banking Systemic Indicator (BSI), reflecting the evolving landscape of sovereign risk assessment.
