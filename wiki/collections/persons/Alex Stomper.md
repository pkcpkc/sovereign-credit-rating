# Alex Stomper

## Affiliations & Roles

- Presenter and Analyst (HU Berlin, 2024)
- Subject Matter Expert on S&P Sovereign Credit Rating Methodology

## Biography & Context

Alex Stomper is a figure involved in the analysis of sovereign credit rating frameworks, specifically focusing on the methodologies employed by S&P Global Ratings. In 2024, Stomper presented at HU Berlin, providing a detailed breakdown of the S&P 2017 methodology. This work highlights the five core pillars of sovereign assessment: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary. 

Stomper’s analysis emphasizes the tension between structured "indicative ratings" and the subjective discretionary judgment used by analysts. Key areas of focus include the assessment of contingent liabilities, the "Flows" and "Stocks" of fiscal flexibility, and the challenges of replicability in rating systems due to subjective criteria like "policy predictability" and "event risk." Stomper is also associated with a planned citizen science project aimed at documenting instances of non-replicability within the European context.
