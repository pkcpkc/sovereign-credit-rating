## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

KimEng Tan serves as an Analytical Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], playing a key role in the dissemination and implementation of the firm's sovereign rating frameworks. Tan is specifically cited as a primary point of contact for the **S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology** published in December 2017. This methodology provides the standardized framework for assessing the creditworthiness of sovereign governments, integrating five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments.

As an analytical contact for this methodology, Tan supports the application of criteria that determine indicative rating levels based on factors such as debt payment culture, external liquidity, fiscal sustainability (specifically net general government debt), and monetary policy credibility. The 2017 methodology, which Tan helps represent, introduced significant updates including a cap on local-currency rating uplifts and a shift to net debt metrics in fiscal assessments, reflecting a rigorous approach to sovereign credit risk analysis.
