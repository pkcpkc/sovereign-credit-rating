# KimEng Tan

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor to S&P Global Ratings methodology development.

## Biography & Context

KimEng Tan is associated with the development and publication of S&P Global Ratings' Sovereign Rating Methodology. They contributed to the framework used to assess the ability and willingness of sovereign states to service financial obligations to nonofficial creditors. Their work involves the evaluation of institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary pillars, including the integration of net general government debt and Current Account Payments (CAP) into global rating standards.
