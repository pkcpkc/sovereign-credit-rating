# Jacques Miniane

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[IMF]]

## Biography & Context

Jacques Miniane is a professional involved in the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. He participated in the IMF staff mission to Budapest between June and July 2025, contributing to the assessment of Hungary's economic stability, fiscal policy, and structural reforms. His work involves analyzing macroeconomic indicators such as GDP growth, inflation targets, and sovereign debt sustainability within the context of geoeconomic fragmentation.
