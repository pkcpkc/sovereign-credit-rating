# Jakree Koosakul

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Jakree Koosakul is an official associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. His work contributed to the assessment of Hungary's economic landscape, focusing on fiscal adjustments, monetary policy, and structural reforms. He participated in the staff mission to Budapest (June 2025) to evaluate issues such as stagnant output, high inflation, and the necessity of rebuilding fiscal buffers to manage public debt and productivity gaps.
