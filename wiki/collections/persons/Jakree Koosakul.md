# Jakree Koosakul

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Team Member at [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Jakree Koosakul is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), specifically involved in the Article IV Consultation for Hungary during the 2025 reporting period. Working as part of the staff team under the leadership of [[Anke Weber]], Koosakul contributed to the analysis of Hungary's economic trajectory, which includes navigating stagnant output, high inflation, and significant fiscal challenges. 

The work involved assessing Hungary's debt sustainability, evaluating the necessity of high-quality fiscal adjustments, and providing recommendations on monetary policy stances to anchor inflation. The team's findings emphasized the need for structural reforms in firm dynamism, energy security, and governance to improve the business environment and unlock EU funding.
