Fabio Signorini serves as the Senior Deputy Governor at the [[Bank of Italy]], playing a key role in the country's central banking apparatus during critical international assessments. His engagement aligns with the IMF's assessment of Italy's financial sector health and institutional strength. The consultation highlighted the Bank of Italy's strong supervisory capacity and the robustness of the Italian banking sector, which maintains high profitability and sound asset quality despite vulnerabilities among some Less Significant Institutions (LSIs). Signorini's role intersects with broader policy concerns regarding the sovereign-bank nexus, a key risk factor in Italy's credit profile, as well as the implementation of macroprudential policies and the management of contingent liabilities. His participation underscores the Bank of Italy's commitment to maintaining financial stability amidst high public debt levels and structural economic challenges, including slow productivity growth and demographic headwinds.
