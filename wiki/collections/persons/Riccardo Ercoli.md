# Riccardo Ercoli

## Affiliations & Roles

- Executive Director for Italy at [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Riccardo Ercoli serves as the Executive Director for Italy at the International Monetary Fund (IMF). In this capacity, he is involved in high-level oversight and representation concerning the Italian economy's fiscal policy, structural reforms, and macroeconomic stability. 

He played a key role during the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation for Italy, which assessed the nation's economic resilience, its progress on the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), and the challenges posed by demographic headwinds and productivity growth. His work involves coordinating with IMF staff and other international officials to monitor Italy's primary surplus targets, sovereign risk assessments, and the transition toward a more sustainable debt-to-GDP trajectory.

Additionally, Ercoli is associated with the IMF's 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta, which concluded in early 2025. This oversight included evaluating Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, fiscal consolidation strategies (including the phasing out of energy subsidies), and the implementation of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms to align with EU Pillar II requirements.
