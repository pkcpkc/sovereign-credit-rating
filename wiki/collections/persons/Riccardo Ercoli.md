# Riccardo Ercoli

## Affiliations & Roles

- Executive Director for Italy at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Riccardo Ercoli serves as the Executive Director for Italy at the International Monetary Fund (IMF). In this capacity, he represents Italian interests during high-level fiscal and macroeconomic evaluations, including the 2025 Article IV Consultation. His role involves overseeing the dialogue between the IMF staff and Italian officials regarding the country's fiscal discipline, debt sustainability, and the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP).

Additionally, as a member of the IMF Executive Board, he participated in the oversight and approval of the 2025 Malta Country Report (Article IV Consultation). In this context, he engaged with the report's findings regarding Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, fiscal consolidation targets to reduce the deficit to 2.6% of GDP by 2027, and the implementation of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II.
