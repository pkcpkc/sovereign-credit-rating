## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Cassellati, as President of the Chamber of Deputies, plays a pivotal role in Italy's political and institutional landscape, directly influencing the sovereign credit rating factors related to political stability and policy predictability. Under his leadership, the Chamber approved the draft constitutional reform aimed at enhancing governmental stability and introducing the direct election of the Prime Minister, a key strength identified in sovereign risk assessments.

However, his tenure is also marked by the intense parliamentary scrutiny and debate surrounding this reform, particularly its proposal to separate the careers of judges and prosecutors. This move has faced significant opposition from the judiciary, including critical opinions from the High Council for the Judiciary, highlighting the tension between executive efficiency and judicial independence.

Cassellati's role extends to overseeing media governance through the Parliamentary Committee for RAI Oversight. He is involved in the stalemate regarding the appointment of the RAI president and the broader structural reforms of the public broadcaster's funding and governance. The Budget Law for 2025, which maintained the RAI licence fee at EUR 90, was a critical decision under his purview that preserved the broadcaster's financial resources, a factor viewed positively by rating agencies for media sector stability.

Furthermore, as the presiding officer of the lower house, Cassellati is central to the legislative process regarding the Security Law and other measures impacting civic space. The adoption of the Security Law, which has raised concerns among international bodies like the UN and the Council of Europe regarding its impact on fundamental freedoms, occurred under his parliamentary oversight. His engagement with stakeholders, including the Minister for Institutional Reforms and various judicial heads, underscores his central position in navigating Italy's complex balance between institutional reform, anti-corruption efforts led by bodies like ANAC, and the protection of democratic norms.
