# Yueshu Zhao

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff member involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta.

## Biography & Context

Yueshu Zhao is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and contributed to the 2024 Article IV Consultation regarding Malta's economy. The work involved analyzing Malta's robust growth in export-oriented service industries, such as tourism and online gaming, while addressing structural challenges including high population density, infrastructure strain, and labor shortages. 

Zhao's involvement included the synthesis of policy recommendations concerning fiscal consolidation (specifically the phasing out of energy subsidies), Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms to align with EU Pillar II, and the management of high real estate exposure within the banking sector. The context of this work includes high-level discussions held in Valletta between November and December 2024, leading to the formal publication of the IMF Staff Report in January 2025.
