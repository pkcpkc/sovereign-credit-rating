# Yueshu Zhao

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member / Contributor at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Yueshu Zhao is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and contributed to the 2024 Article IV Consultation Staff Report for Malta. The report, published in January 2025, focused on Malta's economic growth, fiscal sustainability, and structural reforms. Zhao's work involved analyzing Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, the management of real estate risks in the banking sector, and the implementation of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II.
