# Ben Weyts

## Affiliations & Roles

- Representative of the Belgian Authorities during the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation.

## Biography & Context

Ben Weyts is a key figure within the Belgian government, identified as one of the primary authorities involved in the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation for Belgium. During the consultation period (January 22 – February 4, 2025), he participated in discussions regarding Belgium's economic trajectory, specifically focusing on fiscal consolidation, labor market reforms, and the management of public debt.

The consultations involved addressing significant structural headwinds, including the need to bring the national deficit below 3% of GDP and managing a public debt level that reached 104.1% of GDP in 2024. As part of the Belgian delegation, Weyts was involved in high-level dialogues regarding the seven-year adjustment period under the new EU economic governance framework, which aims to rationalize social spending and improve labor-cost competitiveness.
