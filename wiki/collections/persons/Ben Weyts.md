## Biography & Context

Ben Weyts is a prominent figure within the Belgian Federal Government, involved in the national administration during the 2025 fiscal and economic planning period. He is associated with the government's response to the International Monetary Fund (IMF) Article IV Consultation, which highlighted Belgium's need for sustained fiscal consolidation, labor market reforms, and structural adjustments to address high public debt and aging population pressures.
