## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Jeroen Clicq serves as an Executive Director at the [[IMF]], with specific oversight and coverage responsibilities for [[Belgium]]. In this capacity, he is a key figure in the executive oversight of the [[IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation: Belgium Staff Report and Press Release]], which concluded in March 2025.

His role involves high-level surveillance of Belgium's economic stability, focusing on critical issues such as fiscal sustainability, labor market reforms, and financial sector resilience. During the 2025 consultation, the IMF highlighted urgent challenges for Belgium, including public debt projected to rise to 123% of GDP by 2030 under unchanged policies. As an Executive Director covering the country, Clicq contributes to the assessment of Belgium's "moderate" sovereign stress risk, which is mitigated by strong institutional capacity and high-quality debt management despite rising debt burdens. The consultation emphasized the need for a seven-year fiscal adjustment plan under the new EU Economic Governance Framework, aiming to reduce the structural primary balance by approximately 0.6 percentage points of GDP annually until 2031. Clicq's oversight extends to evaluating the effectiveness of Belgium's coordinated efforts between federal and regional entities regarding climate goals and fiscal frameworks.
