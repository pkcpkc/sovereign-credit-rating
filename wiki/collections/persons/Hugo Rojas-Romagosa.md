# Hugo Rojas-Romagosa

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary.

## Biography & Context

Hugo Rojas-Romagosa is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. His work involved analyzing the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," which included stagnant output and persistent inflation above the 3% target. 

His contributions were part of a comprehensive staff mission that assessed Hungary's fiscal adjustment needs, debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), and the necessity of a tight monetary policy stance. The work highlighted structural reform priorities such as firm dynamism, energy security, and governance reforms aimed at unlocking EU funding.
