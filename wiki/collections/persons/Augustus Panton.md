# Augustus Panton

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Augustus Panton is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. He contributed to the assessment of the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," focusing on stagnant output, inflationary pressures, and the necessity of fiscal adjustments. His work involves analyzing sovereign-bank linkages, debt sustainability (projected 79% of GDP by 2030), and the structural reforms required to address productivity gaps and governance issues within the Hungarian economy.
