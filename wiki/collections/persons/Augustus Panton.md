## Biography & Context

Augustus Panton is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. His work involved analyzing the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," characterized by stagnant output and persistent inflation. 

Panton's involvement included assessing key economic indicators such as the projected GDP growth (0.7% in 2025, 2.0% in 2026), fiscal debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), and the necessity for a structural primary surplus. His role likely involved evaluating the Magyar Nemzeti Bank's (MNB) monetary policy stance, identifying vulnerabilities in corporate FX exposures and sovereign-bank linkages, and outlining structural reform priorities regarding energy security, firm dynamism, and governance reforms to unlock EU funding.
