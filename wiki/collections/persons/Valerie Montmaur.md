# Valerie Montmaur

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor/Analyst at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Valerie Montmaur is associated with the development and publication of S&P Global Ratings' sovereign debt methodologies. She contributed to the "Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)," which established a comprehensive framework for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness across five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments. Her work involved defining critical metrics such as net general government debt, current account payments (CAP), and the integration of Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA) scores to quantify contingent liabilities.
