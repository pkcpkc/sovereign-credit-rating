## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Valerie Montmaur serves as a Criteria Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], where she is involved in the development and maintenance of sovereign rating methodologies. She is listed as a key contact for the "Sovereign Rating Methodology" published and effective on December 18, 2017. This methodology provides the standardized framework for assessing the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities, replacing previous iterations from 2014. Her role entails overseeing the criteria that assess sovereign credit risk across five pillars: institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary assessments. This includes defining adjustments for local versus foreign currency ratings, fiscal metrics based on net general government debt, and the integration of contingent liabilities through the Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA).
