# Fuad Hasanov

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Participant in Malta Article IV Consultation (2025)

## Biography & Context

Fuad Hasanov is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation regarding Malta's economy. His work context includes analyzing Malta's economic growth, fiscal sustainability, and the transition toward a productivity-driven model. Key areas of focus during this period involved energy subsidy reforms, Corporate Income Tax (CIT) alignment with EU Pillar II, and managing macroprudential policies related to real estate exposure.
