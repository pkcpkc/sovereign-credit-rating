## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Fuad Hasanov serves as a Staff Member with the International Monetary Fund, contributing to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. The consultation, which concluded in late 2024 with the Executive Board's approval in January 2025, evaluated Malta’s macroeconomic stability, fiscal consolidation efforts, and structural reforms.

Hasanov’s work within the IMF mission team supported the assessment of key economic indicators, including Malta's strong external position, sound financial system, and high GDP per capita convergence with the EU average. The mission highlighted risks related to real estate exposure, labor shortages, and geopolitical spillovers, while endorsing the Maltese authorities' commitment to fiscal consolidation and exit from broad energy subsidies. The report emphasized the need for continued structural reforms in innovation, digitalization, and climate sustainability to support long-term growth.
