# Fuad Hasanov

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta.

## Biography & Context

Fuad Hasanov is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. His work contributed to the comprehensive economic assessment of Malta, which analyzed the country's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model. 

His involvement included discussions regarding Malta's fiscal policy, specifically the transition away from energy subsidies toward green investment and innovation. He was part of the team addressing critical structural issues such as Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform to align with EU Pillar II requirements, macroprudential policy adjustments to manage real estate risks in the banking sector, and strategies to address labor shortages and the "Malta Vision 2050" objectives.
