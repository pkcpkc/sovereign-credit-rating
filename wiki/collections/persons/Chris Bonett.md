## Biography & Context

Chris Bonett is identified as a key figure in the context of the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Malta. He is involved in the high-level economic and fiscal policy discussions concerning Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model. His role encompasses the navigation of critical structural reforms, including fiscal consolidation targets for 2027, the phasing out of fixed energy price models, and the implementation of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II. He is a stakeholder in the strategic dialogue regarding Malta's Vision 2050 and the nation's efforts to balance economic growth with social and environmental sustainability.
