# Chris Bonett

## Affiliations & Roles

- Stakeholder involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

## Biography & Context

Chris Bonett is identified as a key stakeholder in the context of the IMF's 2024 Article IV Consultation with Malta. This engagement involved high-level economic policy discussions held in Valletta between November 4 and 15, 2024. 

The discussions, which culminated in the IMF Staff Report published on January 15, 2025, focused on Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model. Key areas of involvement included addressing capacity constraints, fiscal consolidation through the phase-out of energy subsidies, and the development of a Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform roadmap to align with EU Pillar II requirements. Furthermore, the context involves managing macroprudential policies regarding real estate exposure in the banking sector and navigating the "Malta Vision 2050" objectives.
