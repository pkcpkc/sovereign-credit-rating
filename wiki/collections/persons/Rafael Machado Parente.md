# Rafael Machado Parente

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Rafael Machado Parente is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). He was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary, a mission aimed at assessing the country's macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy, and structural reforms. His work contributed to the analysis of Hungary's stagnant output, inflationary pressures, and the necessity of fiscal adjustments to manage sovereign debt and productivity gaps.
