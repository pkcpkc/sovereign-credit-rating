# Rafael Machado Parente

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary.

## Biography & Context

Rafael Machado Parente is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team responsible for the 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Hungary. His work involves analyzing the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," specifically focusing on stagnant output, inflation targets, and fiscal adjustment strategies. 

His contributions involve assessing the nation's debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), evaluating the Magyar Nemzeti Bank's (MNB) monetary policy stance, and identifying structural reform priorities such as firm dynamism, energy security, and governance improvements. He is part of the team that navigated complex geoeconomic fragmentation risks and sovereign-bank nexus vulnerabilities to produce the IMF Staff Report.
