# Koshy Mathai

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Consultant (associated with the 2025 Article IV Consultation - Belgium)

## Biography & Context

Koshy Mathai is identified as a key figure involved in the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium. His work contributed to the assessment of the Belgian economy, specifically regarding fiscal sustainability, labor market dynamics, and structural reforms. His involvement included evaluating the country's 7-year adjustment plan, the impact of automatic wage indexation on competitiveness, and the management of public debt and productivity gaps.
