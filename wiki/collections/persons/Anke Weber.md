## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Anke Weber served as the Mission Head for the International Monetary Fund (IMF) during the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Hungary. In this capacity, she led the staff mission that concluded discussions in Budapest in June 2025, culminating in a Staff Report completed in July 2025 and Executive Board conclusion in August 2025.

Weber’s mission characterized the Hungarian economy as being at a "challenging juncture," noting stagnant output over the preceding three years and inflation persistently above the 3 percent target set by the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB). The mission, under her leadership, emphasized urgent needs for high-quality fiscal adjustment to rebuild buffers against elevated public debt (projected to reach 79 percent of GDP by 2030) and high financing costs.

Key policy recommendations advanced by Weber’s mission included broadening the tax base, rationalizing energy subsidies, and strengthening fiscal governance, particularly regarding State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) and contingent liabilities. The mission advised the MNB to maintain a tight monetary policy stance into 2026 to durably return inflation to target, while urging the phasing out of distortive price and margin controls. The Hungarian authorities, including Minister Márton Nagy and Deputy Central Bank Governors, engaged with these findings, acknowledging the challenges but highlighting resilience through strong employment, pro-family measures, and investments in electric vehicle (EV) and battery production.
