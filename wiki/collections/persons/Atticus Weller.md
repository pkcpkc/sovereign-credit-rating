# Atticus Weller

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member (Article IV Consultation Team)

## Biography & Context

Atticus Weller is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) as part of the staff involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. His work involves analyzing Hungary's economic trajectory, focusing on the transition toward a high-quality fiscal adjustment and the maintenance of a tight monetary policy stance to combat inflation. 

His professional context includes assessing Hungary's debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), identifying structural reform priorities such as improving firm dynamism and energy security, and evaluating the risks of geoeconomic fragmentation and sovereign-bank linkages.
