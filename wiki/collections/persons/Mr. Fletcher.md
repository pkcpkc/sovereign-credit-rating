## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Mr. Fletcher served as the Mission Head for the International Monetary Fund's 2025 Article IV Consultation with Austria. In this capacity, he led the assessment of Austria's economic and financial stability, highlighting a challenging environment characterized by successive recessions in 2023 and 2024, followed by weak growth in 2025. The mission emphasized the need for substantial fiscal consolidation to address a deficit of 4.7% of GDP and rising public debt of 81.2% of GDP, driven by demographic aging and green transition costs. Mr. Fletcher's team advocated for bold medium-term adjustments to reduce the deficit to below 2% of GDP through pension reforms and tax mix improvements. Additionally, the mission addressed financial sector vulnerabilities, particularly risks in Commercial Real Estate (CRE) and the expiration of borrower-based mortgage measures, recommending permanent prudent lending standards. The consultation also focused on structural reforms to boost labor supply and productivity amidst demographic headwinds.
