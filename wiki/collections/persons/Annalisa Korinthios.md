# Annalisa Korinthios

## Affiliations & Roles

- **Role**: Senior Advisor to Executive Director
- **Organization**: [[International Monetary Fund]]
- **Countries Covered**: Italy

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Annalisa Korinthios serves as a Senior Advisor to the Executive Director at the International Monetary Fund (IMF), with a specific focus on sovereign risk and economic policy in Italy. In the context of the IMF’s 2025 Article IV Consultation, she supports the Executive Director in overseeing the assessment of Italy’s macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy, and structural reforms.

Her involvement is tied to the broader IMF staff team's analysis of Italy's economic resilience and challenges. Key areas of focus under this advisory role include:
- **Fiscal Sustainability**: Monitoring Italy's public debt levels (135.3% of GDP in 2024) and the implementation of the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP). The IMF staff, under the guidance of the Executive Director's office, recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment to achieve a primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027.
- **Structural Reforms**: Assessing the progress of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), which had achieved 54% of milestones by the consultation period, while noting sluggish spending execution.
- **Financial Stability**: Evaluating the health of the Italian banking sector, including capital buffers, non-performing loans, and the sovereign-bank nexus.

Korinthios collaborates with IMF Staff Team Head [[Lone Christiansen]] and other department heads such as [[Helge Berger]] and [[Rishi Goyal]] to formulate policy recommendations and conduct surveillance of Italy's economy. She also interacts with Italian officials, including Minister of Economy and Finance [[Giancarlo Giorgetti]] and Senior Deputy Governor of the Bank of Italy [[Fabio Signorini]], during consultations to discuss economic outlooks and policy implementation.
