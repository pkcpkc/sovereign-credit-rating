# Mark Horton

## Affiliations & Roles

- Member of the IMF Mission to Belgium
- Member of the IMF Staff for the 2025 Malta Country Report (Article IV Consultation)

## Biography & Context

Mark Horton is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) as part of the mission team conducting the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium. His work involves evaluating the Belgian economy's resilience to shocks, analyzing fiscal sustainability, and assessing structural challenges such as automatic wage indexation and labor market productivity.

Additionally, Horton contributed to the IMF 2025 Malta Country Report. In this capacity, he was involved in analyzing Malta's economic growth, fiscal consolidation efforts (targeting a 2.6% deficit by 2027), and the transition toward a productivity-driven growth model. His work on the Malta report involved evaluating energy subsidy reforms, Corporate Income Tax (CIT) alignment with EU Pillar II, and macroprudential policies regarding real estate exposure.
