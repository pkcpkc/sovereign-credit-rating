# Mark Horton

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Belgium.
- Approver for [[Geremia Palomba]] regarding the IMF (2025) Malta Country Report.

## Biography & Context

Mark Horton is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). He was involved in the 2025 Article IV consultation regarding Belgium's economic landscape, where his work contributed to the assessment of fiscal sustainability, focusing on structural primary balance reductions and the rationalization of public spending. His analysis included challenges such as automatic wage indexation, labor-market efficiency, and the transition toward green energy and fiscal consolidation under the new EU economic governance framework.

Additionally, Horton played a key role in the IMF (2025) Malta Country Report. In this capacity, he served as an approver for the report which analyzed Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model. His work in this context involved overseeing assessments of Malta's fiscal policy—specifically the phasing out of energy subsidies—corporate income tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II, and the implementation of macroprudential policies to manage real estate exposures in the banking sector.
