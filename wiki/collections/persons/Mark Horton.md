# Mark Horton

## Affiliations & Roles

- Member of the IMF Mission to Belgium

## Biography & Context

Mark Horton is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) as part of the mission team conducting the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium. His work involves evaluating the Belgian economy's resilience to shocks, analyzing fiscal sustainability, and assessing structural challenges such as automatic wage indexation and labor market productivity.
