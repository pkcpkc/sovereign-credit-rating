# Maciej Onoszko

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2024 and 2025 Article IV Consultations for Poland and Romania.

## Biography & Context

Maciej Onoszko is an IMF staff member who has played a key role in the International Monetary Fund's economic assessments of Eastern European economies, specifically Poland and Romania. 

In his work regarding Poland (2024), he contributed to the analysis of the country's economic recovery, fiscal position, and structural challenges. His contributions included assessing the impact of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds on domestic demand, monitoring progress under the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP), and analyzing the transition toward a 60% debt-to-GDP ratio. His reporting also covered inflation management, labor market dynamics (including refugee integration), and energy infrastructure decarbonization.

More recently, Onoszko was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. In this capacity, he contributed to a comprehensive staff report evaluating Romania's fiscal consolidation efforts—including VAT rate increases and public sector wage freezes—and the country's "twin deficits" (fiscal and current account). His work touched upon monitoring the sovereign-bank nexus, assessing the resilience of the banking sector, and analyzing structural reforms related to EU fund absorption, labor force participation, and the transition to a low-carbon economy.
