## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Maciej Onoszko serves as a Resident Representative for the International Monetary Fund (IMF), with responsibilities covering both Poland and Romania. His role involves close interaction with national governments and central banks to monitor macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy implementation, and structural reforms.

In Poland, Onoszko was part of the IMF mission team led by Jan Kees Martijn that concluded its 2024 Article IV Consultation discussions in October 2024. The mission assessed Poland's robust economic recovery, noting growth of an estimated 2.8% in 2024 driven by private consumption and Next Generation EU (NGEU) fund absorption. Key policy dialogues focused on managing a fiscal deficit projected to reach 5.9% of GDP in 2024, with recommendations for frontloading fiscal consolidation to rebuild buffers. The IMF emphasized the importance of the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) and identified risks related to legacy CHF mortgage litigation and high defense spending. Onoszko's work supports the IMF's assessment of Poland's sovereign credit profile, highlighting strengths such as strong institutions, a credible commitment to debt repayment, and resilient banking sector capitalization.

Regarding Romania, Onoszko contributes to the IMF's oversight of the country's macroeconomic and financial policies. The 2025 Article IV Consultation highlighted significant challenges, including a fiscal deficit rising to 8.7% of GDP in 2024 due to pension reforms and public wage increases. Onoszko's engagement involves monitoring the government's adoption of a large fiscal consolidation package, including VAT rate hikes, aimed at reducing the deficit to below 3% by 2031. The mission also addressed risks related to the sovereign-bank nexus, unhedged foreign exchange loans, and slow absorption of EU funds. Onoszko works alongside other IMF staff to assess Romania's sovereign credit risk, noting the country's investment-grade rating despite concerns over fiscal execution and inflation dynamics.
