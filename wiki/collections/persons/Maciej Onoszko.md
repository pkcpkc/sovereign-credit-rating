# Maciej Onoszko

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Maciej Onoszko is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). He participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation mission to Romania, which evaluated the country's economic performance, fiscal sustainability, and structural reform progress. His work involved analyzing Romania's "twin deficits," inflationary pressures resulting from energy price cap removals, and the nation's progress in absorbing Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds.
