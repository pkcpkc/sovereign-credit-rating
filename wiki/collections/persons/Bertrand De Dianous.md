## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Bertrand De Dianous serves as a Criteria Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], playing a key role in the development and maintenance of the firm's sovereign rating methodologies. He was listed as a primary contact for the publication of the **S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology** in December 2017, which replaced the previous 2014 framework and integrated the 2013 Monetary Authorities methodology.

In this capacity, De Dianous is associated with the standardized framework used to assess the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities. The 2017 methodology he helped oversee is built on five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments. Key updates under this methodology include:

*   **Local vs. Foreign Currency Ratings**: Limiting the potential uplift for local-currency ratings to one notch above foreign-currency ratings (previously two).
*   **Fiscal Assessment**: Shifting the primary metric to focus on the change in *net* general government debt rather than gross debt.
*   **External Assessment**: Utilizing Current Account Payments (CAP) instead of Current Account Receipts (CAR) for certain ratios when external liquid assets exceed external debt.
*   **Institutional Assessment**: Explicitly adjusting for "debt payment culture" and "external security risks."

His work contributes to S&P's approach in evaluating factors such as debt sustainability, external liquidity, contingent liabilities (including banking sector risks via the Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment), and monetary policy credibility.
