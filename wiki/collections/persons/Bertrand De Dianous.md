# Bertrand De Dianous

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor to S&P Global Ratings methodology development.

## Biography & Context

Bertrand De Dianous is associated with the development and publication of S&P Global Ratings' "Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)". His work involves the framework for assessing sovereign creditworthiness, specifically focusing on the five pillars of assessment: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary. 

His contributions relate to the technical methodology used to evaluate net general government debt, current account payments (CAP), and the integration of Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA) scores to quantify contingent liabilities.
