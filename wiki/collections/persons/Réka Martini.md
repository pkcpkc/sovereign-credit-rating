# Réka Martini

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Economic Consultant (involved in Hungary Article IV Consultation)

## Biography & Context

Réka Martini is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. Her work contributed to the analysis of the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," characterized by stagnant output and inflation exceeding the 3% target. 

She participated in the staff mission in Budapest (starting June 5, 2025) and the subsequent discussions with Hungarian officials, including key figures such as [[Márton Nagy]], [[Máté Lóga]], and [[Károly Mátrai]]. Her involvement included the preparation of the IMF Staff Report, which addressed critical issues such as high-quality fiscal adjustment, debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), and the necessity for a tight monetary policy stance to anchor inflation. The resulting analysis emphasized structural reforms regarding firm dynamism, energy security, and governance to unlock EU funding and improve the business environment.
