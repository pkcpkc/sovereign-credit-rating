## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Joong Shik Kang served as the Mission Head for the International Monetary Fund's 2025 Article IV Consultation with Romania. His mission, which took place in September 2025, focused on assessing the country's macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy, and sovereign credit profile. The consultation highlighted Romania's critical juncture regarding fiscal consolidation, with the government adopting a comprehensive reform package including VAT hikes to reduce the fiscal deficit from 8.7% of GDP in 2024 to below 6% in 2026. Kang's team evaluated risks related to a potential sovereign credit rating downgrade, driven by concerns over fiscal execution and public debt sustainability. The mission also examined monetary policy, noting the National Bank of Romania's cautious stance on interest rates amidst high inflation, and assessed the banking sector's resilience despite vulnerabilities such as unhedged foreign exchange loans.
