# Joong Shik Kang

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of mission team at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Joong Shik Kang served as the Head of the IMF mission team for the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Romania. In this capacity, he oversaw the evaluation of Romania's economic performance, focusing on fiscal consolidation efforts, inflationary pressures, and structural reforms. His work involved coordinating with Romanian government officials to assess the impact of fiscal reform packages, the "sovereign-bank nexus," and the country's progress in absorbing EU funds.
