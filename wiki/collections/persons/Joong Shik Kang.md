# Joong Shik Kang

## Affiliations & Roles

- Mission Lead for the IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Romania

## Biography & Context

Joong Shik Kang served as the Mission Lead for the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff discussions with Romanian officials regarding the 2025 Article IV Consultation. In this capacity, he oversaw the evaluation of Romania's economic landscape, focusing on fiscal consolidation, inflation management, and banking sector resilience. 

The mission was instrumental in assessing Romania's "twin deficits" and the government's fiscal reform package, which included VAT rate increases and public sector wage freezes. Kang's work contributed to the IMF's findings on the "sovereign-bank nexus," banking soundness, and the structural reforms necessary to accelerate the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds.
