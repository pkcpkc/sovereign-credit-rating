# Yao Deng

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member at [[IMF]] (International Monetary Fund)

## Biography & Context

Yao Deng is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and contributed to the 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Italy. This work involved analyzing Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges such as low productivity growth and demographic headwinds. The analysis supported the assessment of Italy's primary surplus, NRRP implementation, and sovereign risk profile.
