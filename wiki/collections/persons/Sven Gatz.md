# Sven Gatz

## Affiliations & Roles

- Government Representative at [[Belgium Authorities]] (involved in IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation)

## Biography & Context

Sven Gatz is identified as a key figure within the Belgian government involved in the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation. During the consultation period of January 22 – February 4, 2025, he was among the Belgian officials engaged in discussions with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) regarding Belgium's economic trajectory.

The discussions centered on critical macroeconomic themes including fiscal consolidation, the management of public debt (which reached 104.1% of GDP in 2024), and structural reforms to address labor-cost competitiveness. Gatz was part of the cohort of officials representing Belgium's interests as the IMF assessed the country's fiscal sustainability, labor market efficiency—specifically regarding automatic wage indexation—and the transition toward a green economy.
