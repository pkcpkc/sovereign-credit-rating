## Biography & Context

Mooi is identified as a key member of the IMF staff team involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic. In this capacity, Mooi contributed to the development of the IMF Staff Report and subsequent press releases concerning Slovakia's economic growth, fiscal consolidation plans (2025-2028), and structural reforms. The work involved analyzing Slovakia's transition toward a 4.7% headline deficit, assessing the resilience of the banking system against real estate vulnerabilities, and addressing long-term challenges such as labor productivity, demographic shifts, and anti-corruption frameworks.
