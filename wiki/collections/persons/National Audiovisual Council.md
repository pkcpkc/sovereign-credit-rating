## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

The National Audiovisual Council (CNA) serves as the primary regulatory body for Romania's audiovisual sector, playing a critical role in the nation's institutional integrity and media freedom landscape. According to the 2025 Rule of Law Report issued by the European Commission, the CNA has demonstrated operational activity, notably issuing decisions on electoral campaigns in February 2025. However, the agency operates within a challenging environment characterized by significant threats to media pluralism and independence.

Key concerns highlighted in the report include the lack of progress in enhancing the independent governance of public service media, which falls under the CNA's purview. The regulatory landscape is further strained by opaque financing of private media by political parties, insufficient transparency in media ownership structures, and ongoing harassment of journalists. The CNA's effectiveness is contextualized by broader legislative unpredictability and the quality of law-making, which impacts its ability to enforce regulations consistently. While the agency remains a key component of Romania's institutional checks and balances, its operational environment is influenced by political pressure on editorial independence and strategic lawsuits against public participation (SLAPPs) targeting journalists.
