# Claudia Mastrapasqua

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta.

## Biography & Context

Claudia Mastrapasqua is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and contributed to the 2024 Article IV Consultation regarding Malta's economic landscape. Her work involves analyzing Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, addressing challenges such as high population density, infrastructure strain, and labor shortages. 

Her involvement includes monitoring key economic indicators, including real GDP growth projections, fiscal consolidation efforts (specifically the phasing out of energy subsidies), and financial stability measures related to real estate exposure in the banking sector. Additionally, she is linked to the oversight of structural reforms, such as the Corporate Income Tax (CIT) roadmap aligned with EU Pillar II, and the assessment of Malta's "Vision 2050" objectives.
