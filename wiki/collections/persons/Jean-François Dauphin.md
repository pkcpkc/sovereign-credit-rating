# Jean-François Dauphin

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of IMF Staff Team at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Jean-François Dauphin served as the Head of the IMF Staff Team for the 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Belgium. In this capacity, he led the technical assessment and discussions with Belgian officials between January 22 and February 4, 2025. His work contributed to the final IMF Staff Report (completed March 3, 2025) and the subsequent press release of the consultation results on March 20, 2025.

His role involved evaluating Belgium's economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and structural challenges, including the management of public debt (which reached 104.1% of GDP in 2024), the impact of automatic wage indexation on labor-cost competitiveness, and the requirements for fiscal consolidation under the new EU economic governance framework.
