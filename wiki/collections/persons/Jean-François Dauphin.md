## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Jean-François Dauphin served as the Mission Chief for the [[International Monetary Fund]]'s 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[Belgium]]. In this capacity, he led the staff team responsible for analyzing Belgium's economic resilience amidst multiple shocks, focusing on fiscal sustainability and structural labor market challenges. Under his leadership, the mission concluded that while Belgium's financial sector remains stable with moderate systemic risks, the country faces urgent needs for fiscal consolidation due to high public debt (104.1% of GDP in 2024).

The consultation, finalized in March 2025, highlighted the necessity for a seven-year fiscal adjustment plan under the new [[European Commission]] Economic Governance Framework. Dauphin's mission recommended an annual reduction in the structural primary balance of approximately 0.6 percentage points of GDP to bring the deficit durably below 3% and put debt on a downward path. The report emphasized the importance of coordinating federal and regional efforts, particularly regarding climate targets and the revitalization of the fiscal council, while also addressing structural impediments to growth such as automatic wage indexation and sluggish labor productivity.
