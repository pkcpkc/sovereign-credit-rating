# Jean-François Dauphin

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of IMF Mission at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Jean-François Dauphin served as the Head of the IMF Mission during the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium. In this capacity, he led the discussions between the International Monetary Fund and the Belgian authorities, focusing on the country's fiscal sustainability, labor market dynamics, and structural reforms. His work involved evaluating Belgium's 7-year adjustment plan, analyzing the impact of automatic wage indexation on international competitiveness, and assessing the country's public debt trajectory, which is projected to reach 123% of GDP by 2030.
