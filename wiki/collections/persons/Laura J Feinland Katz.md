## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Laura J Feinland Katz serves as a Criteria Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], playing a key role in the development and maintenance of the firm's rating methodologies. She is specifically listed as a contact for the **S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)**, which was published and became effective on December 18, 2017. In this capacity, she is associated with the framework used to assess the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities regarding their ability and willingness to service financial obligations to nonofficial creditors. The methodology outlines a standardized framework based on five pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments, and includes specific guidelines on local versus foreign currency rating caps and the treatment of contingent liabilities.
