# Laura J Feinland Katz

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor at [[S&P Global Ratings]]

## Biography & Context

Laura J Feinland Katz is associated with S&P Global Ratings, specifically contributing to the development and publication of sovereign credit rating methodologies. She played a role in the 2017 Sovereign Rating Methodology, which established a comprehensive framework for assessing the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities based on five pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments.
