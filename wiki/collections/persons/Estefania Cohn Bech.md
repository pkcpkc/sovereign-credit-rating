# Estefania Cohn Bech

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary.

## Biography & Context

Estefania Cohn Bech is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) as part of the staff team responsible for the 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Hungary. Her work involved analyzing the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," focusing on stagnant output, high inflation, and the necessity of high-quality fiscal adjustment. 

Her contributions relate to the assessment of Hungary's debt sustainability, where she helped identify the need for a structural primary surplus of approximately 1.75% of GDP. She also participated in the evaluation of Hungary's monetary policy stance, specifically advising on the maintenance of restrictive interest rates and the sustainability of price and margin caps. Additionally, her work encompassed structural reform priorities such as firm dynamism, energy security, and the governance reforms required to unlock EU Cohesion funds.
