# Estefania Cohn Bech

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Estefania Cohn Bech is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). She participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary, contributing to the assessment of the Hungarian economy's fiscal health, monetary policy, and structural reform requirements. Her work involved analyzing the country's debt sustainability, inflation targets, and the impact of geoeconomic fragmentation on regional stability.
