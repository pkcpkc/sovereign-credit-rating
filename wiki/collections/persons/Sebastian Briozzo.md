# Sebastian Briozzo

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor to S&P Global Ratings methodology development.

## Biography & Context

Sebastian Briozzo is associated with the development and publication of the S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017). His work involves establishing frameworks for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness across five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments. This includes the implementation of methodologies for net general government debt, current account payments (CAP), and the integration of Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA) scores to quantify contingent liabilities.
