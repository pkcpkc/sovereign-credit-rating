## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Sebastian Briozzo serves as an Analytical Contact for [[S&P Global Ratings]], playing a key role in the firm's sovereign credit rating operations. He is listed among the primary analytical contacts for the [[S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)]], a critical framework that outlines the standardized assessment of sovereign governments and monetary authorities regarding their ability and willingness to service financial obligations.

His involvement aligns with the methodology's core pillars, which include institutional, economic, external, fiscal, and monetary assessments. The 2017 methodology updates, for which Briozzo provided analytical contact support, introduced significant changes such as capping local-currency rating uplifts to one notch above foreign-currency ratings and shifting the fiscal assessment focus to net general government debt. As an analytical contact, he likely supports the application of these criteria, including the integration of the Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA) and the evaluation of contingent liabilities, to determine indicative rating levels for sovereign entities.
