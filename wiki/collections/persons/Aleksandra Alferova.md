# Aleksandra Alferova

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary.

## Biography & Context

Aleksandra Alferova is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff mission conducted in Hungary during mid-2025. As part of the team responsible for the Article IV Consultation, she contributed to the analysis of the Hungarian economy's "challenging juncture," characterized by stagnant output and persistent inflation.

Her work involved evaluating the country's fiscal adjustment needs, debt sustainability analysis (DSA), and the effectiveness of the Magyar Nemzeti Bank's (MNB) monetary policy. The team's findings emphasized the necessity of high-quality fiscal adjustment, structural reforms to improve firm dynamism, and the management of geoeconomic risks and sovereign-bank linkages.
