# Aleksandra Alferova

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Aleksandra Alferova is an expert involved in the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. She contributed to the assessment of the Hungarian economy, which is currently navigating a period of stagnant growth and elevated inflation. Her work involved evaluating fiscal sustainability, monetary policy stances, and the structural reforms necessary to address productivity gaps and governance issues within the region.
