# Jenny Lee

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Jenny Lee is an expert involved in the IMF's Article IV Consultation for Italy (2024-2025). She contributed to the analysis of the Italian economy, focusing on fiscal consolidation, the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), and structural reforms. Her work encompassed assessing Italy's primary surplus targets, productivity challenges, demographic headwinds, and sovereign risk profiles.
