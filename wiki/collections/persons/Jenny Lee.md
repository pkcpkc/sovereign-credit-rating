# Jenny Lee

- **Role**: IMF Staff Member

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Jenny Lee served as an IMF Staff Member on the 2025 Article IV Consultation team for Italy. As part of the mission led by [[Lone Christiansen]], Lee contributed to the assessment of Italy’s economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and financial sector stability. The consultation, conducted under the framework of the [[International Monetary Fund]], analyzed critical sovereign risk factors including Italy’s high public debt-to-GDP ratio (135.3% in 2024), weak productivity growth, and the sovereign-bank nexus. Lee’s work involved evaluating the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) and the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), providing staff recommendations aimed at ensuring a downward debt trajectory and addressing structural challenges such as population aging and low female labor force participation.
