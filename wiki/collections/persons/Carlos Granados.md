As the Senior Resident Representative for [[Romania]] at the [[International Monetary Fund]], [[Carlos Granados]] plays a pivotal role in monitoring and facilitating the country's macroeconomic stability and sovereign credit profile. His tenure coincides with a critical juncture in Romania's economic trajectory, marked by the 2025 Article IV Consultation.

[[Carlos Granados]] is instrumental in overseeing the implementation of a comprehensive fiscal consolidation package adopted by the Romanian government, which includes VAT rate hikes and tax reforms aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit to below 6 percent of GDP by 2026. He works closely with mission heads and executive directors to assess the sustainability of public finances, particularly regarding rising public debt-to-GDP ratios and high gross financing needs.

His responsibilities include evaluating the risks associated with the sovereign-bank nexus, unhedged foreign exchange loans, and the absorption of EU funds. [[Carlos Granados]] facilitates dialogue between IMF staff and key Romanian policymakers, including Prime Minister [[Ilie Bolojan]], Finance Minister [[Alexandru Nazare]], and National Bank of Romania Governor [[Mugur Isarescu]], to ensure alignment on inflation targeting, monetary policy, and structural reforms necessary for long-term debt sustainability.
