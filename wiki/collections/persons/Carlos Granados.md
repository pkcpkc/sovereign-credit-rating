# Carlos Granados

## Affiliations & Roles

- Senior Regional Resident Representative at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Carlos Granados serves as a Senior Regional Resident Representative for the International Monetary Fund (IMF). In this capacity, he is involved in high-level oversight and coordination regarding Romania's economic performance and fiscal sustainability. He maintains a professional relationship with [[Mugur Isarescu]], the Governor of the National Bank of Romania, facilitating discussions on macroeconomic policy, inflation management, and the country's fiscal consolidation efforts.
