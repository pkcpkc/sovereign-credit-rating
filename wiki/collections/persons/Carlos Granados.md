# Carlos Granados

## Affiliations & Roles

- Senior Resident Representative to [[Mugur Isarescu]] at the International Monetary Fund (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Carlos Granados serves as a Senior Resident Representative within the International Monetary Fund (IMF). In this capacity, he is involved in high-level economic oversight and consultation, specifically regarding the Article IV Consultation for Romania. His work involves monitoring Romania's fiscal consolidation efforts, inflation management, and the stability of the banking sector. He plays a key role in navigating the complexities of the "sovereign-bank nexus" and advising on structural reforms to improve labor force participation and EU fund absorption.
