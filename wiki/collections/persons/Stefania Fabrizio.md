# Stefania Fabrizio

## Affiliations & Roles

- Participant in IMF Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic

## Biography & Context

Stefania Fabrizio is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic. Her work context includes evaluating Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024. She participated in the assessment of the country's ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) and the analysis of structural headwinds such as geoeconomic fragmentation, aging demographics, and low labor productivity. Her professional involvement encompasses monitoring the resilience of the Slovak financial sector, evaluating macroprudential policies regarding real estate, and reviewing government strategies for debt sustainability and anti-corruption frameworks.
