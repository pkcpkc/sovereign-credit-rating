# Stefania Fabrizio

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Official (associated with the 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic)

## Biography & Context

Stefania Fabrizio is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the preparation and assessment of the 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic. Her work contributed to the analysis of Slovakia's economic recovery, fiscal consolidation plans (2025-2028), and structural reforms regarding productivity, demographics, and energy security. She was part of the team that finalized the IMF Staff Report on March 3, 2025, which evaluated the country's transition toward inflation targets and debt stabilization.
