## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Manuela Goretti serves as the Mission Head for the [[International Monetary Fund]]'s Article IV Consultation with France. In this capacity, she led the IMF staff mission that concluded in July 2025, overseeing the assessment of France's macroeconomic stability, fiscal policy, and structural reforms. Goretti's work focused on analyzing France's high public debt, which reached 113.1% of GDP in 2024, and recommending credible fiscal measures to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029. Her mission emphasized the need for structural reforms to boost productivity, particularly in the green and digital transitions, and highlighted the resilience of the French banking sector despite risks from corporate indebtedness and commercial real estate. Goretti's interactions with key French officials, including Minister [[Lombard]] and Prime Minister [[Barnier]], were central to negotiating the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) and ensuring alignment with IMF recommendations on debt sustainability and economic growth.
