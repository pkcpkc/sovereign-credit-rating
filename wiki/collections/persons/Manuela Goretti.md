# Manuela Goretti

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Manuela Goretti served as the Head of the IMF mission for the 2025 Article IV consultation with France. Leading the mission during the conclusion of staff discussions with French officials in May 2025, she oversaw the comprehensive assessment of France's economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and financial sector health. Her work contributed to the IMF Staff Report and Executive Board Assessment, which highlighted France's economic resilience in 2024 despite high uncertainty, while underscoring urgent challenges regarding rising public debt (113.1% of GDP) and fiscal slippages.

Under her leadership, the mission emphasized the need for credible, frontloaded fiscal consolidation under the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029. The assessment also addressed structural reforms to boost productivity, the resilience of the financial sector, and long-term pressures on the pension system and local government fiscal oversight.
