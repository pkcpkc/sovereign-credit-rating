## Biography & Context

Fletcher served as the Head of mission team for the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Austria. During this period, Fletcher led the staff team responsible for evaluating Austria's economic landscape, specifically focusing on fiscal consolidation strategies, labor supply challenges due to demographic aging, and financial stability risks related to the commercial real estate sector.
