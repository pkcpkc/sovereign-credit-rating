## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

As the [[International Monetary Fund]] Mission Head for Austria, Fletcher led the Article IV Consultation with the Austrian government in early 2025. The mission, conducted from March 26 to April 8, 2025, focused on assessing the country's macroeconomic stability and fiscal policy amidst a challenging economic environment. Fletcher oversaw discussions with key Austrian officials, including Finance Minister [[Marterbauer]] and Central Bank Governor [[Holzmann]], to address the impacts of two successive years of recession, a widening fiscal deficit, and rising public debt.

The mission emphasized the need for bold fiscal consolidation, including pension reforms and limits on public-sector wage increases, to ensure debt sustainability. Fletcher's team also evaluated the health of the banking sector, noting strong capitalization but highlighting risks from Commercial Real Estate exposures. The final report, completed in June 2025 and concluded by the Executive Board in July 2025, provided recommendations for structural reforms to boost labor supply and productivity, aiming to secure a recovery from 2026 onwards.
