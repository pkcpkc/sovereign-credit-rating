# Ezequiel Cabezon

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania.

## Biography & Context

Ezequiel Cabezon is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. His work contributed to the analysis of Romania's economic landscape, specifically focusing on the country's fiscal consolidation efforts, inflation management, and banking sector resilience.

The consultation involved evaluating Romania's new fiscal reform package for 2025–26, which included VAT rate increases and public sector wage freezes aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit. Cabezon's context includes the assessment of "twin deficits," the monitoring of the sovereign-bank nexus, and the evaluation of structural reforms necessary to accelerate the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds.
