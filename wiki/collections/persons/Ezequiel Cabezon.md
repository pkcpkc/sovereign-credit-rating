# Ezequiel Cabezon

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Ezequiel Cabezon is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. He contributed to the assessment of Romania's macroeconomic performance, specifically focusing on fiscal consolidation, inflationary pressures, and the structural reforms necessary to manage "twin deficits" and absorb EU funds.
