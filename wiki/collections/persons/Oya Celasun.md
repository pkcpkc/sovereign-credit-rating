## Biography & Context

Oya Celasun is involved with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) regarding the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Austria. She played a key role in the oversight of the economic assessment, specifically approving the report for Bergljot Barkbu. Her work involves analyzing Austria's economic recovery, fiscal consolidation strategies, and systemic risks within the commercial real estate sector.
