# Gee Hee Hong

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Gee Hee Hong is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy. In this capacity, Hong contributed to the assessment of Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges, including productivity growth and demographic headwinds. The work involved evaluating Italy's primary surplus achievements, NRRP implementation progress, and the stability of the Italian banking sector.
