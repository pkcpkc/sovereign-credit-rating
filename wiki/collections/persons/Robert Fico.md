# Robert Fico

## Affiliations & Roles

- Prime Minister of [[Slovakia]]

## Biography & Context

Robert Fico serves as the Prime Minister of Slovakia. Under his leadership, the Slovakian government has navigated a complex regulatory landscape regarding the EU's Rule of Law requirements. His administration has overseen the engagement in dialogues to align national criminal codes with the EU's Public Interest Fund (PIF) Directive. 

However, his tenure is marked by significant scrutiny from the European Commission regarding judicial independence and media freedom. Key issues associated with his government's actions include the perceived "chilling effect" on the judiciary due to criminal liability for judicial decisions, the expansion of the Prosecutor General's powers, and the establishment of the Slovak Television and Radio (STVR) entity, which critics argue reduces editorial independence. His administration has also been noted for the frequent use of fast-track legislative procedures and the implementation of new legal frameworks imposing heavy reporting obligations on NGOs.
