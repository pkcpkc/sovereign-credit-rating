## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

As Acting Governor of the Central Bank of Malta, Alexander Demarco oversees monetary policy and financial sector stability during the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). The consultation, concluded in late 2024 with a staff report issued in early 2025, evaluated Malta’s macroeconomic performance and policy framework.

Under Demarco’s leadership, the Central Bank of Malta has contributed to maintaining a sound and stable financial system characterized by strong capital and liquidity buffers. A key focus of the IMF staff appraisal has been the management of risks associated with the banking sector’s substantial exposure to real estate, including residential, commercial, and construction segments. The IMF has advised the Central Bank to strengthen macroprudential policies, specifically by raising the sectoral systemic risk buffer rate and broadening its scope to cover non-residential real estate, to mitigate potential housing market shocks.

On the monetary policy front, Demarco’s tenure has seen inflationfalling to approximately 2%, aligning with Euro Area averages. However, the Central Bank remains vigilant against persistent inflationary pressures in the service sector, driven by strong tourism demand and tight labor markets. The use of the euro provides a stable monetary policy framework via the European Central Bank, though Demarco’s role involves navigating the implications of this arrangement for Malta’s specific economic conditions, including high GDP per capita convergence with EU averages and robust service exports.
