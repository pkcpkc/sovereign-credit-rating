# Geremia Palomba

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member (Implicitly associated with the 2024 Article IV Consultation)

## Biography & Context

Geremia Palomba is involved in the production of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. He is associated with the development of the IMF Staff Report, which was completed in December 2024 and presented to the IMF Executive Board in January 2025. His work encompasses the analysis of Malta's economic growth, fiscal policy transitions—specifically the shift from energy subsidies to green investment—and the development of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform roadmaps aligned with EU Pillar II. His contributions involve assessing macroprudential policies, real estate risks in the banking sector, and structural reforms aimed at achieving "Malta Vision 2050."
