# Kažimír (National Bank of Slovakia Governor)

## Affiliations & Roles

- Governor at [[National Bank of Slovakia]]

## Biography & Context

Kažimír serves as the Governor of the National Bank of Slovakia. He was a key official involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) regarding the Slovak Republic. During these discussions, he provided insights into the country's economic recovery, financial sector resilience, and the government's fiscal consolidation plans. His role involves overseeing the stability of the Slovak financial sector, which the IMF noted remains well-capitalized despite vulnerabilities in the residential and commercial real estate markets.
