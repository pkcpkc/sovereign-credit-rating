# Attila Steiner

## Affiliations & Roles

- Participant in the IMF Article IV Consultation Staff Mission for Hungary.

## Biography & Context

Attila Steiner was involved in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Hungary. This mission, which took place between June 5 and June 17, 2025, focused on evaluating Hungary's economic trajectory, specifically addressing stagnant output, high inflation, and the necessity of a high-quality fiscal adjustment. 

Steiner's involvement coincided with the analysis of Hungary's debt sustainability (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030) and the recommendation for a structural primary surplus. The work involved assessing the sovereign-bank nexus, geoeconomic fragmentation risks, and structural reforms aimed at improving firm dynamism and energy security.
