## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Arnaud Buisse serves as the Executive Director for France at the International Monetary Fund (IMF) Executive Board. In July 2025, he participated in the conclusion of the 2025 Article IV Consultation with France, representing the French government's stance and engaging with IMF staff and other executive directors.

Buisse’s participation highlighted the French authorities' broad agreement with IMF staff assessments regarding the country's economic resilience and fiscal challenges. He emphasized France's commitment to the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), which aims to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029 through structural fiscal measures rather than tax increases. Key areas of focus included managing high public debt (113.1% of GDP), addressing fiscal slippages, and advancing structural reforms to boost productivity through digital and green transitions.

The consultation underscored France's strong multilateral cooperation and its role in global economic governance, while also addressing risks such as trade tensions, policy uncertainty, and the need for continued financial sector oversight. Buisse's role involves balancing national fiscal priorities with international macroeconomic stability standards, ensuring that France's sovereign debt trajectory remains sustainable amidst global headwinds.
