## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

The European Commission serves as the primary executive body of the European Union, responsible for proposing legislation, implementing decisions, upholding the EU treaties, and managing the day-to-day business of the EU. In the context of Romania's 2025 Rule of Law Report, the Commission plays a critical role in assessing and monitoring the state of the rule of law, judicial independence, anti-corruption efforts, and media freedom.

The Commission's assessment highlights significant progress in completing legislative processes initiated by the Venice Commission regarding Justice Laws, including steps to strengthen the independence of high-ranking prosecutors and judicial police. However, the Commission identifies persistent challenges, such as average perceptions of judicial independence, increased length of proceedings, and legal uncertainty stemming from High Court of Cassation and Justice rulings on statute of limitations.

In the anti-corruption domain, while the implementation of the 2021-2025 Strategy remains on track, the Commission notes systemic issues, including resource constraints for the National Integrity Agency and rulings that have led to the annulment of many corruption convictions. Regarding media freedom, the Commission reports no progress in strengthening public service media independence, with ongoing issues related to opaque financing, harassment of journalists, and strategic lawsuits (SLAPPs).

Legislative unpredictability, characterized by the frequent use of Government Emergency Ordinances and poor legislative quality, remains a primary concern for businesses and civil society organizations. The Commission also monitors institutional checks and balances, noting the Constitutional Court’s annulment of the 2024 presidential election first round and stalled accreditation processes for National Human Rights Institutions. The Commission's reports directly influence Romania's access to EU funds, including the Recovery and Resilience Plan, and its broader economic and political standing within the Union.
