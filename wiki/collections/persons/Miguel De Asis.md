# Miguel De Asis

## Affiliations & Roles

- Involved in the IMF 2025 Malta Country Report - Article IV Consultation

## Biography & Context

Miguel De Asis is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) regarding the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Malta. His work involves analyzing Malta's economic growth, fiscal sustainability, and financial stability. Key areas of focus include the transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, the management of real estate exposure in the banking sector, and the implementation of AML/CFT reforms and Corporate Income Tax (CIT) updates aligned with EU Pillar II.
