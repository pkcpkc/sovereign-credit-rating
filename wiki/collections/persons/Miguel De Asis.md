Miguel De Asis serves as an Administrative Assistant within the International Monetary Fund (IMF), supporting operations related to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. This consultation, concluded in early 2025, focused on Malta’s macroeconomic stability, fiscal consolidation, and structural reforms. De Asis's role supports the executive leadership, specifically Executive Director Riccardo Ercoli, during critical policy discussions that evaluated Malta's sovereign credit factors, including its strong external position, sound financial system, and adherence to EU fiscal frameworks. The mission highlighted Malta’s high GDP per capita convergence with the EU average and its robust service exports, while noting risks from geopolitical spillovers and domestic capacity constraints.
