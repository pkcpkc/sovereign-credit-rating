# Silvio Schembri

## Affiliations & Roles

- Government Official at [[Malta]]

## Biography & Context

Silvio Schembri is a key figure in the Maltese government involved in high-level economic policy discussions. He participated in the 2024 economic policy discussions with various Maltese officials as part of the preparations for the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation. His involvement relates to critical national priorities including fiscal consolidation, the transition away from fixed energy price models, and the implementation of structural reforms aimed at productivity-driven growth under the "Vision 2050" initiative.
