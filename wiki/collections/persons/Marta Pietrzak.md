# Marta Pietrzak

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Marta Pietrzak is a member of the IMF staff team involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. Her work contributed to the analysis of Poland's economic recovery, which is projected to see growth accelerate to 3.5% in 2025-2026 driven by domestic demand and the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. 

Her involvement included evaluating Poland's fiscal position, specifically the widening deficit (5.9% of GDP in 2024) and the subsequent Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP). She participated in the assessment of inflation trends, monetary policy stance by the National Bank of Poland (NBP), and structural reforms related to labor supply, migration, and the climate transition.
