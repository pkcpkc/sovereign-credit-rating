## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Marta Pietrzak serves as the Advisor to the Executive Director for [[Poland]] at the [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]. In this capacity, she provided expert commentary and assessment regarding Poland's macroeconomic stability and fiscal sustainability during the 2024 Article IV Consultation, which concluded in January 2025. Her analysis contributes to the evaluation of Poland's economic outlook, which includes strong near-term growth driven by Next Generation EU (NGEU) fund absorption, but is tempered by significant fiscal pressures. Key issues addressed in the context of her role include the widening general government deficit (reaching 5.9% of GDP in 2024), which triggered an EU Excessive Deficit Procedure, and the resulting need for fiscal consolidation under the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP). Additionally, her oversight covers monetary policy developments led by the [[National Bank of Poland (NBP)]], where maintaining tight policy to anchor inflation expectations amidst sticky core inflation and wage growth remains a priority. The assessment also highlights structural challenges such as population aging, the transition away from coal dependency, and financial sector resilience, particularly regarding litigation risks from foreign-currency mortgages.
