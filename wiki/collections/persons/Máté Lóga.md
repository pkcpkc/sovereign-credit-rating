# Máté Lóga

## Affiliations & Roles

- State Secretary at the Hungarian Ministry for National Economy

## Biography & Context

Máté Lóga is a key government official in Hungary, serving in a leadership capacity within the Ministry for National Economy. He is associated with Minister [[Márton Nagy]] and works closely with State Secretary [[Bence Gerlaki]]. 

Lóga was a primary point of contact during the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. During this period, he participated in high-level discussions with the IMF staff mission (which began in June 2025) regarding Hungary's economic trajectory. The consultations focused on critical issues including fiscal adjustment strategies, debt sustainability, and structural reforms necessary to address stagnant output and high inflation. His role involved navigating complex economic challenges such as geoeconomic fragmentation, the need for high-quality fiscal policy to manage public debt, and the implementation of reforms to improve the business environment and unlock EU funding.
