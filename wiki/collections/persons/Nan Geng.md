## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Nan Geng served as staff member for the International Monetary Fund (IMF) during the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Romania. This consultation marked a critical juncture for the country's macroeconomic stability, focusing on fiscal consolidation and sovereign credit risk. The mission, led by Mission Head Joong Shik Kang, evaluated Romania's efforts to address twin deficits (fiscal and current account) that had slowed real GDP growth to 0.9 percent in 2024.

Geng was part of the team assessing the implementation of a comprehensive fiscal consolidation package adopted by the Romanian coalition government in 2025. This package included VAT rate hikes and other tax measures aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit to below 6 percent of GDP in 2026 and below 3 percent by 2031. The IMF staff, including Geng, monitored risks related to the execution of this plan, persistent inflation (spiking to 9.9 percent in September 2025), and the sustainability of public finances. The consultation highlighted the importance of structural reforms, such as improving state-owned enterprise (SOE) governance and accelerating the absorption of EU funds, to support long-term debt sustainability and economic growth.
