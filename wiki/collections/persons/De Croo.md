## Biography & Context

De Croo is a key political figure within the Belgian Federal Government involved in the country's economic and fiscal management. During the period of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, he served as a primary authority figure representing the Belgian government's stance on fiscal consolidation and structural reforms.

The government under his tenure is navigating significant economic challenges, including a high public debt (projected to reach 123% of GDP by 2030), the "automatic wage indexation" system, and the need for a 7-year adjustment plan to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP. His role involves overseeing the transition toward a more competitive labor market, addressing productivity gaps, and implementing structural reforms in taxation, pensions, and the green transition.
