# De Croo

## Affiliations & Roles

- Belgian Authority involved in IMF Article IV Consultation

## Biography & Context

De Croo is a key figure within the Belgian government, represented as a primary authority during the International Monetary Fund (IMF) 2025 Article IV consultation. During the consultation period in early 2025, De Croo and other Belgian officials engaged with the IMF to discuss the country's economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and structural challenges. 

Key areas of focus involving the Belgian authorities under De Croo's purview include:
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** Addressing public debt (which reached 104.1% of GDP in 2024) and pursuing a seven-year adjustment period to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP.
- **Labor Market Reform:** Addressing concerns regarding automatic wage indexation and the need to improve labor-market efficiency and competitiveness.
- **Economic Strategy:** Managing the transition toward a green economy, improving Total Factor Productivity (TFP), and rationalizing social spending while protecting investments in healthcare and infrastructure.
