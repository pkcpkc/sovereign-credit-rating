## Biography & Context

Alasal is identified as a key participant or associated figure in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding the Slovak Republic. This involvement pertains to the assessment of Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024. The work involved monitoring Slovakia's ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028), analyzing the resilience of the banking system against real estate vulnerabilities, and addressing structural challenges such as labor productivity declines and demographic pressures.
