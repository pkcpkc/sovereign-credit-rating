# Jan Kees Martijn

## Affiliations & Roles

- Head of IMF Staff Team at [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF)

## Biography & Context

Jan Kees Martijn served as the Head of the IMF Staff Team for the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. In this capacity, he oversaw the staff discussions and the subsequent development of the IMF Staff Report regarding Poland's economic trajectory. His work involved analyzing Poland's recovery phase, including its growth projections (estimated at 3.5% for 2025-2026), fiscal deficit challenges under the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP), and the impact of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds on domestic demand.
