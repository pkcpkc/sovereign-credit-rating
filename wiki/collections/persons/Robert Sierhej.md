# Robert Sierhej

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff member involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Poland.

## Biography & Context

Robert Sierhej is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team responsible for the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. He participated in the economic assessment conducted between October and December 2024, which evaluated Poland's recovery phase, fiscal position, and structural reforms. 

His work contributed to the analysis of Poland's projected growth (3.5% in 2025-2026), the impact of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds, and the country's transition toward fiscal consolidation to address a deficit of 5.9% of GDP. The consultation involved evaluating the National Bank of Poland's (NBP) monetary policy, banking sector resilience, and critical structural needs such as labor market integration for refugees and the decarbonization of the energy sector.
