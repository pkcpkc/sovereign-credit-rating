## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Robert Sierhej serves as the Resident Representative for the International Monetary Fund (IMF) in Poland. In this capacity, he is a key participant in the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the Republic of Poland, concluding discussions in Warsaw in October 2024. The mission, led by Head of Mission Jan Kees Martijn, assessed Poland's robust economic recovery and macroeconomic stability.

Sierhej's role involves engaging with Polish authorities, including Executive Director Patryk Loszewski and Advisor Marta Pietrzak, to evaluate sovereign risk factors. The consultation highlighted Poland's growth acceleration to an estimated 2.8% in 2024, driven by private consumption and Next Generation EU (NGEU) fund absorption. However, significant fiscal challenges were identified, with the deficit projected to widen to 5.9% of GDP in 2024 due to permanent increases in public sector wages, social benefits, and high defense spending.

The IMF mission, including Sierhej, emphasized the need for frontloaded fiscal consolidation to rebuild buffers. The Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) was welcomed, but the mission stressed the importance of identifying all consolidation measures upfront to ensure credibility, aiming for a deficit reduction to 2.9% of GDP by 2028. On the monetary front, the mission supported the National Bank of Poland's tight stance, advising that interest rate cuts should be contingent on decelerating wage growth and a return of inflation to the 2.5% target.

Sierhej's work also encompasses monitoring financial sector health, noting the resilience of well-capitalized banks despite litigation risks related to legacy CHF mortgages. Structural reforms remain a priority, with the mission focusing on labor market integration, innovation, and climate transition. As Resident Representative, Sierhej continues to monitor Poland's external position, which remains strong with adequate gross reserves, and the country's progress in meeting EU 2030 decarbonization targets.
