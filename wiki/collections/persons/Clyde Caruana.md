## Biography & Context

Clyde Caruana is identified as a key individual involved in the economic and fiscal policy discussions between Malta and the International Monetary Fund (IMF). His involvement pertains to the 2024 Article IV Consultation, which focused on Malta's economic growth, fiscal sustainability, and the transition toward a productivity-driven growth model. 

Contextual highlights of his involvement include:
- **Fiscal Policy:** Participation in discussions regarding the reduction of the fiscal deficit to 2.6% of GDP by 2027 and the phasing out of fixed energy price models.
- **Taxation & Reform:** Engagement with the roadmap for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform to align with EU Pillar II.
- **Economic Strategy:** Involvement in the broader strategic framework of Malta's Vision 2050, aiming to balance economic development with social and environmental sustainability.
