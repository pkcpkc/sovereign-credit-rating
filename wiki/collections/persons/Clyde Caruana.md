# Clyde Caruana

## Affiliations & Roles

- Minister for Finance and Employment at [[Ministry of Finance and Employment (Malta)]]

## Biography & Context

Clyde Caruana serves as the Minister for Finance and Employment in Malta. He is a key government stakeholder involved in high-level economic policy discussions with international bodies, including the International Monetary Fund (IMF). 

In late 2024, he participated in economic policy discussions with the IMF regarding Malta's fiscal position and growth trajectory. His role involves overseeing critical national initiatives, including the transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, the implementation of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II, and the management of fiscal consolidation strategies. He is also involved in the strategic planning for "Malta Vision 2050," which aims to address structural challenges such as labor shortages, infrastructure strain, and the green energy transition.
