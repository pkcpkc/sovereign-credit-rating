# Moritz Kraemer

## Affiliations & Roles

- Contributor to S&P Global Ratings methodology development.

## Biography & Context

Moritz Kraemer is associated with the development and publication of the S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017). His work involves the framework for assessing sovereign creditworthiness across five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments. This includes the implementation of key methodologies such as the transition to net general government debt focus, the integration of Current Account Payments (CAP) in external ratios, and the utilization of Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA) scores to quantify contingent liabilities.
