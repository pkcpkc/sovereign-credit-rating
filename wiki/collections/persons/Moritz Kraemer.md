## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Moritz Kraemer serves as an Analytical Contact at [[S&P Global Ratings]], contributing to the firm's sovereign credit rating assessments. In this capacity, he is associated with the publication and maintenance of the **S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology**, specifically the version updated in December 2017.

The 2017 methodology, for which Kraemer is listed as a contact, establishes a standardized framework for assessing sovereign creditworthiness based on five key pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments. Key aspects of this framework include:

*   **Fiscal Assessment**: Utilizing net general government debt rather than gross debt to better capture fiscal stance.
*   **External Assessment**: Refining metrics for external liquidity and currency status.
*   **Institutional Assessment**: Evaluating policymaking effectiveness, transparency, and debt payment culture.
*   **Rating Caps**: Limiting local-currency rating uplifts to one notch above foreign-currency ratings.

Kraemer works alongside other analytical and criteria contacts at S&P Global, including [[Christian Esters]], [[Sebastian Briozzo]], [[KimEng Tan]], [[Bertrand De Dianous]], [[Valerie Montmaur]], [[Laura J Feinland Katz]], and [[Mark Puccia]], to ensure consistent application of these rating principles across sovereign entities.
