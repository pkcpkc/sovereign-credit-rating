## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Tina Kang serves as staff within the International Monetary Fund (IMF) focused on Romania. She participated in the 2025 Article IV Consultation mission, which took place from September 3–12, 2025, under the leadership of Mission Head Joong Shik Kang. The mission assessed Romania's macroeconomic stability, noting a critical juncture in 2025 where real GDP growth slowed to 0.9 percent due to weak investment and widening fiscal and current account deficits.

Kang's work contributes to the analysis of sovereign credit rating factors, particularly regarding fiscal sustainability and debt burden. The IMF team highlighted the Romanian government's adoption of a large fiscal consolidation package in mid-2025, including VAT rate hikes and excise increases, aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit below 6 percent of GDP in 2026 and below 3 percent by 2031. The report also emphasized risks related to high headline inflation (peaking at 9.9 percent in September 2025), the sovereign-bank nexus, and unhedged foreign exchange loans. The mission's findings were concluded by the IMF Executive Board in November 2025, with Tina Kang contributing to the staff reports and policy recommendations aimed at restoring fiscal and macroeconomic sustainability.
