# Tina Kang

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania.

## Biography & Context

Tina Kang is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), specifically contributing to the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. Her work involves analyzing critical macroeconomic indicators, including fiscal consolidation efforts, inflation management, and the resilience of the Romanian banking sector. 

During the consultation period in late 2025, she was part of the team evaluating Romania's fiscal reform package, which included VAT rate adjustments and public sector wage freezes aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit. Her context of work includes monitoring the "sovereign-bank nexus," assessing labor force participation rates, and evaluating the progress of Next Generation EU (NGEU) fund absorption.
