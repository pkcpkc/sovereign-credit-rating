# Tina Kang

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Tina Kang is a member of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) team involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Romania. Her work contributes to the analysis of Romania's macroeconomic performance, including fiscal consolidation efforts, inflation management, and the country's structural reform progress regarding EU fund absorption and green transition initiatives.
