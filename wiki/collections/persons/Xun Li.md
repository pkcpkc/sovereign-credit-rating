# Xun Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund]]

## Biography & Context

Xun Li is a professional associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), specifically involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Belgium. In this capacity, Li contributed to the analysis of Belgium's macroeconomic stability, focusing on fiscal consolidation, labor market dynamics, and structural reforms. The work involved evaluating Belgium's 7-year adjustment plan, assessing the impact of automatic wage indexation on competitiveness, and providing recommendations on debt management and productivity growth.
