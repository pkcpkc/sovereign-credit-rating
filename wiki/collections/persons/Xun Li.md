# Xun Li

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Xun Li is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and contributed to the 2025 Article IV consultation for Belgium. This consultation, conducted between January and February 2025, assessed Belgium's economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and structural challenges. The work involved analyzing issues such as automatic wage indexation, labor-cost competitiveness, and the necessity of fiscal consolidation to manage a public debt level that reached 104.1% of GDP in 2024.
