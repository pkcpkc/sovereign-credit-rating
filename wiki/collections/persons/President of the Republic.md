## Key Relationships

As the Head of State, the President of the Republic holds ultimate executive authority and is the primary actor in the political and institutional dynamics described in the 2025 Rule of Law Report. The President's administration is directly implicated in the following areas:

- **Judicial Independence and Magistrate Reform**: The President's government is responsible for the ongoing implementation of the 20 November 2023 law reforming magistrates' status. This includes the repeal of age limits for admissions and the creation of 360° evaluations for court presidents. The administration has faced public attacks on magistrates related to judicial decisions, particularly those involving election-related rulings, necessitating active defense by the [[Superior Council of the Magistracy]].
- **Legislative Procedures and Checks and Balances**: The administration is criticized for the use of accelerated legislative procedures, with 27 out of 39 draft bills in 2024 utilizing this mechanism, which stakeholders argue limits citizen consultation and contributes to legal uncertainty. The interruption of citizen consultation processes (e.g., the Agora application) since June 2024 is a policy decision under executive purview.
- **Public Broadcasting Reform**: The President's office is driving the proposed reform creating a holding company, [[France Médias]], to oversee public broadcasters ([[France Télévisions]], [[Radio France]], [[INA]]). This reform, aiming to pool resources and share investments, has sparked strikes and debates regarding editorial autonomy and the appointment of a common CEO for subsidiaries.
- **Anti-Corruption Framework**: The executive branch oversees the implementation of the 2024-2029 national anti-corruption plan and the reorganization of the [[Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA)]]. It is also responsible for the operations of the [[High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)]], which has seen increased staffing to handle growing workloads.
- **Media Ownership Transparency**: The administration is tasked with preparing a single national database listing direct or indirect owners and ultimate beneficial owners of media outlets, addressing previous recommendations from the 2024 Rule of Law Report.

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

The President's policy decisions directly impact France's sovereign credit rating factors, specifically within political and geopolitical risk, institutional and governance strength, and policy predictability.

- **Political and Geopolitical Risk**:
    - **Threats**: The administration faces risks from public attacks on magistrates related to judicial decisions and political/economic pressures on media outlets.
    - **Strengths**: Geopolitical stability within the EU remains a key strength.

- **Institutional and Governance Strength**:
    - **Threats**: The use of accelerated legislative procedures leads to instability and legal uncertainty. There are also pending implementation issues regarding [[European Court of Human Rights]] judgments (26 leading judgments pending as of 1 January 2025).
    - **Strengths**: The administration maintains strong legal guarantees on access to documents and established ethics codes for the judiciary and parliament.

- **Policy Predictability and Reform Continuity**:
    - **Threats**: The disruption of citizen consultation processes and budgetary restrictions on civil society organizations narrow the civic space and reduce policy predictability.
    - **Strengths**: The ongoing implementation of the 2023 magistrates' status reform demonstrates continuity in key judicial reforms.

- **Financial Sector Health**:
    - **Threats**: There is a perception of corruption in public procurement, which the executive must address through strengthened enforcement mechanisms and asset recovery laws (e.g., Law Warsmann No. 582).

- **Fiscal Performance and Flexibility**:
    - **Threats**: Reductions in funding for associations and budget cuts to public service media (linked to the [[France Médias]] reform) impact the financial environment for civil society.
