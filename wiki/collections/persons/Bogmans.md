# Bogmans

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff / Consultant (Article IV Consultation)

## Biography & Context

Bogmans is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) in the context of the 2025 Article IV Consultation for the Slovak Republic. Participation in this capacity involved evaluating Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024. The work involved analyzing the country's ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028), structural headwinds such as declining labor productivity, and the sustainability of public debt amidst an aging population. Key areas of focus included financial sector resilience, inflation moderation, and governance reforms regarding judicial independence and anti-corruption.
