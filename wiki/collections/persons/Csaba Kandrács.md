## Biography & Context

Csaba Kandrács is identified as a key stakeholder in the Hungarian economic landscape, specifically involved in the interactions during the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation. His role is situated within the context of Hungary's fiscal and monetary policy discussions, which focus on addressing stagnant output, managing high public debt (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030), and implementing structural reforms. He is part of the group of Hungarian officials who engaged with the IMF staff mission in Budapest between June 5 and June 17, 2025, to discuss debt sustainability, inflation targets, and governance reforms.
