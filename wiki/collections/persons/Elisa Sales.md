## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Elisa Sales serves as an Advisor to the Executive Director at the International Monetary Fund (IMF), specifically supporting operations related to Malta. Her role is integral to the IMF’s Article IV Consultation process with the Maltese authorities, which concluded in January 2025.

During the 2024 Article IV Consultation, Malta was recognized for maintaining robust economic growth, driven largely by export-oriented service industries such as tourism and online gaming. The IMF staff, including Sales, noted that while growth remains among the highest in Europe, structural constraints such as labor shortages and infrastructure strain pose risks. Key policy areas addressed during her tenure of support include fiscal consolidation, with a target deficit reduction to around 2¼ percent of GDP by 2029, and the gradual exit from fixed energy prices to create fiscal space for green infrastructure and innovation investments.

The consultation highlighted Malta’s strong external position, substantial net international investment position (NIIP), and sound financial system with high capital buffers. However, risks related to real estate exposure, geopolitical spillovers, and the need for continued judicial and AML/CFT reforms were emphasized. Sales’ advisory role contributes to ensuring that Malta’s policies align with IMF staff recommendations, including the implementation of structural reforms focused on productivity, digitalization, and climate sustainability through initiatives like "Malta Vision 2050".
