Thomas Gade serves as a Staff Member within the International Monetary Fund, contributing to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. This mission involved a comprehensive assessment of Malta's economic policies, fiscal consolidation efforts, and structural reforms. The consultation highlighted Malta's robust economic growth driven by tourism and online gaming, while addressing challenges such as population density strains on public services and the need for fiscal consolidation. Gade's work contributed to the IMF's recommendations regarding the gradual exit from fixed energy prices, investment in green infrastructure and innovation, and the strengthening of the Anti-Money Laundering/Combating the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework. The mission also addressed financial sector health, particularly risks related to real estate exposure, and the importance of structural reforms to boost productivity through digitalization and education.
