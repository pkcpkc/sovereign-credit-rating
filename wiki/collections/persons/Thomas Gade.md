# Thomas Gade

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff member at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Thomas Gade is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and was involved in the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. He worked under the leadership of [[Kotaro Ishi]], who served as the head of the staff team during the period of economic policy discussions and the completion of the IMF Staff Report.
