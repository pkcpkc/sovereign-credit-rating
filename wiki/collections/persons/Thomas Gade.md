# Thomas Gade

## Affiliations & Roles

- Staff Member at [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]

## Biography & Context

Thomas Gade is involved in the IMF's economic oversight and reporting, specifically contributing to the 2024 Article IV Consultation for Malta. He worked under the leadership of [[Kotaro Ishi]], who served as the Head of Staff Team during the preparation of the IMF Staff Report. His work involved analyzing Malta's economic transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, addressing fiscal policy recommendations such as the phasing out of energy subsidies, and evaluating structural reforms including Corporate Income Tax (CIT) alignment with EU Pillar II.
