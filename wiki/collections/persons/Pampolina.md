## Biography & Context

Pampolina is identified as a key figure involved in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding the Slovak Republic. This engagement focused on analyzing Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024, and evaluating the country's ambitious four-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028). 

The work associated with Pampolina involves assessing critical macroeconomic factors including fiscal consolidation, labor productivity, and financial stability. Key themes of the consultation included addressing Slovakia's "secular decline" in productivity, managing debt sustainability amidst an aging population, and evaluating the resilience of the banking sector against real estate vulnerabilities and new financial transaction taxes.
