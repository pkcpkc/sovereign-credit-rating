# Kamenický (Minister of Finance)

## Affiliations & Roles

- Minister of Finance at [[Slovak Republic]]

## Biography & Context

Kamenický serves as the Minister of Finance for the Slovak Republic. He is a key government official involved in the nation's fiscal policy and economic management. In early 2025, he participated in formal discussions with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) regarding Slovakia's economic trajectory. 

Under his leadership, the government has committed to a rigorous 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit and stabilizing public debt. He is tasked with navigating complex economic headwinds, including an aging population, low productivity growth, and the need for structural reforms to meet long-term fiscal targets.
