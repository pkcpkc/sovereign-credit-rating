# Can Ugur

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff member involved in the 2025 Poland Country Report - Article IV Consultation.

## Biography & Context

Can Ugur is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team responsible for the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. As part of the staff involved in this assessment, Ugur contributed to the analysis of Poland's economic recovery, which is projected to see growth accelerate to 3.5% in 2025-2026. The work involved evaluating the impact of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds, monitoring inflation trends, and reviewing Poland's fiscal position, specifically the widening deficit and the implementation of the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP).
