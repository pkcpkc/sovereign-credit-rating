## Biography & Context

Ogawa is identified as a key participant or contributor involved in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding the Slovak Republic. This engagement involved assessing Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth reach 2.0% in 2024, and evaluating the country's ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028). 

The work associated with Ogawa encompassed a wide range of macroeconomic and structural analyses, including:
- **Fiscal Policy:** Evaluating the reduction of the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP and the transition toward expenditure-based rationalization.
- **Financial Stability:** Assessing the resilience of the Slovak banking system, capital buffers, and the impact of the Financial Transaction Tax (FTT).
- **Structural Reforms:** Addressing labor productivity declines, automation risks, and the need for Active Labor Market Policies (ALMPs).
- **Demographics & Governance:** Analyzing long-term public debt sustainability related to an aging population and the necessity of strengthening anti-corruption frameworks.
