## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Mr. Saiyid served as IMF Staff on the mission conducting the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Austria. The mission, which began in Vienna in March 2025 and concluded with Executive Board approval in July 2025, assessed Austria's economic performance amid two successive years of recession (2023 and 2024). Key areas of analysis under his involvement included the assessment of fiscal consolidation measures required to stabilize public debt, which had risen to 81.2% of GDP in 2024, and the widening fiscal deficit of 4.7% of GDP. The staff report emphasized the need for medium-term adjustment to address rising spending pressures from demographic aging, defense, and the green transition.

Furthermore, the mission evaluated financial sector stability, noting high banking capitalization but identifying risks in Commercial Real Estate (CRE) exposures. Mr. Saiyid's work contributed to recommendations regarding the permanent implementation of prudent lending standards following the lapse of temporary borrower-based measures in July 2025. The analysis also covered structural reforms aimed at boosting labor supply and productivity to offset demographic headwinds, as well as the external position and inflation convergence dynamics within the Euro Area.
