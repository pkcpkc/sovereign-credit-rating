## Biography & Context

Kotera is identified as a key participant or staff member involved in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding the Slovak Republic. In this capacity, Kotera contributed to the assessment of Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth acceleration to 2.0% in 2024. The work involved evaluating the country's ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028), analyzing structural headwinds such as geoeconomic fragmentation and aging demographics, and providing recommendations on labor productivity, financial stability, and anti-corruption frameworks.
