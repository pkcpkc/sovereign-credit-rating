# Zoltán Kurali

## Affiliations & Roles

- Official involved in IMF Article IV Consultation (Hungarian Government/MNB)

## Biography & Context

Zoltán Kurali is identified as a key stakeholder in the context of the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation for Hungary. He was involved in the high-level discussions and staff missions conducted in Budapest between June 5, 2025, and June 17, 2025. These consultations focused on Hungary's economic trajectory, specifically addressing stagnant output, inflation targets, and the necessity of high-quality fiscal adjustments to ensure debt sustainability. His involvement pertains to the assessment of structural reforms, monetary policy stances by the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB), and the management of geoeconomic risks facing the Hungarian economy.
