## Biography & Context

Taraba serves as the Minister of the Environment for the Slovak Republic. In the context of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, Taraba is identified as a key government official involved in the country's economic and structural assessments. The ministry's portfolio is particularly relevant to the government's goals regarding energy security, climate targets for 2030, and the transition away from fossil fuel subsidies.
