# Barnabás Virág

## Affiliations & Roles

- Deputy Central Bank Governor at [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB)]]

## Biography & Context

Barnabás Virág serves as the Deputy Central Bank Governor of the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB). He is a key official involved in Hungary's monetary policy and financial stability. In the context of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, he represents the MNB's position on maintaining a restrictive base rate (currently 6.5%) to anchor inflation expectations and manage the country's disinflationary path amidst stagnant growth and high refinancing costs.
