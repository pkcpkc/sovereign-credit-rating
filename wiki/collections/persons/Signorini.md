# Signorini

## Affiliations & Roles

- Official involved in the IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy.

## Biography & Context

Signorini was identified as a key official involved in the IMF Staff Discussions held in Rome between May 14 and May 28, 2025. These discussions were part of the IMF's Article IV Consultation regarding Italy's economic performance, fiscal consolidation, and the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP).

The consultation involved reviewing Italy's transition toward a primary surplus, its record-high employment rates, and significant structural challenges including weak productivity growth and demographic headwinds. Signorini's involvement aligns with the high-level reporting on Italy's fiscal trajectory and its efforts to achieve a 3% primary surplus target by 2027 to manage public debt dynamics.
