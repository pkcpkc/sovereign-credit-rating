## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Signorini, serving as Senior Deputy Governor of the Bank of Italy, played a key role in the 2025 Article IV Consultation with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). The consultation, concluded in July 2025, highlighted Italy's economic resilience despite global uncertainties. As a senior central bank official, Signorini was involved in discussions regarding macroeconomic stability, banking sector health, and fiscal policy implementation.

The IMF noted the return to a primary surplus in 2024, driven by improved tax compliance and strong labor market performance, alongside the banking sector's enhanced soundness with record profits and adequate capital buffers. However, vulnerabilities among less significant institutions and the sovereign-bank nexus remain areas of focus. Signorini's role involves overseeing monetary policy and financial stability measures to support these structural reforms, particularly in addressing productivity malaise and demographic headwinds through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP).
