# Helge Berger

- **Role**: Department Head
- **Organization**: [[International Monetary Fund]]
- **Countries Covered**: Italy

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Helge Berger serves as a Department Head at the [[International Monetary Fund]], playing a senior leadership role in the institution's surveillance activities. He was a key figure in the IMF’s 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[Italy]], serving alongside other Department Heads such as [[Rishi Goyal]] and [[Lone Christiansen]] (Team Head).

In this capacity, Berger is involved in high-level assessments of sovereign risk, fiscal policy, and macroeconomic stability. His work encompasses the analysis of Italy’s debt sustainability, the implementation of the [[National Recovery and Resilience Plan]] (NRRP), and the evaluation of structural reforms aimed at boosting productivity and addressing demographic challenges. The consultation highlighted concerns regarding Italy's high public debt (135.3% of GDP in 2024), weak productivity growth, and the sovereign-bank nexus, areas under Berger’s departmental oversight. He contributed to the IMF Staff Report that recommended a front-loaded fiscal adjustment to achieve a primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027, a proposal that was debated by Italian authorities including [[Giancarlo Giorgetti]], Minister of Economy and Finance.
