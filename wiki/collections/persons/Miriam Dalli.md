## Biography & Context

Miriam Dalli is a key figure involved in Malta's economic and fiscal policy landscape. She was identified as a primary stakeholder during the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the International Monetary Fund (IMF). In this capacity, she participated in high-level economic policy discussions held in Valletta between November 4–15, 2024.

Her involvement relates to the oversight and implementation of Malta's economic transition, including:
- **Fiscal Policy:** Navigating the transition from energy subsidies toward innovation and green investment.
- **Tax Reform:** Addressing the roadmap for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform to align with the EU’s Pillar II Minimum Tax Directive.
- **Economic Strategy:** Contributing to the "Malta Vision 2050" objectives, focusing on productivity-driven growth, infrastructure strain mitigation, and labor market upskilling.
- **Financial Stability:** Engaging with recommendations regarding macroprudential policies and managing real estate exposure within the banking sector.
