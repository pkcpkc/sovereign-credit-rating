# Miriam Dalli

## Affiliations & Roles

- Government Official at [[Malta]]

## Biography & Context

Miriam Dalli is a key Maltese official involved in high-level economic policy discussions and national strategic planning. She participated in the 2024 economic policy discussions regarding Malta's fiscal and monetary framework, which culminated in the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation. Her work involves navigating complex structural reforms, including the transition toward a productivity-driven growth model, the management of energy subsidies, and the implementation of fiscal consolidation measures to meet EU framework targets. She is also associated with the development of "Malta Vision 2050," a strategic initiative aimed at balancing economic development with social and environmental sustainability.
