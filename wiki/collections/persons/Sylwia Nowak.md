# Sylwia Nowak

## Affiliations & Roles

- IMF Staff Member involved in the 2025 Article IV Consultation for Italy.

## Biography & Context

Sylwia Nowak is associated with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team responsible for the 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding Italy. Her work involved analyzing Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural challenges during the 2024-2025 reporting period. 

The team's findings highlighted Italy's return to a primary surplus, record-high employment rates (62.7% in April 2025), and significant progress on the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP). Nowak's context of work included addressing critical headwinds such as weak productivity growth, rapid population aging (increasing the old-age dependency ratio), and high public debt. The staff report, which included her contributions, recommended specific fiscal targets (a 3% primary surplus by 2027) and structural reforms to boost human capital and labor participation, particularly for women.
