# Sylwia Nowak

## Affiliations & Roles

- **Role**: IMF Staff Member
- **Organization**: [[International Monetary Fund]]
- **Countries Covered**: Italy

## Sovereign Credit Rating & Policy Context

Sylwia Nowak served as a member of the IMF staff team responsible for the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Italy. In this capacity, Nowak contributed to the assessment of Italy’s economic resilience, fiscal performance, and financial sector stability under the supervision of Staff Team Head [[Lone Christiansen]] and Department Heads [[Helge Berger]] and [[Rishi Goyal]].

The team’s analysis, which culminated in a staff report completed in May 2025 and published in July 2025, highlighted Italy’s robust labor market and improved fiscal outturns in 2024, noting a reduction in the deficit to 3.4% of GDP and a return to a primary surplus. However, the consultation emphasized persistent structural challenges, including high public debt (135.3% of GDP), weak productivity growth, and rapid population aging. The IMF staff, including Nowak, recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment to achieve a primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027 to ensure a downward trajectory for public debt. The team also assessed the financial sector as generally sound but noted vulnerabilities in Less Significant Institutions (LSIs) and the enduring sovereign-bank nexus. Nowak’s work involved evaluating these factors within the IMF’s Sovereign Risk and Debt Sustainability Framework to provide policy recommendations to Italian authorities, including Minister of Economy and Finance [[Giancarlo Giorgetti]] and Bank of Italy officials.
