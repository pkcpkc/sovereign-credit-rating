# Romania

## Political and Geopolitical Risk

### Strengths
Stable democratic framework, membership in [[European Union]] and [[NATO]], energy self-sufficiency, and durable political consensus despite election irregularities.

### Threats
Political pressure on editorial independence, harassment and intimidation of journalists, strategic lawsuits against public participation (SLAPPs), regional conflict spillovers, and uncertainty regarding [[European Union]] fiscal rule implementation.

## Institutional and Governance Strength

### Strengths
Completion of [[Venice Commission]] recommendations on Justice Laws, transparent disciplinary case-law publication, openness to judicial IT system reforms, strong banking supervision, and progress in [[tax administration]] digitalization.

### Threats
Weak judicial independence perception, frequent use of [[Government Emergency Ordinance]] (GEO), inadequate resources for [[National Integrity Agency]], pending accreditation of National Human Rights Institutions, slow absorption of [[European Union]] funds, administrative capacity constraints, and corruption risks.

## Policy Predictability and Reform Continuity

### Strengths
Implementation of 2021-2025 Anti-Corruption Strategy, adoption of Strategy for Open Government, adoption of large fiscal reform package, and commitment to [[European Union]] fiscal rules.

### Threats
Legislative unpredictability, insufficient public consultation quality, stalled lobbying regulations for MPs, political uncertainty ahead of elections, and implementation delays of fiscal consolidation.

## Debt Payment Culture

### Strengths
Compliance with [[European Union]] fiscal frameworks, investment-grade sovereign credit rating, and established commitment to honoring obligations.

### Threats
High fiscal deficit execution risks and potential for rating downgrade.

## Economic Strength and Tax Base

### Strengths
High confidence in investment protection among companies, robust private consumption, strong wage growth, and broad taxable income base.

### Threats
High perceived corruption in public sector, regulatory burden on businesses, low tax revenue relative to [[GDP]], and weak productivity growth.

## Growth Prospects

### Strengths
Digitalization of public procurement processes, gradual [[GDP]] convergence, and [[NGEU]]-funded investment acceleration.

### Threats
Judicial delays acting as barrier to justice, subdued investment, and weak export performance.

## Economic Diversity

### Strengths
Diverse media landscape and diversified manufacturing base.

### Threats
Commodity dependence (energy prices) and tourism and services sector vulnerabilities.

## External Position and Liquidity

### Strengths
Access to [[European Union]] Recovery and Resilience Plan funds, adequate foreign exchange reserves, and net international investment position stability.

### Threats
Widening current account deficit and reliance on debt-creating flows.

## Currency and Exchange Rate Flexibility

### Strengths
[[Eurozone]] accession trajectory, stable exchange rate regime, and managed flexibility implementation.

### Threats
Financial euroization and unhedged FX corporate loans.

## Financial Sector Health

### Strengths
Stable occupancy rate in prosecution services, well-capitalized banking system, high profitability and liquidity buffers.

### Threats
Sovereign-bank nexus and unhedged FX loans.

## Fiscal Performance and Flexibility

### Strengths
Budgetary allocations for justice system recruitments, adoption of fiscal consolidation package, and prudent fiscal management.

### Threats
Frequent changes to fiscal code via GEOs, large fiscal slippage, and rising public debt.

## Debt Burden

### Strengths
Managed debt levels relative to [[European Union]] standards and investment-grade rating status.

### Threats
Rising public debt-to-GDP ratio and high gross financing needs.

## Fiscal Sustainability

### Strengths
Adherence to European Semester recommendations and medium-term fiscal structural plan.

### Threats
Pension spending pressures and high current fiscal deficit.

## Contingent Liabilities

### Strengths
Digitalization of procurement reducing fraud risk and independent monitoring agency for State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs).

### Threats
State-owned enterprise liabilities.

## Monetary Policy and Price Stability

### Strengths
Stable macroeconomic environment, cautious monetary policy approach, and inflation targeting framework.

### Threats
High headline inflation and unanchored inflation expectations risk.
