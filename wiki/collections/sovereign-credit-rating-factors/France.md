# France

## Political and Geopolitical Risk

### Strengths
- Established democratic institutions
- Geopolitical stability within the [[European Union]]
- Strong multilateral cooperation
- Leading role in digital and green transitions

### Threats
- High economic and political uncertainty following summer 2024 general elections
- Fragmented political landscape and lack of clear parliamentary majority
- Unprecedented delay in budget adoption for 2025
- Geoeconomic fragmentation and trade tensions
- Policy uncertainty stemming from political fragmentation

## Institutional and Governance Strength

### Strengths
- Strong rule of law
- Robust financial supervision
- Independent judiciary
- Robust financial oversight
- Strong legal guarantees on access to documents
- Established ethics codes for judiciary and parliament

### Threats
- Complex administrative procedures and regulatory burden
- Slow implementation of renewable energy permitting
- Challenges in public administration capacity at local levels
- Use of accelerated legislative procedures leading to instability
- Pending implementation of [[European Court of Human Rights]] judgments

## Policy Predictability and Reform Continuity

### Strengths
- Committed medium-term fiscal-structural plan
- Ongoing implementation of 2023 magistrates' status reform

### Threats
- Abrupt policy reversals due to political fragmentation
- Inconsistent reform implementation (e.g., [[SMIC]] reform savings shortfalls)
- Disruption of citizen consultation processes
- Unclear medium-term fiscal measures

## Debt Payment Culture

### Strengths
- Strong record of timely debt repayment
- Deep and liquid sovereign debt market
- Diversified investor base

## Economic Strength and Tax Base

### Strengths
- Large, diversified economy
- Strong export market shares recovery
- High GDP per capita
- Large economic output

### Threats
- High general government deficit (5.8% of GDP in 2024)
- High tax revenues relative to GDP but inefficient tax expenditures
- High corporate indebtedness and rising bankruptcies
- Sluggish domestic demand

## Growth Prospects

### Strengths
- Expected recovery in productivity
- Strong scientific and innovation base
- Resilient consumer spending
- Potential productivity gains from AI and green tech

### Threats
- Slowing economic activity in 2025 (projected 0.6% growth)
- Subdued investment due to uncertainty and high cost of capital
- Weak private investment
- Demographic aging

## Economic Diversity

### Strengths
- Diversified industrial base (nuclear, aerospace, agriculture)
- Strong defense and tech sectors

### Threats
- Dependence on tourism and services
- Energy import dependence

## External Position and Liquidity

### Strengths
- Strong current account balance
- Reserve currency status of the Euro

### Threats
- Net international investment position deficit
- High sovereign refinancing needs
- Negative net international investment position (NIIP)

## Currency and Exchange Rate Flexibility

### Strengths
- Eurozone membership provides stability
- Euro as a global reserve currency
- Free-floating exchange rate regime

## Financial Sector Health

### Strengths
- Well-capitalized banking sector
- Ample liquidity
- Healthy capital (19.8% average regulatory capital ratio)
- Robust liquidity buffers (LCR 145%, NSFR 116%)
- Low non-performing loans (NPLs)

### Threats
- High non-performing loan ratios for non-financial corporations
- Pressure on bank margins from regulated savings rates
- Narrowing net interest margins
- Corporate indebtedness risks
- Commercial real estate exposure

## Fiscal Performance and Flexibility

### Strengths
- Targeted spending reviews
- High revenue-to-GDP ratio
- Comprehensive social security system

### Threats
- Persistent high deficits and rising debt ratios
- High public expenditure levels (57.1% of GDP)
- Fiscal slippages and revenue shortfalls
- Rising debt service costs

## Debt Burden

### Strengths
- Domestic investor base
- Predominantly local currency debt
- Long average maturity (8 years)

### Threats
- High public debt (113% of GDP in 2024)
- Rising interest payments
- High general government gross debt (113.1% of GDP)
- Upward debt trajectory under baseline

## Fiscal Sustainability

### Strengths
- 2023 Pension Reform (raising retirement age)
- Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)

### Threats
- High fiscal sustainability risks in the medium term
- Aging population costs (healthcare, pensions)
- Rising healthcare and defense spending pressures
