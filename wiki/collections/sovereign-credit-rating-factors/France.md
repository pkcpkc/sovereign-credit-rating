# France

## Geopolitical and Political Stability

### Strengths

- Stable macroeconomic framework within the [[Eurozone]]
- Stable democratic institutions and independent judiciary supported by the President of the Republic
- Strong legal framework for checks and balances
- Demonstrated economic resilience in 2024 despite high uncertainty
- Broad political support being built for fiscal plans under [[EU Economic Governance]] rules

### Threats

- Fragmented political landscape following 2024 elections
- Lack of clear political majority in Parliament risking delays in budget adoption
- Political polarization and attacks on magistrates related to judicial decisions on national elections
- Social unrest reflected in strikes by public broadcasters and journalists
- Use of Article 49-3 of the Constitution leading to government censure, indicating political friction
- Deepening geoeconomic fragmentation and trade tensions pose significant downside risks to growth
- Domestic political uncertainty and lack of consensus could delay fiscal consolidation and reform efforts
- Social discontent and potential unrest linked to fiscal adjustments

## Policy Credibility and Institutional Strength

### Strengths

- Implementation of reforms under [[Recovery and Resilience Facility]] (RRF)
- Independent and credible regulatory bodies (ARCOM, HATVP, AFA)
- Strong institutional response to corruption and integrity risks
- High perceived independence of national competition authority
- Diversified economy with strong institutional support for business operations
- Strong financial oversight and macroprudential policy toolkit
- Committed to [[EU Economic Governance]] fiscal rules and reducing deficit below 3% by 2029
- Robust cyber risk framework and AML/CFT regime

### Threats

- High administrative burden and regulatory complexity
- Complexity of administrative procedures impacting businesses
- Perceived legislative instability due to accelerated legislative procedures
- Concerns over lack of consultation with citizens and SMEs in legislative simplification efforts
- Challenges in practical implementation of right to access documents despite strong legal guarantees
- Recent fiscal slippages in 2023 and 2024 have weakened debt dynamics
- Implementation risks remain for the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)
- Complex multi-layered local government structure creates administrative overlaps

## Policymaking Stability

### Threats

- Unprecedented delays in budget adoption
- Political instability affecting timely implementation of fiscal decisions
- Interruption of citizen consultation processes (e.g., Agora application)
- Pending draft laws on lobbying and media reform causing uncertainty
- High domestic policy uncertainty weighing on private investment
- Need for decisive actions and difficult decisions to ensure equity in fiscal consolidation
