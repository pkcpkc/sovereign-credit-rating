# European Council

## Mandate & Role

The European Council provides political direction and sets priorities for the European Union, playing a critical supervisory role in the governance of the Economic and Monetary Union. In the context of sovereign credit risk, the European Council is central to the **European Semester** cycle and the enforcement of the **Stability and Growth Pact**. It holds the authority to issue formal recommendations to Member States regarding their **Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plans**, thereby influencing national fiscal sustainability and policy credibility. The Council acts as a counterbalance to the European Commission's technical analysis, providing the political mandate for corrective actions in cases of excessive deficits or structural imbalances.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Fiscal Oversight and Recommendations**: The European Council endorses Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plans for Member States to ensure alignment with EU fiscal rules. For instance, the **Council Recommendation** endorsing **Malta**'s Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan was issued on **2025-01-21**, setting the trajectory for the country's fiscal consolidation.
*   **Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP)**: The Council, often in coordination with the European Commission, monitors compliance with deficit and debt criteria. It notes deviations from recommended net expenditure growth, such as the case in Malta where cumulative net expenditure deviation exceeded Council recommendations due to energy subsidies and capital expenditures.
*   **Corrective Actions for Romania**: In response to **Romania**'s deteriorating fiscal position, characterized by a government deficit reaching 9.3% of GDP in 2024, the European Council issued two critical recommendations on **2025-01-10** and **2025-01-21**. These included endorsing Romania's national medium-term fiscal-structural plan (covering the adjustment period 2025-2031) and issuing a recommendation to correct the excessive deficit. This action addresses Romania's rapid debt accumulation (54.8% of GDP in 2024) and net expenditure growth that exceeded recommended maximums.
*   **Strategic Policy Setting**: The Council sets the broader economic framework within which the **European Commission** operates, including targets for sustainable development, climate neutrality (2050 target), and social progress, which indirectly impact sovereign risk profiles through long-term structural reforms.
