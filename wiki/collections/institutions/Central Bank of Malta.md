# Central Bank of Malta

## Mandate & Role

The **Central Bank of Malta** serves as the national central bank for Malta, operating within the framework of the **Eurosystem** and the **European Central Bank (ECB)**. Its primary role in sovereign credit analysis is the implementation of monetary policy to ensure price stability, which is a critical component of a country's institutional strength and economic resilience. As a member of the Eurozone, the Central Bank of Malta does not possess independent monetary policy discretion regarding interest rates or money supply but acts as a conduit for ECB policy transmission. Its operational independence is considered a strength for Malta's sovereign credit profile, as it aligns with the credibility of the ECB's monetary framework, thereby eliminating exchange rate risk and benefiting from broader Eurozone monetary stability.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Monetary Policy Implementation**: Executes monetary policy decisions made by the ECB Governing Council to maintain price stability. This contributes to Malta's strong price stability record, with inflation remaining low and stable (2.4% in 2024, projected 2.2% in 2025), aligned with ECB targets.
- **Financial Stability Supervision**: While specific supervisory details are not outlined in the 2025 Country Report summary, the Central Bank plays a role in the oversight of the financial sector, which exhibits high capital adequacy (CET1 ratio 20.2%) and strong liquidity (LCR 358%). The bank's environment supports a resilient balance of payments and adequate foreign exchange reserves.
- **Statistical and Economic Data Provision**: Collaborates with the **National Statistics Office Malta** and **Eurostat** to provide data necessary for the **European Commission's** assessment under the **European Semester**. This data underpins the analysis of Malta's fiscal and macroeconomic performance, including debt sustainability and external liquidity.
