# Italian Parliament

## Mandate & Role

The **Italian Parliament** serves as a central legislative body within the Italian government, playing a critical role in shaping **fiscal policy**, authorizing public spending, and implementing structural reforms. In the context of sovereign credit risk and economic stability, the Parliament is a key actor in the enactment of the **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)** and the oversight of the **National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP)** implementation. Its actions directly influence **institutional strength**, **policy credibility**, and **debt sustainability** assessments by international bodies such as the [[International Monetary Fund]] and the [[European Commission]]. The Parliament's efficiency in passing reforms and maintaining **policymaking stability** is a factor in evaluating Italy's geopolitical and political stability risks.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **NRRP Implementation Oversight**: The Parliament is involved in the legislative processes required to meet the milestones and targets of the **National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP)**, with a critical deadline for full implementation by **2026-06-30**. Delays in legislative action or spending execution are cited as risks to medium-term growth prospects.
- **Fiscal Framework Adoption**: The body plays a central role in adopting and adjusting the **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)**, which aims to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2026 and eventually below 2% by 2029, addressing the high public debt-to-GDP ratio (135.3% in 2024).
- **Structural Reforms**: Legislative initiatives focused on boosting productivity, enhancing labor supply (including female labor force participation), and transitioning to renewable energy are processed through the Parliament. These reforms are essential for offsetting demographic headwinds and improving long-term **growth prospects**.
- **Tax Reform and Compliance**: The Parliament is responsible for legislative changes related to tax reforms aimed at broadening the tax base and improving compliance, which are identified as strengths in **policy credibility**.
