# National Commission on Campaign Accounts and Political Financing (CNCCFP)

## Mandate & Role

The National Commission on Campaign Accounts and Political Financing (CNCCFP) is a key institutional body within France responsible for overseeing political financing. Its primary role involves ensuring transparency and integrity in the funding of political campaigns and parties. The CNCCFP contributes to the broader **Anti-Corruption framework** and **Accountability** mechanisms in France, supporting the country's **Institutional Strength**. It works alongside other regulatory bodies such as the [[High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)]] and the [[Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA)]] to mitigate risks related to illicit political funding.

## Key Activities & Reports

The CNCCFP's activities are integral to maintaining trust in the democratic process and supporting the **Policy credibility** of the French state. Its work is highlighted as a strength in the area of **Accountability**, specifically noting "**Independent auditing by Court of Audit and transparency in political financing**" as a positive factor in France's sovereign credit profile. The institution helps ensure that political entities adhere to legal standards regarding funding, thereby reducing the risk of corruption in public procurement and political administration.
