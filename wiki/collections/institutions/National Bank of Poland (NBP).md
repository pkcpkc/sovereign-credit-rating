# National Bank of Poland (NBP)

## Mandate & Role

The **National Bank of Poland (NBP)** serves as the central bank of Poland, primarily responsible for maintaining price stability through an inflation-targeting framework with a target of 2.5% ±1%. It plays a critical role in the country's sovereign credit rating profile by providing a stable monetary policy anchor and demonstrating strong institutional independence. The NBP is recognized for its credible inflation-targeting record and autonomy, which has been maintained despite political pressure to cut rates to support growth. This independence is cited as a key strength in Poland's policy credibility and institutional strength.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Monetary Policy Stance**: As of late 2024, the NBP maintained a tight monetary policy stance in response to sticky core inflation (approximately 5% year-on-year) driven by tight labor markets and administered energy price increases. The bank resisted political headwinds to cut rates prematurely.
- **Disinflation Strategy**: The IMF Staff Report (2025) commended the NBP's prudent policies which have successfully anchored inflation expectations. The consensus, supported by the IMF, is that monetary policy should remain tight until there is clear evidence of decelerating wage growth and a sustained decline in inflation toward the target, with rate cuts potentially commencing around mid-2025.
- **Inflation Targeting**: The NBP operates under a flexible exchange rate regime, allowing the **Zloty** to float freely, which serves as an automatic adjustment mechanism for external shocks. While headline inflation declined from 2023 peaks, core inflation remained elevated due to wage-price spiral risks, necessitating the NBP's cautious approach.
