# ARCOM

## Mandate & Role

**ARCOM** (Autorité de régulation de la communication audiovisuelle et numérique) is an independent regulatory body in France responsible for overseeing audiovisual and digital communication. It plays a critical role in maintaining media freedom, ensuring judicial independence in media-related cases, and upholding the rule of law regarding media pluralism and transparency.

In the context of sovereign credit risk and institutional strength, ARCOM is cited as an **independent and credible regulatory body** that strengthens policy credibility. Its independence contributes to the stability of the media landscape and the overall quality of governance in France. The agency is tasked with implementing European regulations, specifically the **Digital Services Act (DSA)** and the upcoming **European Media Freedom Act (EMFA)**.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Regulatory Expansion**: ARCOM is expanding its regulatory scope and powers to align with the **Digital Services Act (DSA)**. It is also actively preparing for the implementation of the **European Media Freedom Act (EMFA)**.
*   **Media Ownership Transparency**: The agency has contributed to significant progress in media ownership transparency by assisting in the development of a national database to identify ultimate beneficial owners of media entities.
*   **Resource Constraints**: Despite its growing mandate, ARCOM faces challenges regarding staff resources, which may impact its capacity to fully enforce new regulations.
*   **Institutional Credibility**: Recognized by the European Commission as a pillar of institutional strength in France, contributing to high perceived independence in regulatory oversight.
