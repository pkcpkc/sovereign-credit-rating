# Cour des Comptes

## Mandate & Role

The **Cour des Comptes** is a key French government body involved in fiscal oversight and public finance analysis. It serves as an advisory institution for the government and the [[Council of the European Union]] in the context of sovereign credit risk and fiscal sustainability. Specifically, it is a core component of France's institutional framework for assessing **fiscal sustainability**, **debt burden**, and **public expenditure**. The Court contributes to the credibility of France's **fiscal performance** and **accountability** mechanisms, providing expert analysis that supports the implementation of the **Medium-term fiscal-structural plan** required under the reformed **EU Economic Governance Framework**. Its involvement underscores the institutional strength necessary to address challenges such as **high public expenditure** and **rising interest payments on debt**.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Fiscal Stability Assessment**: Contributes to the analysis of **fiscal sustainability** and **macroeconomic stability** in France, particularly in the context of rising public debt (projected 113% of GDP in 2024) and deficits.
- **Policy Advisory Role**: Participates in the evaluation of France's **medium-term fiscal-structural plan**, which was endorsed by the [[Council of the European Union]] in January 2025.
- **Institutional Coordination**: Works alongside other key entities such as the **High Council of Public Finance** and the **Banque de France** to provide comprehensive economic and fiscal surveillance.
