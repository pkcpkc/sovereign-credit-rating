# European Public Prosecutor’s Office

## Mandate & Role

The European Public Prosecutor’s Office (EPPO) is a centralized European Union body established to protect the EU's financial interests by investigating, prosecuting, and bringing to judgment crimes against the EU budget. Its mandate involves ensuring effective judicial independence and accountability in cross-border criminal matters, particularly concerning fraud, corruption, and money laundering that affect EU funds.

## Key Activities & Reports

The EPPO is monitored within the context of Romania's judicial landscape and anti-corruption framework. The **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Romania** (European Commission, 2025) lists the EPPO among the key institutions relevant to the assessment of institutional strength and the anti-corruption framework in Romania. Its operations are integral to the broader ecosystem of judicial independence and the effectiveness of prosecutions against high-level corruption, areas subject to scrutiny regarding legislative stability and the interpretation of statutes of limitations by national courts like the **High Court of Cassation and Justice**.
