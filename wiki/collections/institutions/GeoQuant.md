# GeoQuant

## Mandate & Role

GeoQuant is a research institution that provides specialized climate risk analysis and data used by credit rating agencies. It is specifically cited in Fitch Ratings' Sovereign Rating Criteria (effective September 2025) as a source for **Climate Vulnerability Signals (Climate.VS)**. GeoQuant's models assist in identifying sovereigns with high exposure to climate risks, aggregating data on physical risks (such as heat, drought, storms, floods, and sea-level rise) and transition risks (such as fossil fuel dependence and green energy costs) into a standardized 0-100 score for future time horizons (2025, 2035, and 2050).

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Climate Vulnerability Signals (Climate.VS):** GeoQuant generates the Climate.VS scores utilized by Fitch Ratings as a screening tool. These scores trigger additional analytical scrutiny for sovereigns with a score of 50 or higher in 2035.
*   **Climate Risk Modeling:** The institution provides data on physical and transition climate risks that are integrated into the **Qualitative Overlay (QO)** of sovereign ratings. If climate risks are material and not fully captured by the standard Sovereign Rating Model (SRM), these signals inform notch adjustments under "Other structural factors."
