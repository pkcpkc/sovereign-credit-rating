# HU Berlin

## Mandate & Role

HU Berlin serves as an academic institution focused on financial economics education and research, specifically regarding sovereign credit rating methodologies and risk analysis. It provides a platform for critical academic examination of rating agency frameworks, such as those used by [[S&P Global Ratings]]. The institution supports initiatives that scrutinize the transparency, replicability, and discretionary elements involved in sovereign credit assessments.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Academic Instruction and Overview**: Hosts lectures and presentations on the [[S&P Sovereign Credit Rating Methodology]], dissecting the five-pillar assessment framework (Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments). Recent presentations in 2024 by [[Alex Stomper]] highlighted the role of analyst discretion and "supplemental adjustment factors" in rating determinations.
- **Citizen Science Initiative**: Proposes and coordinates a "Citizen Science" project aimed at applying the S&P methodology to European countries. The goal is to document discrepancies and test the replicability of sovereign credit ratings, addressing concerns about the subjective nature of institutional assessments and final rating overrides.
- **Methodological Analysis**: Engages in the study of specific rating components including debt payment culture, contingent liabilities (via [[Banking Industry Country Risk Analysis (BICRA)]]), central bank independence, and external liquidity.
