# Eurostat

## Mandate & Role

Eurostat serves as the statistical office of the European Union, providing high-quality, comparable statistics for EU Member States, regions, and cities. It plays a foundational role in the **[[European Semester]]** process by validating and publishing the official macroeconomic and fiscal data used to assess compliance with the **[[Stability and Growth Pact]]**. This includes verifying government deficit and debt figures, which form the empirical basis for the European Commission's **Country Reports** (e.g., the **2025 Country Report - Slovakia** and **2025 Country Report - Malta**). Eurostat ensures that assessments of fiscal sustainability, debt ratios, and economic growth are based on standardized metrics, enabling accurate benchmarking against EU averages and facilitating the monitoring of structural reforms and fiscal policy implementation.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Fiscal and Economic Data Validation**: Validates national accounts data critical for sovereign credit analysis, including general government deficits and debt-to-GDP ratios.
  - *Slovakia*: Validated data reflecting a persistent excessive deficit (5.3% of GDP in 2024) and rising public debt (59.3% in 2024, projected to reach 63.0% by 2026).
  - *Malta*: Validated data showing a deficit of 3.7% and debt of 47.4% in 2024, used in assessing compliance with EU fiscal rules.
- **Support for the European Semester**: Provides the statistical underpinning for the Commission's annual Country Reports, which analyze structural reforms, labor market dynamics, and long-term fiscal sustainability.
  - *Slovakia Context*: Supported the analysis of deteriorating competitiveness (weak labor productivity vs. unit labor costs), structural fiscal imbalances, and the impact of demographic ageing on long-term solvency (age-related expenditure projected to rise from 20% to 25% of GDP by 2070).
- **Harmonization of National Statistics**: Ensures comparability of key indicators such as GDP per capita, inflation rates, and employment figures across the Single Market.
  - *Slovakia Context*: Facilitated benchmarking of Slovakia's low private R&D intensity (0.58% of GDP) and low innovation output against EU standards, highlighting structural weaknesses in the business environment.
- **Data for Policy Monitoring**: Supplies data to monitor the implementation of the **[[Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) Framework]]** and Cohesion Policy.
  - *Slovakia Context*: Tracked metrics related to Slovakia's RRP implementation (33% milestones fulfilled), including challenges in public procurement efficiency and absorption of funds for green transition and digitalization.
