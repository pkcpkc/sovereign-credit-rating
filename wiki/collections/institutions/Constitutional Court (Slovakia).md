# Constitutional Court (Slovakia)

## Mandate & Role

The **Constitutional Court (Slovakia)** serves as the primary guardian of the Constitution and the rule of law in Slovakia. Its mandate includes the review of the constitutionality of legislative acts and other measures, ensuring alignment with the Slovak Constitution and international obligations, including those of the **European Union** and the **Council of Europe**.

In the context of sovereign credit risk and country risk analysis, the Court plays a critical role in maintaining institutional checks and balances. However, its effectiveness and the perception of the broader judicial system's independence remain significant risk factors. The Court is frequently involved in addressing constitutional challenges related to:

*   **Legislative Process**: Reviewing laws adopted through fast-track procedures that may bypass adequate stakeholder consultation.
*   **Media Freedom and Access to Information**: Adjudicating challenges to amendments of the Freedom of Information Act that introduced fees and longer response times.
*   **EU Law Primacy**: Handling cases related to proposed constitutional amendments that potentially challenge the primacy of EU law, a key concern for geopolitical and political stability.
*   **Human Rights Obligations**: Addressing the weak enforcement of judgments from the **European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR)**, with the number of pending leading judgments rising from 31 in January 2025 to 33 by June 2025.

## Key Activities & Reports

The Constitutional Court's activities and rulings are closely monitored by the **European Commission** in the **2025 Rule of Law Report** as indicators of the health of Slovakia's justice system. Key activities and related developments include:

*   **Constitutional Challenges to Legislation**: The Court has faced challenges regarding the **Amendments to the Freedom of Information Act** (entered into force March 2025), which raised concerns about transparency and access to public information.
*   **Review of Constitutional Amendments**: The Court is a central institution in the debate surrounding proposed constitutional amendments (first reading April 2025) that raise concerns about the primacy of EU law, impacting policy credibility and institutional strength.
*   **Judicial Independence Safeguards**: While the Court itself operates within the framework of judicial independence, the report highlights broader systemic issues, such as low public perception of judicial independence (32%) and unresolved safeguards for the dismissal of **Judicial Council (Slovakia)** members, which indirectly affect the constitutional order.
*   **Implementation of International Judgments**: The Court's role is contextualized by the state's poor track record in implementing **European Court of Human Rights** judgments, signaling potential weaknesses in the finality and authority of judicial decisions within the country.
