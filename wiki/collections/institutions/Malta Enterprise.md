# Malta Enterprise

## Mandate & Role

Malta Enterprise is a key government institution in Malta responsible for driving economic development, supporting businesses, and fostering innovation. It plays a critical role in implementing structural reforms and investments outlined in Malta's **Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF)** plan. The institution is central to addressing identified structural weaknesses in the Maltese economy, particularly the historically low investment in **research and innovation** (R&D intensity at 0.64% of GDP). Malta Enterprise works in tandem with **Science Malta (Xjenza Malta)** to enhance the country's innovation ecosystem, aiming to shift the economy towards a more knowledge-based model and reduce reliance on traditional service sectors like tourism and financial services.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Recovery and Resilience Plan Implementation**: Malta Enterprise is instrumental in the execution of the RRP, which aims to address structural challenges such as low R&D spending and skills mismatches. The institution oversees investments focused on digitalization, energy efficiency, and industrial innovation to align with EU strategic priorities, including the **Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform (STEP)**.
*   **Innovation and R&D Support**: In response to the European Commission's highlights regarding Malta's low R&D intensity, Malta Enterprise facilitates partnerships between the public and private sectors to boost **research and innovation investment**. It supports initiatives designed to increase the adoption of advanced technologies among SMEs, contributing to higher productivity growth.
*   **Cohesion Policy Alignment**: The institution coordinates with broader **Cohesion Policy** frameworks to ensure that EU funds are effectively utilized for structural reforms, including improving the business environment and addressing regulatory burdens.
