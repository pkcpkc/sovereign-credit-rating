# Fiscal Council

## Mandate & Role

The **Fiscal Council** is an independent institution established in **Poland** to enhance fiscal governance, policy credibility, and institutional strength. Its primary mandate involves the oversight of fiscal policy, contributing to the credibility of the government's **Medium-term fiscal-structural plan** and efforts to address the **Excessive deficit procedure**.

The establishment of the Fiscal Council is cited as a key strength in **policy credibility and institutional strength**, signaling a commitment to transparent and independent fiscal assessment. It is designed to operate independently to provide objective analysis and recommendations, thereby supporting **fiscal consolidation** efforts amidst challenges such as rising public debt and high budget deficits.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Establishment and Operational Timeline**:
  - Legislation establishing the Fiscal Council was enacted on **2024-12-31**.
  - The institution is projected to become fully operational by **2026**, with a specific milestone noted for **2026-07-05** in relation to its operational status.
  - Its creation is linked to broader efforts to improve **regulatory governance** and reduce **legislative volatility**.

- **Context of Establishment**:
  - Created in response to concerns over **fiscal performance**, including a general government deficit of 6.6% of GDP in 2024 and rising public debt levels.
  - Aims to support the implementation of fiscal adjustment measures endorsed by the **Council of the European Union**.
  - Part of the **Action Plan on Rule of Law** and improvements in **accountability** and **transparency in decision-making**.
