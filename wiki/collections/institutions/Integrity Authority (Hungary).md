# Integrity Authority (Hungary)

## Mandate & Role

The Integrity Authority is a key institutional body within Hungary's anti-corruption framework, tasked with oversight duties related to integrity tests and lifestyle checks of public officials. Its primary mandate is to monitor compliance with asset declaration requirements and detect potential conflicts of interest or unexplained wealth among those in public office.

However, the institution's operational effectiveness and independence are frequently cited as critical weaknesses in the country's sovereign credit risk profile. The Authority faces significant structural and political obstacles that hinder its ability to fulfill its mandate effectively. These challenges include limited access to necessary data, insufficient verification mechanisms within the asset declaration system, and a lack of systematic follow-up or sanctions for non-compliance by public officials. Consequently, the Authority's capacity to serve as a robust check on high-level corruption is severely constrained, contributing to a high perception of corruption (41/100 on the Corruption Perceptions Index) and a lack of track record in prosecuting high-level corruption cases.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Asset Declaration Oversight**: The Authority monitors the submission of asset declarations by public officials. However, the current system is characterized by weak verification processes. There is no systematic follow-up for non-compliance, allowing discrepancies to go uninvestigated.
*   **Integrity Tests and Lifestyle Checks**: As part of the National Anti-Corruption Strategy and Action Plan, the Authority is responsible for conducting integrity tests. Despite these formal measures, practical obstacles limit the depth and impact of these checks.
*   **Operational Obstacles**: Reports from the European Commission (2025 Rule of Law Report) highlight that the Integrity Authority continues to face "obstacles in accessing data and fulfilling oversight duties." These barriers prevent the Authority from effectively verifying the accuracy of declared assets or investigating potential illicit enrichment.
