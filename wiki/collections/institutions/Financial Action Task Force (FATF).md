# Financial Action Task Force (FATF)

## Mandate & Role

The Financial Action Task Force (FATF) is referenced in the context of France's sovereign credit risk profile as a key institution supporting policy credibility and institutional strength. Its primary role in this context involves establishing and enforcing standards for Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT). A robust AML/CFT regime, overseen and aligned with FATF standards, is cited as a strength contributing to France's policy credibility and overall financial system integrity.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **AML/CFT Regime Oversight**: The FATF's standards underpin France's "robust cyber risk framework and AML/CFT regime," which is identified as a strength in the country's policy credibility and institutional strength assessment by the [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]].
