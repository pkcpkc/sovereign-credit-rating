# Austrian Court of Audit

## Mandate & Role

The **Austrian Court of Audit** serves as a key institution for institutional checks and balances in Austria, providing independent oversight of public finances and governance. According to the 2025 EU Commission Rule of Law Report, the Court performs extended mandates on party finance, contributing to the strength of the country's accountability framework. Alongside the Ombudsman Board, it is recognized as an effective oversight body, although the report notes that appointment procedures for such independent authorities remain susceptible to politicization.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Party Finance Audits:** The Court of Audit began audits of party statements under new rules on **2025-01-01**, enhancing transparency in political financing.
*   **Lobbying Framework Reform:** The Court has repeatedly called for reform of the lobbying framework, advocating for a robust transparency register to address weaknesses in the current system, which dates back to 2013.
*   **Institutional Checks:** It functions effectively within the broader ecosystem of institutional checks and balances, complementing the work of the [[Federal Bureau of Anti-Corruption (BAK)]] and the [[Central Public Prosecutor's Office for Combating Economic Crimes and Corruption (WKStA)]].
