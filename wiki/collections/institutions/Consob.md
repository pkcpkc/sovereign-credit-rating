# Consob

## Mandate & Role

Consob is a key Italian regulatory authority operating within the broader framework of financial stability and institutional strength in Italy. It is identified in International Monetary Fund assessments as a primary institution responsible for maintaining market integrity and protecting investors. Its activities contribute to the overall credibility of Italy's financial sector and the effectiveness of its Anti-Money Laundering/Counter-Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework, which are critical components of Italy's policy credibility and institutional strength ratings.

## Key Activities & Reports

Consob functions alongside other major Italian financial regulators, including the Bank of Italy, IVASS, FITD, and COVIP, to ensure the stability and transparency of the Italian financial system. In the context of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, Consob is cited as part of the institutional infrastructure that supports:

- **Financial Stability:** Contributing to the soundness of the financial sector, which is characterized by strong profitability and low non-performing loans, though vulnerabilities remain in some Less Significant Institutions.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Supporting the strengthened AML/CFT framework, which is listed as a strength in Italy's policy credibility and institutional strength.
- **Market Oversight:** Operating under the supervision and coordination with the Bank of Italy and the European Central Bank to mitigate sovereign-bank nexus risks and external vulnerabilities.
