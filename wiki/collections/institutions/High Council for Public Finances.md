# High Council for Public Finances

## Mandate & Role

The High Council for Public Finances (HCPO) is an independent advisory body in France mandated to reinforce the monitoring, forecasting, and transparency of public finances. It plays a critical role in enhancing **policy credibility and institutional strength** by providing objective macroeconomic forecasts and assessing the sustainability of fiscal policies. Its work supports the broader framework of **fiscal consolidation** and helps ensure accountability in government spending, particularly in the context of meeting EU fiscal rules and managing **public debt sustainability**.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Macroeconomic Forecasting and Monitoring**: The HCPO produces independent forecasts of public finances, which are used to validate government projections. In May 2025, the Council held its first meeting of the 'Cercle des prévisionnistes' (Circle of Forecasters) to discuss macroeconomic forecasts, highlighting its active role in refining economic outlooks amidst domestic uncertainty.
- **Fiscal Oversight and Transparency**: The Council reinforces monitoring mechanisms to address threats related to transparency in local government spending. It works alongside the **Courts of Auditors** to provide critical reports on efficiency and to mitigate risks associated with complex multi-layered local government structures.
- **Support for Fiscal Consolidation**: The HCPO's assessments underpin the credibility of the **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)**. By providing independent validation of fiscal trajectories, it aids in building broad political support for fiscal plans and ensuring that the deficit target of below 3% by 2029 remains on track.
