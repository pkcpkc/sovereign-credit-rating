# National Bank of Poland

## Mandate & Role

The National Bank of Poland (NBP) serves as the central bank of Poland, responsible for maintaining price stability and ensuring financial sector resilience. In the context of sovereign credit rating factors, the NBP plays a critical role in managing the flexible exchange rate regime of the złoty. While the appreciation of the złoty poses risks to cost competitiveness, the central bank's management of this flexible exchange rate is considered a strength for the country's external sustainability. The NBP also oversees the health of the financial sector, which is characterized by high capitalization, strong liquidity, and declining non-performing loan ratios, although it monitors legal risks stemming from legacy forex mortgages.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Exchange Rate Management**: The NBP implements a flexible exchange rate policy for the złoty, balancing the need for external sustainability against the risk of excessive appreciation impacting industrial competitiveness.
*   **Financial Sector Supervision**: The institution monitors the resilience of the Polish banking sector, ensuring that capital buffers remain strong to withstand potential costs from banking sector disputes and legacy issues.
*   **Price Stability Surveillance**: The NBP tracks inflation trends, which have been easing (3.7% in 2024), although it remains vigilant regarding inflation concerns driven by high unit labor costs.
