The **High Council of Public Finance** is a key French institutional body referenced in the context of France's sovereign credit risk and fiscal governance. It plays a role in the oversight and assessment of [[Fiscal Policy]] and [[Fiscal Sustainability]] within France, particularly in the context of implementing reforms and maintaining macroeconomic stability under the [[Reformed EU Economic Governance Framework]].
