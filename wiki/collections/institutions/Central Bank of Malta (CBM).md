# Central Bank of Malta (CBM)

## Mandate & Role

The **Central Bank of Malta (CBM)** serves as the national central bank within the [[Eurosystem]], contributing to the implementation of independent monetary policy for the Eurozone. Beyond its core mandate of price stability, the CBM holds a critical macroprudential mandate tasked with ensuring financial stability in Malta. It exercises effective oversight of the financial sector, collaborating closely with the [[Malta Financial Services Authority (MFSA)]] to supervise banking resilience, manage real estate sector risks, and address emerging challenges such as cybersecurity threats and trade-based money laundering.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Macroprudential Policy & Real Estate Risk**: The CBM is actively engaged in tightening macroprudential policies to mitigate systemic risks, particularly the substantial exposure of banks to the real estate sector (which accounts for ~70% of loan portfolios). In response to [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]] recommendations from the 2024 Article IV Consultation, the CBM is advised to raise the sectoral systemic risk buffer (sSyRB) rate and broaden its scope to include construction and commercial real estate loans.
*   **Financial Stability Oversight**: The CBM monitors the health of the financial system, which remains robust with high capitalization (Tier 1 ratio ~21%) and low non-performing loan ratios (~2%). Key surveillance areas include monitoring the limited pass-through of ECB rates to mortgages, which may mask underlying risks, and addressing data gaps in the commercial real estate sector.
*   **Coordination with International Bodies**: The CBM participates in [[IMF]] Article IV Consultations and [[Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP)]] reviews. It works alongside the [[European Central Bank (ECB)]] and the [[European Commission (EC)]] to align national financial policies with EU frameworks.
*   **Acting Leadership**: As of the 2024 consultation period, [[Alexander Demarco]] served as the Acting Governor of the CBM.
