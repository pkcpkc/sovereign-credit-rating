# Polish Parliament

## Mandate & Role

The **Polish Parliament** serves as the national legislative body responsible for enacting laws that shape Poland’s fiscal, economic, and regulatory environment. In the context of sovereign credit risk, the Parliament is a critical actor in implementing the **medium-term fiscal-structural plan** endorsed by the Council of the European Union to address the **excessive deficit procedure**. Its legislative activity directly influences policy credibility and institutional strength; however, concerns regarding **legislative volatility**, complex regulatory frameworks, and the frequent use of fast-track procedures (average preparation time of 31 days) have been identified as threats to investment stability and policymaking consistency. The Parliament is also tasked with enacting reforms to improve **pension sustainability**, enhance the **healthcare system**, and support the **energy transition**, including measures to phase out fossil fuel subsidies and support renewable energy deployment.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Fiscal Consolidation Legislation**: The Parliament is responsible for passing budgetary laws and structural reforms necessary to reduce the general government deficit (which reached 6.6% of GDP in 2024) and stabilize public debt levels amid rising defense spending.
*   **Regulatory Governance**: Recent legislative efforts include measures to limit the use of fast-track procedures and increase the application of impact assessments, aiming to reduce regulatory volatility and improve the business climate.
*   **Structural Reforms**: Legislative action is ongoing regarding **vocational education and training (VET)** reforms to address skills shortages, pension system adjustments to mitigate risks from an aging population, and healthcare system reforms to shift focus from hospital-centric care to prevention.
*   **Implementation of EU Directives**: The Parliament enacts national laws to comply with EU mandates, such as the implementation of **Pillar 2 Directive** on global minimum corporate tax (effective 2025-01-01) and regulations supporting the **Recovery and Resilience Facility** milestones.
