# High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)

## Mandate & Role

The **High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP)** is an independent regulatory body in France responsible for overseeing the ethical conduct and financial transparency of public officials. Its primary mandate involves monitoring asset declarations, preventing conflicts of interest, and ensuring integrity within the public sector. In the context of sovereign credit risk and country risk analysis, the HATVP is cited as a key institution contributing to **policy credibility** and **institutional strength**. It is recognized for its independence and credibility in reinforcing France's anti-corruption framework and accountability mechanisms.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Institutional Strengthening:** The HATVP has increased its staffing levels to manage a significant 48% rise in the processing of asset declarations, demonstrating enhanced operational capacity.
*   **Lobbying Transparency:** The authority is expected to launch an online register for foreign lobbyists in July 2025. This initiative addresses gaps in lobbying transparency, although current disclosure rules still do not apply to officials at the top-executive level.
*   **Anti-Corruption Framework:** The HATVP plays a central role in the implementation of the national anti-corruption strategy, working alongside other bodies such as the **Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA)** and the **National Financial Prosecutor (PNF)** to combat integrity risks and support the rule of law.
