# Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy

## Mandate & Role

The Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy is a key Italian government body responsible for fostering industrial competitiveness, supporting small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), and driving the digital and green transitions. In the context of sovereign credit risk and structural analysis, the Ministry plays a central role in implementing structural reforms aimed at addressing Italy's productivity stagnation and regional disparities. It oversees policies related to the "Made in Italy" brand, venture capital development, and the reduction of administrative burdens for businesses.

According to the **2025 Country Report - Italy** by the [[European Commission]], the Ministry operates within a framework of fragmented governance of industrial policy and state-owned enterprises, which is identified as a threat to policy credibility. Its activities are critical for addressing the underdeveloped capital markets and limited venture capital availability that constrain financing for innovative firms. The Ministry's efforts are also aligned with the [[Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan]] (MTFSP), contributing to the structural pillars required for long-term debt sustainability and growth.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Structural Reform Implementation**: The Ministry is involved in executing reforms under the [[Recovery and Resilience Facility]] (RRP) and the MTFSP, focusing on improving the business environment, digitalizing public administration, and enhancing transparency in procurement to combat bureaucracy and administrative burdens.
- **Support for SMEs and Innovation**: Given the identification of underdeveloped capital markets and low equity investment by households as threats, the Ministry works to facilitate funding for SMEs and startups, addressing the lack of venture capital that hinders R&D investment and productivity growth.
- **Green and Digital Transition**: The Ministry supports the industrial base in transitioning towards clean technologies, where Italy holds leadership positions in solar PV and wind manufacturing. It addresses dependencies on imported critical raw materials and semiconductors through supply chain resilience strategies.
- **Industrial Policy Governance**: The Ministry navigates the challenge of fragmented industrial policy governance, coordinating with other entities to ensure coherent support for state-owned enterprises and local governments, while adhering to EU fiscal surveillance mechanisms.
