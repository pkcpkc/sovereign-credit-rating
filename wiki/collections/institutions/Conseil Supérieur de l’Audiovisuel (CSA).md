# Conseil Supérieur de l’Audiovisuel (CSA)

## Mandate & Role

The Conseil Supérieur de l’Audiovisuel (CSA) is an independent media regulator responsible for overseeing media pluralism and freedom within the French-speaking Community of Belgium. It operates alongside other regional regulators, such as the Flemish Regulator for the Media (VRM) and the Medienrat. The CSA’s legal and operational independence has been strengthened, particularly through the implementation of the Digital Services Act, contributing to Belgium's overall assessment in media freedom and pluralism.

## Key Activities & Reports

The CSA contributes to the maintenance of a strong legal foundation for media freedom in Belgium. Its activities are monitored in the context of sovereign credit rating factors, specifically under "policy credibility and institutional strength," where increased powers for media regulators are cited as a strength. The regulator operates in an environment where public service media, such as Radio-Télévision Belge de la Communauté Française (RTBF), face budgetary pressures and governance scrutiny. The CSA helps ensure that despite these financial challenges, the political independence of the media remains at low risk, as noted in the Media Pluralism Monitor.
