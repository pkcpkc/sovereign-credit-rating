# IVASS

## Mandate & Role

IVASS is the Italian supervisory authority responsible for the oversight of the insurance and pension funds sectors. It plays a critical role in maintaining financial stability by monitoring the health and solvency of insurance companies and institutional pension funds, ensuring they adhere to regulatory standards that mitigate systemic risks within the Italian financial system.

## Key Activities & Reports

IVASS is recognized by the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) as a key institutional actor in Italy's financial architecture. In the context of the **2025 Article IV Consultation**, IVASS's supervisory framework is considered when assessing Italy's overall institutional strength and financial sector stability. While the banking sector receives specific attention regarding the sovereign-bank nexus and Less Significant Institutions (LSIs), IVASS ensures the parallel stability of the non-banking financial intermediaries, contributing to the broader assessment of Italy's capacity to manage contingent liabilities and maintain a resilient financial sector amidst external shocks and domestic structural challenges.
