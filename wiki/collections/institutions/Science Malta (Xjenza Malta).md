# Science Malta (Xjenza Malta)

## Mandate & Role

Science Malta (Xjenza Malta) is a government body established as part of Malta's implementation of reforms under the **Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRP)**. Its primary mandate is to address Malta's critically low investment in **research and innovation**, which stands at only 0.64% of GDP, one of the lowest rates in the European Union. The institution plays a strategic role in the country's economic diversification efforts, aiming to shift the economy away from heavy reliance on tourism and services toward innovation-led growth. By coordinating national research efforts and aligning them with EU strategic priorities, such as the Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform (STEP), Science Malta contributes to strengthening **institutional strength** and long-term **growth prospects**.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Implementation of RRP Reforms**: Science Malta was established through reforms funded by the **Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP)**, which has disbursed over 50% of its grants as of the 2025 reporting period.
- **Addressing Structural Weaknesses**: The institution tackles the structural weakness of **low R&D intensity** by fostering an environment conducive to **research and innovation investment**.
- **Strategic Alignment**: Works to align national scientific policies with broader EU objectives to enhance **economic diversity** and reduce vulnerability to external shocks in service sectors.
