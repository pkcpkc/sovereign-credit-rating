# National Audit Office

## Mandate & Role

The **National Audit Office** (NAO) of Malta serves as a critical institutional pillar for fiscal accountability and public sector oversight. Its primary mandate involves auditing public funds to ensure compliance with regulations and identifying weaknesses in financial management. In the context of sovereign credit ratings, the NAO's effectiveness is a key factor in assessing **fiscal performance and flexibility** and **institutional strength**. The agency plays a vital role in detecting instances of mismanagement of public funds and the bypassing of procurement regulations, which are identified as significant threats to **contingent liabilities** and overall fiscal sustainability.

## Key Activities & Reports

Based on the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Malta**, the NAO is actively engaged in strengthening its capacity to enhance public finance integrity:

*   **Capacity Building:** The institution has seen increased personnel resources, strengthening its ability to conduct comprehensive audits. This expansion is cited as a strength in the policy credibility and institutional strength assessment of Malta.
*   **Identifying Fiscal Risks:** The NAO has identified weaknesses in the use of public funds and bypassing of procurement regulations. These findings highlight ongoing risks related to **public procurement integrity** and **contingent liabilities**, particularly concerning the mismanagement of funds in local government and public procurement sectors.
*   **Collaborative Oversight:** The NAO operates alongside the **Internal Audit and Investigations Department**, both of which have received increased personnel, indicating a broader governmental effort to improve anti-fraud and corruption measures despite high perceived corruption levels (CPI score 46/100).
