# Medienrat

## Mandate & Role

The **Medienrat** (German Community Media Council) serves as an independent regulatory body for media in the German-speaking Community of Belgium. Its primary mandate is to safeguard media pluralism, press freedom, and the independence of public service broadcasters within its jurisdiction. The Medienrat operates alongside other regional regulators, including the [[Vlaamse Radio- en Televisieomroeporganisatie|VRT]]'s regulator (VRM) and the [[Conseil Supérieur de l’Audiovisuel|CSA]], contributing to the broader Belgian framework for media oversight.

Key aspects of its role include:
- **Regulatory Independence**: Maintaining legal and operational independence, which was further strengthened under the European Union's Digital Services Act (DSA).
- **Oversight of Public Service Media**: Monitoring the governance and operations of the [[Belgischer Rundfunk|BRF]], the public service broadcaster for the German-speaking Community, to ensure compliance with public service mandates and financial integrity.
- **Pluralism Protection**: Contributing to the monitoring of media pluralism risks, with assessments indicating low risk for political independence of the media in Belgium.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Implementation of Digital Services Act (DSA)**: The Medienrat has adapted its operational framework to comply with the DSA, enhancing its powers and independence in regulating digital media services. This aligns with broader EU efforts to strengthen institutional checks and balances in the media sector.
- **Collaboration with National Institutions**: Works in conjunction with federal and other community-level bodies to uphold the rule of law principles related to media freedom. The Medienrat's activities are monitored in the context of the **2025 Rule of Law Report** by the **European Commission**, which highlights the overall health of Belgium's media landscape.
