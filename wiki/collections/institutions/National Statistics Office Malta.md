# National Statistics Office Malta

## Mandate & Role

The **National Statistics Office Malta** serves as the primary national statistical authority, responsible for the collection, analysis, and dissemination of official statistical data for Malta. In the context of sovereign credit rating and country risk analysis, the office plays a critical role in providing the empirical foundation for macroeconomic assessments conducted by international bodies such as the **[[European Commission]]** and **[[Eurostat]]**.

Its data underpins key metrics evaluated in the **[[European Semester]]** framework, including GDP growth, employment rates, inflation, and fiscal indicators like tax revenue and deficit levels. The accuracy and timeliness of its data are essential for assessing Malta's **[[fiscal sustainability]]**, monitoring the **[[excessive deficit procedure]]** (where applicable), and evaluating the country's adherence to **[[Stability and Growth Pact]]** rules. The office supports the **[[Malta Fiscal Advisory Council (MFAC)]]** and the **[[Central Bank of Malta]]** by providing the statistical baseline for independent fiscal and monetary analysis.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Macroeconomic Data Compilation**: Produces core economic indicators referenced in the **[[2025 Country Report - Malta]]**, including real GDP growth (6.0% in 2024), employment rates (83.0% in 2024), and inflation data (2.4% in 2024).
*   **Fiscal Indicator Reporting**: Contributes data on government revenue and expenditure, supporting the analysis of the general government deficit (projected to decline from 3.7% of GDP in 2024 to 2.8% in 2026) and debt-to-GDP ratios (47.4% in 2024).
*   **Structural Statistics**: Provides data on structural challenges such as **[[skills mismatches]]**, **[[gender employment gap]]** (13.4 percentage points), and labor market dynamics, including immigration flows impacting employment.
*   **Compliance with EU Standards**: Collaborates with **[[Eurostat]]** to ensure data harmonization across the EU, facilitating cross-country comparisons required for the **[[Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) Framework]]** monitoring and **[[Cohesion Policy Monitoring]]**.
