# Council for Budgetary Responsibility

## Mandate & Role

The **Council for Budgetary Responsibility** is an independent institution in Slovakia responsible for monitoring fiscal policy and ensuring adherence to fiscal rules. It plays a critical role in maintaining policy credibility and institutional strength, serving as a key pillar of the country's fiscal governance framework. The Council is recognized for its independence and robustness, contributing significantly to the transparency of fiscal data and the accountability of public spending.

## Key Activities & Reports

The Council is instrumental in supporting the **Slovak Republic**'s fiscal consolidation efforts, particularly in the context of the government's ambitious four-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025–2028). Its presence and effectiveness are cited as a major strength in sovereign credit rating assessments, specifically under the category of **policy credibility and institutional strength**. The Council aids in:

*   Ensuring **high data adequacy and transparency**, contributing to Slovakia's SDDS Plus status.
*   Providing independent assessment and oversight of the **fiscal deficit** and **public debt** trajectories.
*   Strengthening the **expenditure ceiling framework** and fiscal rules to mitigate risks associated with political polarization and government changes.

The Council's work supports the objectives outlined in international assessments, such as the **IMF** 2025 Article IV Consultation, by helping to anchor fiscal discipline amidst economic headwinds and governance challenges.
