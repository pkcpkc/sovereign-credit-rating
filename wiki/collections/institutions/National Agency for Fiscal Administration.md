# National Agency for Fiscal Administration

## Mandate & Role

The National Agency for Fiscal Administration (ANAF) is the primary government body in Romania responsible for the collection of taxes and duties, serving as the central pillar of the country's fiscal policy implementation. In the context of sovereign credit rating and macroeconomic stability, ANAF plays a critical role in determining the effectiveness of the fiscal framework and the sustainability of public finances.

Recent analyses highlight significant challenges in the agency's operational efficiency, which directly impact Romania's economic strength and tax base. Key issues include a high VAT gap estimated at 33.7% in 2024, indicative of substantial informal economic activity and collection inefficiencies. Consequently, Romania records a low tax revenue-to-GDP ratio of 26.2%, well below the EU average of 39%. The agency is currently tasked with implementing structural reforms and the Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP) to broaden the tax base, improve compliance, and enhance public administration efficiency amidst a challenging business environment marked by regulatory uncertainty.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Tax Revenue Collection and Gap Reduction**: ANAF is central to efforts aimed at reducing the high VAT gap and increasing the overall tax-to-GDP ratio. The effectiveness of these collection mechanisms is a key factor in assessing Romania's fiscal performance and debt sustainability.
*   **Implementation of Fiscal Reforms**: The agency is responsible for executing immediate fiscal consolidation measures adopted in late 2024 and early 2025, including the removal of tax exemptions and reforms to the microenterprise tax regime, intended to save approximately 2.0% of GDP in 2025.
*   **Addressing Administrative Bottlenecks**: ANAF operates within a broader context of weak administrative capacity and suboptimal public services, which are cited as threats to policy credibility. Delays in the implementation of the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP), of which only 14% of milestones were fulfilled recently, reflect broader administrative challenges that impact the agency's ability to modernize and digitize tax collection processes.
*   **Fiscal Code Compliance**: The agency manages compliance with a fiscal code that has been amended hundreds of times since 2015, creating unpredictability for businesses. This legislative instability is cited by 90% of Romanian firms as the top barrier to long-term investment, complicating ANAF's role in fostering a stable investment climate.
