# Austrian Ministry of Justice

## Mandate & Role

The Austrian Ministry of Justice serves as the primary government body responsible for overseeing the administration of justice, the prosecutorial system, and the implementation of rule of law standards in Austria. Its mandate includes managing judicial appointments, enforcing criminal procedure laws, and developing digital justice initiatives. In the context of sovereign credit rating and country risk analysis, the Ministry plays a critical role in maintaining institutional strength and policy credibility. However, it has faced scrutiny regarding the politicization of appointments to independent authorities and delays in reforming prosecutorial independence. The Ministry is tasked with implementing the government's commitment to merit-based appointments and advancing "Justice 3.0" digitalization efforts, which aim for 90% digital case management by the end of 2025.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Prosecutorial Independence Reforms**: The Ministry is currently drafting legislation to establish an independent Federal Prosecution Office, a commitment made in the March 2025 Government Programme. Despite this, the Minister of Justice retains the power to issue instructions to prosecutors in individual cases, a practice under review following an independent investigative commission’s findings of political influence attempts in July 2024.
*   **Judicial Appointment Oversight**: The Ministry oversees procedures for judicial appointments, including those for administrative court presidents. There have been no recent steps to introduce systematic judicial involvement in these appointments, raising concerns about alignment with European standards and increasing risks related to policy credibility.
*   **Digital Justice & Efficiency**: The Ministry is advancing the "Justice 3.0" initiative to enhance court efficiency. Key milestones include the expansion of compulsory online publication of judgments to Higher Regional Courts as of January 1, 2025, and the entry into force of criminal procedure law amendments to increase efficiency on the same date.
*   **Regulatory Enforcement & Fees**: The Ministry implemented significant court fee increases (approx. 23%) effective April 1, 2025. It is also responsible for ensuring access to justice following Constitutional Court rulings, such as the mandate to provide legal aid for all administrative court proceedings, with old rules expected to be suspended by March 31, 2026.
