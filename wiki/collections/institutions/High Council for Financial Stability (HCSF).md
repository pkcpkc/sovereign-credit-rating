# High Council for Financial Stability (HCSF)

## Mandate & Role

The **High Council for Financial Stability (HCSF)** is a key French institution responsible for monitoring financial stability and supporting the implementation of macroprudential policies. In the context of sovereign credit ratings and country risk analysis, the HCSF contributes to the **policy credibility and institutional strength** of France. Its existence and operational effectiveness are cited as a strength in maintaining a robust financial oversight framework, which helps mitigate systemic risks within the banking sector. The HCSF works in tandem with other regulatory bodies to ensure the resilience of the financial system, addressing risks such as cyber threats and climate risks, which are critical components of the broader financial sector assessment.

## Key Activities & Reports

The HCSF is actively involved in the oversight of the French financial sector, contributing to the stability assessments reviewed during the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation**. Key activities and contexts include:

- **Macroprudential Policy Toolkit**: The HCSF is part of the strong macroprudential policy toolkit in France, which allows for the monitoring and mitigation of financial imbalances. This capability is noted as a strength in maintaining financial sector health, despite challenges such as weak bank profitability due to net interest margin compression.
- **Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP)**: The HCSF's framework is evaluated under the **Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP)** conducted by the **International Monetary Fund (IMF)**. The 2025 FSAP confirmed the resilience of the French banking sector, with the HCSF's monitoring contributing to the identification of risks related to non-bank financial intermediaries (NBFIs), corporate indebtedness, and cyber/climate risks.
- **Collaboration with Regulatory Authorities**: The HCSF operates within the broader ecosystem that includes the **Banque de France** and the **European Commission**, supporting the overall stability of the financial system which underpins sovereign debt sustainability.
