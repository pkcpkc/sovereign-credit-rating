# Austrian Chamber of Commerce

## Mandate & Role

The [[Austrian Chamber of Commerce]] serves as a key institutional stakeholder in Austria's economic and fiscal policy landscape, particularly within the framework of the [[European Semester]]. As a primary representative of the business community, the Chamber plays a central role in shaping and responding to structural reforms aimed at boosting competitiveness, productivity, and fiscal sustainability. Its mandate intersects with national efforts to simplify the regulatory environment, reduce administrative burdens, and improve access to risk capital for startups. The Chamber is actively involved in social dialogue and consensus-based policymaking, contributing to the assessment of economic resilience and the implementation of fiscal consolidation measures during periods of economic downturn, such as the recession observed in 2025.

## Key Activities & Reports

The Austrian Chamber of Commerce is engaged in the evaluation and feedback loops of critical economic assessments, including those conducted by the [[European Commission]], [[WIFO]], [[FISK]], and the [[Oesterreichische Nationalbank]]. Key areas of activity include:

- **Regulatory Simplification:** Advocating for the reduction of high complexity in regulations and administrative burdens that hinder investment and business dynamism.
- **Fiscal-Structural Planning:** Participating in the dialogue surrounding Austria's [[Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plans]] and the management of the projected deficit (exceeding 4% of GDP in 2025) and rising debt ratios (84.0% of GDP in 2025).
- **Labor Market and Skills:** Addressing structural challenges such as skills shortages, particularly in ICT and care sectors, and declining labor productivity growth.
- **Energy Transition:** Monitoring high energy costs and grid expansion bottlenecks, supporting the transition to renewable energy while managing affordability concerns for large consumers.
- **Innovation and Investment:** Working to improve access to venture capital and support business dynamism, countering trends of low enterprise birth rates and low business dynamism.
