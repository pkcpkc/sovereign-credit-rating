# Office for the Protection of Whistleblowers

## Mandate & Role

The **Office for the Protection of Whistleblowers** is a key institutional actor in Slovakia's anti-corruption and rule of law framework. It serves as a safeguard mechanism within the country's checks and balances, responsible for receiving reports of wrongdoing and ensuring procedural compliance by public authorities. In the context of sovereign credit risk and institutional strength, the Office functions as a monitoring body that holds government entities accountable for adhering to legal procedures regarding whistleblower disclosures.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Enforcement of Procedural Compliance**: In October 2024, the Office demonstrated its operational capacity by fining the **Ministry of Interior** for procedural violations. This action was cited as a strength in the accountability sector of Slovakia's institutional framework, indicating active enforcement of whistleblower protection laws despite broader challenges in the anti-corruption landscape.
*   **Contextual Challenges**: While the Office remains active, its effectiveness exists within a fragmented anti-corruption framework. The dissolution of specialized entities like the **National Crime Agency (NAKA)** and the **Special Branch for Combating Corruption and Organized Crime (SPO)** has reduced overall oversight capacity, potentially increasing the burden on remaining bodies like the Whistleblower Office.
