# Office of the Attorney General

## Mandate & Role

The **Office of the Attorney General** (OAG) serves as the primary prosecutorial authority in Malta, responsible for handling prosecutions for serious offenses (non-summary cases). Following the completion of the transfer of prosecutorial powers from the Office of the Public Prosecutor in September 2024, the OAG holds centralized authority over the prosecution of non-summary cases, aiming to enhance prosecutorial independence and integrity within the justice sector.

The institution plays a critical role in Malta's **Anti-Corruption Framework** and rule of law architecture. Its effectiveness and independence are key indicators in sovereign credit rating analyses and EU Rule of Law assessments. The OAG is tasked with investigating and prosecuting high-level corruption cases, though it faces challenges in establishing a robust track record of final judgments despite increased resources. The office also enhances anti-money laundering (AML) enforcement capabilities, contributing to financial sector health and regulatory compliance.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Centralization of Prosecutions**: Completed the transfer of prosecutorial powers for non-summary cases in September 2024, a significant step in reforming the justice sector to reduce executive influence and increase independence.
*   **Resource Enhancement**: Increased human resources within investigative and prosecution authorities to support the expanded mandate.
*   **High-Level Corruption Investigations**: Engaged in investigating high-level corruption cases. While investigative capacities have improved, there is noted limited progress in securing final judgments for such cases, impacting the perceived effectiveness of the anti-corruption framework.
*   **Anti-Money Laundering**: Strengthened AML enforcement capabilities, addressing past regulatory weaknesses identified in contexts such as the investor citizenship scheme.
