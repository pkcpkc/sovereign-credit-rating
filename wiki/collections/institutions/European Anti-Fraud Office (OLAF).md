# European Anti-Fraud Office (OLAF)

## Mandate & Role

The European Anti-Fraud Office (OLAF) is a key institutional actor within the European Union's framework for protecting the EU's financial interests and combating fraud. It functions as a specialized anti-fraud body, operating in conjunction with other judicial and law enforcement entities to ensure the integrity of public funds and the rule of law across member states. Its presence is noted in the context of the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Slovakia** as part of the broader institutional landscape involved in anti-corruption and legal compliance efforts.

## Key Activities & Reports

OLAF is referenced in the **2025 Rule of Law Report** regarding Slovakia's institutional checks and balances and anti-corruption framework. While the report highlights significant challenges in Slovakia's domestic anti-corruption efforts—such as the dissolution of specialized entities like the **National Crime Agency (NAKA)** and the **Bureau for Combating Crime (UBOK)**—OLAF remains a relevant external institution for overseeing fraud and ensuring alignment with EU directives, including the Protection of the Union's Financial Interests (PIF) Directive.
