# Ombudsman Board

## Mandate & Role

The **Ombudsman Board** serves as Austria's National Human Rights Institution and functions as a key component of the country's institutional checks and balances. It is recognized by the [[European Commission]] as an effective oversight body within the Austrian rule of law framework. The institution contributes to **policy_credibility_and_institutional_strength** by ensuring accountability in public administration. While it operates effectively, ongoing efforts are underway to strengthen its nomination procedure to mitigate risks of politicization in appointments to independent authorities.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Rule of Law Oversight**: Assessed in the **EU Commission 2025 Rule of Law Report** (Austria Chapter) as an effective mechanism for institutional checks and balances.
*   **Procedural Reform**: Plans are in place to enhance the Ombudsman Board's nomination process, aligning with the Government's commitment to transparent, merit-based appointments for high-level positions at independent authorities.
*   **Human Rights Protection**: Operates as a core entity in the protection of human rights, working in parallel with other bodies such as the **Austrian Court of Audit** to ensure government accountability.
