# Ministry of Finance (Poland)

## Mandate & Role

The **Ministry of Finance (Poland)** is the primary government body responsible for formulating and implementing the country's fiscal policy, managing public debt, and overseeing the national budget. In the context of sovereign credit risk and macroeconomic stability, the Ministry plays a critical role in maintaining fiscal credibility, managing the general government deficit, and ensuring compliance with European Union fiscal frameworks, such as the Stability and Growth Pact.

Following the widening of the fiscal deficit to 5.9% of GDP in 2024, the Ministry has been central to Poland's engagement with the **[[International Monetary Fund]]** (IMF) and the **[[European Union]]** (EU). The Ministry is currently responsible for executing the **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)**, aimed at reducing the deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028 and stabilizing public debt levels, which are projected to rise from ~50% to approximately 65% of GDP by 2029. The Ministry also works closely with the **[[National Bank of Poland]]** (NBP) to coordinate monetary and fiscal policies, particularly in addressing inflationary pressures and supporting disinflation efforts.

Key responsibilities include:
*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** Implementing measures to reduce structural deficits, including managing public sector wage growth and social benefits.
*   **EU Compliance:** Navigating the **EU Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP)** triggered in 2024 and aligning domestic fiscal rules with new EU frameworks.
*   **Institutional Reform:** Overseeing the establishment of an independent fiscal council (planned for 2026) to enhance transparency and accountability.
*   **Debt Management:** Managing sovereign borrowing, maintaining investment-grade ratings, and addressing contingent liabilities associated with the **[[Polish Development Bank]]** (BGK).

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **2024 Article IV Consultation with the IMF:** The Ministry engaged in extensive discussions with the **[[International Monetary Fund]]** staff, culminating in the 2024 Article IV Consultation Staff Report published in January 2025. The Ministry presented its **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)** to address the high fiscal deficit and public debt trajectory.
*   **Submission of Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP):** The Ministry adopted a plan targeting a 2.9% of GDP deficit by 2028. The IMF recommended frontloading consolidation efforts in 2025 (~0.5% of GDP adjustment) to support disinflation and allow for earlier monetary easing.
*   **EU Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) Engagement:** Following the breach of EU deficit thresholds in 2024, the Ministry has been actively working to correct the excessive deficit, a key factor in Poland's sovereign credit risk assessment.
*   **Fiscal Framework Amendments:** The Ministry amended the stabilizing expenditure rule to align with the new EU fiscal framework, enhancing the credibility of fiscal governance.
*   **Transparency Improvements:** The Ministry has taken steps to improve oversight and transparency of extrabudgetary funds, particularly those managed by the **[[Polish Development Bank]]** (BGK), addressing concerns regarding off-budget risks and contingent liabilities.
