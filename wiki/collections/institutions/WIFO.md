# WIFO

## Mandate & Role

WIFO (Wirtschaftsforschungsinstitut) is a key Austrian economic research institution involved in the national framework for fiscal and economic analysis. It shares the role of Independent Fiscal Institution (IFI) tasks with [[FISK]], a division that the European Commission notes may "obscure clear reference points" due to the split of responsibilities between the two institutions. WIFO contributes to the broader institutional strength of Austria's policymaking environment, which is characterized by strong social dialogue and consensus-based approaches. The institution plays a part in generating the economic data and forecasts that underpin Austria's participation in the [[European Semester]] framework and its submission of Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plans.

## Key Activities & Reports

WIFO is cited in the context of Austria's [[Fiscal Policy]] and economic surveillance mechanisms. Its activities are integral to the production of data used in [[European Commission]] reports, such as the 2025 Country Report on Austria. The institution operates within a system where its outputs inform assessments of [[fiscal sustainability]], [[debt sustainability]], and [[macroeconomic stability]]. The existence of split IFI tasks between WIFO and [[FISK]] is identified as a potential threat to policy credibility and institutional strength, highlighting the need for clear reference points in economic forecasting and fiscal analysis.

WIFO is also a key institutional reference in the [[International Monetary Fund]]'s 2025 Article IV Consultation with Austria. The IMF Staff Report highlights the importance of high data quality and transparency in Austria’s institutional framework, citing adherence to Special Data Dissemination Standards (SDDS) Plus. WIFO’s research and data generation support the assessments of Austria's economic strengths, including its high GDP per capita and diversified export base, as well as vulnerabilities such as sluggish productivity growth and demographic aging. The institution's work underpins the analysis of fiscal consolidation needs, particularly regarding the projected deficit and debt trajectories discussed in the consultation.
