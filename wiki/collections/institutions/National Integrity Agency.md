# National Integrity Agency

## Mandate & Role

The **National Integrity Agency** (ANI) is a key Romanian institution mandated to enhance the fight against corruption by managing asset declaration systems and overseeing conflicts of interest for public officials. Its role is critical in maintaining **policy credibility** and **accountability**, which are significant factors in **sovereign credit rating** assessments and **rule of law** evaluations. The Agency is responsible for verifying the assets and income of high-ranking state officials to ensure transparency and prevent illicit enrichment.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Conflict of Interest Management**: The Agency maintains a positive record in managing conflicts of interest, which has been cited as a strength in recent rule of law assessments.
*   **Asset Declaration System**: The ANI manages the asset declaration registry. However, the effectiveness of this system has been recently challenged. A **Constitutional Court of Romania** ruling in May 2025 deemed the requirement for public officials to declare the assets of their spouses and children unconstitutional. This decision is viewed as a threat to institutional strength and transparency, potentially weakening the Agency's ability to detect illicit wealth accumulation.
*   **Strategic Implementation**: The ANI operates within the broader framework of the **2021-2025 National Anti-Corruption Strategy**, which is currently on track for implementation.
