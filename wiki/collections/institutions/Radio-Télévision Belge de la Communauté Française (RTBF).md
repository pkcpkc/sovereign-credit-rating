# Radio-Télévision Belge de la Communauté Française (RTBF)

## Mandate & Role

The **Radio-Télévision Belge de la Communauté Française (RTBF)** is a public service broadcaster operating within the French Community of Belgium. It plays a critical role in ensuring media pluralism and press freedom, contributing to the country's democratic checks and balances. As a key entity in the Belgian media landscape, its operational stability, governance, and financial health are monitored as indicators of the health of the public sphere and institutional integrity.

## Key Activities & Reports

In the context of the **2025 Rule of Law Report**, the RTBF is highlighted regarding fiscal pressures and governance:

*   **Fiscal Pressures**: The institution faces significant budgetary constraints, with a projected shortfall of EUR 78 million, raising concerns about the sustainability of public service media funding.
*   **Media Pluralism**: The RTBF operates under the supervision of independent media regulators, specifically the **Conseil Supérieur de l’Audiovisuel (CSA)**. The Media Pluralism Monitor reports low risk for political independence for public service media, though risks have slightly increased since 2024.
*   **Governance**: The broadcaster is subject to governance scrutiny as part of the broader assessment of public service media in Belgium.
