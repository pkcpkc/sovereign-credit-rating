# Flemish Regulator for the Media (VRM)

## Mandate & Role

The Flemish Regulator for the Media (VRM) is an independent regulatory body in Belgium responsible for overseeing media pluralism and press freedom within the Flemish Community. Its primary mandate involves ensuring compliance with media laws and safeguarding the independence of the media sector. In the context of sovereign credit ratings and rule of law assessments, the VRM serves as a critical institutional check and balance, contributing to the integrity of the political environment by maintaining high standards of media transparency and independence.

## Key Activities & Reports

Recent assessments, particularly the 2025 Rule of Law Report, highlight the VRM's strengthened legal and operational independence. Key developments and activities include:

- **Enhanced Powers under Digital Services Act**: The VRM, alongside other Belgian media regulators (Conseil Supérieur de l’Audiovisuel and Medienrat), has seen an increase in regulatory powers due to the implementation of the EU Digital Services Act. This has bolstered its capacity to enforce digital media standards and protect pluralism.
- **Monitoring Media Pluralism**: The VRM contributes to the overall low risk rating for political independence in media, as reported by the Media Pluralism Monitor. However, this risk level has seen a slight increase compared to 2024, prompting continued vigilance.
- **Governance of Public Service Media**: While the VRM regulates the broader media landscape, its oversight operates within a context where public service broadcasters like Vlaamse Radio- en Televisieomroeporganisatie (VRT) face significant budgetary pressures and governance scrutiny.
