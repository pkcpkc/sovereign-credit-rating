# Guardia di Finanza

## Mandate & Role

The **Guardia di Finanza** is a key Italian law enforcement agency with significant responsibilities in financial surveillance, tax compliance, and anti-money laundering (AML) efforts. In the context of sovereign credit risk and institutional strength, it plays a critical role in strengthening Italy’s **Anti-Money Laundering/Counter-Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework**. Its activities contribute to improved tax compliance and the broadening of the tax base, which are cited as strengths in Italy’s policy credibility and institutional strength assessments by international financial institutions like the **International Monetary Fund** (IMF).

## Key Activities & Reports

The **Guardia di Finanza** is instrumental in the implementation and enforcement of financial regulations aimed at mitigating corruption and illicit financial flows. Its operations support the following areas relevant to sovereign risk analysis:

- **AML/CFT Framework Strengthening**: The agency contributes to the robustness of Italy’s AML/CFT systems, which the IMF identifies as a strength in policy credibility. This includes inter-agency collaboration on corruption detection.
- **Tax Compliance**: By enforcing tax laws, the Guardia di Finanza helps improve tax compliance, a factor that supports fiscal performance and flexibility.
- **Corruption Detection**: Enhanced collaboration with other public bodies aids in detecting corruption, although challenges remain in enforcement levels for foreign bribery cases.
