# Conseil d'Orientation des Retraites (COR)

## Mandate & Role

The **Conseil d'Orientation des Retraites (COR)** serves as an independent advisory body within France, providing critical analysis and forecasts regarding the long-term sustainability of the French pension system. Its primary role is to assess the financial equilibrium of retirement schemes and evaluate the impact of demographic trends and structural reforms on public finances. In the context of sovereign credit risk, the COR's work is essential for understanding long-term fiscal pressures related to aging populations, which are significant determinants of future debt trajectories and fiscal sustainability.

## Key Activities & Reports

The COR plays a pivotal role in monitoring the financial implications of pension reforms, such as the 2023 reform that raised the statutory retirement age to 64. The **International Monetary Fund (IMF)** cites the COR's assessments as integral to evaluating France's long-term fiscal sustainability, noting that while the pension system faces persistent pressures from demographic aging, recent reforms contribute positively to maintaining fiscal health. The institution provides data and projections that support the broader **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)** and help international creditors assess the credibility of France's approach to managing contingent liabilities and long-term expenditure rigidities.
