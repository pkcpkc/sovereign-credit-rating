# Judicial Council (Slovakia)

## Mandate & Role

The Judicial Council is a key body within the [[Slovakia]] justice system responsible for the governance of the judiciary, including the appointment of judges and disciplinary proceedings. Its role is central to maintaining judicial independence and the rule of law, which are critical factors in sovereign credit rating assessments and country risk analysis. The Council's independence and operational safeguards are closely monitored by international bodies such as the [[European Commission]] and the [[European Network of Judicial Councils]].

Recent assessments indicate that the Council's effectiveness and independence are undermined by insufficient safeguards for the dismissal of its members, particularly those not elected by judges. This lack of protection raises concerns about political influence over judicial appointments and disciplinary actions. Additionally, the Council's expanded role in disciplinary proceedings has introduced new risks to judicial independence, contributing to low public and business confidence in the impartiality of the judiciary.

## Key Activities & Reports

The Council's activities have been scrutinized in the context of the [[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Slovakia]], which highlights limited progress in strengthening the institution's independence. Key observations include:

- **Dismissal Safeguards:** No progress was made in establishing sufficient legal safeguards against the arbitrary dismissal of Judicial Council members. This remains a significant risk factor for institutional credibility.
- **Disciplinary Proceedings:** The Council's role in disciplinary cases has expanded, raising concerns about potential misuse to exert pressure on judges, thereby threatening judicial independence.
- **Perception of Independence:** The perceived independence of the judiciary remains low, with only 32% of the public and 34% of companies viewing the judiciary as independent. This perception is directly linked to governance issues within the Judicial Council.
- **Legislative Impact:** Amendments to judges' laws introduced new bonuses and evaluation mechanisms, which, while aiming for efficiency, have raised concerns about potential external influence on judicial conduct.

The Council operates under the broader framework of [[Rule of Law]] safeguards in Slovakia, where its interaction with the [[Prosecutor General’s Office (Slovakia)]] and the [[Slovak Government]] is critical for ensuring checks and balances.
