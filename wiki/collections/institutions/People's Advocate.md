# People's Advocate

## Mandate & Role

The **People's Advocate** (Ombudsman) serves as a key National Human Rights Institution in Romania, mandated to protect individual rights and promote human rights observance. Within the context of sovereign credit rating factors and country risk analysis, the institution plays a critical role in **accountability** and **checks and balances**. Its operational effectiveness and independence are considered part of the broader institutional strength of the Romanian state. However, its current status presents risks related to policy credibility, as the accreditation process with the Global Alliance of National Human Rights Institutions (GANHRI) is stalled due to concerns regarding its independence and resource allocation.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Accreditation Status**: The institution is currently involved in a stalled accreditation process with the **Global Alliance of National Human Rights Institutions** (GANHRI). The delay is attributed to questions raised regarding the institution's independence and sufficient resources, which impacts the overall assessment of Romania's human rights institutional framework.
*   **Rule of Law Monitoring**: As part of the broader human rights ecosystem in Romania, the People's Advocate is monitored in EU Rule of Law reports alongside the **Romanian Institute for Human Rights**. Concerns about the narrowing of civil society space and administrative barriers faced by CSOs indirectly affect the operational environment for the People's Advocate.
*   **Institutional Challenges**: Similar to the Romanian Institute for Human Rights, the People's Advocate faces systemic challenges related to resource adequacy, which is cited as a factor in the stalled GANHRI accreditation. These challenges contribute to the broader narrative of weakening **accountability** mechanisms and **policy credibility** in Romania.
