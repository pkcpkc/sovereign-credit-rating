# Slovak Television and Radio (STVR)

## Mandate & Role

**Slovak Television and Radio (STVR)** is the public service broadcaster of Slovakia. Established by the Act on Slovak Television and Radio which entered into force in July 2024, the institution plays a critical role in the country's media landscape and serves as a key indicator for assessing media pluralism and institutional independence.

In the context of sovereign credit rating and country risk analysis, STVR is monitored under **Media Freedom and Pluralism** and **Institutional Checks and Balances**. The governance structure of STVR is a significant risk factor, as concerns regarding its politicization directly impact the perception of democratic accountability and the rule of law. The European Commission’s 2025 Rule of Law Report highlights that no progress has been made in safeguarding the independence of public service media, with the new STVR governance structure raising specific concerns about political influence. This lack of independence contributes to the "low perceived judicial independence" and broader risks related to the "hostile environment for journalists," which negatively affects the **policy credibility and institutional strength** of Slovakia.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Legislative Framework**: The operational basis for STVR is the **Act on Slovak Television and Radio**, which entered into force in **July 2024**. This legislation defines the institution's governance and operational mandate.
- **Rule of Law Monitoring**: STVR is explicitly cited in the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Slovakia** as an entity where safeguards for independence are insufficient. The report notes that the governance structure poses risks of politicization, undermining media pluralism.
- **Media Environment Risks**: The institution operates in a context where media freedom is under pressure. This includes an increasingly hostile environment for journalists and no legislative progress on defamation reform. These factors are linked to **geopolitical and political stability threats**, specifically political polarization and attacks on media by politicians.
