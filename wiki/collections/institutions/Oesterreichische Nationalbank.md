# Oesterreichische Nationalbank

## Mandate & Role

The **Oesterreichische Nationalbank** (OeNB) serves as the central bank of Austria. As a member of the Eurosystem, it implements monetary policy in accordance with the European Central Bank (ECB) to ensure price stability across the Eurozone. The OeNB plays a critical role in maintaining the institutional strength and financial sector health of Austria, contributing to macroeconomic stability through its oversight of the banking sector and its contribution to the European Stability Mechanism and ECB liquidity frameworks. Its independence is safeguarded under EU law, ensuring that monetary policy remains anchored to price stability objectives without political interference. The IMF highlights the OeNB as a "credible central bank" with high credibility in financial stability oversight, operating within an independent ECB monetary policy framework.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Monetary Policy Implementation**: Executes ECB monetary policy measures, providing liquidity to the Austrian banking sector and maintaining the transmission of monetary policy within the national financial system. The OeNB tracks inflation trends, noting the decline from peak levels in 2022-2023 to a projected 2.9% in 2025, and supporting the convergence of inflation toward the ECB's 2% target by 2026.
- **Banking Supervision**: Collaborates with the **Financial Market Authority** and the ECB within the Single Supervisory Mechanism to monitor the health of the Austrian banking sector. The OeNB's oversight is vital in maintaining a well-capitalized banking sector (CET1 ratio of 17.5-17.7%) and a resilient insurance sector.
- **Macroprudential Policy & Financial Stability**: The OeNB implements prudent macroprudential measures, including the Systemic Risk Buffer (SyRB) and the Commercial Real Estate buffer (SSyRB), which became effective in July 2025. The IMF recommends that the OeNB and relevant authorities make borrower-based measures (KIM-V) for residential real estate permanent to prevent a resurgence of macro-financial risks following their expiration in July 2025. The OeNB monitors risks such as rising Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) in the Commercial Real Estate (CRE) sector and exposure to CESEE regional shocks.
- **Economic Surveillance**: Provides data and analysis that inform sovereign credit rating factors, particularly regarding financial sector health, external liquidity, and currency status. The OeNB's role supports the assessment of Austria's net creditor position and its net international investment position, which is projected to improve to 24.2% of GDP.
- **Price Stability Monitoring**: Tracks inflation trends, noting that headline inflation averaged 3.2% in 2025, exceeding the Euro Area average due to sticky services inflation. The OeNB monitors wage moderation and disinflationary pressures to support the convergence to the 2% target.
