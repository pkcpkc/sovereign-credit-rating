# FISK

## Mandate & Role

FISK (Fiscal Policy Monitoring and Analysis Institute) is an independent fiscal institution in Austria. It shares the tasks of Independent Fiscal Institution (IFI) monitoring with WIFO (Austrian Institute for Economic Research). Its primary role involves overseeing fiscal policy, ensuring transparency in government budgetary operations, and contributing to the institutional strength and credibility of Austria's fiscal framework.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Fiscal Monitoring**: FISK acts as a key reference point for fiscal sustainability and debt sustainability analysis in Austria, alongside WIFO.
- **Institutional Oversight**: It is cited in sovereign credit rating assessments (e.g., by the European Commission) as a component of Austria's policy credibility and institutional strength.
- **Task Allocation**: The institution operates within a split IFI task structure, which has been noted as a potential threat to policy clarity by some observers, as it may obscure clear reference points for fiscal advice compared to jurisdictions with a single unified IFI.
