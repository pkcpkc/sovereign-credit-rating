# Belgian Court of Audit

## Mandate & Role

The **Belgian Court of Audit** serves as a supreme audit institution responsible for overseeing the financial management and efficiency of public authorities in Belgium. In the context of sovereign credit risk, the Court plays a critical role in identifying structural weaknesses in public administration, particularly regarding the digitalisation of the justice sector. Its findings provide essential insights into fiscal risks, fraud vulnerabilities, and the effectiveness of government reforms, which are key factors in assessing Belgium's institutional strength and policy credibility.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Justice Digitalisation Assessment (December 2024):** The Court published a report highlighting serious shortcomings in the digitalisation strategy of the Justice Department. Key findings included:
    *   Lack of an overarching approach to digitalisation.
    *   Significant fraud risks associated with current digital systems.
    *   Poor management of digitalisation projects.
*   **Government Response:** In September 2024, the Belgian Government sent an initial action plan to the Court of Audit addressing these criticisms. The Court's oversight remains relevant as the Government intends to transfer budgetary management powers for justice administration from the executive to the judiciary, a move aimed at improving institutional stability but requiring robust monitoring.
