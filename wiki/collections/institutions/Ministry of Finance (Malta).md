# Ministry of Finance (Malta)

## Mandate & Role

The Ministry of Finance (Malta), led by Minister for Finance and Employment **Clyde Caruana**, is the primary government body responsible for formulating and implementing Malta’s fiscal policy, managing sovereign debt, and ensuring macroeconomic stability. The Ministry oversees the Malta Tax and Customs Administration (MTCA) and plays a central role in aligning national economic strategies with international frameworks, particularly those set by the European Union and the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF).

Key responsibilities include:
*   **Fiscal Consolidation**: Leading efforts to reduce the structural deficit, with a target of 2.6% of GDP by 2027, while managing the transition away from broad energy subsidies.
*   **Tax Reform**: Overseeing the implementation of Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms, including the deferred implementation of the EU’s Pillar II Minimum Tax Directive until 2029.
*   **Debt Sustainability**: Maintaining sovereign debt levels well below the EU’s 60% ceiling (currently ~50% of GDP) and ensuring low rollover risk through local currency debt.
*   **Strategic Planning**: Developing long-term economic visions, such as the "Malta Vision 2050" strategic plan, to shift growth drivers from labor-intensive sectors (tourism, gaming) toward productivity-driven and sustainable development.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **IMF Article IV Consultation (2024)**: The Ministry engaged in comprehensive discussions with the IMF Mission Chief **Kotaro Ishi** and European Department Director **Mark Horton**. The resulting Staff Report (completed December 2024) highlighted Malta’s strong external position and manageable debt burden but urged structural reforms in energy pricing and financial sector regulation.
*   **EU Fiscal Compliance**: The Ministry is committed to fiscal consolidation under the new EU framework, though it faces potential entry into the EU Excessive Deficit Procedure in 2025 due to current deficit levels.
*   **Energy Price Reform**: The Ministry is tasked with gradually phasing out fixed-energy price policies, which currently account for 20% of the fiscal deficit, to reallocate resources toward investment and green transition.
*   **AML/CFT Coordination**: Working alongside the [[Malta Financial Services Authority]] (MFSA) and the [[Central Bank of Malta]] (CBM) to strengthen the Anti-Money Laundering/Combating the Financing of Terrorism framework and address judicial efficiency.
