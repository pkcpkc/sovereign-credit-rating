# Constitutional Court

## Mandate & Role

The **Constitutional Court** serves as the highest constitutional jurisdiction in **Belgium**, responsible for ensuring that laws and decrees comply with the Constitution and the laws of the Community Councils. In the context of sovereign credit ratings and rule of law analysis, the Court acts as a critical institutional check and balance, safeguarding the rule of law by reviewing legislative compliance with constitutional principles.

According to the **2025 Rule of Law Report**, the Court plays a pivotal role in maintaining political and institutional stability by adjudicating disputes regarding government authority, public subsidies, and civic freedoms. Its independence is perceived as high by the general public (64%) and companies (72%), contributing positively to **policy credibility and institutional strength**. The Court's actions are seen as a counterweight to political fragmentation and potential executive overreach, particularly during periods of political transition, such as the caretaker government period between June 2024 and February 2025.

## Key Activities & Reports

The Constitutional Court has been actively involved in several significant rulings and legal challenges relevant to Belgium's institutional framework:

*   **Judicial Review of Subsidies**: On September 19, 2024, the Court annulled a provision of the Flemish Community decree concerning subsidies, highlighting its role in ensuring fiscal and administrative compliance within regional governments.
*   **Protection of Civic Space and Demonstrations**: The Court addressed tensions regarding the right to demonstrate and public order. Notably, on November 14, 2024, it ruled on urgent court orders and strikes, influencing the legal landscape for labor actions and protest rights. Additionally, on October 7, 2024, trade unions and civil society organizations lodged a constitutional complaint against a 'malicious attack on government authority,' reflecting the Court's role in protecting civil liberties against executive measures.
*   **Institutional Checks and Balances**: The Court operates within a broader judicial system that includes the **Court of Cassation** and the **Council of State**. While the Government intends to transfer budgetary management powers to the judiciary, the Constitutional Court remains a distinct entity focused on constitutional conformity. Its rulings contribute to the "accountability" pillar of the rule of law, although challenges remain in ensuring full compliance with final rulings across all public authorities.
