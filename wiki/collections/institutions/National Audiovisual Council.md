# National Audiovisual Council

## Mandate & Role

The **National Audiovisual Council** (CNA) is the regulatory authority responsible for overseeing the audiovisual media sector in Romania. Its mandate includes ensuring compliance with broadcasting regulations, monitoring media pluralism, and enforcing rules regarding political advertising and campaign financing within the audiovisual sphere. In the context of sovereign credit risk and rule of law, the CNA serves as a key indicator for media freedom and institutional accountability. The effectiveness of the CNA is viewed through the lens of its resource allocation, technological infrastructure, and ability to ensure transparent and fair political advertising, which are critical factors for geopolitical and political stability.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Presidential Election Campaign Oversight (2025)**: In February 2025, the CNA issued decisions regarding Presidential election campaign rules, aiming to regulate political advertising and ensure fair play during the electoral process. This activity is closely monitored as part of the broader assessment of electoral integrity and media pluralism in Romania.
- **Operational Challenges**: The CNA faces significant operational constraints, including a lack of sufficient human resources and an outdated IT system. These deficiencies impact its ability to effectively monitor compliance and enforce regulations, raising concerns about its institutional strength and independence.
- **Media Pluralism and Transparency**: The Council is tasked with addressing issues related to untransparent political advertising and the financing of private media by political parties. Despite its mandate, progress in enhancing the independent governance of public service media has been stalled, and the CNA struggles to fully mitigate threats to editorial independence arising from political pressure and opaque financing practices.
