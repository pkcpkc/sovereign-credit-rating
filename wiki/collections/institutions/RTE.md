# RTE

## Mandate & Role

RTE (Réseau de transport d'électricité) is the primary transmission system operator for electricity in France. It plays a critical role in maintaining national energy security and supporting the country's low-carbon electricity mix, which achieved 95% low-carbon production. As a key infrastructure entity, RTE is integral to the deployment of renewable energies and the overall stability of the French energy system, which is a core component of France's economic diversity and industrial base.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Grid Support for Energy Transition**: RTE is involved in the deployment of renewable energy infrastructure. The 2025 Country Report highlights that while France has a strong low-carbon electricity base, the deployment of new renewables is hindered by complex permitting procedures and administrative barriers, which RTE must navigate to facilitate grid connections.
- **Energy Security and Stability**: RTE contributes to the stability of the macroeconomic framework through reliable electricity supply, supporting France's diversified industrial base, including nuclear and aerospace sectors.
