# Sovereignty Protection Office (Hungary)

## Mandate & Role

The **Sovereignty Protection Office** is a Hungarian government body tasked with protecting national sovereignty and national interests. In the context of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the office is identified as a key instrument in the government's strategy to exert pressure on critical actors, including civil society organizations (CSOs) and media outlets. The office is mandated to investigate entities alleged to act on "foreign interests" that may endanger Hungary's sovereignty, functioning as a tool for regulatory scrutiny and legal uncertainty against independent voices.

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Investigations into Civil Society:** The office has launched specific investigations into anti-corruption civil society organizations and entities receiving European Union funds, contributing to a deteriorating civic space.
- **Media Sector Scrutiny:** Journalists and media outlets have been subjected to investigations by the Sovereignty Protection Office under the allegation of promoting "foreign influence." This activity is cited as part of increased pressure on the media landscape, alongside smear campaigns and selective denial of access to public events.
- **Legislative Context:** The activities of the office are exacerbated by proposed draft laws, such as the "Transparency in Public Life" law (proposed May 2025), which aims to restrict foreign funding for entities deemed to endanger sovereignty, sparking widespread protests from CSOs and media outlets.
