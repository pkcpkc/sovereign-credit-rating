# Moneyval

## Mandate & Role

Moneyval (the Committee of Experts on the Evaluation of Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Measures) is an international monitoring body tasked with evaluating compliance with international standards for anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CFT). In the context of sovereign credit ratings and country risk analysis, Moneyval's assessments serve as a critical indicator of **[[Institutional Strength]]** and **[[Rule of Law]]**. Effective AML/CFT frameworks are essential for maintaining the integrity of the financial system, mitigating corruption risks, and ensuring a stable **[[Business Environment]]**. Weaknesses in these areas can signal higher geopolitical and policy credibility risks, impacting investor confidence and the overall stability of the financial sector.

## Key Activities & Reports

Moneyval conducts peer evaluations of member states to assess the effectiveness and compliance of their AML/CFT regimes. In the context of **[[Slovakia]]**, Moneyval is recognized as a key oversight institution monitoring the country's adherence to global financial integrity standards.

Recent reports highlight significant challenges in Slovakia's ability to enforce these standards due to broader institutional backsliding. The dismantling of specialized anti-corruption law enforcement agencies, such as the National Crime Agency and the Special Prosecution Office, has disrupted the capacity to investigate and prosecute high-level financial crimes. This erosion of enforcement capacity directly impacts Moneyval's evaluation criteria regarding the effectiveness of preventive measures and investigative techniques. Consequently, deficiencies in AML/CFT implementation contribute to the high perceived level of corruption in the business environment and undermine **[[Policy Credibility and Institutional Strength]]**.
