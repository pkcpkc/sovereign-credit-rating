# Parliamentary Budget Office

## Mandate & Role

The **Parliamentary Budget Office** (PBO) is an independent fiscal institution in **Italy** with a broad mandate to assess budgetary forecasts and ensure transparency in public finance. It serves as a cornerstone of Italy's strong institutional framework for fiscal surveillance, enhancing the credibility of fiscal policy and supporting accountability. The PBO's independence and analytical rigor are recognized as significant strengths in assessing Italy's policy credibility and institutional strength, particularly in the context of adherence to EU fiscal rules and the implementation of the reformed Stability and Growth Pact framework.

## Key Activities & Reports

*   **Budgetary Forecast Assessment**: The PBO provides independent assessments of the government's budgetary forecasts, offering a critical check on official projections to ensure realistic debt and deficit estimates.
*   **Support for Fiscal Credibility**: By providing transparent and independent analysis, the PBO helps mitigate risks associated with political uncertainty and enhances the reliability of fiscal data used by international observers and credit rating agencies.
*   **Transparency in Procurement**: The institution contributes to enhanced transparency in public procurement through the digitalization of the contract lifecycle, supporting broader goals of administrative efficiency and anti-corruption measures.
