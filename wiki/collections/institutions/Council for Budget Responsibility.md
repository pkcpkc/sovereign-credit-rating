# Council for Budget Responsibility

## Mandate & Role

The **Council for Budget Responsibility** (CBR) is an independent advisory body in **Slovakia** that serves as a critical institutional anchor for fiscal credibility and policy stability. Its primary mandate is to provide non-binding, independent assessments of the government's fiscal position, medium-term fiscal plans, and the sustainability of public finances.

In the context of sovereign credit risk and institutional strength, the CBR is recognized as a key strength that mitigates the negative impacts of political instability and rule-of-law backsliding. While other institutional checks—such as the Civil Service Council and specialized anti-corruption agencies—have been weakened or dissolved, the CBR remains well-resourced and independent. Its existence provides a layer of transparency and technical rigor to Slovakia's fiscal governance, helping to maintain confidence in the country's adherence to EU fiscal frameworks, such as the **European Semester** and the **Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan** (MTP).

## Key Activities & Reports

- **Fiscal Anchor and Credibility**: The CBR is cited as a stabilizing force in **Slovakia**'s policy credibility framework. Despite broader institutional challenges, its independence supports the enforcement of EU legal frameworks regarding excessive deficit procedures.
- **Medium-Term Fiscal Oversight**: The institution contributes to the oversight of Slovakia's **Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan** (MTP), which was endorsed by the **Council of the European Union** in January 2025. The CBR's assessments help validate the feasibility of fiscal consolidation measures, such as the VAT and corporate tax hikes approved in late 2024.
- **Debt Sustainability Monitoring**: The CBR plays a role in monitoring long-term fiscal sustainability, particularly concerning risks posed by demographic aging and rising age-related expenditures (pensions and healthcare), which are projected to increase from 20% to 25% of GDP by 2070.
