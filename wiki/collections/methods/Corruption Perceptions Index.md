# Corruption Perceptions Index

## Overview

The **Corruption Perceptions Index** (CPI) is a globally recognized methodology published annually by **Transparency International** that measures the perceived levels of public sector corruption in countries and territories around the world. Unlike methods measuring actual corruption incidents, the CPI aggregates data from various expert assessments and opinion polls to provide a composite score reflecting how corrupt a country's public sector is perceived to be by business people, academics, and risk analysts. The index serves as a critical benchmark for **Sovereign Credit Ratings** and investment decisions, influencing capital flows and policy reforms.

## Key Pillars & Analytical Components

The methodology relies on the aggregation of data from multiple independent sources, ensuring a robust and multi-faceted view of corruption risks. The core pillars include:

1.  **Perception Aggregation**: Combines scores from standardized surveys and expert assessments conducted by recognized institutions (e.g., World Bank, World Economic Forum, **World Justice Project**).
2.  **Public Sector Focus**: Specifically targets the misuse of public power for private gain, focusing on bribery, misappropriation of public funds, and capture of the state by private interests.
3.  **Judicial and Institutional Integrity**: Evaluates the effectiveness of anti-corruption frameworks, including the independence of the judiciary, the functionality of oversight bodies (such as **National Anti-Corruption Authorities**), and the transparency of public procurement processes.
4.  **Rule of Law Index Correlation**: Often cross-referenced with broader rule of law metrics, including judicial independence, absence of corruption, and order and security.

## Key Metrics & Variables

- **[Public Sector Bribery]**: Measures the frequency and extent of petty and grand bribery in public services, including interactions with law enforcement and public administration.
- **[Judicial Independence and Impartiality]**: Assesses the perception of whether courts are free from political interference, corruption, and undue influence, which is crucial for enforcing anti-corruption laws.
- **[Transparency of Political Financing]**: Evaluates the clarity and regulation of funding sources for political parties and candidates, identifying risks of illicit financial flows and undue influence.
- **[Public Procurement Integrity]**: Assesses the transparency and fairness of public tendering processes, a high-risk area for organized crime infiltration and corruption.
- **[Effectiveness of Anti-Corruption Agencies]**: Measures the operational capacity, independence, and resource adequacy of bodies tasked with preventing and investigating corruption (e.g., **ANAC** in Italy).
- **[Media Freedom and Pluralism]**: Considers the ability of the media to investigate and report on corruption without fear of reprisal or state interference, acting as a watchdog for accountability.

## Application

1.  **Data Collection and Normalization**: Gather scores from multiple accredited data providers for the specific country and year. Normalize these scores to a common scale (typically 0–100, where 0 is highly corrupt and 100 is very clean).
2.  **Aggregation and Composite Scoring**: Calculate the mean of the normalized scores from all included sources. Apply statistical adjustments to account for any known biases or methodological differences between sources.
3.  **Contextual Analysis and Qualitative Overlay**: Integrate qualitative insights from expert reports (such as the **EU Rule of Law Report**) to contextualize the numerical score. This includes analyzing specific institutional reforms, legislative changes (e.g., **Judicial Reform**), and emerging threats (e.g., **Security Law** impacts on civic space) that may not be fully captured in quantitative surveys.
4.  **Rating Committee Review and Publication**: The final scores are reviewed by Transparency International’s technical team and published annually, providing rankings that allow for cross-country comparison and trend analysis over time.
