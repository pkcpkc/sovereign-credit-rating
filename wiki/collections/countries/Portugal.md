## Economic Overview

Portugal is situated within a broader EU economic landscape characterized by a period of slowing growth and fiscal pressure. Based on the 2025 European Commission and IMF contexts, the national trajectory is heavily influenced by regional trends of fiscal consolidation and the management of public debt-to-GDP ratios. Portugal's economic narrative emphasizes the need to address "skills gaps," improve business R&D intensity, and navigate high capital costs that have subdued investment. The country is currently navigating a transition toward a green and digital economy while attempting to balance labor market productivity with fiscal sustainability. Within the broader Eurozone context, there is a heightened focus on structural reforms to improve Total Factor Productivity (TFP) and address the pressures of an aging population.

## Governance & Rule of Law

Portugal operates within the framework of the European Union, adhering to common standards of fiscal sustainability and administrative simplification. Current policy trends include a move toward reducing bureaucracy (the "tell us once" principle) to enhance the ease of doing business and streamline economic life for domestic and international firms. The governance framework is increasingly aligned with the EU Economic Governance Framework (EGF) to ensure long-term fiscal stability.

## Key Risks & Outlook

Key risks for Portugal include broader regional challenges such as high public debt, rising poverty rates (particularly child poverty), and the difficulty of transitioning transport and building sectors to low-carbon models due to administrative complexity. The outlook remains tied to the success of fiscal consolidation strategies and the ability to attract venture capital and equity financing for high-growth firms to bridge the innovation gap. Additionally, the country faces regional pressures regarding labor-cost competitiveness and the need for structural reforms in education and product market dynamics to maintain international standing.
