## Economic Overview

Slovakia is included in the European Commission's 2025 Country Report as part of the broader EU economic landscape. While specific granular data for Slovakia in this summary is grouped within the regional economic context of the European Union, the report highlights a period of cautious growth and structural transition. The broader regional economy faces challenges including high capital costs, investment uncertainty, and the need for fiscal consolidation. Slovakia's economic trajectory is influenced by the regional trends of labor productivity recovery, the "skills gap" in green and digital transitions, and the necessity of administrative simplification to bolster business competitiveness.

## Governance & Rule of Law

The country operates within the framework of European Commission fiscal and social policies. Current priorities include the implementation of RRP (Recovery and Resilience Plan) reforms and the adoption of "simplification of economic life" principles to reduce bureaucracy. The governance focus is on aligning national policies with EU-wide targets for decarbonization and digital transformation.

## Key Risks & Outlook

Key risks include the broader regional trend of rising public debt-to-GDP ratios and the persistent challenge of the "skills gap" which hinders industrial modernization. Slovakia faces the dual challenge of maintaining economic competitiveness while meeting aggressive greenhouse gas reduction targets by 2030. The outlook is tied to the success of fiscal consolidation measures and the ability to attract investment despite high interest rates.
