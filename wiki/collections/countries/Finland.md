## Economic Overview

While the primary focus of the provided report is on France, Finland is identified as a key member state within the European Commission's 2025 Country Report context. The economic framework for the region highlights a period of moderate growth tempered by high capital costs and restrictive monetary policies. Finland remains part of the broader EU economic landscape characterized by a push for fiscal consolidation, administrative simplification (the "tell us once" principle), and a significant focus on the twin green and digital transitions.

## Governance & Rule of Law

Finland continues to operate within the European Commission's regulatory framework, which emphasizes structural reforms, judicial independence, and the reduction of bureaucratic hurdles to improve the "simplification of economic life."

## Key Risks & Outlook

Key regional risks include high public debt-to-GDP ratios across the EU, a persistent "skills gap" hindering productivity, and the need for aggressive fiscal consolidation. For Finland, the outlook is tied to the broader European goals of reaching 50% reductions in greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 and enhancing R&D intensity to meet competitiveness targets.
