## Key Risks & Outlook

- **Structural Hurdles:** Like many EU peers, Estonia faces challenges regarding the pace of the green transition and the need for increased R&D intensity.

- **Geopolitical Context:** As a member of the EU, Estonia's economic outlook is tied to the broader stability of the European fiscal landscape and the success of RRP (Recovery and Resilience Plan) reforms.
