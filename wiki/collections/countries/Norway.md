## Economic Overview

Norway is identified as a key partner in the European energy landscape, specifically regarding energy security. It plays a critical role in diversifying gas supplies for neighboring countries like Austria, Germany, and Italy following the termination of Russian imports. While the provided context focuses heavily on Austrian domestic policy, Norway's role as a primary energy supplier underscores its position as a major exporter of natural gas and a stable economic actor in the North Sea region.

## Governance & Rule of Law

## Key Risks & Outlook

The primary outlook involves the continued transition of European energy markets. Norway's role in securing gas supplies is a cornerstone of European energy security, though long-term dynamics will be shaped by the broader trend toward decarbonization and the shift to renewable energy sources across the continent.
