# Standing Committee of the Council of Ministers adopts draft law on prosecution reform

## Event Details

On December 6, 2024, the Standing Committee of the Council of Ministers adopted a draft law aimed at significant prosecution reform in Poland. This legislative move is part of a broader effort to improve the rule of law and judicial independence in the country. 

The reform is contextualized within a series of actions to separate the office of the Minister of Justice from the Prosecutor General, a move intended to reduce political influence over the prosecution service. This follows the tabling of draft laws in July 2024 and aligns with the European Commission's ongoing monitoring of Poland's judicial and anti-corruption frameworks. The adoption of this law serves as a key milestone in addressing irregularities identified during audits of cases spanning 2016–2023.
