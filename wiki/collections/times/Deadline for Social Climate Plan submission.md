# Deadline for Social Climate Plan submission

## Event Details

On 2025-06-30, the deadline for the submission of the Social Climate Plan is established. This milestone is part of a broader fiscal and structural framework for Slovakia, which includes the Council adoption of fiscal and structural plans on 2025-01-21. 

The submission aligns with the broader objectives outlined in the 2025 Country Report, which emphasizes the need for a high-value, innovation-driven economy and a transition toward green technologies. Key components of the underlying policy context include managing high government deficits (projected at 4.9% of GDP in 2025), addressing R&D underperformance (currently at 1%), and accelerating decarbonization in the manufacturing sector, which contributes 35% of Slovakia's GHG emissions.
