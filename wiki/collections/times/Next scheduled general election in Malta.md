# Next scheduled general election in Malta

## Event Details

The next scheduled general election in Malta is expected to take place in 2027. This timeline aligns with the fiscal goals outlined in the IMF's Article IV Consultation, which notes Malta's commitment to reducing the fiscal deficit to 2.6% of GDP by 2027 under the EU’s new fiscal framework. The political landscape leading up to this election will likely be influenced by the government's "Vision 2050" initiative and ongoing structural reforms regarding energy subsidies, corporate tax alignment with EU Pillar II, and AML/CFT governance.
