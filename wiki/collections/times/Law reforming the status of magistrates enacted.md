# Law reforming the status of magistrates enacted

## Event Details

On 2023-11-20, France enacted a significant law reforming the status of magistrates. This legislation, highlighted in the **2025 Rule of Law Report** by the **European Commission**, aimed to enhance judicial independence and diversity within the French justice system.

Despite the enactment of these reforms, the 2025 report notes that perceived judicial independence remains only average. Magistrates continue to face significant challenges, including severe personal attacks, threats, and political pressure, particularly regarding rulings on national elections. The **Superior Council of the Magistracy (CSM)** has actively defended judicial autonomy in response to these pressures, reiterating concerns about attacks against magistrates as recently as 2025-03-31. The reform is part of broader ongoing efforts to modernize the French judiciary, alongside initiatives such as the digitalization of judicial procedures (e.g., the Portalis tool) and measures to improve case resolution efficiency.
