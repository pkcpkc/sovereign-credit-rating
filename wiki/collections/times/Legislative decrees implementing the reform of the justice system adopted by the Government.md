# Legislative decrees implementing the reform of the justice system adopted by the Government

## Event Details

In March 2024, the Italian Government adopted legislative decrees implementing a comprehensive reform of the justice system. This legislative action serves as the foundational framework for ongoing judicial modernization efforts in Italy, overseen by the [[High Council for the Judiciary (Italy)]].

Following the adoption of these decrees, the [[High Council for the Judiciary (Italy)]] has been tasked with implementing specific duties under the new legislation. Key developments resulting from this reform include the mandatory implementation of the digital case management system (*APP*) in criminal courts and prosecutors' offices, which came into force on January 1, 2025. While the reform aims to enhance judicial efficiency and address persistent staff shortages, the implementation has encountered technical challenges, leading to temporary derogations for some courts. The reforms are part of broader efforts to reduce case backlogs and improve the overall efficiency of the Italian judicial system, although the length of proceedings remains a significant concern noted in the [[European Commission]]'s 2025 Rule of Law Report.
