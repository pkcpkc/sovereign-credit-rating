# Number of pending ECtHR leading judgments rises to 33

## Event Details

As of January 2025, Slovakia had a record of 31 leading judgments from the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR) pending implementation. By June 2025, this number increased to 33, signaling a deterioration in the enforcement of international judicial decisions. This rise occurred despite an intensive dialogue between the Slovak Government and the European Commission regarding rule of law concerns.

The accumulation of pending judgments is highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report on Slovakia as a key indicator of weak enforcement mechanisms for international obligations. The report notes that this trend persists alongside other systemic issues, including low perceived judicial independence and fragmented anti-corruption frameworks. The failure to promptly implement ECtHR judgments contributes to broader concerns regarding the accountability and effectiveness of Slovakia's justice system.
