# Pending ECHR leading judgments decrease to 22

## Event Details

As of June 2025, the number of pending leading judgments from the European Court of Human Rights (ECHR) in France decreased to 22, down from a peak of 26 recorded earlier in the year. This reduction marks progress in the implementation of ECHR judgments within the French legal system, according to the 2025 Rule of Law Report published by the European Commission.

While the total count of leading pending cases has declined, historical backlogs remain a concern. Specific cases, such as those involving land expulsion measures, have remained unresolved for over a decade. The improvement comes amidst broader efforts to enhance judicial efficiency and compliance with international human rights standards, although challenges regarding case resolution times and digitalization implementation persist within the French judiciary.
