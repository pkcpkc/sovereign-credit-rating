# Implementation of new company car tax scheme

## Event Details

On January 1, 2024, Belgium implemented a new company car tax scheme as part of ongoing fiscal policy adjustments. This initiative is situated within a broader economic context where the country faces significant regulatory complexity and a high tax-and-benefit system that often creates disincentives to work.

The scheme reflects efforts to balance the fiscal-structural plan against high labor costs and a complex regulatory environment. While Belgium remains a strong innovator, these types of tax reforms are critical to addressing the "zombie firms" and high barriers to entry that currently hinder business dynamism. The reform is part of a wider suite of policies aimed at managing the country's headline deficit and public debt, which are pressured by aging-related costs and the need for a green/digital economy transition.
