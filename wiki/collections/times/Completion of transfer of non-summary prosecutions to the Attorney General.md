# Completion of transfer of non-summary prosecutions to the Attorney General

## Event Details

In September 2024, Malta completed the structural transfer of non-summary case prosecutions from the Police to the Office of the Attorney General. This move was part of a broader effort to address judicial efficiency and enhance the prosecution framework within the Maltese justice system. 

While this transition represents a key achievement in prosecutorial reform, it occurs alongside ongoing challenges regarding the judge-to-inhabitant ratio and high disposition times for civil, criminal, and administrative cases. The reform is situated within a broader context of judicial oversight and anti-corruption initiatives aimed at improving the rule of law in Malta.
