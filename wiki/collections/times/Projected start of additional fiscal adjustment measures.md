# Projected start of additional fiscal adjustment measures

## Event Details

As part of Romania's long-term fiscal consolidation strategy, additional fiscal adjustment measures are projected to begin on **2027-01-01**. 

These measures are designed to address persistent high fiscal deficits and mitigate the risk of sovereign stress. While a reform package adopted in mid-2025 (including VAT hikes and excise duties) serves as an initial step, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) emphasizes that these additional adjustments starting in 2027 are necessary to stabilize public debt at approximately 60% of GDP by 2030. This timeline is critical for ensuring Romania's medium-term macroeconomic sustainability and managing the "twin deficits" (fiscal and current account) that have pressured the economy.
