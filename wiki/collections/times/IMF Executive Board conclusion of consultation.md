# IMF Executive Board conclusion of consultation

## Event Details

On March 18, 2025, the IMF Executive Board concluded its consultation regarding Belgium's 2025 Article IV review. This followed a consultation period held between January 22 and February 4, 2025, during which discussions were held with Belgian officials to assess the country's economic resilience and structural headwinds.

The conclusion of the consultation resulted in a report highlighting several key areas for Belgium:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** The IMF supported a seven-year adjustment period under the new EU economic governance framework (EGF), aiming for an annual reduction in the structural primary balance of approximately 0.6 percentage points of GDP until 2031 to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP.
- **Labor and Productivity:** The board noted the need for reforms to address declining labor-cost competitiveness caused by automatic wage indexation and suggested reviewing the 1996 wage law to improve market efficiency.
- **Financial Stability:** Systemic risks were assessed as "Moderate," with recommendations to maintain capital buffer requirements and prudential limits on mortgage loans.
- **Sovereign Risk:** While debt levels remained high (reaching 104.1% of GDP in 2024), the risk of sovereign stress was deemed "Moderate" due to a high-quality debt management profile.

The results of this consultation were subsequently released via a press release on March 20, 2025.
