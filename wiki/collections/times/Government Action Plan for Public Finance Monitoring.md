# Government Action Plan for Public Finance Monitoring

## Event Details

In March 2025, the Government of France implemented a comprehensive **Government Action Plan for Public Finance Monitoring** [[Barney]] [[Barnier]]. This initiative was introduced in the context of a deteriorating fiscal position, where public debt had reached 113.1% of GDP and the deficit stood at 5.8%. The plan represented a critical component of the administration's **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan** [[MTFSP]], which aimed to reduce the budget deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2029.

The action plan focused on enforcing fiscal discipline through enhanced monitoring mechanisms to prevent revenue shortfalls and spending slippages. It aligned with the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) recommendations for a credible package of structural measures, including a frontloaded effort of 1.1% of GDP in 2026 followed by 0.9% annually. Key elements included spending reviews across central, local, and social security levels, as well as efforts to simplify regulations and support reindustrialization through **Artificial Intelligence** [[AI]] and green technologies. The plan was closely monitored by IMF Executive Board members, including [[Arnaud Buisse]], to ensure compliance with EU fiscal rules and maintain the country's sovereign credit rating stability.
