# Council initiated excessive deficit procedure for Poland

## Event Details

In January 2023, the Council initiated an excessive deficit procedure for Poland following a fiscal year where the country recorded a deficit of 6.6% of GDP. This move was prompted by the need for significant fiscal consolidation to align with the Stability and Growth Pact.

As a result of this procedure, Poland is currently navigating a binding expenditure path designed to reduce the national deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028. While the country continues to experience economic growth—with a 2.9% GDP increase in 2024—the fiscal adjustment remains a primary strategic priority to ensure long-term economic stability and sustainability.
