# Submission of 5th payment request under RRP

## Event Details

In December 2024, the Slovak Republic submitted its 5th payment request under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRP). This submission is part of the broader economic framework involving the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation, which highlights Slovakia's ongoing fiscal consolidation efforts. The request aligns with the government's commitment to a 4-year fiscal plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit and stabilizing public debt while navigating structural challenges such as an aging population and the need for productivity growth.
