# Peak inflation of 17.0% recorded in Hungary

## Event Details

In January 2023, Hungary recorded a peak inflation rate of 17.0%. This period of high inflation was characterized by significant underlying price pressures and rising food costs, contributing to a challenging economic environment. 

The subsequent economic landscape for Hungary has been marked by a slow recovery, with GDP growth of 0.5% in 2024 and projected growth of 0.8% in 2025. Despite the peak inflation period, the country continues to face structural vulnerabilities including high public debt (reaching 73.5% of GDP in 2024) and high debt-servicing costs, with implicit interest rates on government debt nearing 6% in 2025.
