# Conclusion of discussions with Hungarian officials

## Event Details

On June 17, 2025, the IMF staff mission in Budapest concluded its formal discussions with Hungarian officials. This phase of the Article IV Consultation involved high-level engagements with key stakeholders, including the Ministry for National Economy and the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB).

The discussions focused on addressing Hungary's "challenging juncture," specifically regarding stagnant output and inflation remaining above the 3% target. Key points of dialogue included:

- **Fiscal Adjustment:** The necessity of achieving a structural primary surplus of approximately 1.75% of GDP to ensure debt sustainability amidst elevated public debt projections.
- **Monetary Policy:** The requirement for the MNB to maintain a restrictive interest rate stance into 2026 to anchor inflation.
- **Structural Reform:** Strategies to eliminate barriers to factor mobility, reduce state aid distortions, and transition away from fossil fuel subsidies.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Addressing vulnerabilities in corporate FX exposures, sovereign-bank linkages, and commercial real estate overvaluation.

This conclusion marked a critical milestone before the finalization of the IMF Staff Report, which later detailed the need for high-quality fiscal adjustments and governance reforms to unlock suspended EU funding.
