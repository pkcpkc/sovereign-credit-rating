# Court of Audit reports shortcomings in digitalisation strategy

## Event Details

In December 2024, the Court of Audit issued a report highlighting significant shortcomings in Belgium's digitalisation strategy for the justice system. The report criticized the lack of an overarching, cohesive approach to digital transformation and pointed out an excessive reliance on external consultants rather than building internal structural capabilities.

In response to these criticisms, the Belgian Government has initiated actions to improve the digital infrastructure of the judiciary. This includes addressing resource deficiencies and moving toward a more structured framework for judicial digitalization to improve efficiency and reduce backlogs in the court system.
