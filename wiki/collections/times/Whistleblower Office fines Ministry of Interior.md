# Whistleblower Office fines Ministry of Interior

## Event Details

On **2024-10-23**, the Office for the Protection of Whistleblowers imposed a fine on the **Ministry of Interior** for procedural violations. This enforcement action was highlighted in the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Slovakia** as a notable strength in the area of accountability, amidst broader concerns regarding the erosion of institutional checks and balances.

While the Slovak government faced criticism for the dissolution of specialized anti-corruption entities (such as the National Crime Agency and the Special Prosecution Office) and the broad powers of the **Prosecutor General**, the Whistleblower Office's ability to penalize a key ministry demonstrated remaining functional oversight mechanisms. The fine served as a counter-narrative to the general trend of reduced transparency and weakened independence observed in other sectors, including the judiciary and media landscape.
