# Introduction of 15% margin cap for personal hygiene products

## Event Details

In May 2025, the Hungarian government introduced a **15% margin cap** on personal hygiene products. This measure was implemented as part of a broader strategy of administrative price controls and state intervention aimed at managing inflation and protecting consumers amid economic volatility.

The introduction of this cap followed the implementation of a **10% retailer profit margin cap for food products** in March 2025. These interventions are characterized by the European Commission as distorting the business environment and limiting competition. The Commission's 2025 Country Report on Hungary notes that such administrative controls, alongside subsidized lending and sector-specific taxes, hinder investment and market efficiency. Despite the intention to curb price rises, underlying inflationary pressures persisted, with inflation picking up in Q1 2025 due to rising food prices and other factors, although it had previously declined significantly from a peak of 17.0% in 2023 to 3.7% in 2024.
