# Adoption of Government Emergency Ordinance filling the legal void on statute of limitations

## Event Details

On **2022-05-30**, the Romanian government adopted a **Government Emergency Ordinance (GEO)** intended to address a significant legal void regarding the statute of limitations in criminal law. This legislative measure was a direct response to the [[2018-06-25 Constitutional Court ruling on the statute of limitations (Article 155(1))]], which had annulled previous provisions, creating a period of legal uncertainty and a vacuum in the application of limitation periods for ongoing criminal investigations and prosecutions.

The adoption of this GEO occurred within a broader context of legislative volatility in Romania, characterized by the frequent use of [[Government Emergency Ordinances]] to enact substantive changes, particularly in the justice and fiscal sectors. While the specific GEO of 2022 aimed to restore legal certainty by filling the immediate void, the broader phenomenon of using GEOs for such critical legal adjustments has been cited by the [[European Commission]] in the **2025 Rule of Law Report** as a factor contributing to legislative unpredictability and concerns over policy credibility.

Subsequent judicial interpretations have complicated the landscape established by this period. Notably, the [[2024-06-17 High Court of Cassation and Justice decision 37/2024 on lex mitior]] and the [[2024-09-16 High Court of Cassation and Justice decision 16/2024 on lex mitior]] have applied the principle of *lex mitior* (the most lenient law) to cases spanning this legal void. These rulings have led to the discontinuation of numerous corruption cases and the annulment of convictions, raising systemic concerns about impunity and the effectiveness of anti-corruption efforts. The [[High Court of Cassation and Justice]] has subsequently sought clarification from the [[Court of Justice of the European Union]] (Case C-280/25 Lin II) on these matters, underscoring the lasting impact of the legislative transitions initiated during this period.
