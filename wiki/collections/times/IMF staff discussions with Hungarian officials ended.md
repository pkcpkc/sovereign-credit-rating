# IMF staff discussions with Hungarian officials ended

## Event Details

On **2025-06-17**, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) concluded its staff-level discussions with Hungarian authorities regarding the **2025 Article IV Consultation**. The mission, led by [[Anke Weber]] (Head of Mission), engaged with key Hungarian officials, including [[Márton Nagy]] (Minister for National Economy), to assess the country's macroeconomic performance, fiscal sustainability, and monetary policy framework.

### Context and Economic Assessment
The discussions took place against the backdrop of stagnant real GDP growth over the previous three years and persistently high inflation (4.4–4.6% in mid-2025), which remained well above the [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank]] (MNB) target of 3%. The IMF staff noted that while Hungary recorded a current account surplus of 2.2% of GDP in 2024, the near-term outlook was subdued due to high domestic and external uncertainty, including deepening geoeconomic fragmentation and potential trade escalation.

### Key Policy Recommendations
The IMF staff emphasized the need for additional fiscal effort to ensure debt sustainability, noting that public debt had reached 73.5% of GDP in 2024 and was projected to rise to ~79% by 2030 under current policies. Key recommendations discussed included:
*   **Fiscal Adjustment:** Implementing a structural primary surplus of around 1.75% of GDP in the medium term, achieved through high-quality revenue enhancements and spending rationalization (e.g., phasing out energy subsidies).
*   **Monetary Policy:** Supporting the MNB’s tight monetary stance (base rate at 6.5%) to durably return inflation to target by 2027.
*   **Structural Reforms:** Phasing out distortive regulatory measures such as price, interest, and margin caps, which hinder market signals and monetary policy transmission.
*   **EU Funds:** Urging the completion of rule of law and governance reforms to unlock approximately €19 billion in suspended EU funds, identifying this as a critical downside risk to fiscal and external balances.

### Subsequent Developments
Following these staff discussions, the **IMF Staff Report** was completed on **2025-07-25**. The process culminated on **2025-08-29**, when the IMF Executive Board concluded the 2025 Article IV Consultation, endorsing the staff’s assessment and urging the Hungarian authorities to implement the recommended fiscal and structural adjustments.
