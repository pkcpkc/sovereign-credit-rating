# Number of leading ECtHR judgments pending implementation increases to 18

## Event Details

As of June 16, 2025, Belgium continues to face formal supervision by the Council of Europe due to structural issues regarding the implementation of judgments from the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR). By this date, the number of leading judgments remaining pending implementation had increased to 18. This lack of compliance remains a significant point of concern regarding the country's human rights obligations and its adherence to international legal standards.
