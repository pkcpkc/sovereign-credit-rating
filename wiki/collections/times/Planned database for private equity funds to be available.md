# Planned database for private equity funds to be available

## Event Details

As part of the ongoing regulatory and fiscal adjustments in Hungary, a planned database for private equity funds is scheduled to be available on 2026-07-01. This initiative follows a period of legislative activity, including an amendment to legislation on private equity funds in November 2024. The move is situated within a broader economic context of addressing market distortions and financing gaps, where the country has seen a significant drop in venture capital investment and an underdeveloped stock market compared to the EU average.
