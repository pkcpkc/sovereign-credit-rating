# High Council for the Judiciary approves directive on magistrates' performance appraisal

## Event Details

On November 13, 2024, the High Council for the Judiciary approved a significant directive concerning the performance appraisal of magistrates. This action is part of a broader series of legislative and administrative reforms in Italy aimed at improving the efficiency and transparency of the justice system.

The directive aligns with ongoing efforts to modernize judicial management, following other key milestones such as the approval of the Code of Judicial Management and directives on out-of-tenure appointments. These reforms are intended to balance the need for judicial independence with the requirement for accountability and professional development within the judiciary.
