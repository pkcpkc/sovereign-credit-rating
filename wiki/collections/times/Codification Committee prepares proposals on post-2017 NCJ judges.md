# Codification Committee prepares proposals on post-2017 NCJ judges

## Event Details

In February 2025, the Polish Codification Committee prepared legislative proposals to address the status of judges appointed by the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ) in its composition established after 2017. This initiative was part of the Polish Government's efforts to implement the Action Plan on the Rule of Law and address long-standing concerns regarding judicial independence within the European Union.

The proposed legislation aimed to classify approximately 1,600 judges who were appointed at the request of the post-2017 NCJ, potentially involving their removal or other regulatory measures. The preparation of these proposals involved consultation with the [[Venice Commission]] to ensure alignment with European standards. Subsequently, in April 2025, the [[Ministry of Justice]] formally proposed a draft law detailing the status of these 1,600 judges, advancing the legislative process initiated by the Codification Committee. These developments are closely linked to broader reforms, including plans to dismantle the Supreme Court Chamber of Extraordinary Control and Public Affairs, and remain pending in the context of ongoing tensions between domestic constitutional authorities and [[European Commission]] rule of law requirements.
