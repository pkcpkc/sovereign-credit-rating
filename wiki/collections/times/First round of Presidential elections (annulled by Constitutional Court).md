# First round of Presidential elections (annulled by Constitutional Court)

## Event Details

In November 2024, Romania held the first round of Presidential elections. However, the results were subsequently annulled by the Constitutional Court. This event is part of a broader period of judicial and political scrutiny in Romania, characterized by significant legal developments including the repeated Presidential elections in May 2025 and ongoing challenges regarding judicial independence and anti-corruption frameworks.
