# Introduction of Increased Court Fees (Approx. 23%)

## Event Details

On 1 April 2025, Austria implemented a significant increase in court fees, with an average rise of approximately 23%. This fiscal adjustment was cited by the European Commission as a critical barrier to Access to Justice, exacerbating concerns regarding the affordability of legal proceedings for citizens and businesses. The increase occurred within the broader context of ongoing Judicial Reforms and the "Justice 3.0" digitalization initiative.

The impact of these increased fees was highlighted by subsequent legal challenges. In October 2024, the Constitutional Court ruled that restrictions on legal aid in administrative proceedings were unconstitutional, a decision with suspensions taking effect by March 2026. The timing of the fee increase in April 2025 raised concerns that it could undermine the effectiveness of this judicial protection mechanism, potentially limiting access to justice for lower-income individuals. The European Commission noted high court fees in certain contexts as a threat to fiscal performance and flexibility, recommending targeted actions to ensure that cost barriers do not compromise the rule of law.
