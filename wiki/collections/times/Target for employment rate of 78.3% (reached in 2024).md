# Target for employment rate of 78.3% (reached in 2024)

## Event Details

In 2024, Poland achieved a significant milestone in its labor market by reaching the target employment rate of 78.3%. This achievement coincided with a period of historic low unemployment, where the rate dropped to 2.9% in 2024. 

Despite reaching this target, the Polish labor market faces ongoing structural challenges. The report highlights a shrinking working-age population and a significant gender gap in labor participation, often attributed to insufficient childcare and long-term care services. Additionally, while the employment rate target was met, there remains a critical skills shortage, with 82% of SMEs reporting it as a primary barrier to growth.
