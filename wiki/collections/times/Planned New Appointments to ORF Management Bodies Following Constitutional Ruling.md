# Planned New Appointments to ORF Management Bodies Following Constitutional Ruling

## Event Details

In June 2025, Austria planned new appointments to the management bodies of **Österreichischer Rundfunk** (ORF), the country's public service broadcaster. These appointments were scheduled to take place following a significant **Constitutional Court** ruling that deemed previous restrictions on ORF's management structure unconstitutional. The move is part of broader structural reforms aimed at increasing the independence of the broadcaster's management from political influence.

This development aligns with commitments made in the New Government Programme presented in March 2025, which prioritized judicial and media sector reforms. The reforms seek to address concerns regarding the political control of state media, ensuring that ORF operates with greater autonomy in line with European standards for media pluralism and independence. The Constitutional Court's intervention highlights ongoing tensions between executive oversight and the need for institutional autonomy in key state entities.
