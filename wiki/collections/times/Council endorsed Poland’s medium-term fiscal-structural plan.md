# Council endorsed Poland’s medium-term fiscal-structural plan

## Event Details

In early 2023, the Council initiated an excessive deficit procedure for Poland due to a high deficit reaching 6.6% of GDP. To address these fiscal imbalances, a binding expenditure path was established with the objective of reducing the deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028.

A significant milestone in this fiscal adjustment occurred in January 2025, when the Council officially endorsed Poland’s medium-term fiscal-structural plan. This plan serves as a framework for stabilizing public finances while managing high levels of public spending, which saw a 12.7% increase in 2024 driven by defense and public consumption.

Following this endorsement, Poland continued to monitor its progress, submitting an annual progress report on the fiscal plan on April 30, 2025. The ongoing strategy aims to balance these fiscal constraints with the need for public investment in infrastructure, healthcare reform, and the transition toward a circular economy and climate neutrality by 2050.
