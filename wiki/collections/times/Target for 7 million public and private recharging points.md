# Target for 7 million public and private recharging points

## Event Details

As part of the broader European energy transition and decarbonization goals outlined in the [[2025 Country Report - France]], a significant infrastructure milestone has been established. 

By **January 1, 2030**, the target is to have established **7 million public and private recharging points**. This initiative is a critical component of the strategy to decarbonize the transport sector, which currently faces hurdles due to administrative complexity and infrastructure gaps. This target aligns with France's broader commitment to reaching a 50% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions by the same date and supports the transition toward a low-carbon economy.
