# Mission began in Vienna

## Event Details

On March 26, 2025, an [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) mission arrived in Vienna to conduct the 2025 [[Article IV Consultation]] with Austria. The mission was led by **Mr. Fletcher** (Mission Head) and included IMF staff members **Mr. Mohommad**, **Ms. Pinat**, and **Mr. Saiyid**. Supervisory guidance was provided by **Oya Celasun**, Deputy Director of the IMF European Department, and **Bergljot Barkbu**, Director of the IMF Strategy, Policy, and Review Department.

The mission aimed to assess the economic outlook, fiscal policies, and structural reforms of Austria amidst a challenging environment characterized by two consecutive years of recession (2023 and 2024). Key discussions involved high-level Austrian officials, including **Christian Karner Marterbauer**, Minister of Finance, and **Robert Holzmann**, Governor of the [[Oesterreichische Nationalbank]] (OeNB).

The mission concluded on April 8, 2025, focusing on:
- **Fiscal Consolidation**: Addressing the widened fiscal deficit (4.7% of GDP in 2024) and rising public debt (81.2% of GDP), with recommendations for medium-term adjustment to stabilize debt against demographic and green transition pressures.
- **Structural Reforms**: Boosting labor supply participation (particularly among women and the elderly) to offset demographic aging and improving productivity through regulatory reforms in services and housing.
- **Financial Stability**: Monitoring risks in the commercial real estate (CRE) sector and the expiration of borrower-based mortgage lending measures in July 2025.
- **Inflation and Growth**: Projecting weak growth in 2025 (-0.1%) with a modest recovery in 2026, and inflation converging to 2% by end-2026.

Following the mission's conclusion, the Staff Report was completed on June 9, 2025, and the IMF Executive Board concluded the consultation on July 3, 2025.
