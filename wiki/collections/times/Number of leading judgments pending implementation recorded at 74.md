# Number of leading judgments pending implementation recorded at 74

## Event Details

As of June 16, 2025, Italy recorded a total of 74 leading judgments pending implementation. This figure highlights a persistent systemic challenge regarding the execution of rulings from the European Court of Human Rights (ECHR). 

The broader context of judicial delays in Italy is characterized by an average pending time of over six years for the implementation of ECHR judgments. While the country has seen positive trends in reducing domestic case backlogs—with clearance rates exceeding 100% across most jurisdictions in 2023—the specific delay in executing international human rights rulings remains a significant point of concern for the European Commission and international monitors.
