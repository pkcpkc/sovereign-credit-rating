# Freedom of Information Act amendments enter into force

## Event Details

In March 2025, amendments to the Freedom of Information Act in Slovakia entered into force. These legislative changes introduced fees for accessing public information and extended statutory response times for public authorities. The amendments faced significant criticism from civil society and media freedom advocates, who argued that the measures restricted access to information and undermined transparency. The changes have been subject to constitutional challenge, reflecting broader concerns about the deteriorating media freedom and pluralism environment in Slovakia as highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report.
