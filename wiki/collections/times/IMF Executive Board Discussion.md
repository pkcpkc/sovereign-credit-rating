# IMF Executive Board Discussion

## Event Details

On January 10, 2025, the [[IMF]] Executive Board held a discussion regarding the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the Republic of Poland. This session followed the conclusion of a staff mission in Warsaw on October 17, 2024, and the completion of the staff report on December 16, 2024. The consultation concluded with the Executive Board's final decision on January 21, 2025.

The discussion centered on Poland's robust economic recovery, with growth estimated at 2.8% in 2024 and projected to reach 3.5% in 2025–2026, driven by private consumption and the absorption of [[Next Generation EU]] (NGEU) funds. However, significant fiscal challenges were highlighted, including a widening deficit projected at 5.9% of GDP in 2024 due to permanent increases in public sector wages, social benefits, and high defense spending. The IMF recommended frontloading fiscal consolidation to rebuild buffers, aiming for a deficit of 2.9% of GDP by 2028 through measures such as VAT reforms and pension adjustments.

Regarding monetary policy, the [[National Bank of Poland]] was advised to maintain a tight stance with interest rate cuts contingent on decelerating wage growth and inflation firmly declining toward the 2.5% target, expected by end-2025. The financial sector was assessed as resilient, though risks from legacy [[CHF]] mortgage litigation and weak private credit recovery were noted. Structural reforms were emphasized to sustain convergence, focusing on labor market integration, innovation, and climate transition efforts.

Key participants in the mission included [[Jan Kees Martijn]] (Head of IMF Mission), along with staff members [[Kareem Ismail]], [[Alla Myrvoda]], and [[Yang Yang]], as well as Resident Representatives [[Geoff Gottlieb]], [[Robert Sierhej]], [[Krzysztof Krogulski]], and [[Maciej Onoszko]]. Representing Poland were Executive Director [[Patryk Loszewski]] and Advisor [[Marta Pietrzak]].
