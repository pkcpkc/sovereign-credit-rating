# Conclusion of IMF Staff Discussions with Italian Officials

## Event Details

On May 28, 2025, the IMF staff concluded their official discussions with Italian officials in Rome. These discussions were a critical component of the 2025 Article IV Consultation, which evaluated Italy's economic resilience, fiscal performance, and structural reform progress.

The discussions focused on several key pillars of the Italian economy:
- **Fiscal Performance:** Reviewing Italy's achievement of a primary surplus in 2024 and the path toward a 3% primary surplus target by 2027.
- **NRRP Progress:** Assessing the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan, where Italy had reached 54% of its milestones by the end of 2024.
- **Structural Headwinds:** Addressing productivity malaise, the shortage of high-skilled workers, and the significant demographic drag caused by a low fertility rate and an increasing old-age dependency ratio.
- **Financial Stability:** Evaluating the banking sector's soundness, including the Bank of Italy's 1% Systemic Risk Buffer (SyRB), and the "Moderate" sovereign risk assessment.

The conclusion of these discussions paved the way for the completion of the IMF Staff Report on July 1, 2025, and the subsequent Executive Board consideration in July 2025.
