# Conclusion of IMF staff discussions with Italian officials

## Event Details

The timeline of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation for Italy outlines a rigorous evaluation of the nation's economic trajectory and fiscal health. 

The process commenced on **2025-05-14** with the start of IMF staff discussions in Rome. These discussions culminated on **2025-05-28** with the conclusion of staff discussions with Italian officials, focusing on key metrics such as Italy's primary surplus and the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP).

Following these discussions, the IMF staff worked toward the completion of the formal Staff Report on **2025-07-01**. This report highlighted Italy's record-high employment and successful return to a primary surplus, while also identifying critical structural headwinds including low productivity growth and a rapidly aging population.

The process moved into the governance phase on **2025-07-18**, when the IMF Executive Board began its consideration of the Staff Report. The consultation cycle concluded on **2025-07-22** with the official Press Release of the Executive Board's conclusions, which assessed Italy's sovereign risk as "Moderate" and provided policy recommendations regarding tax expenditures and labor market participation.
