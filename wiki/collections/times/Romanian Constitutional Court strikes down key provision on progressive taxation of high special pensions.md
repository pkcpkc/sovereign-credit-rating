# Romanian Constitutional Court strikes down key provision on progressive taxation of high special pensions

## Event Details

On December 19, 2024, the Romanian Constitutional Court struck down a key provision regarding the progressive taxation of high special pensions. This judicial decision significantly impacted the government's fiscal consolidation efforts and structural reform agenda.

The ruling is highlighted in the European Commission's **2025 Country Report for Romania** as a critical setback to the implementation of necessary structural reforms. Specifically, the report notes that while the government aimed to implement a comprehensive tax framework review and special pension reforms, these efforts were not fully implemented. The Court's decision to invalidate the progressive taxation measure on special pensions contributed to the "weak administrative capacity" and "poor implementation of reforms" identified as threats to policy credibility and institutional strength.

This event occurred against a backdrop of deteriorating public finances, with the government deficit rising to 9.3% of GDP in 2024 and public debt increasing to 54.8% of GDP. The failure to fully implement special pension reforms, exacerbated by this legal challenge, limited the effectiveness of immediate emergency measures adopted in late 2024, which included wage/pension freezes and other tax reforms intended to save 2.0% of GDP in 2025. The incident underscores the legislative instability and regulatory uncertainty cited by businesses as top barriers to long-term investment in Romania.
