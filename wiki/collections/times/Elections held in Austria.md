# Elections held in Austria

## Event Details

In September 2024, Austria held national elections, which set the stage for a significant shift in the country's economic and political landscape. Following these elections, a new coalition government was formed in March 2025.

The new administration faced immediate economic scrutiny as the country navigated a period of recession following energy shocks and monetary tightening. Key milestones in the subsequent fiscal and oversight period include:

*   **March 2025:** The IMF Mission to Austria commenced to evaluate the nation's economic stability and fiscal policy.
*   **April 2025:** Formal discussions between the IMF and Austrian officials, including Minister of Finance [[Marterbauer]] and Central Bank head [[Holzmann]], concluded.
*   **June - July 2025:** The IMF Staff Report was completed, followed by the Executive Board's conclusion of the Article IV consultation on July 3, 2025. The report highlighted a need for "bold and well-designed" fiscal consolidation to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP.
*   **July 2025:** A notable shift in the financial landscape occurred with the lapse of borrower-based residential real estate measures.
*   **2026 Outlook:** The government is tasked with addressing demographic headwinds, specifically regarding labor supply for women and the elderly, while managing risks in the commercial real estate (CRE) sector. A mutual evaluation of the AML/CFT framework is expected to reach completion by February 2026.
