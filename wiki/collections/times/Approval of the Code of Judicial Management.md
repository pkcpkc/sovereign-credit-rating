# Approval of the Code of Judicial Management

## Event Details

On December 3, 2024, the Code of Judicial Management was officially approved as part of a broader series of legislative actions aimed at reforming the Italian justice system. This approval occurred within a dense period of judicial activity, following the High Council for the Judiciary's directives on out-of-tenure appointments (September 2024) and magistrates' performance appraisals (November 2024).

The approval of this Code serves as a foundational component of the structural reforms documented in the 2025 Rule of Law Report. These efforts are part of a larger push toward judicial efficiency, which includes the digitalization of tax, civil, and administrative justice, as well as the implementation of new mediation rules that came into force in January 2025 to reduce case backlogs.
