# Target for 50% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions

## Event Details

As part of the broader energy transition and climate goals outlined in the **2025 Country Report - France**, the target for a 50% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions is set for **2030-01-01**. 

To achieve this, France has already reached a milestone of 95% low-carbon electricity. However, the transition faces significant hurdles in decarbonizing the transport and building sectors. Key obstacles include high administrative complexity, a shortage of skilled renovation workers, and the need for enhanced investment in renewable energy infrastructure and carbon removals. The report emphasizes that meeting these sustainability targets requires aggressive fiscal consolidation, administrative simplification, and bridging the "skills gap" to support both green and digital transitions.
