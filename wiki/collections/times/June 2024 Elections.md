# June 2024 Elections

## Event Details

Following the general elections held in **June 2024**, Belgium experienced a protracted period of coalition negotiations that significantly stalled policymaking and fiscal planning. The political fragmentation resulting from the election results led to a delayed formation of a new federal government, which was not achieved until **February 2025**. This delay had direct consequences for fiscal consolidation efforts, contributing to a widening general government deficit and prolonged uncertainty regarding structural reforms. The eventual formation of the new government initiated a policy agenda focused on a seven-year fiscal adjustment under the **EU Economic Governance Framework**, aiming to reduce the deficit below 3 percent of GDP and address high public debt levels. Key figures involved in the subsequent government formation included Prime Minister **Alexander De Croo**, Deputy Prime Ministers **Jan Jambon** and **Simon Van Peteghem**, and regional finance ministers such as **Sven Gatz** and **Ben Weyts**.
