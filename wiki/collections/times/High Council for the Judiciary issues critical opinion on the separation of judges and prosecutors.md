# High Council for the Judiciary issues critical opinion on the separation of judges and prosecutors

## Event Details

On 8 January 2025, the [[High Council for the Judiciary]] issued a critical opinion regarding the draft constitutional reform aimed at separating the careers of judges and prosecutors. This development occurred shortly after the Chamber of Deputies approved the draft constitutional reform on 16 January 2025, which seeks to enhance governmental stability and introduce the direct election of the Prime Minister.

The High Council for the Judiciary's opposition highlights significant tension between the legislative branch and the judicial establishment. While the reform aims to increase political accountability by separating the career paths of judges and prosecutors, judicial stakeholders argue that such measures undermine judicial autonomy and independence. This controversy is part of a broader context of judicial reform in Italy, where the High Council for the Judiciary has been implementing key directives from earlier justice reforms, including rules on out-of-tenure magistrates, performance appraisals, and judicial management transparency.

Despite the judicial opposition, the reform advanced through the Chamber of Deputies, placing subsequent scrutiny on the Senate. The event underscores persistent challenges in balancing judicial independence with political oversight and governmental stability within the Italian constitutional framework.
