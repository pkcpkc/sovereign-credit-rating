# Constitutional Court cancels the first round of Presidential elections

## Event Details

On December 6, 2024, the [[Constitutional Court of Romania]] issued a ruling canceling the first round of the [[Presidential elections in Romania]], which had originally taken place in November 2024. This decision followed significant irregularities identified during the electoral process, including concerns over untransparent political advertising and the financing of private media by political parties, which raised broader issues regarding media pluralism and electoral integrity.

The annulment led to a repeat of the presidential elections, which were subsequently held in May 2025. This event was cited in the [[European Commission]]'s 2025 Rule of Law Report on Romania as a significant threat to geopolitical and political stability, highlighting the fragility of the electoral process and the impact of judicial decisions on sovereign credit rating factors. The cancellation underscored ongoing challenges in ensuring checks and balances and legislative quality within Romania's political system.
