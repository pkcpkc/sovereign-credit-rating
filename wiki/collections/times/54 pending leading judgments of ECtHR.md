# 54 pending leading judgments of ECtHR

## Event Details

As of June 2025, the number of pending leading judgments from the [[European Court of Human Rights]] (ECtHR) concerning Poland increased to 54. This accumulation of unexecuted or unaddressed judgments represents a significant challenge to Poland's compliance with international human rights obligations and highlights ongoing tensions between domestic authorities and the Court.

This development is documented in the [[European Commission]]'s 2025 Rule of Law Report Country Chapter on [[Poland]], which notes that while Poland has made progress in various rule of law areas, the handling of ECtHR judgments remains a critical area of concern under the "Checks and Balances" section. The increase in pending leading judgments underscores persistent issues regarding the effective implementation of international legal standards and the domestic judicial system's adherence to rulings from Strasbourg.

The rise to 54 pending cases occurs against a backdrop of broader institutional conflicts, including the ongoing constitutional crisis regarding the legitimacy of the [[Constitutional Tribunal (Poland)]] and disputes over the appointment of judges by the [[National Council for the Judiciary (Poland)]]. These systemic issues contribute to delays and complexities in fulfilling Poland's obligations under the European Convention on Human Rights.
