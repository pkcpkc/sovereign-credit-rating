# Council Recommendation endorsing Romania's national medium-term fiscal-structural plan

## Event Details

On **2025-01-10**, the Council issued a Recommendation endorsing Romania's national medium-term fiscal-structural plan (MTP). This endorsement followed the submission of the MTP by Romania on **2024-10-25**, which laid out a roadmap for fiscal consolidation and structural reforms.

The plan commits to a seven-year adjustment period spanning from 2025 to 2031. The endorsement comes in the context of significant macroeconomic vulnerabilities, including a government deficit that surged to 9.3% of GDP in 2024 and public debt rising to 54.8% of GDP. The Council's recommendation supports the government's strategy to restore fiscal sustainability through net expenditure caps and structural reforms.

Key immediate measures associated with the implementation of the MTP, adopted in late 2024 to save 2.0% of GDP in 2025, include:
- Freezing public wages and pensions.
- Removing certain tax exemptions.
- Reforming the microenterprise tax regime.

The endorsed plan aims to reduce the budget deficit to a target of 7% of GDP in 2025, with a gradual reduction thereafter, addressing the concerns raised in the **2025 Country Report - Romania** regarding the deteriorating fiscal position and external imbalances.
