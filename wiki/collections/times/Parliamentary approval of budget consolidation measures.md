# Parliamentary approval of budget consolidation measures

## Event Details

On March 6, 2025, the Austrian parliament approved significant budget consolidation measures in response to deteriorating fiscal conditions and a prolonged economic downturn. This legislative action was a critical component of the new government's strategy to address a projected government deficit exceeding 4% of GDP in 2025, well above the EU Stability and Growth Pact threshold of 3%.

The consolidation package aimed to achieve approximately EUR 6.4 billion in savings for the fiscal year 2025. Key measures included the abolition of paid educational leave (*Bildungskarenz*) effective January 6, 2025, and the implementation of stricter pension reforms to tighten early retirement access, scheduled for the 2025–2026 period. These steps were part of a broader Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (2025–2029) submitted to the European Commission, reflecting the government's commitment to fiscal sustainability amid rising debt burdens, with gross government debt projected to reach 84.0% of GDP in 2025.

The approval occurred against the backdrop of Austria's third consecutive year of recession, with GDP contracting by 1.2% in 2024 and projected to fall by another 0.3% in 2025. The measures were designed to curb rising public spending driven by inflation indexation and aging-related costs, particularly in pensions and healthcare, while maintaining macroeconomic stability and supporting long-term structural reforms.
