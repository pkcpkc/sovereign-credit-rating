# Government agreement signed for 2025-2029 term

## Event Details

On 2025-03, the new federal government of Belgium was formed following the end of the caretaker government period in February 2025. The signing of the government agreement for the 2025-2029 term marks a critical step in restoring political stability and policy continuity after a period of administrative delay.

The new administration has outlined key priorities that directly address longstanding governance challenges identified in the 2025 [[Rule of Law Report]]. Notably, the government intends to transfer budgetary management powers for the justice administration from the executive branch to the judiciary, aiming to enhance judicial independence and managerial autonomy. This reform is coupled with pending changes to evaluation and disciplinary systems for judges.

Furthermore, the agreement includes plans to establish specialized investigative and prosecutorial structures targeting financial crime and corruption. The government also signaled a commitment to continuing and potentially strengthening integrity policies, including reforms regarding lobbying regulations, asset declarations, and "revolving doors" rules, although specific legislative progress on these fronts remained uncertain at the time of formation. This political development aims to mitigate risks associated with fragmented legal frameworks and ensure more consistent enforcement of integrity policies across federal and regional levels.
