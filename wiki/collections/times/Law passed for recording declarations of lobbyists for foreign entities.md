# Law passed for recording declarations of lobbyists for foreign entities

## Event Details

In October 2024, France enacted legislation aimed at increasing transparency in political influence by requiring the recording of declarations for lobbyists representing foreign entities. This measure is part of a broader effort to strengthen the country's anti-corruption framework and ensure greater accountability in how foreign interests interact with domestic institutions. 

While this law represents a significant step forward for lobbying transparency, the broader anti-corruption landscape in France continues to evolve, including the reorganization of the Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA) and the expansion of asset declaration examinations by the High Authority for Transparency in Public Life (HATVP).
