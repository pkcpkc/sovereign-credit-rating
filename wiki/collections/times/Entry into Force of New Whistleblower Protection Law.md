# Entry into Force of New Whistleblower Protection Law

## Event Details

On **March 14, 2025**, the new [[Whistleblower Protection Law]] officially entered into force in Poland, marking a significant step in the country's anti-corruption framework. This legislative development occurred within the broader context of the Polish government's Action Plan on Rule of Law implementation, aimed at addressing long-standing concerns raised by the European Union regarding judicial independence and institutional integrity.

The enactment of this law complements other concurrent reforms, including the operationalization of the [[European Public Prosecutor Office]] in Poland on January 31, 2025, and the revision of legislative consultation rules effective January 1, 2025. These measures are part of an effort to strengthen checks and balances and improve compliance with [[European Union]] standards.

Key aspects of the reform period include:
*   **Date**: March 14, 2025.
*   **Context**: Part of the broader [[Anti-Corruption Framework]] and [[Judicial Independence]] reforms.
*   **Related Reforms**: The law operates alongside the separation of the [[Minister of Justice]] and [[Prosecutor General]] functions (draft adopted December 6, 2024) and the establishment of a new Central Office for Combating Corruption.
*   **Stakeholders**: The reform was noted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report by the [[European Commission]], highlighting it as part of the government's response to systemic risks in public procurement and administrative transparency.

This event contributes to the strengthening of [[Civil Society Engagement]] and improves the overall [[Institutional Framework]] for combating corruption and protecting those who report illegal activities.
