# Forecasted GDP growth of 4.0%

## Event Details

The economic trajectory for Malta shows a period of sustained growth following a strong 2024 performance. In 2024, the real GDP adjusted for inflation grew by 6.0%. This momentum is expected to continue into the following years, with a forecasted growth rate of 4.1% for 2025 and a projected growth of 4.0% for 2026.

During this period, the Maltese economy is undergoing significant fiscal consolidation. The general government deficit is projected to decrease from 3.7% of GDP in 2024 to 2.8% by 2026, while the debt-to-GDP ratio is expected to stabilize below 50% by 2026.

Key milestones and contextual factors surrounding this growth include:
- **June 4, 2025**: Publication of the 2025 Country Report, which assesses Malta's economic, social, and budgetary policies.
- **August 31, 2025**: The established deadline for the completion of measures under the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP).

While growth remains robust, the report notes that this expansion occurs alongside structural challenges, including acute labor shortages in ICT and healthcare, a need for increased R&D intensity (currently at 0.64% of GDP), and a critical push toward energy transition and water scarcity management.
