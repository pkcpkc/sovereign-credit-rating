# IMF Executive Board concludes 2024 Article IV consultation with Malta

## Event Details

On January 15, 2025, the [[IMF]] Executive Board concluded the 2024 Article IV consultation with Malta. This conclusion followed a series of high-level economic policy discussions held in Valletta between November 4 and November 15, 2024, involving officials from the [[Malta]] Ministry of Finance and Employment and the Central Bank of Malta.

The consultation resulted in a comprehensive Staff Report highlighting Malta's robust economic growth, which is projected to moderate to 4.1% in 2025. Key outcomes and recommendations from the consultation include:

*   **Fiscal Policy & Subsidy Reform:** A recommendation to phase out fixed energy price policies to redirect fiscal space toward public services and green innovation.
*   **Taxation & EU Alignment:** The urgent need for a Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform roadmap to align with the EU's Pillar II Minimum Tax Directive.
*   **Financial Stability:** Guidance on tightening macroprudential policies, specifically raising the sectoral systemic risk buffer (sSyRB) to address the banking sector's high exposure (70%) to real estate.
*   **Structural Growth:** Emphasis on transitioning toward a productivity-driven model to address labor shortages and infrastructure strain, supported by the "Malta Vision 2050" objectives.
*   **Governance:** Continued requirements for judicial reform to improve efficiency and investor confidence.
