# New Government programme commitment to independent Federal Prosecution Office

## Event Details

In March 2025, the Austrian Government formally committed to the creation of an independent Federal Prosecution Office. This initiative aims to restructure the prosecutorial system into a collegiate body to enhance judicial independence and oversight. 

As of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, this commitment is currently in the legislative drafting phase. The reform seeks to address ongoing concerns regarding the Minister of Justice's ability to exercise instructional powers over prosecutors in individual cases. This move is a key component of the broader efforts to strengthen the Austrian justice system and ensure compliance with European standards for prosecutorial independence.
