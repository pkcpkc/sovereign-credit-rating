# Law strengthening criminalisation of foreign bribery enters into force

## Event Details

On **2024-12-30**, a new law strengthening the criminalisation of foreign bribery entered into force in Romania. This legislative measure was highlighted in the European Commission’s [[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Romania]] as part of the broader anti-corruption framework and judicial reforms under review during the reporting period. The enactment aligns with Romania's ongoing efforts to address weaknesses in its anti-corruption system, which was assessed as "on track" in the implementation of the 2021-2025 National Anti-Corruption Strategy. This event occurred amidst significant challenges to the rule of law, including controversies surrounding the statute of limitations and judicial independence, which were addressed through various judicial decisions and legislative adjustments in the preceding years.
