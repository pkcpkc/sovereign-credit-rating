# Start of IMF Staff Mission in Paris

## Event Details

On May 12, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) initiated its staff mission in Paris to conduct the 2025 Article IV Consultation with France. This consultation served as a comprehensive assessment of France's macroeconomic policies, financial stability, and structural reforms.

The mission involved close engagement with key French authorities, including [[Manuela Goretti]] (Mission Head), [[Helge Berger]] (IMF Staff EUR), and [[Eugenio Cerutti]] (IMF Staff SPR), alongside French government officials such as [[Lombard]] (Minister for the Economy, Finance, and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty), [[de Montchalín]] (Minister for Public Accounts), [[Villeroy de Galhau]] (Central Bank Governor), and [[Barnier]] (Prime Minister).

Key areas of focus during the mission included:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** Addressing high public debt levels (reaching 113.1% of GDP in 2024) and implementing the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029.
- **Structural Reforms:** Promoting productivity growth through digital and green transitions, ease of regulatory burdens, and enhanced access to finance for productive firms.
- **Financial Sector Health:** Reviewing the resilience of the banking sector, confirmed by the 2025 Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP), while monitoring risks from corporate indebtedness and commercial real estate.
- **Macroeconomic Outlook:** Assessing the slowdown in GDP growth projected for 2025 (0.6%) amidst global uncertainties, including trade tensions and policy uncertainty.

The mission concluded on May 22, 2025, with discussions finalized, leading to the completion of the staff report on June 25, 2025, and the Executive Board's conclusion on July 11, 2025.
