# Establishment of Science Malta (Xjenza Malta)

## Event Details

In 2024, the Government of Malta established **Science Malta** (operating under the name **Xjenza Malta**) as a key institutional reform aimed at streamlining and enhancing the governance of Research and Development (R&D) within the country. This initiative was identified in the European Commission's 2025 Country Report as a critical step to address Malta's structural weaknesses in innovation.

At the time of this establishment, Malta's R&D intensity stood at a critically low 0.64% of GDP, ranking as one of the lowest in the European Union. The creation of Science Malta was intended to improve governance structures, boost investment in innovation, and support the broader goals of the Recovery and Resilience Plan, particularly regarding the green and digital transitions. This structural reform is part of the government's broader effort to mitigate administrative burdens and enhance the country's competitiveness through improved institutional strength.
