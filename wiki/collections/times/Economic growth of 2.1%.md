# Economic growth of 2.1%

## Event Details

In 2024, Slovakia's economy recorded a growth rate of 2.1%. This expansion was primarily driven by robust household and public consumption. However, this growth occurred alongside a contraction in investment and faced several structural headwinds.

While the year saw the unemployment rate reach historic lows, the broader economic landscape was characterized by:
- **Productivity Challenges:** Labor productivity recovery struggled to keep pace with rising unit labor costs, exacerbated by a significant innovation gap relative to other EU nations.
- **Investment Barriers:** Private and public investments were hindered by high levels of regulatory uncertainty, fragmented local governance, and limited access to capital markets.
- **Social & Labor Dynamics:** Despite low overall unemployment, severe skills shortages persisted in healthcare, education, and construction, while the marginalized Roma community continued to face a 53.4% unemployment rate.
- **Fiscal Context:** The year was marked by a government debt-to-GDP ratio of 59.3%, with the economy beginning to face the pressures of high inflation and an aging population requiring significant pension and healthcare spending.
