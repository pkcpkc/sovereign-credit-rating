# Amendments to Criminal Codes adopted (later adjusted)

## Event Details

In February 2024, the Slovak Parliament adopted significant amendments to the Criminal Codes. The primary legislative intent was to clarify procedural aspects of the "abuse of law" crime, specifically regarding its application to judges and legal practitioners.

Despite these initial adjustments, the **2025 Rule of Law Report** by the [[European Commission]] indicates that sufficient safeguards against the misuse of these provisions remain lacking. The report highlights ongoing concerns regarding potential "chilling effects" on judicial independence and the rule of law.

To address further alignment requirements with European Union standards, particularly the Protection of the European Union's Financial Interests (PIF) Directive, additional amendments were adopted in December 2024. These later adjustments aimed to strengthen the legal framework but continue to be scrutinized within the broader context of Slovakia's rule of law challenges, including the perceived low independence of the judiciary and the operational independence of anti-corruption mechanisms.
