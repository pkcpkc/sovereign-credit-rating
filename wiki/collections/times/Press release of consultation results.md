# Press release of consultation results

## Event Details

The timeline of the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium began following the **Elections in Belgium** in June 2024. Significant milestones included the expiration of one-year government bonds in September 2024 and the conclusion of the previous Article IV Consultation in December 2024.

The 2025 cycle intensified in early 2025, with official IMF discussions with Belgian authorities concluding on February 4. A pivotal meeting took place on February 21 with the new Deputy Prime Minister, **Jan Jambon**. Following these discussions, the IMF Staff Report was completed on March 3, 2025. The process culminated in the Executive Board's conclusion of the consultation on March 18, leading to the final **Press release of consultation results** on March 20, 2025.

The results highlighted the need for sustained fiscal consolidation to manage a public debt projected to reach 123% of GDP by 2030, while addressing structural issues such as automatic wage indexation and low Total Factor Productivity (TFP).
