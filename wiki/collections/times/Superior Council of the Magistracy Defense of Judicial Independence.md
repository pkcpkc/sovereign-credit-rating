# Superior Council of the Magistracy Defense of Judicial Independence

## Event Details

On 31 March 2025, the Superior Council of the Magistracy (Conseil supérieur de la magistrature) engaged in high-profile defense of judicial independence, responding to public and political pressures directed at magistrates. This action was a direct counter-measure against attacks on judges, particularly those involving election-related decisions, as highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report for France.

The defense efforts occurred within the broader context of the ongoing implementation of the 20 November 2023 Law Reforming the Status of Magistrates. Key provisions of this reform, including the repeal of age limits for admissions and the introduction of 360° evaluations for court presidents, were being actively enforced. The Superior Council's intervention underscored the tension between the Executive branch, represented by the President of the Republic, and the judiciary, as the government faced criticism for public attacks on judicial decisions.

This event is categorized under the broader themes of [[Judicial Independence]] and [[Magistrate Status Reform]] in France, reflecting persistent challenges in maintaining institutional balance and protecting judicial autonomy from political influence.
