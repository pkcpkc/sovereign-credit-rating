# Jury finding in the Daphne Caruana Galizia murder case

## Event Details

On June 5, 2025, a significant milestone was reached in the legal proceedings surrounding the murder of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia with the jury delivering its finding. This event is situated within a broader context of judicial scrutiny in Malta, as detailed in the 2025 Rule of Law Report.

While the report notes a high level of perceived judicial independence (69% among the general public), it also highlights ongoing challenges regarding the efficiency of the justice system and the need for a "robust track record of final judgments" in high-level corruption investigations. The jury's finding serves as a critical point in the country's efforts to address issues of media freedom and institutional checks and balances.
