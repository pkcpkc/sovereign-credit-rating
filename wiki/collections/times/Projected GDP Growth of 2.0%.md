# Projected GDP Growth of 2.0%

## Event Details

As part of the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation**, the International Monetary Fund projects a modest recovery for the Hungarian economy, with growth expected to reach **2.0% in 2026**. This follows a period of stagnant output and a projected growth rate of 0.7% in 2025.

The path toward this 2026 growth target is contingent on several critical factors identified by the IMF:
- **Fiscal Adjustment:** The necessity of a cumulative fiscal adjustment of approximately 2% of GDP between 2025-2028 to bring the headline deficit below 3% by 2027.
- **Monetary Policy:** Maintaining a tight monetary stance by the [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB)]] to anchor inflation expectations.
- **Structural Reforms:** Addressing productivity gaps, removing barriers to factor mobility, and improving governance to unlock suspended EU funds.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Navigating downside risks including geoeconomic fragmentation, high refinancing costs, and the potential impact of international trade measures (such as US tariffs).

The projection serves as a pivotal milestone in Hungary's economic trajectory, aimed at stabilizing the economy after recent contractions and addressing long-term sovereign-bank nexus vulnerabilities.
