# Press release of Article IV consultation results

## Event Details

On March 20, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) released the results of its Article IV consultation with Belgium. The report concluded a consultation period that ran from January 22 to February 4, 2025.

The consultation, which involved discussions with Belgian authorities and a virtual meeting with the new Deputy Prime Minister [[Jan Jambon]], resulted in an IMF Staff Report completed on March 3, 2025, followed by the IMF Executive Board's conclusion of the consultation on March 18, 2025.

The final press release highlighted several key points regarding the Belgian economy:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** The IMF supported a seven-year adjustment period under the new EU economic governance framework (EGF), requiring an annual reduction in the structural primary balance of approximately 0.6 percentage points of GDP until 2031.
- **Structural Challenges:** The report identified slowing growth, persistent core inflation, and declining labor-cost competitiveness due to [[Automatic Wage Indexation]] as primary headwinds.
- **Policy Recommendations:** The IMF urged Belgium to rationalize social spending and the public wage bill, reform the 1996 wage law to improve labor-market efficiency, and reduce regulatory barriers to boost [[Total Factor Productivity (TFP)]].
- **Risk Assessment:** Sovereign risk was rated as "Moderate," supported by a high-quality debt management profile despite a public debt level of 104.1% of GDP in 2024.
