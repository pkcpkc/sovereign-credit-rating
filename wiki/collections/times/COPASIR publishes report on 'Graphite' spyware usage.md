# COPASIR publishes report on 'Graphite' spyware usage

## Event Details

On June 6, 2025, the Italian National Inspectorate for the Protection of Communications (COPASIR) released a report regarding the usage of "Graphite" spyware. The report was issued amidst growing concerns over the safety of journalists in Italy, who faced a 16% increase in intimidations during 2024.

While the investigation was prompted by allegations of surveillance against members of the press, the COPASIR report specifically concluded that there was no evidence of state-sponsored use of the "Graphite" spyware against journalists. Despite this finding, the issue remains a significant point of concern regarding media pluralism and the "chilling effect" on journalistic freedom in Italy.
