# Deadline for full implementation of Systemic Risk Buffer

## Event Details

The deadline for the full implementation of the **Systemic Risk Buffer** in Italy was set for **June 30, 2025**. This macroprudential measure, overseen by the [Bank of Italy] and the [European Central Bank], represents a key component of the [IMF]'s assessment of Italy's financial sector stability during its 2025 Article IV Consultation.

The activation and subsequent full implementation of the Systemic Risk Buffer (SyRB), increased to 1%, is cited by the IMF as a strength in Italy's financial sector health. This buffer serves to mitigate vulnerabilities, particularly among Less Significant Institutions (LSIs) and to address the persistent sovereign-bank nexus. While the banking sector demonstrated strong profitability, sound asset quality, and adequate capital buffers in 2024, the IMF noted that weaknesses persist in some LSIs. The full implementation by the mid-2025 deadline aims to bolster resilience against potential deterioration of asset quality due to global trade shocks and geopolitical uncertainties.

### Key Context from IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation:
- **Measure**: Systemic Risk Buffer (SyRB) increased to 1%.
- **Deadline**: June 30, 2025.
- **Objective**: Enhance financial stability, address vulnerabilities in Less Significant Institutions (LSIs), and reduce systemic risk linked to the sovereign-bank nexus.
- **Status**: The activation of the SyRB is considered a positive development in Italy's financial sector, contributing to strong profitability and low Non-Performing Loans (NPLs).
