# Parliament adopts the new Security Law

## Event Details

On June 4, 2025, the Italian Parliament adopted a new Security Law. The legislation has drawn significant international scrutiny, with the UN and the Council of Europe expressing concerns that the law may narrow civic space and restrict the freedom of assembly. The adoption of this law is noted as a key development in the report on Italy's Rule of Law, highlighting ongoing tensions between national security measures and the protection of civil liberties.
