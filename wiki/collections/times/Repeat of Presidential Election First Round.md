# Repeat of Presidential Election First Round

## Event Details

On 15 March 2025, Romania held the first round of its [[Presidential Election]] following a landmark decision by the [[Constitutional Court]]. The court had previously annulled the initial election held in November 2024 due to significant irregularities, a move widely seen as an assertion of [[Judicial Independence]] and institutional integrity. This annulment was a pivotal moment in Romania's recent political history, underscoring the judiciary's role in safeguarding democratic processes against potential manipulation.

The repeat first round was conducted in strict adherence to legal frameworks, aiming to restore public confidence in the electoral system. The event was closely monitored by international bodies, including the [[European Parliament]] and the [[Council of Europe]], as part of broader assessments on Romania's rule of law compliance. Key issues surrounding the original election, such as transparency in media financing and the conduct of campaign activities, were central to the court's ruling. The repeat election served as a critical test for Romania's democratic resilience and its commitment to [[European Union]] standards on free and fair elections.
