# Debate on public broadcasting governance reform in National Assembly

## Event Details

On June 30, 2025, the French National Assembly held a significant debate regarding the governance reform of public broadcasting. The central proposal under discussion involves the creation of a holding company, designated as "France Médias," intended to consolidate and manage public broadcasters.

Key points of contention and focus during the debate included:

*   **Governance Structure**: The establishment of the holding company aimed to streamline management but raised substantial concerns among stakeholders regarding the preservation of editorial autonomy and independence for individual public broadcasters.
*   **Funding Model Transition**: The debate addressed the shift in the funding model for public service media, moving away from the traditional license fee system to a funding structure based on a share of Value Added Tax (VAT). Non-governmental organizations and media analysts expressed concern that this new model might be temporary and potentially unstable, posing risks to long-term financial planning.
*   **Labor Concerns**: Uncertainty regarding job security for public broadcaster employees emerged as a critical issue, contributing to broader social unrest, including strikes by journalists and staff within the public broadcasting sector.

This legislative discussion occurred within a broader context of media freedom challenges in France, where the regulator **ARCOM** was simultaneously expanding its scope under the Digital Services Act and preparing for the implementation of the European Media Freedom Act. While France had made progress in media ownership transparency through a national database, the debate highlighted ongoing tensions between legislative simplification, institutional stability, and the protection of media pluralism.
