# Civic space rating updated from 'obstructed' to 'narrowed'

## Event Details

In March 2025, the organization [[CIVICUS]] updated its rating of the civic space in **Poland** from "obstructed" to "narrowed," signaling a moderate improvement in the operating environment for civil society organizations. This re-evaluation reflects progress made by the Polish Government in enhancing the framework for NGOs, including the establishment of working groups with civil society entities and the launch of a new program for NGOs.

Despite this positive shift, significant challenges remain that prevent a further improvement in the rating. These persistent issues include ongoing negative rhetoric directed against NGOs by political actors and continued operational restrictions, particularly at the border. The 2025 Rule of Law Report notes that while the space has "narrowed" rather than being fully "obstructed," the overall environment still requires substantial work to fully align with EU rule of law standards and ensure robust protection for civil society.

Key contributing factors to this assessment include:
- **Government Engagement**: Active dialogue established via working groups and new support programs for NGOs.
- **Persistent Threats**: Continued negative rhetoric and operational hurdles, such as border restrictions, which limit the full effectiveness of civil society activities.
- **Broader Context**: This development is part of a wider set of reforms outlined in the 2025 Rule of Law Report, which also addresses judicial independence, anti-corruption measures, and media freedom.
