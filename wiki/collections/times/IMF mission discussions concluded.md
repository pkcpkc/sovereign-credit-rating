# IMF mission discussions concluded

## Event Details

The IMF concluded its 2025 Article IV consultation with Romania following a series of mission discussions that began on September 3, 2025. The mission, led by [[Joong Shik Kang]], focused on Romania's economic performance, fiscal sustainability, and the progress of structural reforms.

**Key Timeline & Developments:**
*   **September 2025:** The mission engaged with Romanian officials, including Prime Minister [[Ilie Bolojan]] and Finance Minister [[Alexandru Nazare]], to assess the "twin deficits" (fiscal and current account) and high inflation levels (9.9% in September).
*   **October 15, 2025:** The IMF Staff Report was completed, highlighting a period of subdued growth (0.9% in 2024) and identifying risks related to the "sovereign-bank nexus" and REER overvaluation.
*   **November 14, 2025:** A formal press release was issued following the IMF Executive Board's consideration of the report.

**Major Findings & Recommendations:**
The consultation noted that while a mid-2025 fiscal reform package (including VAT hikes and excise duties) is a positive step toward reducing the deficit to below 6% of GDP by 2026, further adjustments are required starting in 2027. The IMF emphasized the need for Romania to improve the absorption of [[Next Generation EU (NGEU)]] funds, which currently stands at 38%, and to address low labor force participation. Additionally, the report recommended a cautious monetary stance by the [[National Bank of Romania]] and highlighted the transition toward a low-carbon economy via [[ETS 2]].
