# Expected entry into force of Freedom of Information Act

## Event Details

In **September 2025**, the Freedom of Information Act in Austria is expected to enter into force. According to the **EU Commission 2025 Rule of Law Report: Austria**, the implementation of this legislation is described as "well underway." The enactment of this Act is significant as it grants subjective rights to information, thereby strengthening institutional checks and balances and enhancing transparency within public administration. This development aligns with broader government commitments to merit-based appointments and transparent governance structures, although the Commission noted that progress on related transparency issues, such as lobbying frameworks and parliamentary asset declarations, remains limited.
