# Adoption of federal law amending administrative transparency framework

## Event Details

On 2024-07, the Belgian federal government adopted a law amending the administrative transparency framework. This legislative action is part of broader efforts to improve **Access to Public Documents** and strengthen the integrity framework within the country.

According to the 2025 Rule of Law Report, while progress has been made in this area, significant challenges remain. Procedural complexities and the existence of non-binding appeal bodies continue to hinder effective access. The new legal amendments aim to address these structural weaknesses, although stakeholders have noted that the scope of transparency remains limited compared to best practices in the European Union.

This event occurred during a period of political transition, following the end of a caretaker government in 2025-02 and preceding the signing of the 2025-2029 Government Agreement in 2025-03. The reform is situated within a broader context of **Checks and Balances** improvements, including ongoing supervision by the Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe regarding compliance with final rulings of national courts and European Court of Human Rights judgments.
