# Conclusion of IMF discussions with Slovak officials

## Event Details

On January 28, 2025, the IMF concluded its discussions with Slovak officials as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. This conclusion followed a period of virtual discussions that began in December 2024.

The discussions centered on the Slovak Republic's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024. Key topics included:
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** The government's commitment to a 4-year plan (2025-2028) to reduce the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP in 2025 and stabilize public debt.
- **Financial Stability:** Assessing the resilience of the banking system, high capital ratios, and vulnerabilities in real estate markets.
- **Structural Reforms:** Addressing "secular decline" in labor productivity, demographic challenges (aging population), and the need for increased R&D spending.
- **Governance:** Strengthening judicial independence and anti-corruption frameworks.

Following the conclusion of these discussions, the IMF Staff Report was completed on March 3, 2025, and subsequently reviewed by the Executive Board on March 18, 2025, leading to the final conclusion of the Article IV consultation on March 24, 2025.
