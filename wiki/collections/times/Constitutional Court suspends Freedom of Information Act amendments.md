# Constitutional Court suspends Freedom of Information Act amendments

## Event Details

On March 12, 2025, the Constitutional Court intervened regarding amendments to the Freedom of Information Act in Slovakia. The legislation had introduced provisions allowing for the imposition of fees on "exceptionally extensive" information requests. These amendments were criticized by stakeholders and observers as potential "paywalls" designed to restrict access to critical information and hinder transparency. The court's suspension of these amendments serves as a significant check on legislative efforts that were perceived to undermine media pluralism and the public's right to information.
