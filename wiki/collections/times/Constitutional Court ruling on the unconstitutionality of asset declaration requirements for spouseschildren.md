# Constitutional Court ruling on the unconstitutionality of asset declaration requirements for spouses/children

## Event Details

On **25 May 2025**, the [[Constitutional Court of Romania]] issued a ruling declaring the legal requirements for public officials to declare the assets of their spouses and children unconstitutional. This decision significantly impacted the integrity framework of Romania, specifically weakening the transparency mechanisms managed by the [[National Integrity Agency]] (ANI).

The ruling was identified as a critical threat to **policy credibility** and **institutional strength** in the European Commission's **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Romania**. The report highlighted that this decision undermined the credibility of the asset declaration system, raising concerns about potential conflicts of interest and reduced accountability among high-ranking officials. While the ANI continues to manage conflicts of interest, the removal of the obligation to report family assets was cited as a regression in the transparency landscape. This event occurs against a backdrop of broader challenges in Romania's rule of law, including legislative unpredictability and ongoing debates regarding judicial independence and the statute of limitations in corruption cases.
