# Constitutional Court returns to full strength

## Event Details

In February 2025, the Italian Constitutional Court returned to full strength. This development occurred amidst a period of significant legislative activity regarding the country's justice system and constitutional framework. 

The restoration of the Court's full capacity follows a series of critical judicial and legislative milestones in early 2025, including:
- **January 8, 2025:** The High Council for the Judiciary issued a critical opinion on proposed constitutional reforms.
- **January 16, 2025:** The Chamber of Deputies approved a draft reform aimed at separating the careers of judges and prosecutors.
- **May 2025:** The Constitutional Court subsequently declared the law abrogating the offense of "abuse of office" to be compliant with the Constitution and the UN Convention against Corruption.

The return to full strength is a key component of Italy's ongoing efforts to maintain judicial independence and institutional checks and balances, particularly as the nation navigates debates over the separation of judicial careers and the implementation of new security laws.
