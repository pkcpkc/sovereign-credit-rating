# High Council for the Judiciary approves directive on out-of-tenure appointments

## Event Details

On September 25, 2024, the High Council for the Judiciary approved a significant directive regarding out-of-tenure appointments. This move is part of a broader series of judicial reforms in Italy, which also included the approval of a directive on magistrates' performance appraisals on November 13, 2024, and the approval of the Code of Judicial Management on December 3, 2024. These actions contribute to the ongoing efforts to reform the Italian justice system and improve judicial efficiency.
