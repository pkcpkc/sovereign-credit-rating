# Microenterprise tax regime threshold reduction to EUR 250,000

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, a significant fiscal adjustment was implemented in Romania regarding the microenterprise tax regime. The threshold for this regime was reduced to EUR 250,000. This move is part of a broader set of economic and fiscal policies evaluated in the 2025 Country Report, which highlights Romania's ongoing efforts to balance fiscal sustainability with economic growth. While the government seeks to manage the public deficit—which rose to 9.3% of GDP in 2024 due to wage and pension hikes—these tax adjustments are critical components of the Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP) aimed at addressing macroeconomic imbalances and transitioning the economy toward an innovation-driven model.
