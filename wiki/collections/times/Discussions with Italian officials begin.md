# Discussions with Italian officials begin

## Event Details

On **May 14, 2025**, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) initiated its 2025 Article IV Consultation discussions with Italian officials. This event marked the start of a comprehensive review of Italy's economic policies, fiscal trajectory, and structural reforms. The consultation period lasted approximately two weeks, concluding on May 28, 2025.

Key participants in the discussions included the IMF Staff Team led by **Lone Christiansen**, along with **Giancarlo Giorgetti**, Minister of Economy and Finance, and **Fabio Signorini**, Senior Deputy Governor of the Bank of Italy. The broader team included department heads **Helge Berger** and **Rishi Goyal**, as well as numerous IMF staff members analyzing various economic sectors.

The consultations focused on assessing Italy's economic resilience, which had seen real GDP growth of 0.7% in 2024, and addressing structural challenges such as weak productivity growth and population aging. Critical topics included the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), fiscal consolidation strategies under the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), and the health of the financial sector. The IMF staff recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment to achieve a primary surplus of 3% of GDP by 2027, while the authorities proposed a more gradual seven-year adjustment path.

Following the conclusion of discussions on May 28, 2025, the IMF Executive Board concluded the consultation on July 18, 2025, with the Staff Report and Press Release published shortly thereafter on July 22, 2025.
