# European Court of Justice ruling that Malta's investor citizenship scheme is contrary to EU Law

## Event Details

On 29 April 2025, the [[European Court of Justice]] issued a landmark ruling declaring that Malta's investor citizenship scheme is contrary to EU Law. The Court cited the "commercialisation" of Union citizenship as the primary reason for its decision, marking a significant geopolitical and legal event for Malta. This ruling highlights the tension between national immigration policies and supranational EU regulations, particularly concerning the rights associated with Union citizenship. The decision has prompted broader scrutiny of Member States' citizenship-by-investment programs and their compatibility with European Union treaties.
