# Government tabled comprehensive constitutional reform regarding the justice sector in Parliament

## Event Details

On 13 April 2025, the Government of [[Malta]] tabled comprehensive constitutional reforms concerning the justice sector in Parliament. This legislative action was part of broader efforts to address identified weaknesses in the institutional framework and judicial independence, as highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report. The reform agenda coincided with other significant legal developments, including the [[European Court of Justice]] ruling on 29 April 2025 that declared Malta's [[Investor Citizenship Scheme]] contrary to EU Law, and the parliamentary approval of Bill No. 125 in April 2025, which reformed magisterial inquiries. The constitutional reforms aimed to strengthen checks and balances, potentially involving the judiciary in the appointment of high-ranking judicial officials, such as the Chief Justice, to enhance perceived judicial independence among the public and businesses.
