# Date of replaced Sovereign Rating Criteria

## Event Details

On October 24, 2024, the previous Sovereign Rating Criteria were replaced by the updated framework. This transition paved the way for the current master criteria, which became effective on September 15, 2025. The updated framework introduced a multi-pillared approach utilizing the **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM)**—a multiple regression OLS model based on 18 variables—and a **Qualitative Overlay (QO)** for adjustments of up to +/- 3 notches. Key updates included the introduction of the **Climate.VS** framework for identifying physical and transition risks, specific protocols for **Distressed Debt Exchange (DDE)**, and refined mapping for short-term ratings based on **Reserve-Currency Flexibility (RCF)** and the **International Liquidity Ratio (ILR)**.
