# High Court of Cassation issued report on the Security Law

## Event Details

On **2025-06-23**, the High Court of Cassation issued a report concerning the new Security Law, which had been adopted by Parliament on 2025-06-04 and entered into force earlier on 2025-04-01 via Decree-Law No. 25.

The Security Law has raised significant stakeholder concerns regarding its impact on civic space and fundamental freedoms. Specifically, the legislation increases penalties for protest-related activities and has prompted questions about the preservation of democratic institutions and institutional checks and balances. The report by the High Court of Cassation was part of the broader developments in Italy's justice system and rule of law framework analyzed in the European Commission's **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Italy**, published on 2025-07-08. The Commission highlighted the Security Law as a threat to geopolitical and political stability, noting that it strains institutional checks and balances alongside the government's frequent use of urgency decrees.
