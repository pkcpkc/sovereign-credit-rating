# Lapse of borrower-based residential real estate measures

## Event Details

In July 2025, Austria is scheduled to see the lapse of specific borrower-based residential real estate measures. This transition occurs within a broader economic context where the IMF has highlighted significant risks regarding commercial real estate (CRE) and the need for structural reforms to manage demographic headwinds. The shift follows a period of economic stagnation in 2024 and 2025, with the government aiming to balance fiscal consolidation—including the reduction of deficits and subsidies—against the need to maintain financial stability in the banking sector.
