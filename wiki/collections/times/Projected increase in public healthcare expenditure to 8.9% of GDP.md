# Projected increase in public healthcare expenditure to 8.9% of GDP

## Event Details

As part of the long-term fiscal outlook for [[Austria]], public spending on age-related items is projected to reach 30% of GDP by 2070. A specific component of this demographic-driven expenditure is the projected increase in public healthcare spending, which is expected to reach 8.9% of GDP by the same year. This trend poses a significant long-term fiscal risk, necessitating strategic consolidation and policy adjustments to manage rising costs associated with an ageing population.
