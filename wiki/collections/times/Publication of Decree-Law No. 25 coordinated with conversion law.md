# Publication of Decree-Law No. 25 coordinated with conversion law

## Event Details

On **2025-05-13**, the Government of Italy published Decree-Law No. 25, which was subsequently coordinated with its conversion law. This legislative action is cited in the European Commission's *2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Italy* as part of a broader pattern of frequent resort to urgency decrees by the executive branch.

### Context and Implications

*   **Separation of Powers Concerns**: The frequent use of law decrees and confidence motions by the Government is identified as a threat to the separation of powers and policymaking stability. The Report notes that such practices raise stakeholder concerns regarding the balance between executive urgency and parliamentary scrutiny.
*   **Legislative Context**: The publication of Decree-Law No. 25 occurred amidst a period of significant legislative activity, including the adoption of the new Security Law (June 2025) and ongoing debates regarding a draft constitutional reform to separate the careers of judges and prosecutors (Chamber of Deputies approval in January 2025).
*   **Institutional Response**: While the [[High Council for the Judiciary (Italy)]] has been advancing tasks under comprehensive justice reform legislation, the reliance on decree-laws continues to be a point of contention for stakeholders who argue it may undermine institutional checks and balances.
