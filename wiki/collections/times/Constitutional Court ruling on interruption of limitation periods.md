# Constitutional Court ruling on interruption of limitation periods

## Event Details

On **2024-06-25**, a significant ruling was issued by the Constitutional Court regarding the interruption of limitation periods. This ruling has contributed to a broader "statute of limitations" crisis within the Romanian judicial system. 

The ruling, alongside subsequent High Court of Cassation and Justice (HCCJ) decisions regarding the *lex mitior* principle, has led to a systemic risk where numerous corruption cases have been closed and previous convictions annulled. This development poses a major challenge to the ongoing 2021-2025 Anti-Corruption Strategy and impacts the ability of the National Anti-Corruption Directorate (DNA) to prosecute high-level corruption.
