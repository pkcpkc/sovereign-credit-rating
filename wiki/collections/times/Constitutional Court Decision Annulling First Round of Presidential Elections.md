# Constitutional Court Decision Annulling First Round of Presidential Elections

## Event Details

On December 6, 2024, the [[Constitutional Court of Romania]] issued a landmark decision annulling the first round of the 2024 Romanian presidential elections. The ruling cited significant irregularities that invalidated the electoral process, necessitating a repeat of the first round of voting.

This decision marked a critical juncture in Romania's political landscape, highlighting tensions between the judiciary and executive branches. The annulment was a key factor in the legislative unpredictability and institutional challenges noted in the [[2025 Rule of Law Report]]. The event directly led to the scheduling of the repeat first round on May 20, 2025. The Constitutional Court's intervention underscored its role in maintaining constitutional order, even as it faced public and political pressure regarding its independence and the efficiency of judicial proceedings.

Key related entities include the [[High Court of Cassation and Justice]], which has been involved in various judicial reviews, and the [[Venice Commission]], whose recommendations on Justice Laws were being implemented during this period. The event also intersected with broader concerns about media freedom, including the surveillance of investigative journalists by the [[National Anti-Corruption Directorate]] and decisions by the [[National Audiovisual Council]] regarding electoral campaigns.
