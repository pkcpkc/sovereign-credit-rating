# Elections in Belgium

## Event Details

The political landscape in Belgium underwent a significant transition following the **Elections in Belgium** in June 2024. This period marked a shift toward a new government structure, notably involving the appointment of **Jan Jambon** as the Deputy Prime Minister (Finance and Pensions).

Following the electoral cycle, the country entered a period of intensive fiscal scrutiny and international consultation. In late 2024, the government navigated the expiration of one-year government bonds and prepared for the conclusion of the previous Article IV Consultation.

In early 2025, a series of high-level discussions were held between the **IMF** (led by **Jean-François Dauphin**) and Belgian authorities. These discussions culminated in February 2025 with a concluding meeting with **Jan Jambon** to address structural economic challenges. The IMF Staff Report was completed in early March 2025, leading to the formal conclusion of the consultation by the Executive Board on March 18, 2025.

The resulting consultation highlighted the need for a 7-year adjustment plan to address high public debt (projected at 123% of GDP by 2030), labor market inefficiencies caused by automatic wage indexation, and the necessity of structural reforms in education and the green transition. The official results were released to the public on March 20, 2025.
