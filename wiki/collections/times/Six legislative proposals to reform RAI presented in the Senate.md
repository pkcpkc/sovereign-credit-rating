# Six legislative proposals to reform RAI presented in the Senate

## Event Details

In October 2024, six legislative proposals aimed at reforming [[Radiotelevisione Italiana]] (RAI), Italy's public service broadcaster, were presented in the Senate. This legislative activity occurred within a broader context of scrutiny regarding media pluralism and institutional checks and balances, as highlighted in the [[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Italy]] published by the [[European Commission]] on July 8, 2025.

While the report noted that RAI retained its license fee levels in 2025, providing financial stability, it emphasized that concerns persisted regarding RAI’s governance independence. The presentation of these six bills reflected ongoing efforts to address these structural issues, although specific outcomes of the proposals during the reporting period were characterized by pending governance reforms. The event is significant as it intersects with broader EU assessments of Italy's adherence to rule of law principles, particularly concerning media freedom and the independence of public service broadcasting from political interference.
