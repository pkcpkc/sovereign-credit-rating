# Adoption of Strategy for Open Government in Romania 2025-2030

## Event Details

On **2025-04-09**, Romania adopted the **Strategy for Open Government 2025-2030**. This strategic framework was introduced to address mounting challenges faced by civil society organizations (CSOs) and to broaden the civil society space, which had been increasingly constrained by administrative barriers and political attacks.

The adoption of this strategy is noted as a positive development in the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Romania**, published by the [[European Commission]] on 2025-07-08. The report highlights the strategy as a key strength in the country's policy credibility and institutional strength, aiming to counter the narrowing of civil society space. Despite these efforts, the report notes that CSOs continue to face significant administrative hurdles and attacks, indicating that the implementation of the strategy will be critical in determining future progress in openness and accountability.

The strategy operates within a broader context of legislative unpredictability, characterized by the frequent use of Government Emergency Ordinances (GEOs) and weaknesses in public consultations, which are often perceived as "box-ticking" exercises. The Open Government Strategy is intended to mitigate some of these issues by fostering greater transparency and engagement with civil society.
