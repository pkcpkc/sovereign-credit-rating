# Removal of cap on political campaign expenses by National Assembly

## Event Details

- **Date**: 2025-02-17

On February 17, 2025, the National Assembly of Hungary removed the cap on political campaign expenses. This legislative change was identified in the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report on Hungary as a significant factor contributing to a "lack of transparency in political party financing and electoral campaigns." The report categorized this development under accountability threats, noting that such opacity undermines the integrity of the democratic process and complicates oversight of potential high-level corruption. The removal of these financial limits aligns with broader concerns raised by the Commission regarding weakened institutional checks and balances and the deterioration of civic space, where civil society organizations face increased scrutiny and legal uncertainty.
