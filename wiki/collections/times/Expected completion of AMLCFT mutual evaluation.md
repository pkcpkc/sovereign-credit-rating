# Expected completion of AML/CFT mutual evaluation

## Event Details

Austria is currently undergoing a mutual evaluation of its Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework. This process is a key component of the country's governance and regulatory oversight.

The evaluation aims to assess the effectiveness of Austria's measures against foreign bribery and to improve transparency within the corporate sector. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has encouraged continued enforcement of these standards as part of the broader effort to ensure financial integrity. The completion of this mutual evaluation is expected in February 2026.
