# Whistleblower protection law enforced largely since September 2024, but fully active in 2025 context

## Event Details

The enforcement of Poland's whistleblower protection law, which had largely commenced in September 2024, became fully active within the 2025 context. This development was highlighted in the [[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Poland]] issued by the [[European Commission]]. The operational status of the law was cited as a key strength in Poland's accountability framework, contributing to the country's efforts to address long-standing rule of law concerns.

While significant progress was noted in implementing the Action Plan on the Rule of Law, including the separation of the [[Minister of Justice]] from the [[Prosecutor General]] and reforms to the [[National Council for the Judiciary (Poland)]], the whistleblower law's full activation represents a concrete step in enhancing institutional transparency and protecting those who report misconduct. This aligns with broader EU standards for anti-corruption and accountability, although challenges remain regarding high-level immunities and the lack of a comprehensive national anti-corruption strategy.
