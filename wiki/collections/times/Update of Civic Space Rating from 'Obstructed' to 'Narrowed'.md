# Update of Civic Space Rating from 'Obstructed' to 'Narrowed'

## Event Details

On **2025-03**, the civic space in **[[Poland]]** was officially updated from a rating of 'Obstructed' to 'Narrowed'. This assessment, detailed in the 2025 **[[Rule of Law Report]]** published by the European Commission, marks a notable improvement in the operating environment for civil society organizations and independent media compared to previous years under the prior administration.

The shift in rating reflects specific positive developments in the **[[Government of Poland]]**'s approach to civil society engagement, including enhanced legislative consultation reforms and the continued independence of key oversight bodies such as the **[[Office of the Commissioner for Human Rights]]** under **[[Waldemar Kowalewski]]**. Despite this improvement, the report notes that significant challenges remain regarding the implementation of **[[European Court of Human Rights]]** judgments and ongoing tensions with judicial bodies like the **[[Constitutional Tribunal]]** and the **[[National Council for the Judiciary]]**. This rating update is part of a broader assessment of Poland's institutional framework, which also highlights progress in anti-corruption measures and media pluralism, even as risks related to judicial politicization and legal uncertainty persist.
