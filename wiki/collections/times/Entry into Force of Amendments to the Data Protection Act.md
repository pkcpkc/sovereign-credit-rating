# Entry into Force of Amendments to the Data Protection Act

## Event Details

On **January 1, 2025**, the amended version of Austria's Data Protection Act officially entered into force. This legislative update was part of a broader series of judicial and administrative reforms aimed at aligning national procedures with European standards and enhancing the efficiency of the justice system, often referred to as "Justice 3.0".

The amendments coincide with other key developments in Austria's legal framework during early 2025, including the implementation of Criminal Procedure Law amendments designed to increase operational efficiency. These measures were highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report Country Chapter on Austria, which noted the country's commitment to transparent appointments and ongoing digitalization of the justice sector.

This legislative change is situated within a broader context of institutional strengthening, including:
- The commitment to establish an independent Federal Prosecution Office.
- Reforms to the public service broadcaster (ORF) management bodies.
- Ongoing efforts to combat corruption and improve transparency in political appointments.

Despite these progress markers, the report also identified persistent challenges, such as high court fees and the need for more effective asset declaration rules for Members of Parliament, underscoring the complexity of Austria's ongoing judicial reform agenda.
