# Expected publication of Malta Vision 2050 details

## Event Details

In March 2025, the details of "Malta Vision 2050" are expected to be published. This strategic roadmap is a central pillar for the country's long-term economic transition, focusing on moving toward a productivity-driven growth model. 

The vision aims to address structural challenges such as high population density, infrastructure strain, and labor shortages. Key components linked to these objectives include:
- **Economic Transformation:** Shifting from a model reliant on high-growth service industries (tourism and online gaming) toward innovation and green investment.
- **Fiscal & Tax Reform:** Implementing a Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform roadmap to align with the EU’s Pillar II Minimum Tax Directive.
- **Labor & Skills:** Addressing the critical need for STEM graduates and upskilling to meet the workforce demands outlined in the 2050 vision.
- **Sustainability:** Achieving a 19% GHG reduction target by 2030 through renewable energy expansion and road transport mitigation.
