# Expected publication of Malta Vision 2050 details

## Event Details

As part of the broader economic landscape detailed in the [[IMF 2025 Malta Country Report - Article IV Consultation]], the Maltese government is working toward a strategic transition. A key milestone in this progression is the expected publication of the **Malta Vision 2050** details in **March 2025**.

This initiative is designed to reconcile Malta's development goals with social and environmental sustainability. The vision aims to shift the economy from a labor-intensive model toward a productivity-driven growth model. Key pillars of this long-term strategy include:

*   **Innovation and Education:** A focus on STEM education and digital skills to enhance national productivity.
*   **Fiscal Reform:** Transitioning away from fixed energy price models to reallocate funds toward R&D and public services (health and education).
*   **Climate Action:** Implementing aggressive measures to meet the 19% GHG reduction target by 2030, specifically targeting the transportation and buildings sectors.
*   **Tax Alignment:** Developing a roadmap for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform to align with the EU's Pillar II Minimum Tax Directive.
