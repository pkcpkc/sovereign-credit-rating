# High Court of Cassation and Justice seeks clarification from CJEU (Case C-280/25 Lin II)

## Event Details

In April 2025, the [[High Court of Cassation and Justice]] (HCCJ) of Romania referred a question to the [[Court of Justice of the European Union]] (CJEU) for clarification under Case C-280/25 Lin II. This reference arose from significant legal developments regarding the statute of limitations and the principle of *lex mitior* (the more lenient law).

The referral followed controversial case-law issued by the HCCJ, including decisions 37/2024 and 16/2024, which interpreted *lex mitior* in a manner that led to the discontinuation of numerous corruption cases and the annulment of existing convictions due to expired limitation periods. These rulings extended beyond the previously recognized legal void of 2018-2022, raising systemic concerns regarding impunity and compliance with [[European Union]] law.

The CJEU reference aims to clarify the compatibility of these national interpretations with EU standards, particularly in the context of ongoing efforts to strengthen the [[Anti-Corruption]] framework in Romania. The European Commission’s 2025 Rule of Law Report highlighted these developments as a critical issue, noting that while the implementation of the [[2021-2025 National Anti-Corruption Strategy]] remains on track, the legal uncertainties surrounding statute of limitations pose risks to the effectiveness of anti-corruption measures.
