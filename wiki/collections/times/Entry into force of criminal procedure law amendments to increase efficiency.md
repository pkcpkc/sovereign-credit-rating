# Entry into force of criminal procedure law amendments to increase efficiency

## Event Details

On **January 1, 2025**, amendments to the Austrian criminal procedure law officially entered into force, aimed at increasing the efficiency of the justice system. This legislative change was part of broader efforts to enhance judicial performance, which remains high in Austria, with civil and commercial cases resolved in an average of 141 days.

The implementation coincided with other significant judicial developments in early 2025, including the expansion of compulsory online publication of judgments to Higher Regional Courts on the same date. These measures align with the Austrian government's commitment to merit-based appointments and the advancement of digital justice initiatives, such as "Justice 3.0," which aims for 90% digital case management by the end of 2025.

While the justice system maintains high overall efficiency and public trust in judicial independence (86% of the general population in 2025), the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report noted that structural reforms, particularly regarding prosecutorial independence and administrative court appointments, require further progress to fully meet European standards.
