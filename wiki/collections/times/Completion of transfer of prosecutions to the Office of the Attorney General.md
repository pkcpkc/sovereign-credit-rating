# Completion of transfer of prosecutions to the Office of the Attorney General

## Event Details

On September 1, 2024, the **Completion of transfer of prosecutions to the Office of the Attorney General** was finalized in Malta. This significant reform involved the transfer of prosecutorial powers for non-summary (serious) offenses to the **Office of the Attorney General**, thereby centralizing prosecutorial authority for serious crimes and enhancing prosecutorial independence.

### Context and Impact
- **Judicial Independence**: The move was highlighted in the **European Commission**’s **2025 Rule of Law Report** as a major step toward removing executive involvement from the justice sector. It contributed to high perceived judicial independence among the Maltese public (69%) and companies (63%).
- **Anti-Corruption Framework**: The transfer occurred alongside increased human resources in investigative and prosecution authorities. However, while the structural capacity for investigations improved, the report noted that a robust track record of final judgments in high-level corruption cases has yet to be established.
- **Timeline Context**: This event followed the enactment of the Strategic Lawsuits Against Public Participation Order in July 2024 and preceded the government tabling comprehensive constitutional reform concerning the justice sector in May 2025.
