# UN Human Rights Committee requests improvement of whistleblower protection

## Event Details

On **August 1, 2024**, the UN Human Rights Committee issued a request for Malta to improve its whistleblower protection mechanisms. This recommendation highlights ongoing concerns regarding institutional accountability and the protection of individuals who disclose misconduct, particularly within the context of Malta's broader anti-corruption framework.

The context for this request is further illuminated by the **2025 Rule of Law Report**, which notes that integrity measures for high-level politicians and officials remain inadequate. While significant progress has been made in transferring prosecutorial powers to the Office of the Attorney General and addressing the independence of the judiciary, challenges persist in ensuring effective oversight and protection for those exposing wrongdoing. The UN committee's intervention underscores the need for robust legal and practical safeguards to complement existing anti-corruption strategies and enhance public trust in institutional integrity.
