# ECtHR judgment in J.B. and Others v. Malta

## Event Details

On October 22, 2024, the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR) issued a judgment in the case of *J.B. and Others v. Malta*. This ruling is noted as a significant development within the broader context of Malta's rule of law and judicial landscape, appearing in the 2025 Rule of Law Report as a key chronological milestone alongside other significant legal and institutional shifts in the country.
