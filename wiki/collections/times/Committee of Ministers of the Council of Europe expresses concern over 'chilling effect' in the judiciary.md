# Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe expresses concern over 'chilling effect' in the judiciary

## Event Details

On **2025-03-06**, the Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe formally expressed concern regarding a "chilling effect" within the judiciary. This expression of concern is situated within a broader context of judicial reforms and institutional pressures in Hungary as detailed in the **2025 Rule of Law Report**.

Key contextual factors contributing to this situation include:
*   **Judicial Independence:** Public perception of judicial fairness remains low, with only 38% of companies viewing the system as "fairly or very good."
*   **Legislative Pressures:** The National Judicial Council (NJC) withdrew from a four-party agreement in early 2025 due to a lack of consultation on amendments that raised the minimum age for judges to 35 and extended service eligibility to age 70.
*   **Institutional Oversight:** The report highlights a lack of progress in high-level corruption investigations and concerns over the transparency of asset declarations for senior political functions.
*   **Legal Uncertainty:** The prolonged "state of danger" and the use of emergency powers have been noted as factors undermining legal certainty and the rule of law.
