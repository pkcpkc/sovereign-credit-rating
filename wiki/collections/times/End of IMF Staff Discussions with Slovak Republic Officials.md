# End of IMF Staff Discussions with Slovak Republic Officials

## Event Details

On January 28, 2025, IMF staff concluded discussions with Slovak Republic officials as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. These consultations preceded the formal conclusion of the IMF Executive Board on March 24, 2025, which endorsed the staff appraisal.

The discussions highlighted Slovakia's economic resilience, with growth reaching 2.0 percent in 2024, driven by strong private consumption and public investment. However, the IMF noted that growth is projected to moderate to 1.8 percent in 2025 due to ongoing fiscal consolidation efforts, before recovering to 2.1 percent in 2026. Key topics of discussion included the government's ambitious fiscal consolidation plan, aiming to reduce the headline deficit to 4.7 percent of GDP in 2025 and close to 2 percent by 2028.

Participants in the discussions included [[Kažimír]] (Governor, National Bank of Slovakia), [[Kamenický]] (Minister of Finance), and [[Taraba]] (Minister of the Environment) from the Slovak side, alongside [[Oya Celasun]] (Director, European Department, IMF) and [[Stefania Fabrizio]] (Director, Strategic Policy, Research and Statistics Department, IMF).

The IMF stressed the need for structural reforms to address vulnerabilities in the real estate market, diversify the economy away from heavy dependence on the automotive sector, and strengthen governance and judicial independence to mitigate corruption risks and ensure effective absorption of EU funds.
