# Target Year for R&D Spending (2% of GDP) and Phase-out of Tax Refund System

## Event Details

According to the [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]]'s 2024 Article IV Consultation for [[Malta]], the year **2030** serves as a critical target date for two significant structural reforms aimed at shifting the economy toward productivity-driven growth.

First, the authorities are expected to reach the target of allocating **2% of GDP to Research and Development (R&D)** spending. This initiative is part of a broader strategy to enhance innovation, digitalization, and educational outcomes, moving away from the previous reliance on labor-intensive sectors such as tourism and online gaming. The IMF emphasizes that supporting innovation through effective tax incentives and venture capital is essential for long-term sustainability.

Second, **2030** marks the targeted phase-out of the fixed-price energy subsidy system, often referred to in the context of the tax refund system for energy costs. The current policy, which has accounted for approximately 20% of the fiscal deficit, is recommended by the IMF to be gradually phased out in favor of targeted subsidies. The fiscal space saved from this phase-out is intended to be reallocated toward public investment, green transition initiatives, and improved public services, aligning with the goals of [[Malta Vision 2050]].
