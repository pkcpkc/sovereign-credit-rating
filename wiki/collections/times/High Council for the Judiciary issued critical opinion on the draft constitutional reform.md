# High Council for the Judiciary issued critical opinion on the draft constitutional reform

## Event Details

On January 8, 2025, the High Council for the Judiciary of Italy issued a critical opinion regarding the draft constitutional reform that seeks to separate the careers of judges and prosecutors. This document was released as the reform advanced through the legislative process, having previously been approved by the Chamber of Deputies on January 16, 2025.

The High Council's criticism highlighted concerns that the separation of careers would undermine judicial autonomy and the representation of magistrates within the council itself. The European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report noted that the draft reform faced strong opposition from judicial bodies and magistrates' associations, who argued that the changes threatened the independence and integrity of the judiciary. The reform continued to face scrutiny in the Senate following the Chamber's approval.
