# End of 2024 fiscal year data cut-off

## Event Details

The end of the 2024 fiscal year serves as a critical data cut-off point for assessing France's economic and fiscal trajectory. Based on the 2025 Country Report, this period marked a significant milestone in identifying structural economic challenges:

*   **Fiscal Performance:** The general government deficit reached 5.8% of GDP in 2024, highlighting the urgent need for fiscal consolidation as public debt is projected to climb to 116% of GDP by the end of 2025.
*   **Labor Market Status:** Data from this period shows a high employment rate of 75.1%, contrasted against a persistent unemployment rate of 7.4% and a widening "skills gap" affecting the green and digital transitions.
*   **Social Indicators:** The 2024 data revealed a rising poverty rate, with 20.5% of the population at risk of social exclusion and child poverty rates (26.2%) exceeding the EU average.
*   **Energy Milestones:** By the close of this period, France successfully maintained a 95% low-carbon electricity mix, even as the transition in transport and building sectors faced administrative hurdles.
