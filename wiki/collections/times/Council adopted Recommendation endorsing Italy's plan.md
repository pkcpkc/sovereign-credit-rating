# Council adopted Recommendation endorsing Italy's plan

## Event Details

On January 21, 2025, the Council adopted a Recommendation endorsing Italy's plan, which is a critical component of the country's broader economic and structural reform strategy. This endorsement aligns with Italy's efforts to manage its fiscal position while pursuing growth through the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP).

The endorsement supports Italy's objectives to address key structural challenges, including:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** Managing a high debt-to-GDP ratio while achieving primary surpluses.
- **Productivity Growth:** Bridging the innovation gap and increasing R&D expenditure.
- **Labour Market Reforms:** Addressing skills mismatches and improving female labor participation through better childcare and long-term care services.
- **Regional Development:** Tackling the economic disparities between Northern and Southern Italy (the Mezzogiorno).

This move provides a framework for Italy to continue its transition toward a more sustainable and digitally advanced economy, focusing on decarbonization and high-tech manufacturing while stabilizing its macroeconomic position within the EU framework.
