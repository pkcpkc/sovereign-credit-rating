# Abolition of paid educational leave (Bildungskarenz)

## Event Details

On **January 6, 2025**, Austria formally abolished the paid educational leave scheme, known as *Bildungskarenz*. This measure was a key component of the broader fiscal consolidation package approved by parliament on March 6, 2025, under the new government.

The abolition is cited in the **2025 Country Report - Austria** by the **European Commission** as one of the fiscal consolidation measures intended to generate savings and address the country's significant deficit, which is projected to exceed 4% of GDP in 2025. The report notes that such consolidation measures, including the end of *Bildungskarenz*, contribute to policymaking stability but also create uncertainty in the policy environment. The move aligns with the government's commitment to EUR 6.4 billion in savings for the 2025 fiscal year to support debt sustainability and macroeconomic stability.
