# Adoption of the Law Reforming the Status of Magistrates

## Event Details

On November 20, 2023, France adopted the Law Reforming the Status of Magistrates, a significant legislative measure aimed at restructuring the judicial framework. As detailed in the 2025 Rule of Law Report issued by the [[European Commission]], the implementation of this reform has been ongoing, serving as a key strength in France's institutional governance.

Key provisions of the reform include the repeal of age limits for judicial admissions and the introduction of 360-degree performance evaluations for court presidents. The implementation process has faced scrutiny, with the [[Superior Council of the Magistracy]] actively defending judicial independence against public and political attacks, particularly concerning judges who made decisions related to elections.

The reform is part of broader efforts to enhance judicial efficiency and integrity, occurring alongside the digitalization of justice systems through tools like [[Portalis]]. Despite these advancements, challenges remain regarding judicial efficiency, with high resolution times for civil and criminal cases noted in subsequent assessments through mid-2025.
