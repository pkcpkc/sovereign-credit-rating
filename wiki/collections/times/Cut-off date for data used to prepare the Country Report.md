# Cut-off date for data used to prepare the Country Report

## Event Details

The cut-off date for data used to prepare the 2025 [[Country Report]] for France was established as 5 May 2025. This date marks the final point for data inclusion in the European Commission's assessment of France's economic and fiscal performance, as outlined in the document SWD(2025) 210 final.

This timeline is distinct from other key temporal markers in the 2025 fiscal cycle, including the 30 April 2025 deadline for reforms and investments underpinning the extension of the fiscal adjustment period, and the expected entry into force in 2025-Q4 of the draft law on the simplification of economic life. The data captured up to this cut-off reflects an economy facing high political and economic uncertainty following the summer 2024 general elections, characterized by a fragmented political landscape, an unprecedented delay in the adoption of the 2025 budget, and a general government deficit of 5.8% of GDP.
