# Submission of Parliamentary Bill on Corporate Governance in Companies with State Treasury Shareholding

## Event Details

On **November 29, 2024**, the Polish government submitted a parliamentary bill focusing on corporate governance for companies in which the State Treasury holds a shareholding. This legislative initiative is part of broader efforts to address corruption risks and improve transparency within state-owned enterprises (SOEs).

The bill targets vulnerabilities in **Public Procurement** and seeks to enhance the oversight of state-controlled entities. According to the 2025 Rule of Law Report, systemic risks in public procurement and a lack of transparency in state-owned enterprises are significant threats to Poland's institutional and governance strength. The government aims to mitigate these risks through improved audit mechanisms and stricter corporate governance standards, contributing to fiscal sustainability and debt burden management.

Key aspects of the bill include:
- Enhancing transparency in the operations of companies with State Treasury shareholding.
- Addressing corruption risks associated with **Public Procurement** processes within these entities.
- Strengthening audit mechanisms to ensure better accountability and compliance with EU fiscal frameworks.

This submission aligns with the government's Action Plan on Rule of Law implementation, which aims to tackle systemic issues in public spending and state fund management. The bill reflects the government's recognition that improving the governance of state-owned enterprises is crucial for maintaining economic stability and ensuring the efficient utilization of **EU funding**.
