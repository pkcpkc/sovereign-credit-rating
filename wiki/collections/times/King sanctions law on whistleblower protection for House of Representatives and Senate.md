# King sanctions law on whistleblower protection for House of Representatives and Senate

## Event Details

On **2025-03-27**, the King of Belgium sanctioned a law establishing whistleblower protection measures specifically for the **House of Representatives** and the **Senate**. This legislative action represents a significant step in enhancing institutional checks and balances within the Belgian parliamentary system.

The enactment of this law was highlighted in the **European Commission’s 2025 Rule of Law Report** on Belgium as a strength under the "Policy Credibility and Institutional Strength" pillar, noting "some progress on... whistleblower protection in Parliament." While the broader rule of law report identifies persistent challenges in Belgium—such as shortcomings in the verification of asset declarations, limited progress on lobbying transparency, and issues regarding judicial efficiency—this specific measure contributes to the anti-corruption framework by providing legal safeguards for individuals reporting misconduct within the legislative branch.

The law operates within a wider context of efforts to strengthen integrity policies for public officials. Although the report notes that integrity policies remain dispersed and that the system for asset and interest declarations still lacks adequate verification and transparency, the creation of specific whistleblower protections for parliamentarians aims to mitigate risks of corruption and undue influence. This development aligns with ongoing reforms intended to improve the integrity landscape, even as challenges remain in other areas such as the enforcement of the Code of Conduct for federal public office holders and the handling of high-level corruption cases by the **Central Office for the Repression of Corruption (CDBC-OCRC)**.
