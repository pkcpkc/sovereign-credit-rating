# End of IMF discussions with Belgian officials

## Event Details

The 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium reached a critical juncture in early 2025, involving high-level negotiations between the IMF mission and Belgian authorities.

- **February 4, 2025:** The core discussions between the IMF and Belgian officials concluded. These talks centered on Belgium's fiscal sustainability, specifically addressing a projected public debt of 123% of GDP by 2030 and the structural deficits caused by an aging population.
- **February 21, 2025:** A significant concluding meeting took place with the new Deputy Prime Minister of Finance and Pensions, [[Jan Jambon]]. This meeting solidified the dialogue regarding the government's 7-year adjustment plan under the EU Economic Governance Framework (EGF).
- **March 3, 2025:** The IMF completed its official Staff Report, which highlighted the need for structural reforms to address "automatic wage indexation," improve Total Factor Productivity (TFP), and rationalize social benefits.
- **March 18, 2025:** The IMF Executive Board officially concluded the consultation.
- **March 20, 2025:** The final results of the consultation were released to the public, outlining the IMF's support for fiscal consolidation provided it is paired with labor market and education reforms.
