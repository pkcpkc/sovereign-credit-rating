# Revision of rules on mediation and assisted negotiation came into force

## Event Details

On January 25, 2025, the revised rules regarding mediation and assisted negotiation officially came into force in Italy. This legislative update is part of broader efforts by the Italian government and the [[High Council for the Judiciary]] to improve [[judicial efficiency]] and reduce the significant backlog of cases within the [[Justice System]]. 

According to the [[EU Commission]]'s 2025 Rule of Law Report on Italy, while the length of judicial proceedings remains a serious issue, there is a positive trend in reducing the case backlog across all jurisdictions. The implementation of these mediation rules aligns with the comprehensive justice reform tasks taken forward by the [[High Council for the Judiciary]] under implementing legislation adopted in March 2024. These measures aim to promote alternative dispute resolution mechanisms to alleviate pressure on the courts, complementing other digitalisation efforts such as the mandatory use of the digital case management system (APP) in criminal courts, which began on January 1, 2025.
