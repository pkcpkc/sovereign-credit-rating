# Target year for climate neutrality and offshore renewable capacity

## Event Details

By **2050**, Malta targets achieving climate neutrality and expanding its offshore renewable energy capacity. This goal is part of the country's broader alignment with European Union strategic priorities, including the green transition supported by Cohesion Policy and the [[Recovery and Resilience Facility]] (RRF).

According to the **European Commission's 2025 Country Report - Malta**, the path to this target faces significant challenges. As of 2024, Malta's renewable energy share in electricity generation remains low at **17%**. The transition is hampered by high reliance on fossil fuel subsidies (accounting for 1.6% of GDP), severe water stress (Water Exploitation Index at 34% in 2022), and critical issues in waste management. The report emphasizes the need for accelerated investments in energy efficiency and renewable deployment through the RRP to meet these long-term sustainability targets amidst structural economic and environmental constraints.
