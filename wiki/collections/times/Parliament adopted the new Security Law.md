# Parliament adopted the new Security Law

## Event Details

In June 2025, the Italian Parliament adopted the new Security Law. This legislative action occurred shortly after the Security Decree entered into force on April 1, 2025, and followed the publication of Decree-Law No. 25, coordinated with conversion law, in May 2025.

The adoption of the law drew significant attention from stakeholders and international bodies. According to the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report, the new legislation raised concerns regarding its impact on civic space and fundamental freedoms. Stakeholders highlighted that the law increases penalties for protest-related activities, thereby affecting institutional checks and balances and the separation of powers.

Following the parliamentary adoption, the High Court of Cassation issued a report on the Security Law on June 23, 2025. The event is contextualized within a broader period of political and judicial developments in Italy, including the frequent use of urgency decrees by the Government, which raised further concerns about separation of powers during the same reporting period.
