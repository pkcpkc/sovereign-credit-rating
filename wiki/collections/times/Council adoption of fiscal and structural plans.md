# Council adoption of fiscal and structural plans

## Event Details

On January 21, 2025, the Council adopted the fiscal and structural plans for Slovakia as part of the broader EU economic framework. This adoption occurred within a period of significant economic transition for the country, where the 2025 Country Report highlights a move toward fiscal consolidation.

Key contextual factors surrounding this adoption include:
- **Fiscal Management:** The plans address a general government deficit projected to be 4.9% of GDP in 2025, impacted by permanent spending measures such as the 13th pension payment.
- **Debt Projections:** The structural plans are set against a backdrop of rising government debt-to-GDP, expected to reach 63.0% by 2026.
- **Tax Reform:** The framework aligns with a new tax mix intended to improve fairness, including value-based property taxation and adjustments to the tax wedge on lower-income earners.
- **Economic Strategy:** These structural plans support Slovakia's transition from a low-cost manufacturing model toward an innovation-driven economy, aiming to address R&D underperformance (currently at 1%) and improve the business environment through regulatory simplification.
