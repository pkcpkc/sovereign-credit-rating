# Adoption of Strategy for Open Government 2025-2030

## Event Details

On **1 January 2025**, Romania officially adopted the **Strategy for Open Government 2025-2030**. This strategic framework was highlighted in the **2025 Rule of Law Report** issued by the **European Commission** as a key strength in the country’s policy predictability and reform continuity efforts.

The adoption of this strategy serves as a counterbalance to other legislative challenges identified in the report, such as legislative unpredictability and the frequent use of **Government Emergency Ordinances**. By committing to a multi-year strategic plan, the Romanian government aims to improve transparency, enhance public consultation quality, and strengthen institutional integrity.

This policy decision is part of a broader set of reforms that includes the implementation of the **2021-2025 Anti-Corruption Strategy** and ongoing judicial IT system reforms. The strategy's adoption is viewed positively by international bodies, including the **Venice Commission** and the **European Parliament**, as a step toward stabilizing the democratic framework and improving governance standards within the **European Union**.

Despite these positive developments, the report notes that challenges remain regarding the quality of public consultations and the need for sustained implementation of open government principles across state institutions.
