# Adoption of Strategy for Open Government 2025-2030

## Event Details

On April 9, 2025, Romania officially adopted the **Strategy for Open Government 2025-2030**. This strategic initiative was launched in response to concerns regarding a "narrowed" civic space and the need for improved transparency in governance.

The strategy aims to address several key areas of public administration and civic engagement:
- **Civic Space Protection:** Counteracting the shrinking space for civil society organizations (CSOs).
- **Public Consultations:** Moving beyond "box-ticking" exercises by improving the quality and timing of consultations with stakeholders.
- **Transparency:** Enhancing the openness of government processes to rebuild public trust, which has seen a decline in perceived judicial and governmental independence.

The adoption of this strategy serves as a primary policy mechanism to mitigate the legislative unpredictability and lack of transparency noted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report.
