# Government Proposal for National Human Rights Institutions Accreditation

## Event Details

On June 6, 2024, the Romanian Government submitted a proposal regarding the accreditation of National Human Rights Institutions. This initiative is part of broader efforts to align national frameworks with international standards and European Union expectations regarding institutional integrity and human rights protection.

The proposal emerged within a context where the perception of judicial independence remained average, and the National Integrity Agency faced resource constraints. The accreditation process for these institutions had been identified as pending, highlighting a gap in the country's institutional checks and balances. This event is situated alongside other significant legal and political developments, including the Constitutional Court's annulment of the 2024 presidential election first round and ongoing challenges related to media freedom and anti-corruption enforcement.

The status of this accreditation remains a key indicator for Romania's adherence to [[European Semester]] recommendations and its trajectory toward deeper integration with European Union judicial and human rights frameworks.
