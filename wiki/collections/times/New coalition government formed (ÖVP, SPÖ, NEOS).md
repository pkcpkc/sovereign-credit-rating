# New coalition government formed (ÖVP, SPÖ, NEOS)

## Event Details

Following the [[Austrian legislative election, 2024|legislative election]] held in September 2024, which resulted in no single party securing an absolute majority, a new coalition government was formally established in March 2025. The administration is composed of three major political parties: the center-right [[Austrian People's Party|ÖVP]], the center-left [[Social Democratic Party of Austria|SPÖ]], and the liberal [[NEOS (political party)|NEOS]] party.

This new administration prioritized undertaking comprehensive reforms aimed at boosting economic growth and addressing the country's fiscal deficit, which had widened to 4.7% of GDP in 2024 due to successive recessions and one-off expenditures. Key policy priorities included implementing frontloaded fiscal consolidation measures, such as pension reforms, limits on public-sector wage increases, and increased reliance on property, inheritance, and excise taxes. The government's stability was noted by the [[International Monetary Fund|IMF]] during its Article IV Consultation mission in March 2025, although concerns were raised regarding weak cohesion and potential policy reversals, particularly on inheritance taxes.
