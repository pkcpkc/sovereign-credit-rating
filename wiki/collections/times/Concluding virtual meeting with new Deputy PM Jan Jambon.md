# Concluding virtual meeting with new Deputy PM Jan Jambon

## Event Details

On February 21, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) held its concluding virtual meeting for the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium. The session featured **Jan Jambon**, the newly appointed Deputy Prime Minister of Belgium, who had been part of the federal government formed in February 2025 following the June 2024 elections.

The meeting served as the final stage of discussions prior to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on March 3, 2025, and the Executive Board's conclusion on March 18, 2025. Key themes discussed with Deputy PM Jambon included:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation**: The IMF expressed support for the new government’s plan to implement a seven-year fiscal adjustment under the new EU Economic Governance Framework (EGF). The goal is to reduce the structural primary balance by approximately 0.6 percentage points of GDP annually until 2031, aiming to bring the deficit durably below 3% of GDP and reduce public debt (which stood at 104.1% of GDP in 2024) to a sustainable level.
*   **Structural Reforms**: Discussions highlighted the urgency of reforming the wage-setting mechanism, specifically addressing automatic wage indexation and the 1996 wage law, to improve labor cost competitiveness and potential growth.
*   **Coordination**: Emphasis was placed on improving coordination between federal and regional governments regarding climate targets and fiscal accountability, with plans to revitalize the fiscal council.

The insights from this meeting were incorporated into the subsequent IMF Staff Report, which assessed Belgium's economy as resilient but facing significant structural challenges related to fiscal sustainability and labor market efficiency.
