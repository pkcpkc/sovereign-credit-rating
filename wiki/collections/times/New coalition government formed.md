# New coalition government formed

## Event Details

Following the elections held in **2024-09**, a new coalition government was formed in Austria in **March 2025**. 

This government has initiated a series of fiscal and economic strategies to navigate a challenging landscape marked by two years of recession following energy shocks and monetary tightening. Key priorities for the new administration include:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** Implementing a "bold and well-designed" plan to reduce the national deficit to below 2% of GDP. Specific measures involve abolishing the "climate bonus" and reducing green subsidies.
*   **Labor and Demographic Reform:** Addressing structural issues to offset 20 years of demographic aging by increasing labor force participation among women and the elderly.
*   **Economic Recovery:** Managing a projected near-zero growth period in 2025 with the goal of achieving a rebound in 2026 through private consumption recovery and monetary easing.
*   **Financial Oversight:** Maintaining vigilance over commercial real estate (CRE) risks and the banking sector's capital ratios, while continuing the mutual evaluation of AML/CFT frameworks.
