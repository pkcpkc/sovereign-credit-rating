# Target for legal pension age to rise to 67

## Event Details

As part of Belgium's long-term fiscal sustainability strategy, the country has established a target to increase the legal pension age to 67 by **2030-01-01**. 

This measure is a critical component of the broader effort to address the rising costs of an aging population. According to the **2025 Country Report - Belgium**, pension spending is projected to increase by 3.5% of GDP between 2022 and 2070, a rate significantly higher than the EU average. The move to raise the retirement age is intended to mitigate these fiscal pressures and manage the growing costs associated with long-term care and social security.
