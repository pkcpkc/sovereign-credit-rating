# Entry into force of the Government Decree later converted into Security Law

## Event Details

On 2025-04, the Government Decree later converted into Security Law entered into force in Italy. This legislative measure was subsequently adopted by Parliament as a formal Security Law on 2025-06-04, aiming to combat terrorism and organized crime. The enactment has drawn significant scrutiny from international bodies, including the United Nations Special Rapporteurs and the Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights, who have expressed concerns regarding its potential impact on fundamental freedoms and civic space.

Stakeholders have raised specific fears that the broad powers granted by the Security Law could be applied arbitrarily, potentially restricting freedom of assembly and expression. Civil society organizations have rated Italy’s civic space as 'narrowed' in the context of this legislation. The High Court of Cassation issued a periodic report on legislative measures, including this Security Law, on 2025-06-23, highlighting ongoing legal and political tensions surrounding the balance between national security and individual rights.
