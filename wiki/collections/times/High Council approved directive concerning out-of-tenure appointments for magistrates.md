# High Council approved directive concerning out-of-tenure appointments for magistrates

## Event Details

On **2024-09-25**, the High Council for the Judiciary in Italy approved a directive concerning out-of-tenure appointments for magistrates. This decision occurred within the broader context of the comprehensive justice reform in Italy, following the adoption of legislative decrees implementing the reform by the Government in March 2024. The directive represents a specific regulatory measure taken by the High Council to manage judicial appointments, potentially addressing staffing shortages and procedural adjustments required under the new reform framework.

The European Commission's **2025 Rule of Law Report** notes that the High Council has taken forward tasks under the implementing legislation for the comprehensive justice reform. While the report highlights persistent shortages of administrative staff and magistrates (noting a 17% magistrate shortage at the time of reporting), it also recognizes the Council's role in managing recruitment and implementation tasks. This directive is part of the ongoing efforts to stabilize and reform the Italian justice system amidst broader institutional changes, including the debate over the separation of judges' and prosecutors' careers.
