# Tabling of draft law separating Prosecutor General and Minister of Justice

## Event Details

On July 14, 2024, a draft law was tabled aimed at separating the office of the Minister of Justice from the Prosecutor General. This legislative move is a significant component of the broader prosecution reform in Poland.

The initiative follows a period of scrutiny regarding judicial independence and the concentration of power within the justice system. By separating these roles, the reform seeks to improve the procedural framework for judicial appointments and ensure a more independent prosecution service. This effort is part of a larger suite of reforms, including the auditing of cases from 2016–2023 by the National Prosecution Office to identify irregularities and the ongoing consultation with the [[Venice Commission]] regarding reforms for the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ) and the Constitutional Tribunal.
