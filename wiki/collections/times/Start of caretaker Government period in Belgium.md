# Start of caretaker Government period in Belgium

## Event Details

The caretaker Government period in Belgium commenced in **June 2024**, following the conclusion of the previous legislative term and prior to the formation of a new coalition government. This period of political fragmentation lasted until **February 2025**, when a new government was successfully formed.

The extended duration of this caretaker period is cited in the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Belgium** as a significant threat to geopolitical and political stability. The report highlights that such delays in forming a government contribute to policymaking instability and raise concerns regarding institutional continuity. During this interim phase, the government operated with limited mandate, which may have impacted the speed and scope of legislative actions, including reforms related to the justice system and anti-corruption frameworks. The end of this period in February 2025 marked the restoration of full governmental functions, allowing for the implementation of the new government agreement's intentions for judicial reform and integrity improvements.
