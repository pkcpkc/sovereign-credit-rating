# Government sends initial action plan to Court of Audit

## Event Details

On September 2, 2024, the Belgian Government submitted an initial action plan to the Court of Audit. This move was a direct response to criticisms regarding the country's digitalization strategy in the justice system. The Court of Audit had previously highlighted a lack of an overarching approach and an excessive reliance on external consultants for judicial digitalization. The government's plan aims to address these structural deficiencies and improve the efficiency of the justice system.
