# IMF Executive Board Discussion of Staff Report

## Event Details

On July 11, 2025, the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) Executive Board concluded its 2025 Article IV Consultation with [[France]]. The discussion focused on the nation's economic resilience amidst high global uncertainty, acknowledging robust labor market conditions and progress in disinflation. However, Directors emphasized urgent concerns regarding [[France]]'s high and rising public debt, which reached 113.1% of [[GDP]] in 2024, alongside significant fiscal slippages.

The Executive Board recommended a credible package of structural fiscal measures to reduce the deficit below 3% of [[GDP]] by 2029, prioritizing spending rationalization over increasing the already high tax burden. Key risks identified included geoeconomic fragmentation, trade tensions, and domestic policy uncertainty, which were projected to slow [[GDP]] growth to 0.6% in 2025. The Board also stressed the importance of structural reforms to boost productivity, particularly in the digital and green transitions, and noted the financial sector's resilience as confirmed by the 2025 Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP).

Key participants in the consultation included [[Arnaud Buisse]], [[IMF]] Executive Director for [[France]], and French government officials including [[Christine Lagarde]] (IMF Managing Director), [[Helge Berger]], [[Eugenio Cerutti]], and [[Manuela Goretti]]. The discussion took place following the completion of the IMF Staff Report on June 25, 2025, and the conclusion of staff mission discussions in Paris in May 2025.
