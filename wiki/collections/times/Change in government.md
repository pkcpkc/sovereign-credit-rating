# Change in government

## Event Details

On 2024-10-01, a change in government occurred in the Slovak Republic. This transition preceded the 2025 Article IV Consultation with the IMF, during which the new administration committed to an ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit and stabilizing public debt.
