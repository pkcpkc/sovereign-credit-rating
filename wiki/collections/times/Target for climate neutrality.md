# Target for climate neutrality

## Event Details

Austria has established a strategic timeline for its environmental and energy transition. A key milestone in this roadmap is the target for **climate neutrality by 2040**. 

To support this overarching goal, the country is focusing on several interconnected pillars:
- **Renewable Energy:** Austria aims for 100% renewable electricity and VHCN coverage by 2030, having already achieved a record 87% renewable electricity generation in 2024.
- **Infrastructure Investment:** To meet these targets, the country requires an estimated EUR 53 billion in grid expansion investments by 2040.
- **Industrial Decarbonisation:** A primary focus is placed on decarbonizing energy-intensive manufacturing sectors, which currently account for 35% of emissions.
- **Energy Security:** The transition is being bolstered by diversifying gas supplies from partners such as [[Norway]], [[Germany]], and [[Italy]] following the termination of Russian imports in early 2025.
