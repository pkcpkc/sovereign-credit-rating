# Expected adoption of the 2024-2029 national anti-corruption plan

## Event Details

In July 2025, France is expected to adopt its new 2024-2029 national anti-corruption plan. This strategic framework is designed to strengthen the country's integrity measures, with a specific focus on combating organized drug crime and ensuring municipal integrity.

The adoption of this plan follows a period of significant institutional strengthening, including the reorganization of the Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA) into dedicated public and private sector departments. It also aligns with broader legislative efforts to improve the seizure of criminal assets (via the Law Warsmann) and the increased resource allocation to the National Financial Prosecutor (PNF).
