# New Coalition Government Formation

## Event Details

In June 2025, Romania established a new [[Coalition Government]] following the 2025 Romanian Presidential Elections. This political development marked a critical juncture for the country's macroeconomic stability and sovereign credit profile, directly influencing the implementation of urgent fiscal reforms.

The newly formed government prioritized a comprehensive fiscal consolidation package to address twin deficits (fiscal and current account) that had widened significantly in 2024. Key actions included the adoption of large fiscal reforms, such as [[VAT]] rate hikes and excise increases, aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit to below 6 percent of [[GDP]] in 2026 and eventually below 3 percent by 2031. These measures were designed to restore fiscal and macroeconomic sustainability, though they contributed to a spike in headline inflation to 9.9 percent by September 2025.

The formation of this government was closely watched by international institutions, including the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF), which noted the adoption of the fiscal reform package as a strength in its sovereign credit rating assessment. The government's commitment to [[EU]] fiscal rules and progress in tax administration digitalization were highlighted as key factors in maintaining Romania's investment-grade [[Sovereign Credit Rating]], despite risks related to political uncertainty and implementation delays.

Key figures involved in this period included Prime Minister [[Ilie Bolojan]] and Finance Minister [[Alexandru Nazare]], who worked alongside Central Bank Governor [[Mugur Isarescu]] to navigate the transition and policy execution.
