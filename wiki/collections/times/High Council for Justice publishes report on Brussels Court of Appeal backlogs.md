# High Council for Justice publishes report on Brussels Court of Appeal backlogs

## Event Details

On November 28, 2024, the High Council for Justice published a significant report highlighting persistent backlogs within the Brussels Court of Appeal. This report underscores long-standing structural resource deficiencies within the Belgian justice system, where the budget and number of judges per 100,000 inhabitants remain below the EU average.

The issue of court efficiency is part of a broader institutional effort to modernize the judiciary. Following criticisms from the Court of Audit in December 2024 regarding a lack of an overarching digitalization strategy, the government has begun responding by initiating projects to improve statistics and file management. A specific project funded by the Recovery and Resilience Facility is currently underway to address these administrative bottlenecks and improve the overall transparency of judicial proceedings in the Brussels region.
