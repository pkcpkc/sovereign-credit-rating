# Publication of Principles of Credit Ratings

## Event Details

On **February 16, 2011**, [[S&P Global Ratings]] published the *Principles of Credit Ratings*. This foundational document established the general philosophy and overarching standards that guide the firm's credit analysis and rating assignments across all sectors, including sovereign, corporate, and structured finance entities.

The publication serves as a precursor to more specific methodologies, such as the [[S&P Global Ratings Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)]], which was published later on December 18, 2017. The 2017 Sovereign Methodology builds upon the foundational principles outlined in 2011 by providing a detailed, five-pillar framework (Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments) for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness. Key elements of this subsequent methodology include the shift to net general government debt assessments, specific caps on local-currency rating uplifts, and the integration of contingent liabilities from the banking sector via the Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA).

The *Principles of Credit Ratings* document itself emphasizes the importance of consistency, transparency, and the holistic assessment of a borrower's ability and willingness to meet financial obligations, setting the stage for the specialized criteria developed in subsequent years, including the 2012 criteria for assigning 'CCC+', 'CCC', 'CCC-', and 'CC' ratings and the 2013 Monetary Authorities Rating Methodology.
