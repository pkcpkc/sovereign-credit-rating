# Passage of law removing cap on political campaign expenses

## Event Details

In June 2025, the Hungarian government passed legislation that removed the statutory cap on political campaign expenses. This legislative change was identified in the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report as a significant development within the country's broader legal and political framework. The removal of spending limits has raised concerns regarding transparency and fair competition in the electoral process, contributing to the report's assessment of deteriorating rule of law standards. This event occurred alongside other major judicial and constitutional amendments, including the Fifteenth Amendment which facilitated the election of a new Prosecutor General and reforms affecting the Kúria.
