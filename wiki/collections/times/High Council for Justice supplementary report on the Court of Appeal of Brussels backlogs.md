# High Council for Justice supplementary report on the Court of Appeal of Brussels backlogs

## Event Details

On 28 November 2024, the High Council for Justice published a supplementary report detailing persistent backlogs within the Court of Appeal of Brussels. This event highlights a critical challenge in Belgium's justice system efficiency, where structural resource deficiencies and budget constraints continue to impede judicial performance despite some progress in data collection and magistrate vacancy publishing. The report underscores the disparity between the Court of Appeal of Brussels' case duration statistics and EU averages, contributing to ongoing supervision by the Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe regarding compliance with final rulings. The issue remains a focal point for government reform efforts, including plans to transfer budgetary management of the justice administration from the executive to the judiciary to enhance managerial autonomy and address these systemic bottlenecks.
