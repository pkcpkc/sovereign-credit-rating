# National Audiovisual Council decision on Presidential election campaign rules

## Event Details

On **2025-02-25**, the [[National Audiovisual Council]] (CNA) issued a decision regarding the rules for Presidential election campaigns. This regulatory action occurred against a backdrop of heightened concerns over media freedom and electoral integrity in Romania, as highlighted in the [[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Romania]].

The decision is significant in the context of ongoing challenges to media pluralism and transparency. The European Commission’s report notes that untransparent political advertising and the financing of private media by political parties have increased, negatively impacting media independence. Specifically, the report cites "untransparent political advertising and financing of private media by political parties" as a geopolitical and political stability threat. The CNA, responsible for overseeing audiovisual media, faced its own operational challenges during this period, including a lack of sufficient human resources and an outdated IT system, which the report suggests impacts the Council's overall effectiveness in enforcing regulations.

This event preceded the controversial first round of the 2024 Presidential elections (held in November 2024), which were subsequently annulled by the Constitutional Court in December 2024 due to irregularities, leading to a repeat election in May 2025. The CNA's campaign rules were thus a critical component of the regulatory framework governing these high-stakes electoral processes, amidst broader concerns regarding legislative unpredictability and the narrowing of civil society space.
