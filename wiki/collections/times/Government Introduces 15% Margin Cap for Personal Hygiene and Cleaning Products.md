# Government Introduces 15% Margin Cap for Personal Hygiene and Cleaning Products

## Event Details

In May 2025, the Hungarian government implemented a 15% cap on retailer profit margins for personal hygiene and cleaning products. This measure was part of a broader regulatory framework aimed at controlling consumer prices, following a previous 10% margin cap introduced in March 2025 for food products. The policy has drawn scrutiny from the European Court of Justice, which previously ruled against similar Hungarian agricultural price-fixing measures for violating single market rules. The intervention reflects the government's approach to addressing inflationary pressures and consumer protection, though it contributes to concerns regarding regulatory volatility and market distortions identified in the 2025 Country Report for Hungary.
