# Expansion of compulsory online publication of judgments to Higher Regional Courts

## Event Details

On **January 1, 2025**, Austria implemented a significant digital justice initiative by expanding the compulsory online publication of judgments to include **Higher Regional Courts**. This measure was part of the broader "Justice 3.0" strategy, which aims to achieve 90% digital case management by the end of 2025.

The expansion is cited in the **EU Commission 2025 Rule of Law Report** as a positive step towards enhancing the efficiency and transparency of the Austrian justice system. The report notes that the Austrian justice system remains highly efficient, with civil and commercial cases resolved in an average of 141 days. The online publication of judgments contributes to the high level of perceived judicial independence among the general public, which stood at 86% in 2025. This reform aligns with the government's commitment to advancing digital justice initiatives and improving access to judicial information, despite other ongoing challenges regarding judicial appointment procedures and prosecutorial independence.
