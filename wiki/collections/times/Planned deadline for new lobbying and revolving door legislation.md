# Planned deadline for new lobbying and revolving door legislation

## Event Details

The Hungarian government has established a planned deadline of November 30, 2025, for the implementation of new legislation regarding lobbying and "revolving door" regulations. 

This initiative is part of a broader context of judicial and administrative reforms in Hungary, as highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report. While the government has made strides in judicial remuneration and digitalization, the report notes that progress in lobbying regulations and high-level corruption oversight remains a significant area of concern. The upcoming deadline for these specific legislative measures is intended to address transparency gaps in political influence and the movement of individuals between public office and private sector lobbying roles.
