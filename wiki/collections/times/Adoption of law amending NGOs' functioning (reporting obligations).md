# Adoption of law amending NGOs' functioning (reporting obligations)

## Event Details

On **2025-04-16**, the Slovak government adopted a law amending the functioning of non-governmental organizations (NGOs), specifically introducing enhanced reporting and disclosure obligations. This legislative move is part of a broader pattern of **Fast-Track Legislation** utilized by the administration, which in 2024 saw 58 out of 131 laws adopted with limited public participation, including 28 via fast-track procedures.

The new law imposes annual reporting and disclosure requirements on NGOs, significantly increasing their administrative burden. Stakeholders, including the **European Commission**, view this legislation as a mechanism for political control that narrows the space for **Civil Society**. This development contributes to the overall deterioration of institutional checks and balances and media pluralism in **Slovakia**, as noted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report. The measure aligns with other recent actions affecting independent institutions, such as the restructuring of **Public Service Media** and reforms to the judicial system.
