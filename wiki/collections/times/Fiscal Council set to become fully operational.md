# Fiscal Council set to become fully operational

## Event Details

As part of Poland's broader fiscal adjustment strategy and commitment to the Stability and Growth Pact, the Fiscal Council is scheduled to become fully operational in January 2026. This move follows a period of significant fiscal oversight, including the Council's initiation of an excessive deficit procedure for Poland in January 2023. The operationalization of the council aligns with the country's medium-term fiscal-structural plan, which was endorsed in January 2025 and saw a progress report submission in April 2025. The council plays a key role in enforcing a binding expenditure path intended to reduce Poland's deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028.
