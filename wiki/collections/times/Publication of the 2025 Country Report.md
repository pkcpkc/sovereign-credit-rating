# Publication of the 2025 Country Report

## Event Details

On **2025-06-04**, the European Commission released the **2025 Country Report for Belgium** (SWD(2025) 201 final). This comprehensive assessment evaluated Belgium's economic, social, and budgetary policies, highlighting the country's progress toward EU targets and its transition to a green and digital economy.

Key findings from the report include:
*   **Economic Outlook:** A projected GDP growth of 0.8% and a headline deficit of 5.4% for 2025, with public debt reaching 107.1%.
*   **Labor Market Challenges:** Despite a record employment rate of 72.3% in 2024, Belgium faces significant skills mismatches in STEM and healthcare, alongside high job vacancy rates.
*   **Fiscal Pressures:** Significant concerns were raised regarding the rising costs of an aging population, with pension spending projected to increase by 3.5% of GDP between 2022 and 2070.
*   **Sustainability:** The report noted that Belgium is currently projected to fall short of its 2030 climate effort-sharing targets, requiring accelerated investment in the energy transition and circular economy.
*   **Innovation:** While recognized as a "strong innovator" with high R&D intensity, the report identified regulatory complexity and high labor costs as primary barriers to business dynamism.
