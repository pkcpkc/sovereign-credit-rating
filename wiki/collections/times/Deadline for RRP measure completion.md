# Deadline for RRP measure completion

## Event Details

As of mid-2025, Malta has fulfilled 39% of the milestones and targets associated with its Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP). The funding status shows that 50.7% of grants have been disbursed, with primary focus areas directed toward the green transition, digital transformation, and healthcare. The final deadline for the completion of RRP measures is set for August 31, 2025.
