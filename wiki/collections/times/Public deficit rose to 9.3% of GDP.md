# Public deficit rose to 9.3% of GDP

## Event Details

In 2024, Romania's public deficit reached 9.3% of GDP. This fiscal expansion was primarily driven by significant hikes in wages and pensions. The rise in the deficit occurred alongside a deceleration in real GDP growth (down to 0.8% in 2024), creating a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by high fiscal and current account deficits.

While the government has established a Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP) to cap net expenditure growth at 5.1% in 2025, the cumulative growth for the 2024-2025 period is projected to exceed recommended limits. Additionally, government debt rose to nearly 55% of GDP by the end of 2024, reflecting a period of rapid fiscal expansion.
