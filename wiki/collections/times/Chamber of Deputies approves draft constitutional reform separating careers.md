# Chamber of Deputies approves draft constitutional reform separating careers

## Event Details

On January 16, 2025, the Italian Chamber of Deputies approved a significant draft constitutional reform aimed at separating the careers of judges and prosecutors. 

This legislative move is part of a broader effort to restructure the Italian justice system, though it has met with mixed reactions from the legal community. While the reform seeks to clarify roles within the judiciary, the High Council for the Judiciary and various magistrates' associations have expressed reservations regarding the authority of separate councils and the potential impact on judicial independence. The reform sits within a period of intense legislative activity in Italy, including the 2025 Rule of Law Report's observations on judicial independence and the ongoing digitalization of court systems.
