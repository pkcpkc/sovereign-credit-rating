# Minister of Culture initiative for public broadcaster coordination

## Event Details

In March 2025, a significant initiative was launched by the French Minister of Culture regarding the coordination and governance of public broadcasters. This move is part of a broader regulatory evolution in France's media landscape, which includes:

- **Structural Oversight:** The initiative is linked to the ongoing debate over the creation of "France Médias," a proposed holding company designed to oversee public broadcasting entities.
- **Funding Reform:** This period coincides with a shift in the funding model for public broadcasters, moving from a traditional license fee to a mechanism based on VAT.
- **Regulatory Context:** These actions occur alongside the expansion of ARCOM's scope to include Digital Services Act (DSA) mandates and the development of a national media ownership database to ensure media pluralism.
