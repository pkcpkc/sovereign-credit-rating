# Start of IMF staff discussions in Warsaw

## Event Details

The IMF staff discussions in Warsaw commenced on October 8, 2024, as part of the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. These discussions were led by the IMF staff team, headed by Jan Kees Martijn, and involved key Polish government officials and representatives from the National Bank of Poland (NBP).

The primary focus of these discussions centered on Poland's economic recovery, with a specific emphasis on the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds and their role in driving projected growth of 3.5% for 2025-2026. Key topics included:

*   **Fiscal Policy:** Addressing the widening fiscal deficit (estimated at 5.9% of GDP in 2024) and the implementation of the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) to stabilize debt at 60% of GDP.
*   **Monetary Policy:** Evaluating the NBP's tight stance (5.75% policy rate) in response to persistent inflation and wage growth.
*   **Structural Challenges:** Discussing labor supply issues, the integration of Ukrainian refugees, and the transition toward decarbonizing the economy.
*   **Banking Stability:** Reviewing the resilience of the banking system and recommendations regarding the mortgage credit holiday.

The discussions concluded on October 17, 2024, leading to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on December 16, 2024, and the official press release of the Article IV consultation conclusion on January 21, 2025.
