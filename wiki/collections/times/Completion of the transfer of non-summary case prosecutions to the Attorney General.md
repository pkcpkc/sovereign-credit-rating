# Completion of the transfer of non-summary case prosecutions to the Attorney General

## Event Details

In September 2024, Malta completed the transfer of prosecution powers for non-summary cases from the Police to the Attorney General. This structural reform significantly increased the [[Attorney General]]'s prosecutorial authority, representing a key advancement in the country's judicial independence framework. 

The reform has been cited as a strength contributing to high perceived judicial independence among the general public (69%) and businesses (63%). It is part of a broader effort to strengthen institutional checks and balances, although concerns remain regarding the independence of specialized tribunals and the capacity to deliver final judgments in high-level corruption cases. This event is contextualized within the 2025 Rule of Law Report on Malta, which highlights mixed progress in upholding the rule of law, with notable advancements in prosecutorial reforms alongside persistent challenges in corruption accountability and media freedom.
