# Extension of Compulsory Online Publication of Judgments to Higher Regional Courts

## Event Details

On 1 January 2025, Austria implemented the extension of compulsory online publication of judgments to Higher Regional Courts. This measure serves as a key component of the broader "Justice 3.0" digitalization initiative, which aims to achieve 90% digital case management across the Austrian judiciary by year-end.

The reform enhances transparency and public access to legal precedents, aligning with the European Commission's assessment of Austria's commitment to judicial transparency and digital transformation. While perceived judicial independence in Austria remains very high (86% of the general public), this digitalization effort supports broader structural goals to improve the efficiency and accessibility of the justice system. The move complements other ongoing judicial reforms, including efforts to establish an independent Federal Prosecution Office and address vulnerabilities in administrative court appointment mechanisms.
