# European Court of Justice Rules Against Hungarian Agricultural Price Fixing

## Event Details

In September 2024, the **[[European Court of Justice]]** issued a ruling against the **[[Hungary]]** government, declaring its agricultural price-fixing measures incompatible with European Union law. This decision highlighted significant non-compliance with single market regulations, specifically regarding the government's introduction of maximum price caps on agricultural products. The ruling underscored tensions between national populist economic interventions and EU single market principles, serving as a key instance of the broader rule-of-law concerns affecting Hungary's integration with EU financial frameworks.
