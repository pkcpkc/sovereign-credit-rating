# Projected operational start of the Polish Fiscal Council

## Event Details

The Polish Fiscal Council is projected to become operational by **June 30, 2025**. This independent institution was established through legislation enacted on **December 31, 2024**, aiming to strengthen policy credibility and institutional strength in Poland. 

The creation of the Fiscal Council is highlighted in the **EU Commission's 2025 Country Report on Poland** as a key strength in the country's policy credibility and institutional framework. Its establishment is part of broader efforts to improve regulatory governance and transparency, counteracting threats such as complex regulatory frameworks and legislative volatility. The Fiscal Council's operational launch coincides with the implementation of a medium-term fiscal-structural plan endorsed by the Council of the European Union in January 2025, which aims to address Poland's high budget deficit (6.6% of GDP in 2024) and rising public debt levels.
