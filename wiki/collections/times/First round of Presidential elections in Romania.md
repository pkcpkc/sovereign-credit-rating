# First round of Presidential elections in Romania

## Event Details

The first round of the Romanian Presidential elections took place in November 2024. However, the electoral process was subsequently invalidated due to identified irregularities. On December 6, 2024, the [[Constitutional Court of Romania]] officially canceled the results of the first round, citing these irregularities as grounds for annulment.

This cancellation triggered a requirement for repeated elections, which were held in May 2025.
