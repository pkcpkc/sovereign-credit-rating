# Surveillance of Investigative Journalist by National Anti-Corruption Directorate

## Event Details

On March 2025, the [[National Anti-Corruption Directorate]] engaged in the surveillance of an investigative journalist. This incident is cited within the 2025 [[Rule of Law]] Report for Romania as a specific example of the broader threats to media freedom and pluralism in the country.

The report highlights that despite some progress in judicial reforms, there are persistent challenges regarding the harassment and intimidation of journalists. The surveillance of this journalist contributes to a pattern of pressure on editorial independence, which includes strategic lawsuits against public participation (SLAPPs) and opaque financing of private media by political parties. This event underscores the lack of progress in ensuring the independent governance of public service media and the ongoing risks faced by the press in [[Romania]].
