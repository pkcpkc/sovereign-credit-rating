# Fall of Prime Minister Barnier's Government

## Event Details

On 12 December 2024, the French government led by Prime Minister Michel Barnier collapsed following a historic vote of no confidence (motion de censure) in the National Assembly. This event marked a significant political rupture, resulting in the earliest government fall in the Fifth Republic's history.

The collapse was driven by intense political fragmentation and lack of consensus within the parliament, where the ruling coalition failed to secure sufficient support for its budget measures. The government's inability to pass its budget led to the automatic adoption of a provisional draft via Article 49.3 of the Constitution, which triggered the no-confidence motion. The vote passed with 331 votes in favor, exceeding the required majority of 289.
