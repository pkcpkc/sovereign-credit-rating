# IMF Staff Report completed

## Event Details

On June 9, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) completed its [[Staff Report]] for the 2025 [[Article IV Consultation]] with [[Austria]]. This conclusion followed a mission conducted by IMF staff from March 26 to April 8, 2025, involving discussions with key Austrian officials, including [[Marterbauer]] (Minister of Finance), [[Holzmann]] (Central Bank Governor), and mission head [[Fletcher]].

The report assessed a challenging economic environment marked by two successive years of recession (2023 and 2024), with real GDP falling by 1.3% in 2024. The IMF highlighted risks including a fiscal deficit widening to 4.7% of GDP and public debt rising to 81% of GDP. The staff report provided recommendations for fiscal consolidation, including pension reforms and limits on public-sector wage increases, to address rising spending pressures from demographic aging, defense commitments, and the green transition.

This event preceded the final conclusion of the Article IV consultation by the IMF Executive Board on July 3, 2025. The completed staff report emphasized that while near-term growth remained weak due to fiscal consolidation and trade uncertainty, a recovery was projected from 2026 onwards, driven by unwinding household savings and monetary easing.
