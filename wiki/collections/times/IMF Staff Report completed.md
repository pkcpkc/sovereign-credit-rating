# IMF Staff Report completed

## Event Details

On June 9, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) completed its Staff Report regarding the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Austria**. This report followed a mission to Austria conducted between March 26 and April 8, 2025, during which IMF staff engaged with key stakeholders including the Ministry of Finance (represented by **Marterbauer**) and the Central Bank (led by **Holzmann**).

The report serves as a critical assessment of Austria's economic trajectory following two years of recession caused by energy shocks and monetary tightening. Key findings and milestones included in the report's timeline are:

*   **Economic Outlook:** Analysis of the 1.3% GDP contraction in 2024 and the projected near-zero growth for 2025, with a recovery expected in 2026.
*   **Fiscal Policy:** Evaluation of the government's consolidation plan to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP by addressing "climate bonuses" and green subsidies.
*   **Labor and Demographics:** Identification of structural reforms needed to increase labor supply, specifically targeting female and elderly participation to counter 20 years of demographic aging.
*   **Financial Stability:** Assessment of the banking sector's capital ratios (CET1 at 17.7%) against rising Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) in the commercial real estate sector.
*   **Governance:** Ongoing monitoring of Austria's AML/CFT framework mutual evaluation and efforts to reduce corporate "red tape."

The completion of this report preceded the final conclusion of the Article IV consultation by the IMF Executive Board on July 3, 2025.
