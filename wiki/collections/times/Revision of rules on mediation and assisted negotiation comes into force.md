# Revision of rules on mediation and assisted negotiation comes into force

## Event Details

On January 25, 2025, new rules regarding mediation and assisted negotiation came into force in Italy. These regulations were designed to encourage the use of alternative dispute resolution mechanisms as a means to reduce the overall volume of legal proceedings within the national justice system. This initiative is part of a broader effort to improve judicial efficiency and manage case backlogs.
