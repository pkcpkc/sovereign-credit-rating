# Revision of rules on mediation and assisted negotiation comes into force

## Event Details

On 25 January 2025, revised regulations governing mediation and assisted negotiation entered into force in Italy, marking a key component of the broader justice system reform implemented following the government's adoption of the legislative package in March 2024. This reform, which has been scrutinized by the [[High Council for the Judiciary]] (Consiglio Superiore della Magistratura), aims to enhance the efficiency of dispute resolution mechanisms and reduce the backlog in civil courts.

The update aligns with the European Commission's assessment in its 2025 [[Rule of Law Report]], which notes that while comprehensive justice system reforms are in place, challenges such as staff shortages and the need for further implementation measures persist. The revision of mediation rules is part of a wider strategy to improve judicial independence and effectiveness, complemented by other measures such as the approval of directives on magistrate performance appraisals and out-of-tenure appointments later in 2024. Despite these advancements, stakeholders continue to monitor the practical application of these reforms to ensure they contribute to reducing case backlogs and improving access to justice for citizens.
