# Mission discussions ended

## Event Details

On **April 8, 2025**, the IMF staff mission for the 2025 Article IV Consultation with Austria concluded its discussions in Vienna. The mission, which began on **March 26, 2025**, was led by Mr. Fletcher (Mission Head) and included staff members Mr. Mohommad, Ms. Pinat, and Mr. Saiyid. The discussions involved high-level officials including **Christian Karner Marterbauer** (Minister of Finance) and **Robert Holzmann** (Governor of the Oesterreichische Nationalbank).

### Key Discussion Points & Outcomes

The mission concluded amidst a challenging economic backdrop for Austria, characterized by two successive years of recession (2023 and 2024) and sluggish productivity growth. The primary focus of the discussions included:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation**: With the fiscal deficit widening to **4.7% of GDP** in 2024 and public debt rising to **81.2%**, the IMF emphasized the need for "bold" medium-term fiscal adjustment. The new coalition government (formed in March 2025) committed to near-term measures to reduce the deficit to 4.3% in 2025, though the IMF recommended further steps to bring the deficit below 2% to counter rising costs from demographic aging, defense, and the green transition.
*   **Inflation and Monetary Policy**: Headline inflation averaged **3.2%** in 2025, exceeding the Euro Area average due to sticky services inflation and the expiration of energy support measures. The mission noted that inflation was projected to converge toward 2% by 2026, supported by fiscal consolidation and wage moderation.
*   **Financial Sector Stability**: While Austrian banks remained well-capitalized (CET1 ratio of **17.7%**), significant attention was given to risks in the **Commercial Real Estate (CRE)** sector and rising Non-Performing Loans (NPLs). The IMF highlighted that borrower-based measures for residential mortgages were set to lapse in July 2025 and recommended enacting legislation to make these prudent lending standards permanent.
*   **Structural Reforms**: To address demographic headwinds and low potential growth (<1% medium-term), the IMF urged reforms to boost labor supply, particularly among women and the elderly. Other priorities included reducing regulatory barriers in services, speeding up renewable energy permits, and easing land-use regulations for housing.

### Mission Participants

*   **IMF Delegation**: Mr. Fletcher (Mission Head), Mr. Mohommad, Ms. Pinat, Mr. Saiyid.
*   **Austrian Officials**: Christian Karner Marterbauer (Minister of Finance), Robert Holzmann (Governor, OeNB).
*   **Oversight**: The broader consultation process was overseen by **Oya Celasun** (Deputy Director, IMF European Department) and **Bergljot Barkbu** (Director, IMF Strategy, Policy, and Review Department).
