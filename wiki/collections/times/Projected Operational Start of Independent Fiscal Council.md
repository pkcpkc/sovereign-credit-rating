# Projected Operational Start of Independent Fiscal Council

## Event Details

The operational start of Poland's Independent Fiscal Council is projected for **2026**. This institutional development is highlighted in the [International Monetary Fund (IMF)](wiki/assets/2026-07-05/sources/IMF (2025) Poland Country Report.md) 2024 Article IV Consultation Staff Report (released January 2025) as a critical measure to enhance fiscal accountability and governance.

The establishment of the council is part of broader institutional improvements aimed at strengthening the fiscal framework amidst significant macroeconomic challenges. Specifically:

*   **Context:** The initiative follows Poland's entry into the EU Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) and the adoption of a Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP) targeting a deficit reduction to below 3% of GDP by 2028.
*   **Purpose:** The council is intended to provide independent oversight and analysis of fiscal policy, addressing previous concerns regarding limited transparency of extrabudgetary funds and fiscal credibility.
*   **IMF Assessment:** The IMF Staff and Executive Board welcomed the plan to establish the independent fiscal council, noting it as a positive step toward enhanced accountability and institutional strength in the country's policymaking stability.

This event follows other key institutional milestones, including recent amendments to the stabilizing expenditure rule to align with the new EU fiscal framework and improved oversight of funds managed by the [Polish Development Bank (BGK)](wiki/assets/2026-07-05/sources/IMF (2025) Poland Country Report.md).
