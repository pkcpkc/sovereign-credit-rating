# Constitutional Court annuls subsidy provision excluding certain organizations

## Event Details

On 19 September 2024, the **Constitutional Court** of Belgium delivered a ruling concerning provisions related to public subsidies and civic space. The court annulled specific legislative clauses that excluded certain organizations from receiving subsidies, a move situated within broader concerns about the state of **Civic Space** in Belgium.

This ruling occurred against a backdrop where stakeholders had raised significant concerns about disproportionate measures against protesters and legislative obstacles restricting the **Right to Demonstrate**. While the government faced criticism for these restrictive measures, the Constitutional Court's intervention helped remove some of the most contentious legal barriers. However, reports indicate that despite this judicial correction, significant obstacles to free assembly and association remained in place. The decision highlights the ongoing tension between state security or administrative frameworks and the protection of fundamental democratic rights in Belgium.
