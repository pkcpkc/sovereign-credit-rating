# Deadline for uploading criminal court decisions

## Event Details

As part of the ongoing efforts to improve judicial transparency and digitalization in France, the government established a target for the digital accessibility of court records. While online access to over one million decisions was achieved by late 2024, a specific deadline of December 31, 2025, was set for the completion of uploading all criminal court decisions to the public platform. This initiative is part of a broader strategy to expand the *Portalis* tool and enhance the efficiency and accessibility of the French justice system.
