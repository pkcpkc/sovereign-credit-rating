# Concluding meeting with new DPM Jan Jambon

## Event Details

The chronological progression of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation with Belgium highlights a period of intense fiscal and structural review:

- **June 2024**: General elections in Belgium set the political stage for the new government's agenda.
- **September 2024**: Expiration of one-year government bonds marks a key point in the sovereign debt cycle.
- **December 2024**: Conclusion of the previous Article IV Consultation period.
- **January - February 2025**: A series of high-level discussions between the IMF mission, led by [[Jean-François Dauphin]], and Belgian authorities. This period culminated on **February 4, 2025**, with the end of formal discussions.
- **February 21, 2025**: A pivotal concluding meeting was held with the new Deputy Prime Minister of Finance and Pensions, [[Jan Jambon]], to finalize the scope of the consultation.
- **March 3, 2025**: The IMF officially completed the Staff Report, identifying critical concerns regarding automatic wage indexation and public debt sustainability.
- **March 18, 2025**: The IMF Executive Board concluded the consultation, reviewing the proposed 7-year adjustment plan.
- **March 20, 2025**: The official press release was issued, detailing the need for sustained fiscal consolidation and structural reforms to improve Total Factor Productivity (TFP) and labor market participation.
