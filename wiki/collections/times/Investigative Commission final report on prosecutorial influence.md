# Investigative Commission final report on prosecutorial influence

## Event Details

In July 2024, the Investigative Commission released its final report regarding prosecutorial influence. This report serves as a critical precursor to the broader judicial reforms outlined in the EU Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report for Austria. The findings contributed to the ongoing discourse regarding the need for a structurally independent Federal Prosecution Office and have informed the government's commitment to transition toward a collegiate body for prosecution, aiming to limit the current instructional powers of the Minister of Justice.
