# Adoption of the Fourteenth Amendment to the Fundamental Law

## Event Details

On December 1, 2024, Hungary officially adopted the Fourteenth Amendment to the Fundamental Law. This legislative action occurred within a broader period of significant judicial and structural reforms in the country. 

The amendment followed a series of developments including the signing of a four-party agreement on judicial salaries and reforms in November 2024. However, the subsequent implementation of these changes—specifically regarding judicial appointment rules and retirement age extensions (which took effect on January 1, 2025)—faced criticism from the National Judicial Council (NJC). The NJC withdrew from the agreement in early 2025, citing a lack of consultation on legislative amendments that raised the minimum age for judges to 35 and allowed service until age 70.
