# Completion of the IMF Staff Report

## Event Details

The IMF has completed Staff Reports for both Hungary and Romania, providing critical assessments of their respective economic trajectories and fiscal sustainability.

### Hungary
On July 25, 2025, the IMF completed its Staff Report following an Article IV Consultation mission in Budapest. The report highlights a "challenging juncture" characterized by stagnant output and persistent inflation above the 3% target. Key findings include:
- **Fiscal Policy:** The necessity of a structural primary surplus of approximately 1.75% of GDP to address elevated public debt (projected at 79% of GDP by 2030).
- **Monetary Policy:** A recommendation for the [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank]] (MNB) to maintain a restrictive stance into 2026.
- **Structural Reforms:** Focus on eliminating barriers to factor mobility, reducing state aid distortions, and transitioning from fossil fuel subsidies.
- **Risk Assessment:** Identification of "Moderate" sovereign stress risks due to geoeconomic fragmentation and corporate FX exposures.
- **Key Officials:** Discussions included [[Márton Nagy]] and [[Máté Lóga]]. This report serves as the basis for the Executive Board's consideration on August 29, 2025.

### Romania
On October 15, 2025, the IMF completed the Staff Report for Romania's Article IV Consultation. The report addresses "twin deficits" and identifies a "Negative Outlook" for growth and inflation. Key highlights include:
- **Fiscal Policy:** Evaluation of a consolidation package including VAT increases (to 21%) and public sector wage/pension freezes in 2026. The goal is to reduce the fiscal deficit below 6% of GDP in 2026 and below 3% by 2030.
- **Monetary Policy:** Support for the National Bank of Romania’s (NBR) cautious stance (maintaining rates at 6.5%) and a recommendation for increased two-way exchange rate flexibility.
- **Financial Sector:** Recognition of high capital adequacy (24.2%) but warnings regarding the "sovereign-bank nexus" (26% of bank assets in sovereign bonds) and unhedged FX loans.
- **Structural Reforms:** Emphasis on accelerating the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds and improving labor force participation, which remains below 70%.
- **Key Personnel:** The mission included Joong Shik Kang (Mission Lead), Ilie Bolojan, and Carlos Granados (Senior Resident Representative) interacting with Mugur Isarescu.
