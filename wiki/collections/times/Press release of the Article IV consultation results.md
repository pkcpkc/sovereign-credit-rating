# Press release of the Article IV consultation results

## Event Details

On November 14, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) released the results of the Article IV consultation for Romania. The press release detailed a cautious economic outlook characterized by subdued growth (0.9% in 2024) and elevated inflation (9.9% in September 2025).

Key findings presented in the release included:
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** Recognition of the government's 2025–26 reform package, including VAT rate increases to 21% and a freeze on public sector wages/pensions to reduce the fiscal deficit toward a 3% target by 2030.
- **Monetary Policy:** Support for the National Bank of Romania’s (NBR) decision to maintain interest rates at 6.5% to combat inflationary pressures from electricity price cap removals and tax hikes.
- **Financial Stability:** Acknowledgement of a robust banking sector (24.2% capital adequacy) while highlighting risks associated with the "sovereign-bank nexus" and unhedged FX loans.
- **Structural Reform:** Emphasis on the need to accelerate the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds and improve labor force participation, which remains below 70%.
