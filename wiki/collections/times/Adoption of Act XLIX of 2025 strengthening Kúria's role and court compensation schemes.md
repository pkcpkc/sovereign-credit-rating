# Adoption of Act XLIX of 2025 strengthening Kúria's role and court compensation schemes

## Event Details

Adopted in June 2025, Act XLIX of 2025 represents a significant legislative intervention in the Hungarian judicial system, focusing on two primary areas: the strengthening of the role of the [[Kúria]] (Supreme Court) and the introduction or adjustment of court compensation schemes. This legislation is situated within a broader context identified by the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report, which notes a systemic deterioration in judicial independence and legal certainty.

Key aspects of the legislative environment surrounding Act XLIX include:

- **Judicial Structure and Independence**: The report highlights that the [[National Judicial Council]] continues to exercise powers acquired through 2023 reforms, but consultation processes are often deemed ineffective. The Act aligns with broader trends where structural reforms to ensure independence are lacking, despite increases in judicial salaries.
- **Impact on Legal Certainty**: The adoption of such acts, alongside frequent legislative changes and the extensive use of emergency powers (such as the "state of danger"), contributes to an environment of legal uncertainty. This uncertainty affects the single market and investor confidence, particularly regarding foreign companies in strategic sectors.
- **Context of Judicial Pressure**: The legislation operates against a backdrop of undue pressure on judges, including public criticism from the [[Kúria President]] and smear campaigns. The report notes that judicial associations argue that recent changes, including the Fourteenth Amendment to the Fundamental Law (which raised the minimum age for judges and extended retirement), compromise independence.

This event is part of a series of judicial and political developments in 2025, including the implementation of new judicial appointment criteria and the election of a new [[Prosecutor General]] following the Fifteenth Amendment.
