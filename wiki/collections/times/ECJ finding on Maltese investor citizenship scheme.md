# ECJ finding on Maltese investor citizenship scheme

## Event Details

On April 29, 2025, the European Court of Justice (ECJ) issued a significant ruling declaring that Malta's investor citizenship scheme is contrary to EU law. This finding highlights ongoing tensions regarding the integrity of Malta's institutional checks and balances, specifically concerning the legal framework surrounding citizenship-for-investment programs.
