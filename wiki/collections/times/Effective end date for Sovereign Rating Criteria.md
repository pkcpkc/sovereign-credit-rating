# Effective end date for Sovereign Rating Criteria

## Event Details

**April 27, 2026** marks the **effective end date** for the **[[Fitch Ratings Sovereign Rating Criteria]]** that came into force on **September 15, 2025**. This criteria document served as the master methodology for assigning and maintaining sovereign credit ratings during its validity period, distinguishing between Local-Currency (LC) and Foreign-Currency (FC) Issuer Default Ratings (IDRs).

During this period, **[[Fitch Ratings]]** utilized a framework based on four analytical pillars: **Structural Features**, **Macroeconomic Performance/Policies/Prospects**, **Public Finances**, and **External Finances**. Key characteristics of this criteria version included:

*   **Quantitative Modeling**: The **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM)** relied on an Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression of 18 variables, with **Structural Features** carrying the highest weight (53.7%), followed by Public Finances (19.1%), External Finances (17.3%), and Macroeconomic Performance (9.8%).
*   **Qualitative Overlay (QO)**: Adjustments were allowed for factors not captured by the SRM, with a general cap of ±3 notches from the model output, unless crisis conditions applied.
*   **Climate Risk Integration**: The criteria introduced **Sovereign Climate Vulnerability Signals (Climate.VS)** as a screening tool. Sovereigns with a Climate.VS score of 50 or higher in 2035 were subject to additional analysis regarding physical and transition risks.
*   **Recovery Ratings**: For issuers rated 'B+' or below, **Recovery Ratings (RRs)** were incorporated to assess potential loss severity in default scenarios.

This period concluded with the expiration of these specific methodological guidelines on April 27, 2026, following the previous criteria effective from October 24, 2024.
