# Publication of 2025 Country Report for Poland

## Event Details

The economic and social trajectory of Poland is marked by significant fiscal adjustments and structural transitions. In **2022**, the country introduced the PIT-0 tax relief for senior citizens, part of a broader social policy framework. By **January 2023**, the Council initiated an excessive deficit procedure for Poland due to a deficit reaching 6.6% of GDP.

In response, the Council endorsed Poland’s medium-term fiscal-structural plan in **January 2025**, following the submission of an annual progress report on **April 30, 2025**. This plan aims to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2028. Economic performance showed resilience in **2024** with a GDP growth of 2.9%, with projections for **2025** suggesting growth will surpass that figure, driven by high private consumption and investment.

The labor market reached a historic low unemployment rate of 2.9% in **2024**, hitting the **2030** target for an employment rate of 78.3% ahead of schedule. However, long-term challenges remain regarding an aging population and a shrinking working-age demographic.

Looking forward, the Fiscal Council is set to become fully operational in **January 2026**. Poland continues to work toward its long-term sustainability goals, specifically aiming for climate neutrality by **2050** while navigating the challenges of decarbonizing its heavy industry and energy grid.
