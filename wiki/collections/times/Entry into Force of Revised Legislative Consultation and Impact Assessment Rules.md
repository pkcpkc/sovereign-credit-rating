# Entry into Force of Revised Legislative Consultation and Impact Assessment Rules

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, the revised [[Legislative Consultation and Impact Assessment Rules]] entered into force in [[Poland]]. This measure was implemented as part of the government's Action Plan on Rule of Law implementation, aiming to strengthen checks and balances within the legislative process.

While the reforms represent a positive step in improving institutional frameworks, stakeholders have noted that recommendations from the [[Ombudsperson]] are not always fully reflected in the final adopted legislation. This event is part of a broader effort by the [[Government of Poland]] under Prime Minister [[Donald Tusk]] to address long-standing rule of law concerns, alongside other major reforms such as the separation of the [[Minister of Justice]] and [[Prosecutor General]] roles, and the operationalization of the [[European Public Prosecutor Office]]. Despite these administrative improvements, perceived judicial independence remains low, and tensions with the [[Constitutional Tribunal]] continue to pose significant political and legal challenges.
