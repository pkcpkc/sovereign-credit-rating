# Projected Real GDP growth of 2.2%

## Event Details

As part of the economic outlook for Romania, the country is projected to see a Real GDP growth of 2.2% in 2026. This projection follows a period of significant economic fluctuation, including a deceleration to 0.8% in 2024 caused by weak net exports and high inflation pressures. The growth target is set against a backdrop of structural reforms aimed at transitioning Romania from a low-cost labor model to an innovation-driven economy, addressing high fiscal deficits, and improving R&D intensity.
