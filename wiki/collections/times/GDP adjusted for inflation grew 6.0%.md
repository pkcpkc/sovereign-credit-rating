# GDP adjusted for inflation grew 6.0%

## Event Details

In 2024, Malta's economy demonstrated significant momentum, with real GDP growth reaching 6.0% (adjusted for inflation). This growth occurred alongside a high employment rate of 83.0%, though the economy simultaneously faced systemic challenges including acute labor shortages in the ICT, construction, and healthcare sectors. 

The growth period was characterized by a transition toward fiscal consolidation, with the general government deficit projected to decrease from 3.7% in 2024 to 2.8% by 2026, while the debt-to-GDP ratio was expected to stabilize below 50%. Despite this expansion, the report highlighted significant structural hurdles, including very low R&D intensity (0.64% of GDP) and high regulatory burdens, which remain key areas for reform in the coming years.
