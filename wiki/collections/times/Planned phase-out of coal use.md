# Planned phase-out of coal use

## Event Details

As part of France's broader energy transition and decarbonization strategy, a planned phase-out of coal use is scheduled to take effect on **2027-01-01**. 

This move aligns with the country's efforts to maintain its high share of low-carbon electricity (currently at 95%) and achieve broader sustainability goals. The transition is a critical component of addressing structural hurdles in the energy mix, aiming to reduce carbon emissions while navigating the challenges of administrative complexity and the need for significant investment in renewable energy infrastructure.
