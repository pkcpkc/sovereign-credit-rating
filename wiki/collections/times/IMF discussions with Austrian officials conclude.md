# IMF discussions with Austrian officials conclude

## Event Details

On April 8, 2025, the IMF mission to Austria concluded its formal discussions with key government officials. The mission, led by the head of the mission team [[Fletcher]], involved extensive consultations with the Ministry of Finance, represented by [[Marterbauer]], and the Central Bank, represented by [[Holzmann]].

The discussions focused on Austria's economic trajectory following a period of recession triggered by energy shocks and monetary tightening. Key areas of dialogue included:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** The government's strategy to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP, including the potential abolition of the "climate bonus" and reductions in green subsidies.
*   **Labor Market Reforms:** Strategies to mitigate demographic headwinds by increasing labor force participation among women and the elderly.
*   **Financial Stability:** Addressing risks in the commercial real estate (CRE) sector and maintaining the banking sector's strong capital ratios.
*   **Governance:** Progress on the mutual evaluation of Austria's AML/CFT framework and efforts to reduce regulatory "red tape" in the services and housing sectors.

These discussions served as the foundation for the subsequent IMF Staff Report and the final Article IV consultation concluded by the Executive Board in July 2025.
