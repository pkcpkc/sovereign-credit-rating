# Submission of the 2026 budget law to Parliament

## Event Details

On May 6, 2024, the 2026 budget law was submitted to Parliament as part of Hungary's ongoing fiscal planning. This submission occurred within a broader economic context characterized by high public debt (reaching 73.5% of GDP in 2024) and significant debt-servicing costs, which are among the highest in the European Union.

The budgetary framework surrounding this submission has been noted for a lack of transparency and reliability, primarily due to frequent revisions and the absence of multiannual spending ceilings. While the budget reflects a period of slow economic recovery—with GDP growth forecast to reach 2.5% in 2026—it also faces pressures from elevated debt-servicing costs and an expansionary budgetary bias. The fiscal outlook remains complex, as net expenditure growth in 2025 was forecast to exceed Council recommendations, despite remaining within cumulative limits due to national escape clauses for defense spending.
