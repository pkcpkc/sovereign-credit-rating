# Presentation of the New Government Programme Committing to Judicial Reforms

## Event Details

In March 2025, the newly formed Austrian Government presented its official programme, which explicitly committed to fundamental judicial reforms. A central pillar of this agenda was the establishment of an independent Federal Prosecution Office, a key recommendation from the 2025 Rule of Law Report to reduce executive oversight and ensure prosecutorial independence. While the Government Programme signaled strong political will, specific draft legislation remained pending at the time of publication, leaving current structures where the Minister of Justice retains the ability to instruct prosecutors in individual cases.

This commitment aligns with broader efforts to strengthen judicial independence and anti-corruption frameworks in Austria, addressing perceived politicization in high-level appointments and enhancing the institutional capacity of the justice system.
