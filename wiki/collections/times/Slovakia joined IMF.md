# Slovakia joined IMF

## Event Details

Slovakia's engagement with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) has been marked by a series of critical fiscal and economic milestones. Following its entry into the IMF, the country has faced significant scrutiny regarding its fiscal trajectory and economic resilience.

In July 2024, the European Council initiated an Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) for Slovakia, highlighting concerns over high fiscal deficits. By December 2024, Slovakia submitted its 5th payment request under the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRP).

The most recent major milestone occurred in early 2025 during the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation**. This period involved intensive staff discussions beginning on January 28, 2025, leading to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on March 3, 2025. The process culminated on March 24, 2025, when the IMF Executive Board concluded the consultation.

The resulting assessment highlights a recovery phase with 2.0% growth in 2024, tempered by structural challenges such as an aging population and low productivity. The IMF has recommended a rigorous 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) to reduce the headline deficit, with specific focus on:
- Reducing expenditure and subsidies.
- Strengthening the national anti-corruption strategy.
- Implementing structural reforms to address labor force shrinkage.
- Managing vulnerabilities in the residential real estate market.
