# Slovakia joined IMF

## Event Details

Slovakia's engagement with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and its subsequent economic oversight progressed through several key milestones in 2024 and 2025:

- **January 1, 2024**: Slovakia officially joined the IMF.
- **October 1, 2024**: A change in government occurred, influencing the subsequent economic dialogue.
- **December 4, 2024**: Virtual IMF discussions commenced to review the Slovak Republic's economic standing.
- **January 28, 2025**: IMF discussions with Slovak officials concluded.
- **March 3, 2025**: The IMF Staff Report was completed, highlighting a recovering economy with 2.0% growth in 2024 and identifying structural headwinds such as geoeconomic fragmentation and low productivity.
- **March 18, 2025**: The IMF Executive Board began consideration of the staff report.
- **March 24, 2025**: The IMF Executive Board concluded the Article IV consultation.

The consultation emphasized Slovakia's commitment to a 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP in 2025 and stabilizing public debt. Key focus areas included financial sector resilience, the need for expenditure-based fiscal rationalization, and structural reforms to address labor productivity and demographic challenges.
