# Law abrogating the offence of abuse of public office entered into force

## Event Details

In August 2024, a law formally abrogating the offence of "abuse of public office" (*abuso d'ufficio*) entered into force in Italy. This legislative change was part of broader reforms affecting the anti-corruption framework and the justice system. The validity of this abolition was subsequently confirmed by the [[Constitutional Court (Italy)]], which declared the law compliant with the Constitution in May 2025. The European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report on Italy notes this compliance validation as a strength in the policy credibility and institutional strength category, while observing that legislative gaps remain in other areas such as lobbying regulation and political party financing transparency. The entry into force followed earlier legislative steps, including the adoption of legislative decrees implementing the reform of the justice system in March 2024.
