# Effective date of Sovereign Rating Criteria

## Event Details

On **September 15, 2025**, Fitch Ratings officially implemented the updated **Master Criteria for Sovereign and Central Bank Issuers**. This update established a transparent, multi-pillared framework for assigning and maintaining **Issuer Default Ratings (IDR)**. 

The criteria introduced a dual-component methodology:
- **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM):** A quantitative multiple regression OLS model utilizing 18 variables, with **Structural Features** (53.7%) as the primary weight.
- **Qualitative Overlay (QO):** A mechanism allowing analysts to adjust ratings by up to +/- 3 notches to account for qualitative factors such as political stability, policy credibility, and fiscal sustainability.

Key components of this effective period include:
- **Default Protocols:** Formalized procedures for moving sovereigns to 'RD' (Restructured/Default) status and the application of 30-day grace periods for Payment Force Majeure.
- **Currency Differentiation:** Specific mapping for Local-Currency (LC) vs. Foreign-Currency (FC) IDRs based on debt distribution and **Reserve-Currency Flexibility (RCF)**.
- **Climate Risk Screening:** The integration of the **Climate.VS** framework to identify physical and transition risks, with specific scrutiny thresholds set for the year 2035.
- **Recovery Ratings (RR):** Implementation of RR1-RR6 for sovereigns rated 'B+' or below to provide clarity on creditor recoveries.

This framework remains the active standard until the next scheduled review period in April 2026.
