# New appointments to ORF management bodies

## Event Details

In March 2025, new appointments were made to the management bodies of the Austrian public service broadcaster, ORF. These appointments were part of a reform of ORF's management structure, which followed a ruling by the Austrian Constitutional Court aimed at enhancing the broadcaster's independence.

This development was noted in the **2025 Rule of Law Report on Austria** issued by the European Commission. While the report highlighted that the media regulator **KommAustria** operates independently, it emphasized that the economic situation for private media operators was deteriorating due to shifts in advertising revenue. Regarding the public broadcaster, the Commission acknowledged the structural reforms but raised concerns regarding the fair distribution of state advertising, noting that state advertising spending had more than doubled to EUR 417.9 million in 2024, leading to scrutiny over political proximity and distribution fairness.
