# New appointments to ORF management bodies

## Event Details

In June 2025, new appointments were made to the management bodies of the Austrian public broadcaster, ORF. This occurred following a reform of the ORF Act in March 2025, which was prompted by a Constitutional Court ruling aimed at increasing the independence of the broadcaster's governance structures.
