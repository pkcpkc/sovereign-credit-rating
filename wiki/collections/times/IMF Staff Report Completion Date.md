# IMF Staff Report Completion Date

## Event Details

On **December 16, 2024**, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) completed the staff report for its 2024 Article IV Consultation with the Republic of Poland. This date marks the conclusion of the technical assessment phase conducted by the IMF Mission led by **Jan Kees Martijn**, with staff members including **Kareem Ismail**, **Alla Myrvoda**, and **Yang Yang**.

The completion of the report followed discussions held in Warsaw starting October 17, 2024. The finalized staff assessment highlighted a robust economic recovery in Poland, with growth accelerating to an estimated 2.8% in 2024, driven by strong nominal wage growth, rebounding private consumption, and the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. Projections for 2025-2026 suggest further growth of 3.5%, supported by public investment and private consumption.

Key findings from the completed report emphasized significant fiscal challenges, noting a widened deficit of 5.9% of GDP in 2024 due to permanent increases in public sector wages, social benefits, and high defense spending. The IMF recommended frontloading fiscal consolidation to rebuild buffers, aiming for a deficit of 2.9% of GDP by 2028 through measures including VAT reforms and pension adjustments. The report also assessed the financial system as resilient, though noting litigation risks related to legacy CHF mortgages, and advised that monetary policy should remain tight until inflation firmly declines toward the 2.5% target.

This event precedes the scheduled IMF Executive Board discussion on January 10, 2025, and the final conclusion of the Article IV Consultation on January 21, 2025.
