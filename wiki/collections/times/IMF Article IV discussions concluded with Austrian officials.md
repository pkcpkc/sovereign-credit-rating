# IMF Article IV discussions concluded with Austrian officials

## Event Details

On 8 April 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) concluded its Article IV consultation discussions with Austrian officials. This event marked the end of a mission conducted by an IMF staff team from 26 March to 8 April 2025, during which they engaged with key Austrian stakeholders including [[Marterbauer]] (Minister of Finance), [[Holzmann]] (Central Bank Governor), and various government bodies, financial institutions, and private-sector representatives. The mission was led by [[Fletcher]] alongside IMF staff members [[Oya Celasun]] and [[Bergljot Barkbu]].

The discussions took place in the context of Austria’s challenging economic environment, characterized by two successive years of recession (2023 and 2024) driven by energy price shocks, monetary tightening, and global trade uncertainty. The IMF assessed that while near-term growth remained weak, a recovery was projected from 2026 onwards. Key topics of discussion included the need for substantial medium-term fiscal adjustment to offset rising spending pressures from demographic aging, defense commitments, and the green transition. The IMF emphasized the importance of maintaining financial stability, noting the banking sector's strong capitalization (CET1 ratio of 17.7%) despite risks from Commercial Real Estate exposures and regional spillovers.

The culmination of the consultation process occurred later in the year, with the IMF Staff Report completed on 9 June 2025 and the IMF Executive Board formally concluding the Article IV Consultation on 3 July 2025.
