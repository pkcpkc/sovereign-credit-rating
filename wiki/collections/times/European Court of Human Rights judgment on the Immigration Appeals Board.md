# European Court of Human Rights judgment on the Immigration Appeals Board

## Event Details

On 22 October 2024, the [[European Court of Human Rights]] issued a judgment concerning the [[Immigration Appeals Board]] in [[Malta]]. This ruling highlighted significant concerns regarding the independence of specialized tribunals within the Maltese judicial system. The judgment served as a catalyst for subsequent government proposals aimed at removing executive involvement in the appointment of members to such tribunals, addressing identified weaknesses in institutional safeguards. This event is part of broader ongoing constitutional reforms in [[Malta]] aimed at strengthening judicial independence and ensuring the rule of law, as noted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report.
