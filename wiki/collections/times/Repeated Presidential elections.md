# Repeated Presidential elections

## Event Details

The Romanian presidential election cycle was marked by significant legal and procedural complexities. In November 2024, the first round of Presidential elections took place but was subsequently annulled by the Constitutional Court. This legal intervention necessitated a restructuring of the electoral process, leading to the repeated Presidential elections held in May 2025. These events highlight the ongoing judicial oversight and the volatile legal landscape surrounding national leadership transitions in Romania.
