# Executive Board consideration of staff report

## Event Details

On March 18, 2025, the IMF Executive Board considered the Staff Report regarding the Slovak Republic's 2025 Article IV Consultation. This review followed the completion of the Staff Report on March 3, 2025, and served as a critical step in the consultation process that concluded on March 24, 2025.

The consideration focused on Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024. The Board reviewed the government's ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP in 2025 and stabilizing public debt. Key points of discussion included:

- **Fiscal Strategy:** The need for expenditure-based rationalization, such as reducing subsidies and targeting social spending, to ensure long-term debt sustainability amidst aging-related costs.
- **Financial Stability:** Assessment of the banking system's resilience, high capital ratios, and the potential impacts of the new Financial Transaction Tax (FTT).
- **Structural Reforms:** Recommendations to counter "secular decline" in labor productivity through intensified Active Labor Market Policies (ALMPs), increased R&D spending, and improved governance to combat corruption.
