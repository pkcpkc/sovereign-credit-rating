# Conclusion of IMF Staff Discussions with Hungarian Officials

## Event Details

On June 17, 2025, the IMF staff concluded their mission in Budapest following a series of discussions with Hungarian officials, including [[Márton Nagy]] (Minister of National Economy) and [[Barnabás Virág]] (Deputy Central Bank Governor). The discussions focused on the "challenging juncture" of the Hungarian economy, marked by stagnant output and inflation exceeding the 3% target.

Key outcomes and points of discussion included:

*   **Economic Outlook:** The mission addressed the need for a recovery plan to move from a 0.7% projected growth in 2025 to 2.0% in 2026, while navigating risks from geoeconomic fragmentation and high refinancing costs.
*   **Fiscal Adjustment:** The IMF emphasized a required cumulative fiscal adjustment of approximately 2% of GDP between 2025 and 2028 to bring the headline deficit below 3% by 2027.
*   **Monetary Policy:** Discussions centered on maintaining a restrictive base rate (currently 6.5%) to anchor inflation expectations and addressing vulnerabilities in corporate FX exposures and the sovereign-bank nexus.
*   **Structural Reform Priorities:** The staff highlighted the necessity of removing barriers to factor mobility, improving governance to unlock suspended EU funds, and phasing out unsustainable distortions such as price caps and retail energy subsidies.

The mission's findings were synthesized into the 2025 Article IV Consultation, with the final report being completed on July 25, 2025, and presented to the IMF Executive Board on August 29, 2025.
