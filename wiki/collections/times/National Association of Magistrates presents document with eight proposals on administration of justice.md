# National Association of Magistrates presents document with eight proposals on administration of justice

## Event Details

On 5 March 2025, the [[National Association of Magistrates]] submitted a formal document outlining eight proposals regarding the administration of justice in Italy. This initiative occurred amidst broader judicial reforms, including the High Council for the Judiciary's approval of directives on out-of-tenure appointments, magistrate performance appraisals, and the Code of Judicial Management. The proposals were presented during a period of significant tension regarding the constitutional reform separating the careers of judges and prosecutors, which the judiciary viewed with skepticism. The National Association of Magistrates' intervention contributed to the ongoing debate on enhancing judicial independence, staff resources, and procedural efficiency within the Italian legal system.
