# Deadline for submitting draft law for Public Consultation Department and National Human Rights Institution

## Event Details

On **2025-07-01**, the deadline arrived for the Maltese government to submit draft legislation regarding the establishment of a **Public Consultation Department** and a **National Human Rights Institution** (NHRI). 

This deadline was highlighted in the European Commission’s *2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Malta* as a key milestone within the **Checks and Balances** pillar. The report notes that, as of the reporting period, no formal framework for public participation in the legislative process had yet been introduced. While the creation of a Public Consultation Department was planned to address this gap, the specific draft laws remained pending submission.

Simultaneously, the report indicates that draft legislation for the National Human Rights Institution remained "unbundled" in Parliament, signaling limited progress in establishing this independent body despite ongoing constitutional reforms aimed at enhancing institutional accountability and justice sector independence. The delay in these submissions was cited as part of broader concerns regarding the narrowing of civil society space and limited mechanisms for citizen engagement in the legislative process.

The European Commission emphasized that effective implementation of these institutions is crucial for strengthening checks and balances, ensuring compliance with EU standards on public participation, and providing robust protection for human rights defenders.
