# Publication of previous S&P Sovereign Rating Methodology

## Event Details

On December 23, 2014, [[S&P Global Ratings]] published the previous version of its Sovereign Rating Methodology. This framework served as the standard for assessing the creditworthiness of sovereign governments and monetary authorities until it was superseded by the 2017 update on December 18, 2017.

The 2014 methodology provided the foundational structure for evaluating sovereign credit risk, which was later refined in the 2017 iteration to include stricter caps on local-currency rating uplifts and a shift toward net debt metrics. The publication of this 2014 methodology followed earlier foundational documents, including the *Principles of Credit Ratings* (2011) and criteria for lower investment-grade ratings (2012). It preceded the *Monetary Authorities Rating Methodology* (2013), which was subsequently integrated into the comprehensive 2017 Sovereign Rating Methodology.
