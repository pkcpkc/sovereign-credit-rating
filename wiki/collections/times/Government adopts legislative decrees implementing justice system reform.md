# Government adopts legislative decrees implementing justice system reform

## Event Details

In March 2024, the Italian government adopted legislative decrees aimed at implementing comprehensive reforms to the justice system. These measures were part of a broader effort to enhance judicial efficiency and modernize legal procedures. 

The reforms are situated within a period of significant legislative activity in Italy, which includes subsequent developments such as:
- **September 2024:** The High Council for the Judiciary approved directives regarding out-of-tenure appointments.
- **November 2024:** Approval of directives on magistrates' performance appraisals.
- **December 2024:** The approval of the Code of Judicial Management.
- **January 2025:** The High Council for the Judiciary issued a critical opinion on constitutional reform, followed by the Chamber of Deputies approving a draft to separate the careers of judges and prosecutors.
- **January 2025:** New rules for mediation and assisted negotiation came into force to reduce legal backlogs.

These legislative actions align with the broader goals of improving judicial independence—which saw a rise in public perception to 46% in 2025—and advancing the digitalization of various court branches.
