# Publication of amended House Rules in Belgian Official Gazette

## Event Details

On 2025-04-30, the amended House Rules were officially published in the Belgian Official Gazette. This development occurs within the broader context of the 2025 Rule of Law assessment for Belgium, which highlights ongoing efforts to improve judicial independence and structural resource management. The publication follows a period of significant legislative activity, including the adoption and sanctioning of whistleblower protection laws in early 2025 and the government's ongoing response to digitalization criticisms from the Court of Audit.
