# Deadline for implementation of RRP reforms

## Event Details

On April 30, 2025, France faces the deadline for the implementation of reforms related to the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP). This milestone is part of a broader economic context where France's growth is projected to slow to 0.6% in 2025 due to political uncertainty and restrictive monetary policy.

The deadline coincides with critical fiscal sustainability goals, as the country works to manage a public debt-to-GDP ratio projected to reach 116% by the end of 2025. The reforms are intended to address structural hurdles, including the need for aggressive fiscal consolidation, administrative simplification (such as the "tell us once" principle), and enhanced investment in R&D and renewable energy infrastructure to maintain long-term competitiveness within the [[European Commission]] framework.
