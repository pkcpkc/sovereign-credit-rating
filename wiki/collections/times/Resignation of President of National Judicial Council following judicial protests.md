# Resignation of President of National Judicial Council following judicial protests

## Event Details

On December 3, 2024, the President of the [[National Judicial Council (Hungary)]] resigned amid widespread judicial protests in Hungary. The resignation was a direct consequence of intense pressure on the judiciary and contentious reforms proposed by the government. These reforms included raising the minimum age requirement for judges to 35 and allowing service until age 70, measures that were implemented with an ineffective consultation process involving the Judicial Council.

The resignation marked a significant escalation in the tension between the Hungarian government and the judiciary, occurring shortly before the [[National Judicial Council (Hungary)]] withdrew from a four-party agreement on judicial reforms on January 15, 2025. The European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report cited this event as evidence of ongoing concerns regarding judicial independence, noting that the resignation reflected the pressure on judges and the lack of adequate dialogue regarding structural changes to the justice system.
