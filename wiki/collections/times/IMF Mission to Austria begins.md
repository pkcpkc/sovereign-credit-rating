# IMF Mission to Austria begins

## Event Details

The IMF mission to Austria commenced on March 26, 2025, as part of the Article IV consultation process. The mission was led by a team headed by [[Fletcher]], with key participants including [[Mohommad]], [[Oya Celasun]], [[Bergljot Barkbu]], [[Pinat]], [[Saiyid]], and [[Just]]. 

The primary objective of the mission was to assess Austria's economic landscape following a period of recession driven by energy shocks and monetary tightening. During the consultation period (March 26 – April 8, 2025), the IMF team engaged with key Austrian officials, including [[Marterbauer]] (Minister of Finance) and [[Holzmann]] (Central Bank).

Key discussion points included:
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** Evaluating the government's plan to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP through measures such as abolishing the "climate bonus" and cutting green subsidies.
- **Economic Outlook:** Addressing the 1.3% GDP contraction in 2024 and the projected near-zero growth for 2025, with a focus on recovery strategies for 2026.
- **Labor and Demographics:** Strategies to narrow the gap in female and elderly labor force participation to offset 20 years of demographic aging.
- **Financial Stability:** Monitoring risks associated with the commercial real estate (CRE) sector and Non-Performing Loans (NPLs).
- **Governance:** Reviewing the mutual evaluation of Austria's AML/CFT framework and efforts to improve corporate transparency.
