# VAT Rate Hike and Excise Increases Implementation

## Event Details

In August 2025, the Romanian government implemented a significant fiscal consolidation package, primarily characterized by a hike in the Value Added Tax (VAT) rates and increases to excise taxes. This policy decision was a core component of the comprehensive fiscal reform package adopted by the new coalition government in July 2025, aiming to reduce the fiscal deficit from 8.7 percent of GDP in 2024 to below 6 percent in 2026.

The VAT rate increase, combined with the removal of electricity price caps, acted as a primary driver for inflationary pressures, causing headline inflation to spike to 9.9 percent in September 2025. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) noted that these measures were necessary for restoring fiscal and macroeconomic sustainability, though they contributed to a temporary surge in prices. The National Bank of Romania (NBR) responded with a cautious monetary policy stance, maintaining the policy rate at 6.5 percent and pausing further cuts until inflation showed a firm downward trend. The IMF mission head, Joong Shik Kang, and the Executive Board welcomed these reforms as a critical step toward debt sustainability, despite risks regarding the execution of the consolidation plan and its social impact.
