# Executive Board Consideration of Italy Staff Report

## Event Details

On July 18, 2025, the IMF Executive Board met to consider the Staff Report regarding Italy's Article IV Consultation. This session followed a period of intensive dialogue, which began with staff discussions in Rome on May 14, 2025, and concluded with official meetings between IMF staff and Italian officials on May 28, 2025.

The Board reviewed a report finalized on July 1, 2025, which highlighted Italy's economic resilience, including a return to a primary surplus and record employment levels. However, the discussion also centered on critical structural headwinds such as weak productivity growth, significant demographic challenges (aging population), and the need for continued fiscal consolidation to manage public debt. 

Key policy recommendations discussed included:
- Achieving a primary surplus target of 3% by 2027.
- Enhancing labor force participation, particularly among women, through childcare expansion.
- Maintaining momentum on the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) milestones.
- Addressing regional disparities and improving R&D investment to combat long-term productivity malaise.

The consideration of this report served as the final formal review before the public release of the Executive Board's conclusions on July 22, 2025.
