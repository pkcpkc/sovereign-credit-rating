# Implementation of court fee increases (approx. 23%)

## Event Details

On April 1, 2025, Austria implemented a significant average increase of approximately 23% in court fees. This measure was highlighted in the [[European Commission]]'s [[2025 Rule of Law Report: Austria]] as a factor potentially affecting access to justice. The report noted that high court fees remained a challenge for litigants despite the overall high efficiency of the justice system.

Concurrently, the legal landscape regarding financial barriers to justice was evolving. In October 2024, the [[Austrian Constitutional Court]] had ruled that previous restrictions on legal aid within the Administrative Procedural Code were unconstitutional. This ruling mandated that legal aid must be available for all administrative court proceedings. While the old rules restricting this access were scheduled for suspension by March 31, 2026, the implementation of the fee increase in early 2025 preceded the full operationalization of these expanded legal aid protections, raising considerations regarding the immediate impact on access to the judicial system.
