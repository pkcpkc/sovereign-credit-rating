# Belgian Federal Elections

## Event Details

The Belgian Federal Elections took place in June 2024, resulting in a new government formation. These elections served as the precursor to the subsequent IMF 2025 Article IV consultation, which involved key figures from the newly formed administration, including [[Jan Jambon]] (Deputy Prime Minister) and others such as [[De Croo]], [[Wunsch]], [[Van Peteghem]], [[Bertrand]], and [[Sven Gatz]]. The political transition following the elections set the stage for the fiscal consolidation and structural reforms discussed in the 2025 IMF report.
