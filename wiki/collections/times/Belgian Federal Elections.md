# Belgian Federal Elections

## Event Details

The **Belgian Federal Elections** took place in **June 2024**, marking a significant political transition in Belgium. The electoral outcome led to a period of protracted government formation, characteristic of the country's complex coalition dynamics.

Following the elections, negotiations resulted in the **formation of a new Belgian Federal Government in February 2025**. This new administration, including **Jan Jambon** as Deputy Prime Minister, established a clear agenda focused on structural reforms and fiscal consolidation. The formation of this government was a key milestone referenced in the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation**, which noted the new government's commitment to a seven-year fiscal adjustment plan under the new EU Economic Governance Framework (EGF). The IMF's staff held their concluding virtual meeting with Deputy PM Jan Jambon on **February 21, 2025**, shortly after the government's formation, to discuss these priorities and the country's economic outlook.
