# Forecasted GDP growth of 1.5%

## Event Details

In the 2025 economic outlook for Slovakia, the GDP growth is forecasted at 1.5% for the year 2025. This projection is influenced by several macroeconomic factors, including trade tensions and high inflation, which is expected to rise to 4.0% in 2025. 

This growth forecast is part of a broader economic context where Slovakia's economy grew by 2.1% in 2024, driven primarily by household and public consumption. However, the 1.5% forecast for 2025 reflects a cooling trend, further followed by a projected 1.4% growth in 2026. The economic outlook is further complicated by a persistent shortage of skilled labor, an innovation gap compared to other EU countries, and high government deficits.
