# Polish GDP growth of 2.9%

## Event Details

In 2024, Poland's economy recorded a gross domestic product (GDP) growth rate of 2.9%, positioning it as one of the fastest-growing economies within the [[European Union]]. This expansion was primarily driven by strong private consumption and investment, including investments funded by the [[European Union]]. Despite this robust economic performance, the country faced structural challenges such as competitiveness issues, an ageing population, and fiscal pressures. The general government deficit reached 5.3% of GDP in 2023, which exceeded the reference value and contributed to the initiation of an excessive deficit procedure by the Council in January 2025.
