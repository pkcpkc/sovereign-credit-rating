# Polish GDP growth of 2.9%

## Event Details

In **2024**, Poland's economy demonstrated significant resilience, recording a GDP growth of 2.9%. This expansion was primarily driven by robust private consumption and substantial investment activity, positioning Poland as one of the fastest-growing economies within the European Union. 

Despite this growth, the economic landscape is characterized by several structural tensions:
- **Labor Market Dynamics:** While growth was supported by a historic low unemployment rate of 2.9% in 2024, the economy faces a shrinking working-age population and a significant skills shortage, particularly in manufacturing and STEM fields.
- **Productivity Challenges:** Labor productivity remains at approximately 67% of the EU average, hampered by regional disparities and a slow rate of digital adoption among small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
- **Fiscal Context:** This growth period occurred while Poland navigated an excessive deficit procedure, necessitating a binding expenditure path to reduce the deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028.

The 2024 growth serves as a baseline for the 2025 projections, which anticipate even higher growth rates as the country continues to navigate its transition toward a more modernized, decarbonized, and digitally integrated economy.
