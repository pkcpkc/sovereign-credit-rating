# Publication of 'flash missions' reports on justice efficiency

## Event Details

On May 13, 2025, the French government published the results of its "flash missions" aimed at improving the efficiency of the justice system. These initiatives were launched in response to persistent challenges regarding case resolution times, which remained significantly high during the 2024–2025 reference period. Specifically, average durations reached 354 days for civil and commercial cases and extended to 1,335 days for bribery cases.

The flash missions sought to propose concrete solutions to alleviate these backlogs, including the exploration of out-of-court dispute resolution mechanisms. This event occurred within the broader context of ongoing judicial reforms in France, including the implementation of the 2023 law reforming the status of magistrates and the continued digitalization of judicial procedures through the Portalis tool. While digitalization efforts progressed, magistrates reported slow implementation and technical issues, prompting the government's targeted intervention to enhance operational efficiency. The publication aligns with the recommendations and observations outlined in the European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report for France, which highlighted efficiency as a key area requiring improvement alongside judicial independence and anti-corruption measures.
