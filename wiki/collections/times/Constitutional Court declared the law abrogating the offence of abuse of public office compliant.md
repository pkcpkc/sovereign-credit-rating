# Constitutional Court declared the law abrogating the offence of abuse of public office compliant

## Event Details

In May 2025, the [[Constitutional Court (Italy)|Constitutional Court]] declared the law abrogating the offence of abuse of public office compliant with the Constitution. This ruling validated the legislative change that had entered into force in August 2024, effectively removing the specific criminal charge for abuse of public office from Italian law.

The decision was highlighted in the [[European Commission|EC]]'s 2025 Rule of Law Report Country Chapter on Italy, published in July 2025. While the Court's validation confirms the constitutional legality of the abrogation, the move remains a significant point of discussion within the broader context of Italy's anti-corruption framework. The report notes that while the [[National Anti-Corruption Authority (ANAC)|ANAC]] has updated its National Anti-Corruption Plan and strengthened its resources, legislative gaps persist in other areas, such as lobbying regulation and political party financing transparency. The removal of this offence is considered in conjunction with other institutional checks and balances, including ongoing debates regarding judicial independence and the separation of careers between judges and prosecutors.
