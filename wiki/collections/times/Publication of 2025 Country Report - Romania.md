# Publication of 2025 Country Report - Romania

## Event Details

The economic and social trajectory of Romania has been marked by significant milestones and structural challenges leading up to the release of the **2025 Country Report - Romania** on **June 4, 2025**.

**Historical Context and Recent Trends (2024):**
During 2024, the Romanian economy faced a period of cooling, with real GDP growth decelerating to 0.8%—a sharp decline from the 2.4% recorded in 2023. This slowdown was compounded by high inflation pressures and weak net exports. Simultaneously, the fiscal landscape became strained as the public deficit rose to 9.3% of GDP, largely driven by substantial wage and pension increases. While government debt remained below the EU average at nearly 55% of GDP, the rapid pace of its increase prompted calls for stricter fiscal-structural planning.

**Strategic Transitions (2025):**
The year 2025 serves as a pivotal period for integration and regulatory shifts. On **January 1, 2025**, Romania officially joined the **Schengen Area**, a major step for regional mobility. The same day saw the implementation of a new microenterprise tax regime, reducing the threshold to EUR 250,000. The current **Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP)** aims to stabilize the economy by capping net expenditure growth at 5.1% for 2025, though implementation remains a critical hurdle.

**Future Outlook (2026–2030):**
Looking ahead, the 2025 Country Report projects a recovery in real GDP growth to 2.2% by **2026**. A primary focus for the next decade is the energy transition; Romania has set a target of 38.3% renewable energy in gross final consumption by **2030**. To achieve this, the nation must overcome significant barriers including high electricity prices, low R&D intensity (currently at 0.52%), and a lagging digital transformation. The overarching goal remains transitioning from a low-cost labor model to an innovation-driven economy while addressing severe skills gaps and infrastructure deficiencies.
