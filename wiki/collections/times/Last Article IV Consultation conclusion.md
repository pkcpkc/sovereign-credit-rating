# Last Article IV Consultation conclusion

## Event Details

The 2025 Article IV Consultation with Belgium reached its conclusion in March 2025, following a series of high-level discussions and technical reviews.

**Timeline of Key Events:**
- **June 2024:** Elections in Belgium set the political stage for the subsequent economic review.
- **September 2024:** The expiration of one-year government bonds signaled early fiscal pressure points.
- **December 2024:** The previous cycle of the Article IV Consultation concluded.
- **February 2025:** Intensive discussions took place, culminating in the end of formal IMF discussions with Belgian officials on February 4th. This period included a critical concluding meeting with the new Deputy Prime Minister, [[Jan Jambon]], on February 21st.
- **March 2025:** The IMF Staff Report was completed on March 3rd. The Executive Board officially concluded the consultation on March 18th, followed by the public release of the consultation results on March 20th.

**Core Findings:**
The consultation highlighted Belgium's economic resilience despite structural challenges such as slowing growth and declining labor-cost competitiveness due to automatic wage indexation. The IMF emphasized the necessity of a 7-year adjustment plan to bring the public debt (projected at 123% of GDP by 2030) under control. Key recommendations included labor market reforms, rationalizing social benefits, and addressing the productivity gap to ensure long-term fiscal sustainability and a successful green transition.
