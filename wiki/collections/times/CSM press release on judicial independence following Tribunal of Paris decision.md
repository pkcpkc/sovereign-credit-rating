# CSM press release on judicial independence following Tribunal of Paris decision

## Event Details

On March 31, 2024, the Superior Council of Magistracy (CSM) issued a significant press release regarding judicial independence. This action was taken in response to the decision made by the Tribunal of Paris, specifically aimed at defending judges against personal attacks and ensuring the integrity of the judiciary. The CSM's intervention highlights a period where judicial independence in France remained stable despite increasing public and political pressures. This event is a key component of the broader judicial landscape in France, which includes ongoing reforms to magistrate status and efforts to balance judicial autonomy with public accountability.
