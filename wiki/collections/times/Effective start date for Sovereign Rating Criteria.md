# Effective start date for Sovereign Rating Criteria

## Event Details

On **September 15, 2025**, [[Fitch Ratings]] officially implemented its updated Sovereign Rating Criteria, establishing a new methodology for assigning and maintaining sovereign credit ratings. This update replaced the previous criteria dated October 24, 2024, and remained the active framework until April 27, 2026.

The new criteria introduced several key structural changes to the assessment framework:

*   **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM) Weighting**: The updated model heavily weighted **Structural Features** at 53.7%, driven by governance indicators, GDP per capita, and share in world GDP. This was followed by Public Finances (19.1%), External Finances (17.3%), and Macroeconomic Performance (9.8%).
*   **Qualitative Overlay (QO)**: A formalized mechanism allowing notch adjustments of ±2 per pillar (capped at ±3 overall) to account for factors such as geopolitical risk, financial sector stability, and policy credibility. Exceptions for wider adjustments were permitted for countries in crisis or with recent default histories.
*   **Climate Risk Integration**: The introduction of **Sovereign Climate Vulnerability Signals (Climate.VS)**, a screening tool aggregating physical and transition risks. Sovereigns with a Climate.VS score of 50 or higher in 2035 were subject to additional analytical scrutiny, with potential notch adjustments if risks were material.
*   **Financial Sector Monitoring**: Enhanced focus on macro-prudential risks utilizing the Macro-Prudential Indicator (MPI) and Banking Systemic Indicator (BSI).

The methodology distinguished between Local-Currency (LC) and Foreign-Currency (FC) Issuer Default Ratings (IDRs) and incorporated Recovery Ratings (RRs) for issuers rated 'B+' or below to reflect loss severity. The criteria were developed with input from analysts including James Longsdon, Ed Parker, Carlos Masip, and Rob Shearman at [[Fitch Ratings]], and utilized data from institutions such as the [[World Bank]], [[IMF]], and [[BIS]].
