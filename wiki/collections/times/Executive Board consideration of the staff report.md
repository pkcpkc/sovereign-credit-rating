# Executive Board consideration of the staff report

## Event Details

On November 7, 2025, the IMF Executive Board considered the staff report regarding Romania's Article IV Consultation. This review followed a period of intensive staff discussions (September 3–12, 2025) and the completion of the formal Staff Report on October 15, 2025.

The consideration focused on Romania's economic landscape, characterized by subdued growth (0.9% in 2024) and elevated inflation (9.9% in September 2025). Key points of discussion included:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** The review of the government's 2025–26 fiscal reform package, including VAT rate increases to 21% and public sector wage/pension freezes.
*   **Debt Sustainability:** Addressing the "twin deficits" and the projected rise of public debt-to-GDP toward 70% by 2030.
*   **Monetary Policy:** The National Bank of Romania's (NBR) cautious stance on interest rates and the recommendation for increased exchange rate flexibility.
*   **Financial Stability:** Risks associated with the sovereign-bank nexus (where 26% of bank assets are in sovereign bonds) and unhedged FX loans.
*   **Structural Reforms:** The necessity of accelerating the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds and improving labor force participation.

The results of this consideration were subsequently released in a press release on November 14, 2025.
