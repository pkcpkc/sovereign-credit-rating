# Target for 100% renewable electricity and VHCN coverage

## Event Details

As part of Austria's broader decarbonisation strategy, the country has established a significant milestone for **2030**: achieving **100% renewable electricity** and reaching **VHCN (Very High Capacity Network)** coverage. 

This target is supported by recent progress, as Austria achieved a record 87% renewable electricity generation in 2024. However, meeting the 2030 goals requires overcoming significant infrastructure hurdles, including a projected need for EUR 53 billion in grid expansion investments by 2040 to support the transition and accommodate the demands of an industrial sector that currently accounts for 35% of the country's emissions.
