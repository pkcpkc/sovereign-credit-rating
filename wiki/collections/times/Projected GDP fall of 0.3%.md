# Projected GDP fall of 0.3%

## Event Details

In 2025, Austria is projected to experience a GDP contraction of 0.3%. This marks the third consecutive year of recession for the country, following a 1.2% contraction in 2024 and a 1% decline in 2023. This economic downturn is driven by a combination of declining industrial production, broad-based cost pressures—specifically in energy and labor—and significant structural shortfalls in skilled labor.

To manage the fiscal impact of this recession and address high deficits, the government has initiated a consolidation plan aiming for savings of EUR 6.4 billion in 2025 and EUR 8.4 billion in 2026. Despite the contraction, the labor market shows some resilience, though it remains pressured by demographic headwinds and a high reliance on part-time employment.
