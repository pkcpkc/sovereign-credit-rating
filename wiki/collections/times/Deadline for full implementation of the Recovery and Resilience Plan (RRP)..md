# Deadline for full implementation of the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP).

## Event Details

The deadline for the full implementation of Slovakia's [[Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP)]] is set for **31 August 2026**. By July 2026, Slovakia has fulfilled approximately 33% of the plan's milestones, with implementation progress hampered by inefficient public procurement processes, administrative fragmentation, and insufficient project preparation.

The successful absorption of these EU funds is critical for the country's economic outlook, particularly given the forecasted GDP growth slowdown to 1.4% in 2026. The RRP investments are intended to provide near-term economic stimulus and support essential structural reforms, including:

*   **Green Transition**: Addressing Slovakia's heavy dependence on Russian fossil fuels and accelerating the shift toward renewable energy sources, which stood at only 24% of electricity generation in 2024.
*   **Digital and Structural Modernization**: Mitigating structural bottlenecks in skills and administrative capacity.
*   **Fiscal Stabilization**: Supporting public investment while the government addresses a high structural deficit (projected at 5.1% of GDP for 2026) and rising debt levels.

Failure to meet this deadline would result in the loss of significant EU funding, exacerbating risks related to debt sustainability and hindering efforts to improve competitiveness and innovation output, which currently remains low with private R&D intensity at just 0.58% of GDP.
