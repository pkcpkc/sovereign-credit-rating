# IMF Staff Mission Concludes Discussions in Warsaw

## Event Details

On October 17, 2024, the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) staff mission concluded its discussions in Warsaw regarding the 2024 Article IV Consultation with the Republic of Poland. The mission was led by Jan Kees Martijn, Head of the IMF European Department, alongside staff members Kareem Ismail, Alla Myrvoda, and Yang Yang.

The consultations focused on Poland’s robust economic recovery, characterized by an estimated 2.8% growth in 2024, driven by private consumption, strong nominal wage growth, and the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. The IMF highlighted significant fiscal challenges, noting a projected deficit of 5.9% of GDP due to permanent increases in public sector wages, social benefits, and high defense spending. The mission recommended frontloading fiscal consolidation to rebuild buffers, aiming for a deficit of 2.9% of GDP by 2028 through measures such as VAT reforms and pension adjustments.

Monetary policy was assessed as appropriately tight, with the National Bank of Poland maintaining a 5.75% policy rate. The IMF advised that interest rate cuts should commence only when wage growth decelerates and inflation is firmly declining towards the 2.5% target. The financial system was noted as resilient, though litigation risks related to legacy CHF mortgages remain a concern.

The mission emphasized the importance of structural reforms to sustain growth, including labor market integration, innovation support, and a credible climate transition. The discussions were followed by the completion of the staff report on December 16, 2024, and concluded with IMF Executive Board approval on January 21, 2025.

Key participants from the Polish side included Patryk Loszewski, Executive Director for Poland, and Marta Pietrzak, Advisor to the Executive Director. Resident Representatives Geoff Gottlieb, Robert Sierhej, Krzysztof Krogulski, and Maciej Onoszko supported the mission operations.
