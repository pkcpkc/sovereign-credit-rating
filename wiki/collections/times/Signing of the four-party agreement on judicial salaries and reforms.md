# Signing of the four-party agreement on judicial salaries and reforms

## Event Details

On November 22, 2024, a four-party agreement was signed regarding judicial salaries and structural reforms in Hungary. This agreement was intended to address the remuneration of the justice system, aiming for significant salary increases for judges and prosecutors (with a target of a 48% increase by 2027).

However, the progress of this agreement faced significant friction in early 2025. The National Judicial Council (NJC) subsequently withdrew from the agreement, citing a lack of meaningful consultation by the government on subsequent legislative amendments. These specific amendments included raising the minimum age for judges to 35 and extending the retirement age to 70, moves that were perceived as proceeding without sufficient dialogue with judicial stakeholders.
