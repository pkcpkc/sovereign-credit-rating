# Launch of deposit return system for single-use beverage containers

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, Austria officially launched its deposit return system (DRS) for single-use beverage containers. This regulatory milestone marks a significant step in the country's efforts to enhance recycling rates, reduce litter, and promote circular economy principles in the packaging sector.

The introduction of the DRS coincided with other major economic and structural shifts in Austria during early 2025, including the end of Russian gas transit through Ukraine and the implementation of various fiscal consolidation measures by the new government. As part of the broader regulatory framework assessed in the European Commission's 2025 Country Report, the system is designed to improve waste management infrastructure and support Austria's environmental sustainability targets.
