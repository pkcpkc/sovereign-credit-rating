# Deadline for submitting draft law for civil society regulatory framework

## Event Details

On **July 24, 2025**, the deadline arrived for submitting the draft law for the civil society regulatory framework in Malta. This legislative development is part of the ongoing review of the operating environment for non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and civil society actors.

According to the European Commission's **[[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Malta]]**, the civil society space in Malta has been assessed as "narrowed." The preparation of this draft regulatory framework has been noted as a key development affecting the sector, occurring alongside other legislative reforms that have drawn scrutiny regarding public participation and accountability. This deadline follows the earlier July 1, 2025 deadline for submitting draft laws for the Public Consultation Department and the National Human Rights Institution, reflecting a broader government initiative to formalize various institutional frameworks, albeit amidst concerns about the independence and operational capacity of these bodies.

The context of this regulatory drafting occurs against a backdrop of mixed progress in the rule of law, with specific concerns raised about the "Checks and Balances" pillar, including limited progress on establishing a formal framework for public participation in legislative processes. The regulatory framework for civil society is being developed while the Permanent Commission Against Corruption (PCAC) continues to face challenges in demonstrating concrete results, and while debates continue over the independence of public service media and the integrity of high-level political appointments.
