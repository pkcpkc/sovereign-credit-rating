# Publication of S&P's methodology for sovereign credit rating

## Event Details

In 2017, S&P published its comprehensive methodology for determining sovereign credit ratings. The framework is structured around five primary pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments.

The methodology evaluates:
- **Institutional Assessment:** Policymaking stability, transparency, and "debt payment culture."
- **Economic Assessment:** GDP per capita (tax base), growth prospects, and economic diversity.
- **External Assessment:** Currency status, external liquidity, and resident assets/liabilities.
- **Fiscal Assessment:** Analysis of "flows" (fiscal performance and flexibility) and "stocks" (debt burden and contingent liabilities, including BICRA-regulated financial institution risks).
- **Monetary Assessment:** Central bank independence and the effectiveness of exchange rate regimes.

The methodology includes significant "room for discretionary judgment," allowing analysts to override indicative ratings by more than one notch based on subjective criteria such as "cohesive civil society" or supplemental adjustment factors like event risk. This framework served as the basis for subsequent analysis by [[Alex Stomper]] in 2024 to evaluate the replicability of sovereign ratings.
