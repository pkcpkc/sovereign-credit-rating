# Start of audits of party statements by Court of Audit under new rules

## Event Details

On **January 1, 2025**, the Austrian Court of Audit initiated audits of political party financial statements under a new regulatory framework. This operational milestone was cited in the **EU Commission 2025 Rule of Law Report on Austria** as a key strength in the country's institutional checks and balances.

The report highlights that the Court of Audit is performing "extended mandates on party finance," contributing to improved transparency and accountability in the political sector. This development occurs within a broader context of ongoing efforts to strengthen anti-corruption measures and parliamentary transparency in Austria, although challenges remain regarding asset declaration requirements for Members of Parliament and lobbying transparency. The implementation of these audits aligns with the government's commitment to merit-based and transparent governance structures, as noted by the European Commission in its assessment of Austria's rule of law situation up to mid-2025.
