# Law reforming the status of magistrates

## Event Details

The law reforming the status of magistrates was enacted on 2023-11-20 as part of a broader effort to modernize the French judicial system. 

### Key Implementation Details
*   **Admission Reform:** The legislation removed previous age limits for admissions to the magistracy.
*   **Evaluation Framework:** It established a "360° evaluation process" specifically designed for court presidents to ensure comprehensive oversight.
*   **Current Status:** As of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the implementation of this law is actively underway, aimed at balancing judicial independence with institutional accountability.
