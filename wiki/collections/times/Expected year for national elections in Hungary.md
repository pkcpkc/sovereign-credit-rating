# Expected year for national elections in Hungary

## Event Details

National elections in Hungary are expected to take place in early 2026. The timing of these elections poses significant political economy challenges for the implementation of necessary structural reforms, as noted in the IMF’s 2025 Article IV Consultation Staff Report.

The electoral calendar introduces specific risks to Hungary’s macroeconomic outlook:
*   **Fiscal Stability:** There is a heightened risk of expansionary fiscal measures being triggered by the upcoming elections, which could undermine the authorities' commitment to medium-term fiscal structural plans and deficit reduction targets.
*   **Policy Credibility:** Frequent regulatory changes associated with the political cycle create an unpredictable business environment, potentially delaying fiscal adjustments and heightening market concerns about debt sustainability.
*   **Reform Implementation:** The political uncertainty surrounding the elections complicates the governance reforms required to unlock approximately €19 billion in suspended EU funds due to rule of law concerns.

The IMF Executive Board highlighted that the potential for election-driven fiscal slippage is a key downside risk, alongside geoeconomic fragmentation and trade tensions. While the government has demonstrated the ability to implement significant fiscal adjustments recently, the proximity of the 2026 elections remains a critical factor in assessing the country's policy credibility and institutional strength.
