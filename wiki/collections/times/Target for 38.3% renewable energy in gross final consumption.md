# Target for 38.3% renewable energy in gross final consumption

## Event Details

As part of Romania's broader energy transition and sustainability goals, a specific milestone has been established for the year 2030. The country aims to reach a target of 38.3% renewable energy in gross final consumption.

This target is situated within a complex energy landscape characterized by:
- **Current Progress:** In 2024, renewables reached 48% of electricity generation, though the overall transition toward final consumption targets remains hindered by regulatory barriers and grid limitations.
- **Economic Context:** The transition is complicated by high electricity prices in Romania, which are exacerbated by a heavy fiscal burden on electricity compared to gas.
- **Sustainability Goals:** This target aligns with broader efforts to improve the circular economy and decarbonize the national infrastructure, addressing Romania's current position as having one of the lowest circular material use rates in the EU.
