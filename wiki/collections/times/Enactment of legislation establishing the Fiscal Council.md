# Enactment of legislation establishing the Fiscal Council

## Event Details

On **2024-12-31**, legislation was enacted in Poland establishing an independent **Fiscal Council**. This institution was created to strengthen policy credibility and institutional strength, addressing previous concerns regarding complex regulatory frameworks and weak ex post evaluation of legislation.

According to the **EU Commission 2025 Country Report: Poland**, the establishment of the Fiscal Council is cited as a key strength in Poland's policy credibility, with the body projected to become operational in **2026**. The Council is intended to provide independent oversight and advice on fiscal matters, supporting Poland's commitments under the new EU fiscal framework and its medium-term fiscal-structural plan. This move aims to improve fiscal sustainability and accountability amidst challenges such as rising public debt and high budget deficits.
