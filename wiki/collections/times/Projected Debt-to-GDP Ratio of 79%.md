# Projected Debt-to-GDP Ratio of 79%

## Event Details

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) projects that Hungary's public debt will rise toward a ratio of 79% of GDP by the year 2030. This projection is part of the broader fiscal outlook outlined in the 2025 Article IV Consultation.

While the IMF notes a "moderate" risk of sovereign stress, the outlook is complicated by several factors:
*   **Refinancing Costs:** High costs associated with servicing existing debt.
*   **SOE Liabilities:** Contingent liabilities stemming from State-Owned Enterprises.
*   **Fiscal Adjustment:** To manage this trajectory, the IMF recommends a cumulative fiscal adjustment of approximately 2% of GDP between 2025 and 2028, aiming to bring the headline deficit below 3% by 2027.

The sustainability of this debt position is linked to the government's ability to rationalize spending, specifically regarding retail energy subsidies and administrative overhead, while navigating geoeconomic fragmentation and regional instability.
