# Projected deadline for NGEU project completion

## Event Details

The projected deadline for the completion of Next Generation EU (NGEU) projects in Romania is set for **August 1, 2026**. 

This deadline is a critical milestone for the country's structural reforms and economic integration. Currently, Romania faces challenges with the absorption of EU funds, with a completion rate of only 38% for NGEU projects due to significant administrative bottlenecks. Accelerating these reforms under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) is identified by the IMF as a vital necessity to unlock funding, drive growth, and support the transition to a low-carbon economy.
