# Virtual mission discussions began

## Event Details

On **2024-12-04**, virtual mission discussions began between the [[IMF]] and the Government of Slovakia as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. The IMF delegation was led by Mission Head [[Saxegaard]], with oversight from Department Heads [[Oya Celasun]] (IMF EUR) and [[Stefania Fabrizio]] (IMF SPR).

These discussions were conducted with key Slovak officials, including [[Kamenický]] (Minister of Finance), [[Kažimír]] (Governor, National Bank of Slovakia), and [[Taraba]] (Minister of the Environment). The dialogue aimed to assess Slovakia's economic developments through early 2025, focusing on the significant widening of the fiscal deficit (reaching 5.8% of GDP in 2024) which had triggered an Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) by the European Commission.

The virtual sessions preceded the in-person staff discussions that concluded on 2025-01-28. The data and policy analysis gathered during these initial virtual talks contributed to the staff report completed on 2025-03-03, which was subsequently considered by the IMF Executive Board on 2025-03-18 and concluded with the final consultation on 2025-03-24. Key topics included the government's ambitious four-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025–2028), debt sustainability, and structural reforms needed to address automotive sector dependence and demographic challenges.
