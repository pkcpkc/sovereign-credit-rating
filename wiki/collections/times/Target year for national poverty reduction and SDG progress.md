# Target year for national poverty reduction and SDG progress

## Event Details

The year **2030** serves as the critical target deadline for Malta to achieve significant progress in national poverty reduction and the broader Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), as outlined in the [[European Commission]]'s 2025 Country Report. This timeline is part of the EU's overarching commitment to the 2030 Agenda, requiring member states to demonstrate measurable improvements in social welfare, inclusion, and economic resilience.

As of the 2025 report, Malta faces substantial challenges in meeting these targets, particularly concerning child poverty and social inequality. Key issues identified include:

*   **Rising Child Poverty:** Child poverty rates increased to **25.9% in 2024**, exceeding the EU average. This trend is driven by factors such as low parental education levels and high housing costs, posing a direct threat to achieving 2030 poverty reduction goals.
*   **Social Exclusion of Vulnerable Groups:** Non-EU nationals and low-skilled workers face precarious working conditions, higher risks of poverty, and increased workplace accidents, indicating a need for stronger social protection measures.
*   **Gender Inequality:** A wide gender employment gap of 13.4 percentage points persists, hindering inclusive economic progress.
*   **Healthcare Access:** High out-of-pocket healthcare costs contribute to financial vulnerability, with healthcare spending projected to rise significantly from 5.1% to 7.2% of GDP by 2070 due to an aging population.

To meet the 2030 targets, the European Commission emphasizes the need for Malta to accelerate the implementation of its [[Recovery and Resilience Plan]] (RRP) and Cohesion Policy investments. Priority actions include improving social protection adequacy, addressing skills mismatches to boost inclusive growth, and enhancing the efficiency of public services to support vulnerable populations. Failure to address these structural weaknesses may jeopardize Malta's ability to meet its SDG commitments by the 2030 deadline.
