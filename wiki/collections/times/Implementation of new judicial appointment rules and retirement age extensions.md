# Implementation of new judicial appointment rules and retirement age extensions

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, Hungary implemented new judicial appointment rules and retirement age extensions as part of a series of legislative reforms. These changes included raising the minimum age for judges to 35 and allowing judicial service to continue until the age of 70. 

The implementation of these rules was marked by significant friction with the National Judicial Council (NJC), which withdrew from a four-party agreement in early 2025. The NJC cited a lack of consultation on these specific legislative amendments as a primary reason for its withdrawal, highlighting ongoing tensions regarding judicial independence and the law-making process in Hungary.
