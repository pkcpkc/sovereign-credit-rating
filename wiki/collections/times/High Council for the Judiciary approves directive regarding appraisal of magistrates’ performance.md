# High Council for the Judiciary approves directive regarding appraisal of magistrates’ performance

## Event Details

On 13 November 2024, the **High Council for the Judiciary** of Italy approved a directive concerning the appraisal of magistrates’ performance. This measure is part of a broader, comprehensive reform of the justice system adopted by the government in March 2024, aimed at enhancing efficiency, transparency, and accountability within the Italian judicial sector.

The directive represents a key implementation step of the 2024 Justice System Reform, following earlier measures such as the approval of rules for out-of-tenure appointments on 25 September 2024 and the Code of Judicial Management on 3 December 2024. These initiatives collectively aim to address persistent challenges including staff shortages and case backlogs.

The reform process has been marked by significant stakeholder engagement and some political friction. While the High Council for the Judiciary has taken forward tasks under the implementing legislation, the broader constitutional reform context—including a draft proposal to separate the careers of judges and prosecutors—has faced strong opposition from judicial bodies, who argue it undermines judicial autonomy. The High Council for the Judiciary itself issued a critical opinion on this separation of careers in January 2025.

Despite these political debates, the implementation of performance appraisal directives and other judicial management codes is viewed by the European Commission as a positive development in ensuring impartiality and improving the clearance rates across civil, criminal, administrative, and tax jurisdictions.
