# European Council initiated Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP)

## Event Details

In July 2024, the European Council initiated the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) regarding the Slovak Republic. This action was taken in response to fiscal imbalances that necessitated a structured correction of the country's public finances. 

Following this initiation, the Slovak government committed to an ambitious 4-year fiscal consolidation plan spanning 2025–2028. The primary objectives of this plan include reducing the headline deficit—with a 2025 budget target of 4.7% of GDP—and stabilizing public debt levels. The IMF has highlighted that while the recovery phase is underway, additional expenditure-based measures (estimated at 3.1% of GDP over three years) may be required to meet long-term stability targets and address structural economic challenges.
