# Constitutional Court declares law abrogating abuse of office compliant with Constitution

## Event Details

In May 2025, the Italian Constitutional Court issued a significant ruling regarding the country's anti-corruption framework. The Court declared that the law abrogating the offense of "abuse of public office" is compliant with the Constitution and aligns with the requirements of the UN Convention against Corruption. 

This ruling follows a period of legislative updates aimed at refining Italy's anti-corruption efforts. While the judicial validation provides a legal green light for the abrogation, the broader context of Italy's anti-corruption landscape remains complex. Despite this progress, challenges persist in areas such as the lack of a comprehensive national lobbying register and high risks in public procurement, where 98% of contracts were still allocated via direct allocation in 2024.
