# IMF mission discussions with Romanian officials began

## Event Details

On September 3, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) initiated its mission discussions with Romanian officials to conduct the 2025 Article IV Consultation. The mission, led by head of mission team [[Joong Shik Kang]] and involving senior regional representative [[Carlos Granados]], aimed to assess Romania's economic performance, fiscal sustainability, and progress on structural reforms.

Key focus areas of the discussions included:
*   **Fiscal Sustainability:** Addressing "twin deficits" and the high risk of sovereign stress. The IMF reviewed a mid-2025 reform package (including VAT hikes and excise duties) intended to reduce the deficit to below 6% of GDP by 2026.
*   **Inflation and Monetary Policy:** Analyzing headline inflation (9.9% in September 2025) driven by the removal of energy price caps and VAT increases. The mission discussed the National Bank of Romania's (NBR) cautious monetary stance under [[Mugur Isarescu]].
*   **Structural Reforms:** Evaluating the low absorption of [[Next Generation EU (NGEU)]] funds (38%) and the need for governance improvements regarding State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) and labor force participation.
*   **Financial Stability:** Assessing the "sovereign-bank nexus" and the resilience of the banking system.
*   **External Balance:** Reviewing the current account deficit (8.2% of GDP in 2024) and the overvaluation of the Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER).

The discussions concluded on September 12, 2025, leading to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on October 15, 2025, and the final press release on November 14, 2025.
