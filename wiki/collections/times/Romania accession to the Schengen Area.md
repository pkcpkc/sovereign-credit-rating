# Romania accession to the Schengen Area

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, Romania officially joined the Schengen Area, marking a significant milestone in its integration into the European Union's internal border-free zone. This accession is part of a broader trajectory of economic and structural convergence, as highlighted in the 2025 Country Report.

While the entry into Schengen represents a major step for mobility and trade, the report notes that Romania continues to navigate complex macroeconomic challenges. These include a public deficit of 9.3% in 2024 and the need to transition from a low-cost labor model to an innovation-driven economy. The accession serves as a critical infrastructure and policy milestone intended to bolster Romania's position within the EU, even as the country addresses internal hurdles such as R&D underinvestment and a lagging digital transformation.
