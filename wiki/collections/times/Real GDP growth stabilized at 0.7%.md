# Real GDP growth stabilized at 0.7%

## Event Details

In 2024, Italy's economy experienced a stabilization of Real GDP growth at 0.7%. This moderate growth was supported by investments from the [[Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP)]] despite a landscape of global uncertainties and high public debt.

During this period, Italy's fiscal position showed significant movement:
- **Deficit Reduction:** The government deficit dropped to 3.4% of GDP in 2024, a sharp decline from the 7.2% recorded in 2023.
- **Primary Balance:** The country achieved its first primary surplus since 2019, reaching 0.4% of GDP.
- **Debt Challenges:** Despite the deficit reduction, the debt-to-GDP ratio is projected to rise toward 138.2% by 2026, driven by high interest costs and borrowing requirements for past housing renovation tax credits.

The growth dynamics are currently being sustained by household consumption and corporate investment, though the economy faces ongoing structural hurdles including a significant skills mismatch, low R&D expenditure (1.31% of GDP), and regional disparities between the North and the [[Mezzogiorno]].
