# Expiration of one-year government bond

## Event Details

In September 2024, the expiration of the one-year government bond occurred as part of the broader fiscal landscape in Belgium. This event aligns with a period of significant fiscal scrutiny, as the country faces a projected public debt of 123% of GDP by 2030. The management of such instruments is critical to the IMF-supported 7-year adjustment plan aimed at bringing the deficit below 3% of GDP and addressing the structural challenges posed by automatic wage indexation and an aging population.
