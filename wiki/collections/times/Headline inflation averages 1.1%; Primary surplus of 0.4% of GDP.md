# Headline inflation averages 1.1%; Primary surplus of 0.4% of GDP

## Event Details

In 2024, the Italian economy demonstrated notable resilience amid global headwinds, achieving a real GDP growth of 0.7% and reaching record-high employment levels. A key feature of this fiscal year was the improvement in macroeconomic stability indicators: headline inflation averaged **1.1%**, marking a significant convergence toward the [[European Central Bank]]’s 2% target, while the country returned to a **primary surplus of 0.4% of GDP**.

This return to a primary surplus was a critical milestone following years of fiscal adjustment pressures. The overall general government deficit shrank to **3.4% of GDP**, a better-than-expected outturn that helped stabilize the trajectory of public debt, which ended the year at **135.3% of GDP**. Despite the high debt burden, the improved primary balance provided a buffer against rising interest costs, although the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) staff noted that the interest-growth differential remained positive, posing challenges for long-term sustainability.

These figures were highlighted during the IMF’s 2025 Article IV Consultation with Italy, where the 2024 performance served as a baseline for future policy recommendations. While the 2024 primary surplus was a positive development, IMF staff recommended a more front-loaded fiscal adjustment to achieve a primary surplus of **3% of GDP by 2027** to firmly place the debt stock on a downward path. The Italian authorities, however, maintained their commitment to the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), which envisions a more gradual seven-year adjustment.

The inflationary backdrop in 2024 was characterized by moderating price pressures, with headline inflation easing significantly from previous highs. This stability was supported by moderate wage growth and the convergence of core inflation, although risks such as sticky core inflation and regulated energy price adjustments persisted. The combination of low inflation and a primary surplus contributed to an improved external position, with Italy posting a current account surplus of 1.1% of GDP in 2024.

## Key Indicators (2024)

- **Headline Inflation**: 1.1% (Average)
- **Primary Balance**: +0.4% of GDP (Surplus)
- **General Government Deficit**: 3.4% of GDP
- **Public Debt-to-GDP Ratio**: 135.3%
- **Real GDP Growth**: 0.7%
- **Current Account Balance**: +1.1% of GDP
