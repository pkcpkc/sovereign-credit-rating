# Government tables constitutional reform for the justice sector

## Event Details

On May 13, 2025, the Maltese government tabled a comprehensive constitutional reform aimed at enhancing the independence of the judiciary. A primary objective of this reform is to involve the judiciary directly in the appointment process of the Chief Justice. 

Additionally, the government proposed measures to remove executive involvement in the appointment of members to specialized tribunals. These moves are part of a broader effort to address concerns regarding judicial independence and the efficiency of the justice system, despite high public perception of independence.
