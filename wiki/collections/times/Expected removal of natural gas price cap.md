# Expected removal of natural gas price cap

## Event Details

The removal of the natural gas price cap is scheduled for **March 2026**. This policy shift is identified by the IMF as a significant supply-side shock that will contribute to elevated headline inflation in Romania through mid-2026. 

The removal of this cap, combined with electricity price cap expirations and VAT increases, is expected to create substantial inflationary pressures. Consequently, the International Monetary Fund advises the National Bank of Romania (NBR) to maintain a cautious monetary stance, delaying interest rate cuts until wage and price growth show signs of sustainable moderation in response to these energy market shifts.
