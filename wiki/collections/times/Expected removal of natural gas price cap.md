# Expected removal of natural gas price cap

## Event Details

The removal of the natural gas price cap is a significant milestone in Romania's economic landscape, contributing to the temporary elevation of headline inflation. According to the [[IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Romania]], this policy shift, combined with VAT hikes, has pressured inflation levels (reaching 9.9% in September 2025). The transition is part of a broader fiscal and energy strategy, including the move toward a low-carbon economy and the implementation of the EU Emissions Trading System 2 (ETS2), aimed at long-term energy security and fiscal sustainability. The IMF expects inflation to return to the National Bank of Romania’s (NBR) tolerance band by the end of 2026 as these price adjustments stabilize.
