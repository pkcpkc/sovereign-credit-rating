# End of economic policy discussions with Malta officials

## Event Details

On November 15, 2024, the economic policy discussions between the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and Maltese officials concluded. These discussions were a critical component of the 2024 Article IV Consultation, focusing on Malta's transition toward a productivity-driven growth model.

Key outcomes and focus areas of the discussions included:
*   **Fiscal Sustainability:** Agreements were reached regarding Malta's commitment to reduce the fiscal deficit to 2.6% of GDP by 2027 in alignment with the EU’s new fiscal framework.
*   **Energy Policy:** A significant focus was placed on the strategy to exit the fixed-energy price model, with a plan to redirect current energy subsidies toward R&D, innovation, and public services such as health and education.
*   **Taxation Reform:** Discussions covered the necessity of a roadmap for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform to align with the EU's Minimum Tax Directive (Pillar II).
*   **Financial Stability:** The talks addressed the need to manage high bank exposure to the real estate sector (currently 70% of loan portfolios) by raising the sectoral systemic risk buffer (sSyRB).
*   **Structural Development:** The dialogue underscored the importance of the "Vision 2050" initiative, emphasizing STEM education, digital skills, and aggressive measures to meet the 19% GHG reduction target by 2030.

This conclusion paved the way for the completion of the IMF Staff Report in December 2024 and the subsequent Executive Board conclusion in January 2025.
