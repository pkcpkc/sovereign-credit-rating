# Implementation of the reformed judicial map

## Event Details

On June 1, 2023, Slovakia implemented the reformed judicial map. This structural change was part of a broader effort to reorganize the nation's judiciary, though subsequent reports from 2025 indicate that despite these reforms, public and corporate trust in judicial independence remains low (only 32% of the general population perceives it as "fairly or very good"). 

The report also highlights a deterioration in the efficiency of administrative courts following these structural shifts, with resolution times reaching 1,040 days in 2023. Furthermore, concerns have persisted regarding the Judicial Council's expanded role in disciplinary proceedings and the lack of independent guarantees regarding the dismissal of its members.
