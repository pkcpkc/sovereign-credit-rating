# Deadline for meeting NRRP milestones and targets

## Event Details

On **2026-06-30**, Italy faced a critical deadline to meet the remaining milestones and targets of the **National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP)**. This deadline marked the final phase for the implementation of reforms and investments funded by the European Union's recovery instrument, which had been instrumental in supporting Italy's economic resilience following the pandemic.

As of the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation, the implementation of the NRRP had accelerated, with 54% of milestones already achieved. However, the IMF staff noted that spending execution remained sluggish compared to the ambition of the plan. The successful meeting of the June 2026 deadline was crucial for unlocking the final tranches of funding and ensuring that the structural reforms—aimed at boosting productivity, enhancing labor supply, and transitioning to renewable energy—were effectively institutionalized.

The IMF emphasized that post-NRRP reforms would be essential to sustain growth and offset demographic headwinds, particularly given Italy's challenges with weak productivity growth and population aging. The authorities had committed to a durable reform agenda under both the NRRP and the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), aiming to ensure that the investments translated into long-term economic diversification and reduced regional disparities.

Failure to meet these targets risked delays in disbursements and could negatively impact Italy's fiscal position and debt sustainability, which remained a key concern with public debt at 135.3% of GDP in 2024. The deadline also coincided with broader efforts to strengthen the banking sector and address vulnerabilities among Less Significant Institutions (LSIs), as outlined in the concurrent Financial Sector Assessment Program.
