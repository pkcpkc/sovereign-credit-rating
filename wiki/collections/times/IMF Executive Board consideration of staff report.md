# IMF Executive Board consideration of staff report

## Event Details

On January 10, 2025, the IMF Executive Board considered the staff report regarding the 2024 Article IV Consultation for the Republic of Poland. This review followed a series of staff discussions held in Warsaw between October 8 and October 17, 2024, culminating in the completion of the formal IMF Staff Report on December 16, 2024.

The consideration of the report focused on Poland's economic recovery, with projected growth of 3.5% for 2025-2026 driven by domestic demand and the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. Key points of discussion included:

*   **Fiscal Position:** The board reviewed the widening fiscal deficit (estimated at 5.9% of GDP in 2024) which placed Poland under the EU's Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP).
*   **Policy Recommendations:** The report highlighted the need for a rebalanced policy mix, moving toward fiscal consolidation to reduce the deficit to 2.9% by 2028 and stabilizing debt at 60% of GDP.
*   **Monetary Policy:** The board noted the National Bank of Poland's (NBP) tight stance and the potential for gradual rate cuts in mid-2025 as inflation approaches the 2.5±1% target range.
*   **Structural Reforms:** Discussion included critical needs for labor supply increases, decarbonization of the power grid (aiming to halve coal-fueled generation by 2030), and addressing population aging.

The process concluded with the official press release of the Article IV consultation findings on January 21, 2025.
