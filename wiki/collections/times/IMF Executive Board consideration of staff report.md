# IMF Executive Board consideration of staff report

## Event Details

On March 18, 2025, the IMF Executive Board considered the staff report regarding the Slovak Republic's 2025 Article IV Consultation. This review followed a period of intense economic evaluation where the IMF assessed Slovakia's transition from a sharp slowdown in 2022-23 to a recovery phase with 2.0% growth in 2024.

The board's consideration focused on several critical pillars of the Slovak economy:
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** The evaluation of a 4-year fiscal plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP and stabilizing public debt.
- **Financial Stability:** Reviewing the resilience of the banking sector against vulnerabilities in the residential and commercial real estate markets, specifically regarding high house prices and mortgage refixations.
- **Structural Reforms:** Addressing demographic challenges such as a shrinking labor force, aging population, and the need for increased productivity through innovation and migrant integration.
- **Energy & Governance:** Assessing the progress of reducing natural gas reliance on Russia and the necessity of a new national anti-corruption strategy to improve the investment climate.

The board's consideration was part of a broader timeline that included the conclusion of staff discussions on January 28, 2025, and the finalization of the IMF Staff Report on March 3, 2025, ultimately leading to the conclusion of the Article IV consultation on March 24, 2025.
