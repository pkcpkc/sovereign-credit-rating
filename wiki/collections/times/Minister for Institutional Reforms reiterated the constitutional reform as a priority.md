# Minister for Institutional Reforms reiterated the constitutional reform as a priority

## Event Details

In December 2024, the **[[Minister for Institutional Reforms]]** explicitly reiterated that constitutional reform was a top priority for the Italian Government. This statement reinforced the administration's commitment to advancing structural changes within the country's judicial and political framework, specifically focusing on the separation of careers for judges and prosecutors.

This prioritization set the stage for subsequent legislative actions in early 2025. Following this declaration, the **[[Chamber of Deputies]]** approved the draft constitutional reform in January 2025. However, the initiative faced significant resistance; the **[[High Council for the Judiciary]]** issued a critical opinion on the draft in January 2025, raising concerns about judicial autonomy and representation. The reform continued to face scrutiny in the Senate, with judicial bodies arguing that separating the careers of judges and prosecutors could undermine the balance of power and judicial independence.

This event is part of a broader sequence of institutional reforms and debates detailed in the **[[2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Italy]]**, which notes that while the draft constitutional reform aimed at enhancing governmental stability was advancing, it remained a point of contention regarding the separation of powers and the independence of the judiciary.
