# High Council for the Judiciary issues critical opinion on constitutional reform

## Event Details

On January 8, 2025, the High Council for the Judiciary issued a critical opinion regarding proposed constitutional reforms in Italy. This intervention came amidst a period of significant legislative activity aimed at restructuring the nation's justice system.

The primary point of contention involved the proposed separation of the careers of judges and prosecutors. While the Chamber of Deputies moved forward with a draft reform to create separate careers (approved on January 16, 2025), the High Council for the Judiciary expressed significant reservations. Specifically, the Council and various magistrates' associations raised concerns regarding the authority and autonomy of the separate councils that would be established under such a framework. 

This critical stance highlights the ongoing tension between legislative efforts to streamline judicial roles and the judiciary's efforts to preserve institutional independence and structural integrity.
