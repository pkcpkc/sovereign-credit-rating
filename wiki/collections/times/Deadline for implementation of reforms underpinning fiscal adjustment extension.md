# Deadline for implementation of reforms underpinning fiscal adjustment extension

## Event Details

On **2025-04-30**, the deadline passed for the implementation of structural reforms underpinning the fiscal adjustment extension for France. This deadline was a critical component of the reformed EU Economic Governance Framework, specifically linked to the seven-year fiscal adjustment period agreed upon to address France's excessive deficit situation.

The context for this deadline was established earlier in the year:
- On **2025-01-21**, the Council of the European Union endorsed France's medium-term fiscal-structural plan and issued a recommendation to end the excessive deficit procedure. This endorsement contingent upon the timely implementation of specific structural reforms designed to support fiscal sustainability and boost potential growth.
- France had previously submitted this plan on **2024-10-31**.

The importance of this April 2025 deadline was underscored by the deteriorating fiscal position described in the *2025 Country Report - France*, published on **2025-06-04**. The report highlighted that France faced persistently high general government deficits (5.8% of GDP in 2024) and rising public debt (113% of GDP). The fragmented political landscape following the **2024** general elections had led to unprecedented delays in budget adoption and reduced fiscal ambitions, complicating the implementation of these necessary reforms.

While France was advanced in implementing its Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) measures, the Commission emphasized in its 2025 report that strict adherence to the endorsed medium-term fiscal-structural plan and effective spending reviews were necessary to ensure fiscal sustainability. The reforms due by this deadline were intended to address structural challenges such as high administrative burdens, stagnant business R&D intensity, and complex regulatory environments that hinder competitiveness. Failure to meet these reform milestones risked undermining the credibility of the fiscal adjustment path required to reduce the deficit and stabilize debt levels in the medium term.
