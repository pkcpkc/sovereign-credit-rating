# Virtual meeting with new DPM Jan Jambon

## Event Details

On February 21, 2025, a virtual meeting was held with the new Deputy Prime Minister (DPM), [[Jan Jambon]]. This meeting was a key component of the IMF's 2025 Article IV consultation period with Belgium (January 22 – February 4, 2025). The discussions focused on Belgium's economic outlook, specifically addressing the need for sustained fiscal consolidation to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP and managing public debt, which reached 104.1% of GDP in 2024.
