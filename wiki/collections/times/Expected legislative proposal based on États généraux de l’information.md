# Expected legislative proposal based on États généraux de l’information

## Event Details

In July 2025, a legislative proposal is expected to be introduced based on the findings and recommendations of the *États généraux de l’information*. This initiative is part of a broader regulatory evolution in France concerning media pluralism and freedom.

The proposal emerges within a context of significant media reform, including:
*   **Regulatory Expansion:** The expansion of ARCOM's scope to include Digital Services Act (DSA) mandates and the development of a national media ownership database.
*   **Public Broadcasting Reform:** Ongoing debates regarding the creation of "France Médias," a holding company intended to oversee public broadcasters, alongside a shift in funding mechanisms from license fees to VAT-based systems.
*   **Transparency and Access:** Efforts to balance the "disproportionate burden" exception used in administrative document access with the need for journalist safety and information transparency.
