# Constitutional Court ruling on the statute of limitations (Article 155(1))

## Event Details

On June 25, 2018, the [[Constitutional Court of Romania]] issued a landmark ruling regarding Article 155(1) of the Criminal Code, which dealt with the statute of limitations for criminal offenses. This decision created a significant legal void in Romanian criminal law, as it annulled the statutory provisions governing limitation periods without providing an immediate legislative alternative.

The ruling had profound implications for the country's anti-corruption efforts and judicial stability:

*   **Creation of Legal Void:** The annulment of Article 155(1) led to a period where the rules on statute of limitations were uncertain, causing significant disruptions in ongoing criminal proceedings, particularly those involving high-level corruption.
*   **Legislative Response:** The legal vacuum was eventually addressed by the government through the adoption of a [[Government Emergency Ordinance]] on May 30, 2022, which aimed to fill the void left by the Constitutional Court's decision and restore clarity to the criminal justice system.
*   **Systemic Impact:** The European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report identifies this period and its aftermath as a critical factor in the weakening of the anti-corruption framework. The uncertainty surrounding limitation periods contributed to the discontinuation of numerous corruption cases and the annulment of convictions.
*   **Judicial Interpretation Challenges:** Subsequent rulings by the [[High Court of Cassation and Justice]], particularly concerning the principle of *lex mitior* (the application of the more lenient law), have further complicated the situation. These interpretations have led to the expiration of limitation periods for many pending cases, raising concerns about impunity and compatibility with EU law. The HCCJ has referred questions to the [[Court of Justice of the European Union]] (Case C-280/25 Lin II) for clarification on these matters.
*   **Perception of Justice:** The events stemming from this 2018 ruling continue to affect the perception of judicial independence and the effectiveness of anti-corruption institutions. The lack of clear and stable legal frameworks during this period is cited as a contributing factor to the decreased public confidence in the judiciary and the perceived ineffectiveness of anti-corruption measures.

This event remains a pivotal reference point in discussions regarding the rule of law in Romania, highlighting the intersection of constitutional jurisprudence, legislative responsiveness, and the practical enforcement of anti-corruption laws.
