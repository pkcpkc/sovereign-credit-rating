# IMF Executive Board concludes Article IV consultation

## Event Details

The timeline of the 2025 IMF Article IV consultation for Austria reflects a structured assessment of the nation's economic trajectory following a period of recession. 

The process began in **September 2024** with the Austrian elections, followed by the formation of a new coalition government in **March 2025**. The formal IMF mission commenced on **March 26, 2025**, involving high-level discussions with key stakeholders, including the Ministry of Finance (represented by Marterbauer) and the Central Bank (led by Holzmann).

These discussions concluded on **April 8, 2025**, leading to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on **June 9, 2025**. The formal consultation was concluded by the IMF Executive Board on **July 3, 2025**. 

The resulting assessment highlighted a 1.3% GDP contraction in 2024 and projected near-zero growth for 2025, with a recovery expected in 2026. Key findings emphasized the need for "bold and well-designed" fiscal consolidation to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP, structural reforms to address demographic aging—specifically increasing labor participation among women and the elderly—and heightened vigilance regarding Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) in the commercial real estate sector. Additionally, the report noted the ongoing mutual evaluation of Austria's AML/CFT framework, with a target for completion in **February 2026**.
