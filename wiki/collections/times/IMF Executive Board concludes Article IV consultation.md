# IMF Executive Board concludes Article IV consultation

## Event Details

The timeline of the 2025 IMF Article IV consultations reflects a structured assessment of the economic trajectories for Austria, Malta, and the Slovak Republic.

### Austria Assessment
The process for Austria began in **September 2024** with national elections, followed by the formation of a new coalition government in **March 2025**. The formal IMF mission commenced on **March 26, 2025**, involving high-level discussions with the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank. These discussions concluded on **April 8, 2025**, leading to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on **June 9, 2025**. The formal consultation was officially concluded by the IMF Executive Board on **July 3, 2025**. The assessment highlighted a 1.3% GDP contraction in 2024 and projected near-zero growth for 2025, emphasizing the need for fiscal consolidation to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP and structural reforms to address demographic aging.

### Malta Assessment
Concurrently, the IMF conducted an Article IV consultation for Malta, which reached a milestone in **November 2024** with the end of economic policy discussions with Maltese officials. The IMF Staff Report for Malta was completed on **December 17, 2024**, and the IMF Executive Board concluded the consultation on **January 15, 2025**. The report noted Malta's robust growth driven by tourism and online gaming, while highlighting the need to transition toward a productivity-driven model. Key priorities identified include exiting fixed-energy price models, implementing Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II, and tightening macroprudential policies to manage high real estate exposure.

### Slovak Republic Assessment
The IMF also conducted a comprehensive review of the Slovak Republic. Following Slovakia's entry into the IMF in **2024** and the initiation of the Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP) by the European Council in **July 2024**, the 2025 consultation process unfolded rapidly. Staff discussions with Slovak officials concluded on **January 28, 2025**, with the Staff Report completed on **March 3, 2025**. Following board consideration on **March 18**, the IMF Executive Board officially concluded the Article IV consultation for Slovakia on **March 24, 2025**. The report noted a recovery to 2.0% growth in 2024 but warned of risks regarding high house prices, an aging population, and the need for a 4-year fiscal consolidation plan to reduce the headline deficit.

### Shared Themes and Future Outlook
Across all three nations, the assessments underscored critical regional priorities:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** Aggressive targets to reduce deficits, such as Slovakia's goal to stabilize public debt and Austria's push for a deficit below 2% of GDP.
- **Financial Stability:** Vigilance regarding Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) and systemic risks in the residential real estate sector, specifically noted in both Malta and Slovakia.
- **Structural & Governance Reforms:** Emphasis on improving judicial independence, anti-corruption strategies, and labor force expansion to counter demographic shifts.
- **Future Milestones:** Key upcoming dates include the expected completion of AML/CFT mutual evaluations in **February 2026** and the next scheduled general election in Malta in **2027**.
