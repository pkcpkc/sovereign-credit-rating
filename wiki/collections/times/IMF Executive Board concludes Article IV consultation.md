# IMF Executive Board concludes Article IV consultation

## Event Details

On March 24, 2025, the IMF Executive Board concluded the Article IV consultation for the Slovak Republic. This followed a series of discussions and staff reports culminating in an assessment of Slovakia's economic recovery, which saw growth accelerate to 2.0% in 2024.

The conclusion of the consultation highlighted several key pillars of the Slovak economy:
- **Fiscal Consolidation:** The board reviewed an ambitious 4-year plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP in 2025 and stabilizing public debt through expenditure-based rationalization.
- **Economic Outlook:** While growth is projected to moderate to 1.8% in 2025 due to consolidation measures, the IMF noted structural headwinds including geoeconomic fragmentation and an aging population.
- **Financial Stability:** The banking system was deemed resilient with high capital ratios, though the board advised monitoring the new Financial Transaction Tax (FTT) and real estate market vulnerabilities.
- **Structural Reforms:** The consultation emphasized the need for Slovakia to address "secular decline" in labor productivity by intensifying Active Labor Market Policies (ALMPs), increasing R&D spending, and strengthening anti-corruption frameworks.
