# IMF Executive Board concludes Article IV consultation

## Event Details

The timeline of the 2025 IMF Article IV consultations reflects a structured assessment of the economic trajectories for both Austria and Malta.

### Austria Assessment
The process for Austria began in **September 2024** with national elections, followed by the formation of a new coalition government in **March 2025**. The formal IMF mission commenced on **March 26, 2025**, involving high-level discussions with the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank. These discussions concluded on **April 8, 2025**, leading to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on **June 9, 2025**. The formal consultation was officially concluded by the IMF Executive Board on **July 3, 2025**. The assessment highlighted a 1.3% GDP contraction in 2024 and projected near-zero growth for 2025, emphasizing the need for fiscal consolidation to reduce the deficit below 2% of GDP and structural reforms to address demographic aging.

### Malta Assessment
Concurrently, the IMF conducted an Article IV consultation for Malta, which reached a milestone in **November 2024** with the end of economic policy discussions with Maltese officials. The IMF Staff Report for Malta was completed on **December 17, 2024**, and the IMF Executive Board concluded the consultation on **January 15, 2025**. The report noted Malta's robust growth driven by tourism and online gaming, while highlighting the need to transition toward a productivity-driven model. Key priorities identified include exiting fixed-energy price models, implementing Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reforms aligned with EU Pillar II, and tightening macroprudential policies to manage high real estate exposure (currently 70% of loan portfolios).

### Shared Themes and Future Outlook
Both assessments underscored critical areas of focus for the region:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** Efforts to reduce deficits and reallocate subsidies toward R&D and public services.
- **Financial Stability:** Vigilance regarding Non-Performing Loans (NPLs) and systemic risks in the real estate sector.
- **Governance:** Progress in AML/CFT frameworks and judicial reforms to bolster investor confidence.
- **Future Milestones:** Key upcoming dates include the expected completion of AML/CFT mutual evaluations in **February 2026** and the next scheduled general election in Malta in **2027**.
