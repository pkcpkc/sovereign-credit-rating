# Law abrogating the offence of abuse of public office enters into force

## Event Details

In **2025-08**, the law abrogating the offence of abuse of public office officially entered into force in [[Italy]]. This legislative change is part of a broader comprehensive reform of the justice system, which had been adopted by the Government in **2024-03** and implemented through various directives approved by the [[High Council for the Judiciary]] throughout late 2024 and early 2025.

The abrogation of this specific offence was previously declared compliant with the Constitution by the [[Constitutional Court]] in **2025-05**. The entry into force of the abrogation law in August 2025 marks a significant shift in the legal framework governing public officials, aligning with ongoing efforts to update the [[National Anti-Corruption Plan]] and strengthen anti-corruption institutions such as the [[National Anti-Corruption Authority (ANAC)]].

Key related developments in this period include:
- **2025-05**: The [[Constitutional Court]] declared the law abrogating abuse of office compliant with the Constitution.
- **2024-03**: Adoption of Justice System Reform by the Government.
- **2025-01**: [[National Anti-Corruption Authority (ANAC)]] approved an update to the [[National Anti-Corruption Plan]].
