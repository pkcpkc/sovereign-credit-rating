# Implementation of Sectoral Systemic Risk Buffer (SSyRB) for Commercial Real Estate

## Event Details

In July 2025, Austrian authorities implemented a Sectoral Systemic Risk Buffer (SSyRB) targeting the Commercial Real Estate (CRE) sector, setting the buffer at 1%. This regulatory measure was introduced to address rising non-performing loan (NPL) ratios on CRE exposures, which reached approximately 5% by mid-2024, despite the broader banking sector maintaining strong capitalization (CET1 ratio of 17.7%) and profitability. The implementation coincided with the expiration of borrower-based macroprudential measures (KIM-V regulation) for residential real estate, marking a strategic shift in Austria's macroprudential framework. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) welcomed this action as a prudent step to mitigate credit risks associated with the CRE sector and potential spillovers from Central, Eastern, and Southeastern Europe (CESEE). This decision followed an IMF Article IV Consultation concluded in July 2025, which highlighted the need for vigilance regarding financial stability risks amid a challenging economic environment characterized by recent recessions and fiscal deficits.
