# GDP contraction of 0.8% and inflation peak of 17.0%

## Event Details

In 2023, the Hungarian economy experienced a significant downturn characterized by a GDP contraction of 0.8% and a peak inflation rate of 17.0%. This period marked a sharp reversal from previous growth trajectories and set the stage for the subdued economic performance observed in subsequent years.

According to the European Commission’s 2025 Country Report on Hungary, this contraction was part of a broader challenging economic landscape. The severe inflationary pressure peaked at 17.0% in 2023 before declining significantly to 3.7% in 2024, though underlying price pressures and rising food costs caused inflation to pick up again in early 2025. The 2023 contraction preceded a period of stagnation, with GDP growing by only 0.5% in 2024, reflecting persistent structural vulnerabilities such as low labor productivity, high energy import dependence, and distortions in the business environment due to state intervention.
