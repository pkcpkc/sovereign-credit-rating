# Adoption of law amending NGO functioning acts

## Event Details

On **April 16, 2024**, Slovakia adopted a law amending acts related to the functioning of Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs). This legislative move is cited in the 2025 Rule of Law Report as a factor contributing to increased pressure on civil society organizations. The amendments introduced new reporting obligations for NGOs, which, combined with reduced funding for certain human rights advocates, has created a more constrained environment for civic engagement. This event is part of a broader trend identified by the European Commission, where frequent use of fast-track legislative procedures has bypassed adequate stakeholder consultation, undermining democratic accountability and the space for civil society.
