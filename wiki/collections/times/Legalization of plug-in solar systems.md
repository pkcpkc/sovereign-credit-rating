# Legalization of plug-in solar systems

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, the legalization of plug-in solar systems was enacted in Belgium as part of the broader energy transition and sustainability framework. This move aligns with the country's efforts to address high electricity-to-gas price ratios and promote renewable energy rollout. 

The initiative is situated within a complex economic landscape where Belgium aims to meet its 2030 climate targets while managing significant fiscal pressures, including a projected headline deficit of 5.4% of GDP and high public debt. By facilitating the adoption of electric solutions and solar technology, the policy seeks to mitigate high energy costs—a primary obstacle to private investment—and support the transition toward a green economy.
