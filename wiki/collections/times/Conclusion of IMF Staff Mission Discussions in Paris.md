# Conclusion of IMF Staff Mission Discussions in Paris

## Event Details

On **May 22, 2025**, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) concluded its staff mission discussions in Paris, marking a key phase of the 2025 Article IV Consultation with France. The mission, led by **Manuela Goretti**, preceded the finalization of the staff report on June 25, 2025, and the subsequent Executive Board conclusion on July 11, 2025.

During the mission, IMF staff engaged with senior French officials, including **Manuel Lombard** (Minister for the Economy, Finance, and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty), **Sébastien Lecornu** (Minister for Public Accounts, though referenced as de Montchalín in source roles, context implies key fiscal dialogue), **François Villeroy de Galhau** (Central Bank Governor of Banque de France), and Prime Minister **Michel Barnier**. The discussions focused on assessing France's economic resilience amidst high global uncertainty, specifically addressing risks related to **Geoeconomic Fragmentation**, trade tensions, and domestic policy uncertainty.

Key outcomes of the mission included:
- An assessment of France's **Fiscal Policy**, noting public debt had reached 113.1% of GDP in 2024, with a deficit of 5.8%. The IMF highlighted the need for credible structural fiscal measures to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029.
- A review of **Structural Reforms**, emphasizing the importance of boosting productivity through the green and digital transitions, including Artificial Intelligence (AI) adoption.
- A confirmation of **Financial Sector Health** via the 2025 Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP), which showed resilient banking capital and liquidity buffers, though risks from corporate indebtedness and commercial real estate remained.
- Discussions on **Debt Sustainability** and the Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP), with the French authorities reaffirming their commitment to spending reviews and regulatory simplification.

The mission concluded ahead of the formal completion of the staff report, setting the stage for the Executive Board's final assessment which welcomed France's economic resilience and robust labor market conditions.
