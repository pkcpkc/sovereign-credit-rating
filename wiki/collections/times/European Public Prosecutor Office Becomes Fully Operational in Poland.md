# European Public Prosecutor Office Becomes Fully Operational in Poland

## Event Details

On January 31, 2025, the [[European Public Prosecutor Office]] (EPPO) became fully operational in Poland. This milestone represents a significant advancement in the country's anti-corruption framework, aligning with broader efforts to strengthen institutional integrity and compliance with [[European Union]] mechanisms.

The operationalization of the EPPO is part of a wider government Action Plan aimed at addressing long-standing rule of law concerns. While this marks progress in combating corruption, the [[2025 Rule of Law Report]] notes that systemic risks remain, particularly regarding public procurement and the independence of the prosecution service. The government has also been drafting laws to separate the functions of the [[Minister of Justice]] from that of the [[Prosecutor General]] to ensure greater functional independence, although full implementation and legislative refinement are ongoing.

This development occurs amidst broader judicial tensions, including a crisis involving the [[Constitutional Tribunal]] and controversies surrounding the [[National Council for the Judiciary]]. Despite these challenges, the full operation of the EPPO is cited as a positive step in improving fiscal sustainability and financial sector health through better regulatory compliance and EU fund management.
