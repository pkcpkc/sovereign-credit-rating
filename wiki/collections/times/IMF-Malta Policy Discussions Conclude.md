# IMF-Malta Policy Discussions Conclude

## Event Details

On 15 November 2024, the **International Monetary Fund** (IMF) concluded its staff mission and policy discussions with **Malta** in Valletta. This event marked a critical phase in the 2024 Article IV Consultation, following the mission's start on 4 November 2024 and preceding the completion of the staff report on 17 December 2024.

The discussions focused on assessing **Malta**'s macroeconomic stability, fiscal performance, and financial sector health. Key outcomes of the concluded policy dialogue included:

- **Fiscal Consolidation:** Agreement on a trajectory to reduce the fiscal deficit to approximately 2¼ percent of GDP by 2029, with public debt projected to remain around 50 percent of GDP. A major point of discussion was the planned exit from fixed energy price subsidies to create fiscal space for investment in green infrastructure and innovation.
- **Monetary and Inflation Dynamics:** Acknowledgment that inflation had fallen to around 2 percent, aligning with Euro Area averages, despite persistent pressures in the service sector driven by tourism and tight labor markets.
- **Financial Sector Risks:** Recognition of a sound financial system with strong capital buffers, alongside identified risks from substantial bank exposure to real estate (residential, commercial, and construction). The IMF recommended tightening macroprudential policies, including raising sectoral systemic risk buffers.
- **Structural Reforms:** Emphasis on continuing reforms to boost productivity through innovation, digitalization, and education, supported by the "Malta Vision 2050" strategic framework. Discussions also covered **Anti-Money Laundering/Combating the Financing of Terrorism** (AML/CFT) framework strengthening and judicial efficiency improvements.

The conclusions drawn during these policy discussions informed the final Article IV Consultation Report, which was endorsed by the IMF Executive Board on 15 January 2025.
