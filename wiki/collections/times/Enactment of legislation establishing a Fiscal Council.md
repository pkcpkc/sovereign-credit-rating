# Enactment of legislation establishing a Fiscal Council

## Event Details

In December 2024, Poland enacted legislation to establish a [[Fiscal Council]], a key structural reform aimed at strengthening fiscal surveillance and governance. This institutional development is part of Poland's broader efforts to address its fiscal pressures, including a general government deficit that reached 5.3% of GDP in 2023, which exceeded the European Union's 3% reference value and triggered an [[Excessive Deficit Procedure]] initiated by the [[Council of the European Union]] in January 2025.

The newly established [[Fiscal Council]] was set to become fully operational in January 2026. Initially, it was designed to play an advisory role, supporting the government's compliance with EU fiscal rules. This measure contributes to Poland's medium-term fiscal-structural plan, which includes a binding expenditure path endorsed by the Council to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2028. The establishment of this body is highlighted as a strength in Poland's institutional and governance framework, alongside improved regulatory governance and digitalisation progress.
