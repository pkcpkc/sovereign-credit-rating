# Expected entry into force of the Freedom of Information Act

## Event Details

The Austrian government is currently preparing the implementation of a new Freedom of Information Act. This legislative move is intended to provide a subjective right to information from public authorities, enhancing transparency in governance.

Preparations for the act were highlighted in the EU Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report, which noted that the legislation is scheduled to enter into force in September 2025. This reform is part of a broader set of measures to strengthen institutional checks and balances, alongside other initiatives such as the "Justice 3.0" digitalization project and reforms to the ORF Act.
