# Implementation of Pillar 2 Directive on global minimum corporate tax

## Event Details

On **January 1, 2025**, Poland officially implemented the [[Pillar 2 Directive]] on global minimum corporate tax. This regulatory change aligns Poland with international standards aimed at ensuring multinational enterprises pay a minimum level of tax. The implementation is part of broader structural and fiscal adjustments highlighted in the [[EU Commission (2025) 2025 Country Report: Poland]], which notes the complex tax framework in Poland as a factor hampering innovation and competitiveness. While the directive aims to standardize corporate taxation, the report emphasizes that Poland's overall tax system remains highly complex, ranking last in the EU for tax complexity, which continues to pose challenges for investment and regulatory stability.
