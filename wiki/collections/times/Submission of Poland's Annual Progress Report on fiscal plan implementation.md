# Submission of Poland's Annual Progress Report on fiscal plan implementation

## Event Details

On April 30, 2025, Poland submitted its Annual Progress Report on the implementation of its medium-term fiscal-structural plan to the European Commission. This submission occurred within the context of the **[[Excessive Deficit Procedure]]** (EDP) initiated by the Council of the European Union on January 21, 2025, following the endorsement of Poland’s fiscal plan.

The report detailed Poland's efforts to address a general government deficit of 6.6% of GDP in 2024, which exceeded the EU Treaty reference value of 3%. The fiscal consolidation strategy is complicated by high and rising defense spending (projected to reach 2.8% of GDP in 2025) and the need for sustained public investment. The progress report serves as a key monitoring tool to assess whether Poland is on track to reduce its deficit below the 3% threshold by 2028, utilizing the flexibility provided by the "national escape clause" under the new EU fiscal framework.

This event is part of a broader sequence of fiscal and institutional developments in Poland, including:
- The enactment of legislation establishing the independent **[[Fiscal Council]]** on December 31, 2024, with projected operational start by June 30, 2025.
- The publication of the EU Commission’s 2025 Country Report on Poland on June 4, 2025, which highlighted both accelerating economic growth (2.9% GDP growth in 2024) and structural challenges such as low labor productivity and complex regulatory environments.
- The implementation of the Pillar 2 Directive on global minimum corporate tax effective January 1, 2025.
