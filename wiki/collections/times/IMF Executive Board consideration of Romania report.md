# IMF Executive Board consideration of Romania report

## Event Details

The 2025 Article IV consultation process for Romania involved a series of key milestones beginning with the IMF mission discussions which commenced on **2025-09-03** and concluded on **2025-09-12**. Following these discussions, the IMF Staff Report was finalized on **2025-10-15**.

The formal review culminated in the IMF Executive Board consideration of the Romania report on **2025-11-07**, followed by the official press release marking the conclusion of the Article IV Consultation on **2025-11-14**.

The consultation highlighted several critical economic milestones and projections:
- **2026-03**: The anticipated removal of the natural gas price cap, expected to contribute to inflationary pressures.
- **2026-08**: The deadline for identifying project feasibility regarding Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds.
- **2027-01-01**: The projected commencement of additional fiscal adjustment measures and the implementation of the ETS 2 for buildings and road transport.

Key findings from the period include Romania's struggle with "twin deficits," high inflation (9.9% in September 2025), and the need for structural reforms to improve labor force participation and EU fund absorption. The report specifically notes a resilient banking system despite the "sovereign-bank nexus" and emphasizes the necessity of fiscal consolidation to stabilize public debt by 2030.
