# High Council for the Judiciary approves directive on out-of-tenure appointments for magistrates

## Event Details

On 25 September 2024, the High Council for the Judiciary of Italy approved a directive concerning out-of-tenure appointments for magistrates. This decision was part of a broader comprehensive reform of the justice system adopted by the Government in March 2024. The directive aimed to implement specific provisions regarding the appointment and tenure of judges, contributing to the ongoing efforts to enhance judicial independence and efficiency.

This event occurred alongside other significant judicial reforms, including the approval of a directive regarding the appraisal of magistrates' performance on 13 November 2024, and the approval of the Code of Judicial Management on 3 December 2024. These measures were intended to address persistent staff shortages and improve the functioning of court levels, although challenges regarding political financing and conflicts of interest remained.
