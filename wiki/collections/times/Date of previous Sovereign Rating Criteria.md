# Date of previous Sovereign Rating Criteria

## Event Details

The previous iteration of the **Fitch Ratings Sovereign Rating Criteria** was effective starting on **2024-10-24**. This methodology preceded the current framework, which became effective on **15 September 2025**.

The 2024 criteria served as the basis for sovereign credit assessments until the updated framework was introduced, marking a transition in how Fitch Ratings evaluates sovereign issuers' capacity and willingness to honor debt obligations. The update in September 2025 introduced significant refinements, including enhanced integration of **Climate Vulnerability Signals (Climate.VS)** and adjusted weightings within the **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM)**, particularly increasing the prominence of Structural Features. The period from the previous criteria's start date (2024-10-24) to the current criteria's effective date (2025-09-15) represents the operational window for the former methodology.
