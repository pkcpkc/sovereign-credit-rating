# IMF Staff Mission Discussions (September 3–12)

## Event Details

From September 3 to 12, 2025, a mission from the [[International Monetary Fund]] (IMF) held discussions with Romanian authorities as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. The mission was led by [[Joong Shik Kang]], with participation from IMF staff members including [[Ezequiel Cabezon]], [[Nan Geng]], [[Fazurin Jamaludin]], [[Tina Kang]], [[Cheryl Li]], and [[Maciej Onoszko]]. The team included [[Carlos Granados]] as Senior Resident Representative and [[Georgia Babici]] as Local Economist. Oversight was provided by IMF Executive Directors [[Helge Berger]], [[Bergljot Barkbu]], [[Clyde Caruana]], and [[Clicq]].

The discussions focused on Romania's macroeconomic stability, sovereign credit profile, and the implementation of recent fiscal consolidation measures. Key topics included the execution of a comprehensive fiscal reform package adopted in July 2025, which included VAT rate hikes and excise increases aimed at reducing the fiscal deficit to below 6 percent of GDP in 2026. The IMF mission engaged with Romanian officials, including Prime Minister [[Ilie Bolojan]], Finance Minister [[Alexandru Nazare]], and National Bank of Romania Governor [[Mugur Isarescu]].

The mission highlighted significant risks, including a sovereign credit rating downgrade due to execution uncertainties and high current fiscal deficits. Inflation was noted to have spiked to 9.9 percent in September 2025 following the removal of energy price caps and VAT increases, though it is projected to return to the National Bank of Romania’s target band by end-2026. The IMF emphasized the need for prudent monetary policy, accelerated structural reforms to unlock EU funds (NGEU), and measures to address vulnerabilities in the banking sector, particularly regarding unhedged foreign exchange loans and the sovereign-bank nexus.
