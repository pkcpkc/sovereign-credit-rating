# Principles Of Credit Ratings published

## Event Details

On February 16, 2011, the **Principles Of Credit Ratings** were published, establishing a foundational framework for credit assessment. This milestone was part of a broader evolution in rating methodologies, eventually leading to the comprehensive **S&P Global Ratings: Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)**.

The evolution of these principles highlights a shift toward sophisticated risk modeling, including:
- **Methodology Refinements:** Progressing from the 2013 Monetary Authorities Rating Methodology and the 2014 Previous Sovereign Rating Methodology to the 2017 standards.
- **Metric Evolution:** A transition toward focusing on **net general government debt** and incorporating **Current Account Payments (CAP)** into external assessment ratios.
- **Risk Quantification:** The integration of the **Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA)** to quantify contingent liabilities and systemic risks from the banking sector.
- **Structural Adjustments:** Refining local-currency caps and establishing criteria for "notching" based on fiscal flexibility, monetary independence, and external liquidity.
