# Targeted Inflation Return to 3.0%

## Event Details

The Hungarian economy is navigating a challenging period of stagnant output and elevated inflation. According to the IMF's 2025 Article IV Consultation, while disinflationary trends are underway, core inflation is projected to remain high (4.5% in Q4:2025). 

A key milestone in the economic recovery plan is the **Targeted Inflation Return to 3.0% by 2027**. To achieve this target, the IMF emphasizes several critical policy pillars:

*   **Monetary Policy:** The [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB)]] is advised to maintain a restrictive base rate (currently 6.5%) to anchor inflation expectations through a data-dependent approach.
*   **Fiscal Adjustment:** A cumulative fiscal adjustment of approximately 2% of GDP is recommended between 2025 and 2028 to bring the headline deficit below 3% by 2027.
*   **Structural Reforms:** Addressing productivity gaps, removing barriers to factor mobility, and phasing out unsustainable price and margin caps are considered essential for long-term price stability.
*   **Subsidy Rationalization:** The phase-out of retail energy subsidies and housing-related fiscal incentives is highlighted as a necessary step to reduce market distortions.

The success of this transition faces risks from geoeconomic fragmentation, high refinancing costs, and potential delays in implementing governance reforms required to unlock EU funding.
