# Introduction of new agency regulation and skills pass for non-EU nationals

## Event Details

In 2024, Malta introduced new agency regulations and a specific "skills pass" mechanism aimed at non-EU nationals. This policy adjustment was implemented within the broader context of addressing severe labor shortages and skills mismatches identified in key sectors such as Information and Communication Technology (ICT), construction, and healthcare.

The initiative reflects the Maltese government's response to a structural constraint on economic competitiveness: despite an exceptionally low unemployment rate of 3.1%, domestic labor supply proved insufficient to meet the demands of a strong economy driven by tourism, financial services, and digital exports. By streamlining regulatory frameworks for non-EU workers possessing specific skills, Malta aimed to mitigate the negative impacts of these labor shortages while maintaining high immigration flows to support continued GDP growth projected at 4.0-4.1% through 2026. This move aligns with the country's broader structural reforms under the Recovery and Resilience Plan, which emphasizes social inclusion and the integration of vulnerable groups, including non-EU nationals.
