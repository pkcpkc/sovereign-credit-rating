# Conclusion of IMF staff discussions

## Event Details

The conclusion of IMF staff discussions represents a critical milestone in the Article IV Consultation process for both Poland and Romania, marking the transition from field assessment to formal reporting and board review.

### Republic of Poland
The IMF staff discussions regarding the Republic of Poland's 2024 Article IV Consultation concluded on October 17, 2024. These discussions followed the initial start of the process in Warsaw on October 8, 2024. The conclusion marked a critical phase in assessing Poland's economic recovery, which is projected to accelerate to 3.5% in 2025-2026 driven by domestic demand and the absorption of Next Generation EU (NGEU) funds. The staff analysis highlighted a widening fiscal deficit of approximately 5.9% of GDP in 2024 due to public sector wage increases and defense spending, placing the country under the EU's Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP). Following these discussions, the process moved toward the completion of the formal IMF Staff Report on December 16, 2024, with subsequent Executive Board consideration in January 2025 and a final press release on January 21, 2025.

### Romania
In a separate consultation for Romania, the IMF staff discussions with Romanian officials concluded on September 12, 2025. This process followed the start of discussions on September 3, 2025. The staff analysis for Romania focused on addressing "twin deficits" (fiscal and current account) through a large consolidation package, including VAT rate increases to 21% and a freeze on public sector wages/pensions in 2026. Key findings included:
*   **Economic Outlook:** Subdued growth (0.9% in 2024) and elevated inflation (9.9% in Sept 2025).
*   **Fiscal Targets:** A goal to reduce the fiscal deficit to below 6% of GDP in 2026 and eventually below 3% by 2030.
*   **Financial Stability:** While the banking sector remains robust, concerns were noted regarding the "sovereign-bank nexus" (26% of bank assets in sovereign bonds) and unhedged FX loans.
*   **Structural Reform:** Emphasis on accelerating the absorption of NGEU funds (currently at 38%) and improving labor force participation.

Following the conclusion of the Romanian staff discussions, the process proceeded to the completion of the IMF Staff Report on October 15, 2025, Executive Board consideration on November 7, 2025, and the final press release of the Article IV consultation results on November 14, 2025.
