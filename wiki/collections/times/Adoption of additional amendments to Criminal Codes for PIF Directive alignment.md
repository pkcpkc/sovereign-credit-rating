# Adoption of additional amendments to Criminal Codes for PIF Directive alignment

## Event Details

In December 2024, Slovakia adopted additional amendments to its Criminal Codes specifically aimed at aligning national legislation with the EU's Protection of the Financial Interests (PIF) Directive. 

While these legislative changes were intended to strengthen the framework for combating financial crimes and ensuring compliance with EU standards, they have drawn scrutiny from various stakeholders. Specifically, critics and legal observers have raised concerns regarding the "chilling effect" these amendments may have on the judiciary. The primary concern is that the lack of sufficient safeguards within the updated criminal liability framework for judges could discourage independent decision-making. 

Despite these legislative efforts to align with EU directives, the broader Rule of Law context in Slovakia remains complex, characterized by ongoing debates over judicial independence and the balance between anti-corruption measures and the protection of legal practitioners.
