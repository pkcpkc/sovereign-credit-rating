# Amendments to Freedom of Information Act coming into force

## Event Details

In March 2025, Slovakia implemented amendments to its Freedom of Information Act, marking a significant development in the nation's transparency and anti-corruption framework. This legislative update was part of a broader effort by the Slovak government to align domestic regulations with European Union standards, specifically ensuring compliance with the Protection of Financial Interests (PIF) Directive.

The enactment of these amendments occurred amidst a period of heightened scrutiny regarding judicial independence and public sector transparency. While the government engaged in dialogue with the European Commission to address systemic risks, the introduction of these information access reforms was viewed as a countermeasure to concerns about fragmented oversight and political interference in administrative processes. The timing of this legislative change coincided with other significant legal adjustments, including the adoption of laws affecting non-governmental organizations and the restructuring of public service media, reflecting a complex regulatory environment characterized by both alignment with EU directives and domestic political polarization.
