# Council endorsement of France's medium-term fiscal-structural plan

## Event Details

On **2025-01-21**, the Council of the European Union officially endorsed France's medium-term fiscal-structural plan. This endorsement was a pivotal step under the reformed EU economic governance framework, replacing the previous excessive deficit procedure with a structured seven-year fiscal adjustment period. The Council’s decision coincided with a recommendation to end France's excessive deficit situation, acknowledging the government's commitment to bringing public finances back on a sustainable path.

The plan, originally submitted by the French government on **2024-10-31**, outlines a roadmap to address France's persistently high general government deficits (recorded at 5.8% of GDP in 2024) and rising public debt (113% of GDP). The endorsed plan mandates strict adherence to net expenditure growth limits and requires robust spending reviews to ensure fiscal sustainability amidst political fragmentation following the 2024 elections.

The endorsement comes at a time of significant economic headwinds for France, with GDP growth forecast to slow to 0.6% in 2025 and structural challenges regarding labor productivity, energy transition delays, and high administrative burdens. The Council emphasized that while the fiscal path is accepted, the timely implementation of supporting structural reforms remains critical. A deadline for implementing reforms underpinning this fiscal adjustment was set for **2025-04-30**, underscoring the urgency for the French administration to align its budgetary measures with the endorsed framework despite the fragmented political landscape.
