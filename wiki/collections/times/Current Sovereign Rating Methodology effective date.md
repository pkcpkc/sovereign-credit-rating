# Current Sovereign Rating Methodology effective date

## Event Details

On **2017-12-18**, S&P Global Ratings implemented the **Current Sovereign Rating Methodology**. This update established a comprehensive framework for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness across five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments.

Key transitions in this methodology included:
- **Fiscal Metrics:** A shift toward focusing on **net general government debt** (debt minus liquid financial assets) and the integration of **Current Account Payments (CAP)** into external assessment ratios.
- **Local-Currency Caps:** A restriction on local-currency ratings, limiting them to no more than one notch above the foreign-currency rating.
- **Risk Quantification:** The formal integration of **Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA)** scores to quantify contingent liabilities from the banking sector and nonfinancial public-sector enterprises.
- **Scenario Adjustments:** Specific protocols for monetary unions, countries with limited data (utilizing "host" country assessments), and supplemental adjustments for extreme debt burdens or exceptional liquid buffers.
