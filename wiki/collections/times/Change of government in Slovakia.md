# Change of Government in Slovakia

## Event Details

On **October 2024**, Slovakia underwent a significant political transition resulting in a Change of Government. This event occurred against the backdrop of intense political polarization, which the International Monetary Fund (IMF) identified as a key threat to policy predictability and reform continuity.

The political shift was characterized by the following dynamics:

*   **Political Polarization**: The environment was described as highly polarized, complicating the policymaking process and contributing to a perception of governance weakness.
*   **Governance Concerns**: The transition highlighted vulnerabilities related to institutional quality and corruption risks. These concerns had already begun to impact the timely disbursement of European Union (EU) funds, creating pressure for structural reforms.
*   **Key Figures**: The post-election political landscape involved key actors including **Kažimír** (Governor of the [[National Bank of Slovakia]]), **Kamenický** (Minister of Finance), and **Taraba** (Minister of the Environment).
*   **Policy Implications**: The IMF noted that the implementation of policy advice following this change was "mixed," raising risks regarding fiscal consolidation slippages. However, the subsequent administration committed to ambitious fiscal consolidation targets for 2025–2028, aiming to stabilize public debt and reduce the fiscal deficit.

This event is closely linked to broader macroeconomic challenges, including high exposure to automotive sector shocks, demographic headwinds, and the need for judicial independence reforms to strengthen institutional resilience.
