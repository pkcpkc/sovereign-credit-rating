# Adoption of law protecting whistleblowers in the House of Representatives and Senate

## Event Details

On 27 December 2024, the Belgian [[House of Representatives]] and [[Senate]] adopted legislation aimed at protecting whistleblowers within the parliamentary context. This legislative step was highlighted in the 2025 [[Rule of Law Report]] for Belgium, issued by the [[European Commission]].

While general whistleblower protection mechanisms are largely in place for other civil servants, this specific law addresses the parliamentary sphere. However, reports indicate that the scope of protection for members of parliament and staff under this law is narrower than that extended to other categories of civil servants. This development occurs within a broader context of ongoing efforts to strengthen integrity frameworks, including reviews of asset declarations and lobbying regulations, though comprehensive transparency reforms remain a subject of political debate.
