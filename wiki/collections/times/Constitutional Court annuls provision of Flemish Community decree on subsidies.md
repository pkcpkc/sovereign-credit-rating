# Constitutional Court annuls provision of Flemish Community decree on subsidies

## Event Details

On **2024-09-19**, the [[Constitutional Court]] of Belgium annulled a specific provision within a decree issued by the [[Flemish Community]] concerning subsidies.

### Context and Significance
This ruling was highlighted in the **2025 Rule of Law Report: Country Chapter on Belgium** by the [[European Commission]] as a key development regarding civic space and institutional checks. The annulment is part of a broader context where the Civic Space in Belgium is rated as "narrowed."

The decision coincides with other significant judicial interventions in late 2024, including:
*   A subsequent ruling on **2024-11-14** regarding urgent court orders and strikes.
*   Broader concerns cited by the European Commission regarding "obstacles to the right to demonstrate," including proposals for judicial demonstration bans against individuals convicted of acts during protests.
