# Introduction of PIT-0 tax relief for senior citizens

## Event Details

In 2022, Poland introduced the PIT-0 tax relief specifically designed for senior citizens. This fiscal measure is part of a broader national context involving significant demographic shifts, including a rapidly aging population and a shrinking working-age population. While the country has faced challenges such as a complex tax framework—ranking last among EU Member States in the Tax Complexity Index 2022—the introduction of specific reliefs like PIT-0 serves as a targeted social policy to address the needs of the elderly. These measures are implemented alongside broader fiscal adjustments aimed at managing an excessive deficit and modernizing the social welfare system to ensure social fairness.
