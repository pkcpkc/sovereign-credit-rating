# Forecasted GDP growth of 1.4%

## Event Details

In the economic outlook for Slovakia provided in the **2025 Country Report**, the GDP growth is forecast to reach 1.4% in 2026. This projection reflects a decelerating trend from the 1.5% growth expected in 2025 and the 2.1% growth recorded in 2024.

The slowdown is attributed to several structural and external factors:
- **Trade Tensions:** Ongoing global trade pressures impacting export stability.
- **Inflationary Pressures:** High inflation, expected to reach 4.0% in 2025, continues to weigh on economic momentum.
- **Fiscal Constraints:** While the government is undergoing consolidation, high spending on pensions and healthcare due to an aging population creates long-term sustainability risks.
- **Productivity Gaps:** A significant innovation gap and a failure of labor productivity to keep pace with unit labor costs hinder the transition from a low-cost manufacturing model to a high-value, innovation-driven economy.

Despite these headwinds, the growth remains part of a broader fiscal strategy to manage a government debt-to-GDP ratio expected to rise to 63.0% by 2026.
