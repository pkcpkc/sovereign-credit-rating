# Expected launch of online register for foreign lobbyists by HATVP

## Event Details

**Date**: July 2025 (Expected)

In July 2025, the [[High Authority for Transparency in Public Life]] (HATVP) is expected to launch an online register specifically for foreign lobbyists. This initiative is part of France's broader anti-corruption framework and institutional strengthening efforts highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report.

The launch addresses a specific gap in lobbying transparency while operating within a system that remains partially incomplete. While the HATVP has strengthened its institutional capacity—including a significant increase in staff to handle a 48% rise in asset declarations—the disclosure requirements for lobbying do not yet extend to officials at the top-executive level. The new register aims to enhance visibility into foreign influence operations, complementing other measures such as the strengthening of the [[Anti-Corruption Agency]] (AFA) and the adoption of the national anti-corruption plan (2024-2029). This event underscores the ongoing tension between enhancing legal transparency frameworks and addressing practical implementation gaps in political accountability.
