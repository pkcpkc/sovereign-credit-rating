# Publication of 2025 Country Report

## Event Details

The chronological progression of the economic and fiscal landscape for Slovakia, as detailed in the 2025 Country Report, highlights a period of transition and structural challenge:

- **2024**: Slovakia recorded an economic growth of 2.1%, primarily driven by household and public consumption, despite a contraction in investment. During this year, the unemployment rate reached historic lows, though the dismantling of specialized anti-corruption units raised concerns regarding institutional integrity.
- **2025-01-21**: The Council adopted key fiscal and structural plans aimed at managing the national economy.
- **2025-06-04**: The official publication of the **2025 Country Report - Slovakia** by the European Commission. The report notes a forecasted GDP growth of 1.5% for 2025, tempered by trade tensions and a projected inflation rise to 4.0%. It identifies critical gaps in R&D intensity (1% vs. the EU average of 2.2%) and a significant innovation gap.
- **2025-06-30**: Deadline established for the submission of the Social Climate Plan.
- **2026**: Economic projections indicate a continued slowdown with a forecasted GDP growth of 1.4%. The report anticipates a rise in government debt-to-GDP to 63.0% by this period due to permanent spending measures like the 13th pension payment.

The overarching narrative reflects a shift from a low-cost manufacturing model toward a high-value, innovation-driven economy, necessitated by severe skills shortages in healthcare and construction, a high tax wedge on lower-income earners, and the urgent need for decarbonization in the manufacturing sector.
