# Romania GDP growth decelerated to 0.8%

## Event Details

In 2024, Romania experienced a significant economic slowdown, with real GDP growth decelerating to 0.8% from a previous rate of 2.4% in 2023. This contraction was primarily driven by weak net exports and persistent high inflation pressures. 

The economic landscape in 2024 was further complicated by fiscal challenges, as the public deficit rose to 9.3% of GDP due to substantial wage and pension increases. During this period, nominal wages rose by 15%, a growth rate that significantly outpaced productivity gains and began to erode the country's external cost competitiveness. Additionally, while government debt remained below the EU average at nearly 55% of GDP by the end of 2024, it was noted as rising rapidly, contributing to the broader macroeconomic imbalances identified in the 2025 Country Report.
