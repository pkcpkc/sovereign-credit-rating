# Publication of Flash Missions Reports on Justice Efficiency

## Event Details

On 13 May 2025, the [[French Government]] published flash mission reports concerning justice efficiency as part of broader judicial reforms. These reports were produced to assess the performance and operational capacity of the justice system, particularly in light of ongoing digitalization efforts such as the expansion of the *Portalis* tool and the implementation of the 2023 law reforming the status of magistrates.

The publication occurred within a context where judicial efficiency remained a significant concern for the [[European Commission]]. Despite digital advancements, average resolution times for civil and commercial cases remained high at 354 days in 2023, with bribery cases taking an average of 1,335 days. The flash missions aimed to identify bottlenecks and measure the impact of recent structural changes, including the creation of 360° evaluations for court presidents and the defense of judicial independence by the [[Superior Council of the Magistracy]].

This event is categorized under the broader review of [[Checks and Balances]] and [[Judicial Independence]] in France, reflecting ongoing EU scrutiny regarding the quality and accessibility of the French justice system.
