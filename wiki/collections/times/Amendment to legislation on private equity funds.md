# Amendment to legislation on private equity funds

## Event Details

In November 2024, Hungary implemented an amendment to legislation regarding private equity funds. This legislative action is part of a broader regulatory landscape characterized by frequent amendments and a surge in decrees over laws, which has created an unpredictable environment for long-term business planning. 

Following these legislative changes, the government has planned the introduction of a dedicated database for private equity funds, which is scheduled to become available on July 1, 2026. This initiative aims to address transparency and structural issues within the investment environment, where venture capital investment has seen significant drops and market capitalization remains underdeveloped compared to the EU average.
