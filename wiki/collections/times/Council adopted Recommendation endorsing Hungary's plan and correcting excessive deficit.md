# Council adopted Recommendation endorsing Hungary's plan and correcting excessive deficit

## Event Details

In early 2025, the European Union's governing bodies moved to address Hungary's fiscal trajectory. On **2025-02-18**, the Council adopted a formal Recommendation endorsing Hungary's medium-term fiscal-structural plan, specifically aimed at correcting the country's excessive deficit. This followed the submission of the plan in late 2024, which was designed to address high public debt—which reached 73.5% of GDP in 2024—and unsustainable debt-servicing costs.

The process culminated on **2025-03-18** with the formal endorsement of the Council Recommendation on Hungary's medium-term fiscal-structural plan. This move was critical given the 2025 Country Report's findings regarding Hungary's expansionary budgetary bias and the lack of transparent multiannual spending ceilings. The endorsement serves as a framework to stabilize Hungary's fiscal position while navigating high inflation and structural vulnerabilities in the labor market and energy sector.
