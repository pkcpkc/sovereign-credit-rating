# King sanctions whistleblower protection law

## Event Details

On March 13, 2025, the House of Representatives adopted a new whistleblower protection law. This legislative milestone was subsequently sanctioned by the King on March 27, 2025, formalizing the legal framework for protecting individuals who report misconduct. This development is part of a broader effort to strengthen the rule of law and anti-corruption measures in Belgium.
