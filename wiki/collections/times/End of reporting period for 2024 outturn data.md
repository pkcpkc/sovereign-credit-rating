# End of reporting period for 2024 outturn data

## Event Details

The date marks the conclusion of the reporting period for the **2024 outturn data**, which serves as the foundational dataset for the [[European Commission]]'s **2025 Country Report - Malta**. This data release underpins the macroeconomic and fiscal assessments for Malta within the [[European Semester]] framework.

Key outturns and assessments derived from this period include:

*   **Economic Performance**: Real GDP grew by **6.0%** in 2024, driven by robust domestic demand and exports in the service sector (tourism, iGaming, financial services).
*   **Labor Market**: The employment rate reached **83.0%**, one of the highest in the EU, though this relies heavily on immigration to address severe structural skills shortages and mismatches in sectors like ICT, construction, and healthcare.
*   **Fiscal Position**: The general government deficit stood at **3.7%** of GDP, with a debt-to-GDP ratio of **47.4%**, well below the EU average and the 60% treaty reference value.
*   **Inflation**: Inflation remained low and stable at **2.4%**, aligned with ECB targets.
*   **Structural Indicators**:
    *   Tax revenue remained low at **26.6%** of GDP (vs EU average of 39%).
    *   R&D intensity was recorded at **0.64%** of GDP.
    *   Renewable energy share in electricity generation was **17%**.

This data informs subsequent policy recommendations, including the endorsement of Malta's Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan and the monitoring of [[Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF) Framework]] implementation.
