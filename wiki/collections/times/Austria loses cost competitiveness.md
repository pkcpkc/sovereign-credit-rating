# Austria loses cost competitiveness

## Event Details

Starting in 2019, Austria began experiencing a significant decline in industrial competitiveness due to rising cost pressures. Specifically, nominal unit labor costs in the country have risen 10% more than the Euro area average since this period. 

This trend has been compounded by broader economic challenges, including:
- **Persistent Inflation:** While inflation peaked in 2022, it is projected to remain above 2% in 2025 due to the phase-out of energy relief measures.
- **Energy and Labor Costs:** Broad-based cost pressures have hindered industrial production, contributing to a three-year recessionary trend where GDP is projected to contract by 0.3% in 2025.
- **Regulatory Hurdles:** High administrative burdens and complex permitting procedures (taking up to 22-23 months for industrial projects) further exacerbate the difficulty of maintaining a competitive business environment.
