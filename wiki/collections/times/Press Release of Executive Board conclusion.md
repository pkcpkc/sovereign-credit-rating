# Press Release of Executive Board Conclusion

## Event Details

On July 22, 2025, the IMF issued the press release regarding the Executive Board's conclusion of the Article IV Consultation for Italy. This announcement follows a structured timeline of analysis and consultation:

- **May 14, 2025:** Initiation of IMF Staff Discussions in Rome.
- **May 28, 2025:** Conclusion of staff discussions with Italian officials.
- **July 1, 2025:** Completion of the formal IMF Staff Report.
- **July 18, 2025:** Executive Board consideration of the Italy Staff Report.
- **July 22, 2025:** Official Press Release of the Executive Board Conclusion.

The conclusion highlights Italy's economic resilience, including a return to a primary surplus and record employment levels (62.7% in April 2025). However, the Board emphasized critical structural challenges, specifically weak productivity growth, demographic headwinds (increasing old-age dependency), and the need for a primary surplus target of 3% by 2027 to manage public debt dynamics. The report also noted significant progress on the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), with 54% of milestones achieved by the end of 2024.
