# Press Release of Executive Board conclusion

## Event Details

The timeline for the [[IMF]] 2025 Article IV Consultation regarding [[Italy]] culminated in the official press release of the Executive Board's conclusion on July 22, 2025. This milestone followed a structured series of evaluations beginning on May 14, 2025, with the start of staff discussions in Rome.

The process involved a deep dive into Italy's economic resilience, which showed 0.7% growth in 2024 and a successful return to a primary surplus of 0.4% of GDP. Key focus areas during these discussions included the implementation of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP), where Italy emerged as a top EU performer in milestone completion.

The final assessment, considered by the Executive Board on July 18, highlighted several critical areas for the Italian government:
- **Fiscal Discipline:** While the headline deficit was halved to 3.4% in 2024, the board emphasized the need for a more aggressive target of a 3% primary surplus by 2027.
- **Structural Reforms:** The report underscored the urgency of addressing low productivity growth, demographic headwinds (specifically the aging population), and the need to boost female labor force participation.
- **Financial Stability:** The Board noted the sound nature of the banking sector and welcomed the activation of a 1% Systemic Risk Buffer (SyRB).
- **Sovereign Risk:** Italy's risk of sovereign stress was assessed as "Moderate," balanced by long debt maturities despite high debt-to-GDP levels (135.3% in 2024).

The conclusion reached on July 22 serves as the formal guidance for Italy's fiscal and structural trajectory, urging a shift from hiring subsidies toward productivity-enhancing measures and a faster transition to renewables.
