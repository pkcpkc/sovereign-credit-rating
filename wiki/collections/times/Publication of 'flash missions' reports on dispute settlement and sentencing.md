# Publication of 'flash missions' reports on dispute settlement and sentencing

## Event Details

On **2025-05-13**, the reports on 'flash missions' regarding dispute settlement and sentencing were published as part of the broader evaluation of the French justice system. 

These reports contribute to a period of significant legislative and administrative reform in France, which includes:
- The implementation of the **2023 law reforming magistrate status**, which removed age limits for admissions and introduced a 360° evaluation process for court presidents.
- Efforts to digitalize judicial procedures through the *Portalis* tool, aiming for universal online access to criminal court decisions by the end of 2025.
- The ongoing development of the **2024-2029 national anti-corruption plan** and the reorganization of the Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA).
- Ongoing efforts to address efficiency challenges, particularly in bribery cases which currently face some of the longest resolution times in the EU.
