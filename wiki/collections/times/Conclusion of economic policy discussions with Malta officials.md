# Conclusion of economic policy discussions with Malta officials

## Event Details

Between November 4 and November 15, 2024, a series of economic policy discussions were held in Valletta, Malta. These consultations involved key stakeholders from the [[International Monetary Fund (IMF)]], the Ministry of Finance and Employment, and the Central Bank of Malta.

The discussions focused on several critical pillars for Malta's economic stability and long-term growth:
- **Fiscal Policy:** The transition away from fixed energy price subsidies to redirect fiscal space toward public services and innovation.
- **Financial Stability:** Addressing the significant real estate exposure in the banking sector (comprising 70% of loan portfolios) through tightened macroprudential policies and the potential raising of the sectoral systemic risk buffer (sSyRB).
- **Structural Reforms:** The development of a roadmap for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) reform to align with the EU’s Pillar II Minimum Tax Directive.
- **Growth Strategy:** Moving toward a productivity-driven growth model to mitigate capacity constraints caused by high population density and infrastructure strain.

These discussions served as the foundation for the 2024 Article IV Consultation report, which ultimately concluded on January 15, 2025.
