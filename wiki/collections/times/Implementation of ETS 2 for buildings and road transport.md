# Implementation of ETS 2 for buildings and road transport

## Event Details

On **2027-01-01**, Romania is scheduled to implement the **ETS 2 (Emission Trading System)** specifically targeting buildings and road transport. This initiative is a key component of the country's transition toward a low-carbon economy and is supported by updated National Energy Strategies.

The implementation serves as a critical mechanism for:
*   **Carbon Pricing:** Strengthening the price on carbon to incentivize private investment in renewable energy and energy efficiency.
*   **Fiscal Sustainability:** Aligning with broader macroeconomic goals to stabilize the economy while addressing environmental targets.
*   **Structural Reform:** Integrating green transition goals into the national framework to ensure medium-term sustainability.

This move is part of a larger suite of reforms discussed in the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Romania**, which emphasizes the need for aggressive structural reforms and a robust green transition to successfully absorb **Next Generation EU (NGEU)** funds.
