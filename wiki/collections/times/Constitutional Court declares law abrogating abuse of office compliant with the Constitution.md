# Constitutional Court declares law abrogating abuse of office compliant with the Constitution

## Event Details

In May 2025, the [[Constitutional Court]] of Italy issued a ruling declaring that the law abrogating the offence of "abuse of public office" (abuso d’ufficio) is compliant with the Constitution. This legal development followed the entry into force of the abrogation law in August 2025, which had been part of a broader judicial reform package aimed at decriminalizing certain administrative violations committed by public officials.

The ruling is a significant component of Italy's wider judicial reform implemented between 2024 and 2025. The [[High Council for the Judiciary]] had been tasked with implementing various directives, including those on magistrate appointments and performance appraisals, as part of a comprehensive justice system overhaul. The abrogation of the abuse of office charge was intended to reduce the criminal liability of public administrators for administrative errors, a move supported by the government to improve the business environment and reduce judicial backlog.

However, the reform faced scrutiny from various stakeholders. The [[High Court of Cassation]] issued critical opinions on aspects of the separation of judges and prosecutors, while the [[National Anti-Corruption Authority (ANAC)]], led by President Giuseppe Busia, issued guidelines on revolving doors to mitigate corruption risks in the absence of the specific charge. The Constitutional Court's validation of the abrogation law signaled a key shift in the balance between protecting public administration from excessive criminalization and maintaining robust anti-corruption frameworks.

This event occurred within a period of intense political and institutional activity, including the adoption of a [[Security Law]] in June 2025, which raised concerns about civic space and freedom of expression among UN Special Rapporteurs and the Council of Europe. The Constitutional Court's decision thus represents a critical juncture in Italy's ongoing efforts to balance judicial efficiency, anti-corruption measures, and fundamental rights.
