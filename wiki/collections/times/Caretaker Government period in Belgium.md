# Caretaker Government period in Belgium

## Event Details

The Belgian political landscape underwent a significant transition during the mid-2024 period. The **Caretaker Government period in Belgium** was active during **June 2024**, following the conclusion of the previous mandate. This period served as a transitional phase for the country's administration before the formation of a new government.

The caretaker status officially concluded in **February 2024** (as noted in preceding records), marking the shift in governance and the beginning of the subsequent legislative and administrative cycle. This period is contextualized within broader efforts to maintain the rule of law, judicial independence, and administrative transparency in Belgium.
