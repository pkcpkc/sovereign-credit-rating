# Start of IMF staff discussions with Romanian officials

## Event Details

On September 3, 2025, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) initiated staff discussions with Romanian officials as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. These discussions were led by Mission Lead [[Joong Shik Kang]] and involved key interactions with Romanian authorities, including [[Mugur Isarescu]] and [[Ilie Bolojan]].

The primary objective of these discussions was to evaluate Romania's economic trajectory, specifically focusing on the "twin deficits" (fiscal and current account) and the government's fiscal reform package. Key areas of dialogue included:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** Reviewing the 2025–26 reform package, which includes VAT rate increases to 21% and a freeze on public sector wages and pensions for 2026.
*   **Monetary Policy:** Assessing the National Bank of Romania's (NBR) stance on maintaining interest rates at 6.5% amidst elevated headline inflation (9.9% in September 2025).
*   **Financial Stability:** Examining the "sovereign-bank nexus," where 26% of bank assets are held in sovereign bonds, and the risks associated with unhedged FX loans.
*   **Structural Reforms:** Discussing the acceleration of EU fund absorption, particularly for the Next Generation EU (NGEU) project, and improvements to labor force participation.

The discussions served as the foundational phase for the eventual completion of the IMF Staff Report in October 2025 and the subsequent publication of the consultation results in November 2025.
