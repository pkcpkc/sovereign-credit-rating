# Government deficit fell to 3.4% of GDP

## Event Details

On December 31, 2024, Italy's government deficit saw a significant reduction, falling to 3.4% of GDP from a much higher 7.2% recorded in 2023. This fiscal improvement was accompanied by Italy achieving its first primary surplus since 2019, reaching 0.4% of GDP.

Despite this reduction in the deficit, the country's debt-to-GDP ratio is projected to trend upward, potentially reaching 138.2% by 2026. This upward pressure is attributed to high interest costs and the ongoing need for borrowing to cover past housing renovation tax credits. These fiscal developments are part of the broader economic landscape described in the [[2025 Country Report - Italy]], which highlights a transition toward fiscal sustainability amidst significant structural challenges like low productivity and high public debt.
