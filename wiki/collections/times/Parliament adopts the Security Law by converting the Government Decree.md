# Parliament adopts the Security Law by converting the Government Decree

## Event Details

On **2025-06-04**, the Italian Parliament formally adopted the Security Law by converting a preceding Government Decree. This legislative action was part of broader efforts to combat terrorism and organized crime. However, the adoption raised significant concerns regarding its potential impact on fundamental freedoms and civic space.

Key aspects of this event include:

- **Legislative Origin**: The law originated from a Government Decree which was subsequently converted into law by Parliament, a procedure that has been noted as a frequent resort by the Italian government.
- **Civic Space Concerns**: Stakeholders, including the Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights and United Nations Special Rapporteurs, expressed concerns that the Security Law’s provisions might allow for arbitrary application and negatively affect freedom of assembly and expression.
- **Context**: This event occurred amidst ongoing judicial reforms and political stability measures. The threat to civic space was identified as a key risk factor in Italy’s sovereign credit rating assessment, alongside concerns about political fragmentation and the use of urgency decrees.
- **Oversight**: The Parliamentary Committee for the Security of the Republic (COPASIR) was involved in overseeing related security measures, including issues regarding spyware use.
