# Poland submitted annual progress report on fiscal plan

## Event Details

In response to the excessive deficit procedure initiated by the Council in January 2023, Poland has been navigating a structured fiscal adjustment path. Following the Council's endorsement of Poland’s medium-term fiscal-structural plan in January 2025, the government moved to formalize its progress.

On April 30, 2025, Poland officially submitted its annual progress report on the fiscal plan. This submission is a critical component of the binding expenditure path designed to reduce the national deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028. The report serves as a milestone in Poland's effort to balance high public consumption and defense spending with the requirements of the Stability and Growth Pact, while simultaneously addressing structural economic hurdles such as labor shortages and the need for increased public investment efficiency.
