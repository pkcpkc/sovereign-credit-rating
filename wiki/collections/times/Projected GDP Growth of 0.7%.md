# Projected GDP Growth of 0.7%

## Event Details

In the 2025 Article IV Consultation, the [[IMF]] projected a modest economic recovery for Hungary with a GDP growth rate of 0.7% for the year 2025. This follows a period of stagnant output and a contraction noted in Q1:2025.

The projection is framed within a "challenging juncture" for the Hungarian economy, where the IMF identifies significant downside risks including:
*   **Geoeconomic Fragmentation:** Potential escalation of trade measures and regional conflicts.
*   **Refinancing Costs:** High costs associated with maintaining the debt position.
*   **Fiscal Constraints:** The need for a cumulative fiscal adjustment of approximately 2% of GDP between 2025-2028 to bring the headline deficit below 3% by 2027.

To support this growth trajectory and ensure long-term stability, the IMF emphasizes the necessity of maintaining a tight monetary stance by the [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB)]], implementing structural reforms to address productivity gaps, and rebuilding fiscal buffers.
