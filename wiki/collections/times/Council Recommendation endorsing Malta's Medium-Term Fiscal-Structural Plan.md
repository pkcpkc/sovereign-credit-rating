# Council Recommendation endorsing Malta's medium-term fiscal-structural plan

## Event Details

On **21 January 2025**, the Council of the European Union issued a Recommendation endorsing Malta's medium-term fiscal-structural plan. This decision was part of the broader European Semester process, aimed at ensuring that Malta's national economic and fiscal policies align with EU stability and growth objectives.

The endorsement signified the European Commission's approval of Malta's strategic approach to fiscal consolidation and structural reforms. Key elements of this plan included commitments to:
- Reduce the general government deficit, which was projected to decrease from 3.7% of GDP in 2024 to 2.8% in 2026.
- Maintain government debt below 50% of GDP, ensuring long-term fiscal sustainability despite rising age-related expenditures.
- Implement structural reforms through the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP), focusing on digitalization, green transition, and institutional strengthening.

This recommendation served as a foundational step for subsequent measures, including the Council Recommendation to correct the excessive deficit in Malta, also issued on 21 January 2025. The plan aimed to address structural weaknesses such as labor market constraints, low R&D investment, and administrative burdens, while leveraging strengths like a robust service sector and strong banking system.
