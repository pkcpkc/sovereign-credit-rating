# Target for completion of RRP reforms and investments

## Event Details

The year 2026 marks the established target for the completion of reforms and investments under Italy's [[Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP)]]. This milestone is a critical component of the country's economic strategy to address structural challenges, including low productivity growth and significant skills mismatches.

The completion of these reforms is intended to stabilize the economy, which saw a real GDP growth of 0.7% in 2024. Key objectives tied to this timeline include:
- **Fiscal Sustainability:** Managing the debt-to-GDP ratio, which is projected to rise to 138.2% by 2026 due to high interest costs and past tax credits.
- **Productivity & Innovation:** Closing the R&D expenditure gap (currently 1.31% of GDP vs. the EU average of 2.24%) and strengthening the venture capital market.
- **Sustainability:** Advancing the digital transition and decarbonisation efforts, particularly in overcoming high wholesale electricity prices and hydrogeological climate risks.
- **Regional Development:** Addressing the "Mezzogiorno Divide" by improving infrastructure and accessibility in Southern Italy.
