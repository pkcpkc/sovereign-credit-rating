# First reading of proposed constitutional amendments

## Event Details

On **April 9, 2025**, the **Slovak Parliament (National Council)** held the first reading of proposed constitutional amendments. These amendments have raised significant concern regarding the primacy of EU law. Following this initial parliamentary stage, the proceedings were postponed. The European Commission's 2025 Rule of Law Report highlights these proposals as part of broader risks related to political polarization and potential challenges to institutional checks and balances within Slovakia. The proposed changes are scrutinized for their potential impact on the country's alignment with EU legal frameworks and democratic safeguards.
