# Council Recommendation to correct the excessive deficit in Malta

## Event Details

On 21 January 2025, the [[Council of the European Union]] issued a formal Recommendation to correct the excessive deficit in [[Malta]]. This directive is part of the EU's Fiscal Governance framework, specifically under the Stability and Growth Pact, aimed at ensuring Member States adhere to deficit and debt limits.

The recommendation was issued alongside another Council Recommendation endorsing [[Malta]]'s medium-term fiscal-structural plan. The context for this decision stems from the European Commission's assessment detailed in the 2025 Country Report, which highlighted that while [[Malta]]'s general government deficit was projected to decrease from 3.7% of GDP in 2024 to 2.8% in 2026, structural fiscal challenges persisted. These included high net expenditure growth, deviations from recommended net expenditure paths, and distortions caused by energy subsidies.

The recommendation underscores the need for [[Malta]] to implement specific fiscal consolidation measures to align with EU fiscal rules. Key areas of focus included:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** Reducing the general government deficit through increased tax revenues and reduced expenditure growth.
*   **Structural Reforms:** Implementing measures outlined in the [[Recovery and Resilience Plan]] (RRP) to support long-term fiscal sustainability.
*   **Debt Sustainability:** Maintaining government debt below 50% of GDP, which was projected to stabilize in the near term.

This event marks a critical step in [[Malta]]'s economic governance, reinforcing its commitment to macroeconomic stability and compliance with European Union fiscal regulations.
