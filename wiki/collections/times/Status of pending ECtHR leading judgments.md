# Status of pending ECtHR leading judgments

## Event Details

As of June 2025, the number of leading judgments from the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR) pending implementation in Slovakia rose to 33. This figure is highlighted in the 2025 Rule of Law Report as a key indicator of the ongoing challenges regarding judicial compliance and the integration of European human rights standards into the Slovak legal framework.
