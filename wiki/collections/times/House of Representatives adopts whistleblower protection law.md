# House of Representatives adopts whistleblower protection law

## Event Details

On March 13, 2025, the Belgian House of Representatives adopted a new law regarding whistleblower protection. This legislative action was followed on March 27, 2025, by the King sanctioning the law, officially integrating it into the national legal framework. This development is part of a broader effort to strengthen the rule of law and anti-corruption measures in Belgium.
