# IMF Executive Board Consideration of Hungary Report

## Event Details

The timeline for the IMF's assessment of the Hungarian economy follows a structured consultation process culminating in an Executive Board review.

**2025-06-05 to 2025-06-17**: The IMF initiated a formal staff mission in Budapest to conduct on-the-ground evaluations. These discussions involved key Hungarian authorities, including representatives from the Ministry for National Economy and the [[Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB)]].

**2025-07-25**: The IMF staff concluded their findings, completing the formal Staff Report. The report highlighted a "challenging juncture" for Hungary, noting stagnant output and inflation remaining above the 3% target.

**2025-08-29**: The IMF Executive Board officially considered the Hungary Report. The review focused on the necessity of rebuilding fiscal buffers and implementing structural reforms to address productivity gaps.

**Economic Projections**:
- **2025**: The economy is projected to see a modest growth of 0.7% despite a contraction in Q1:2025.
- **2026**: Growth is forecasted to accelerate to 2.0%.
- **2027**: The government aims to bring headline inflation back down to a target of 3.0%.
- **2030**: Public debt is projected to reach approximately 79% of GDP, prompting the IMF to advise on fiscal adjustments to ensure long-term sustainability.
