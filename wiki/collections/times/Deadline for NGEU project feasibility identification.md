# Deadline for NGEU project feasibility identification

## Event Details

In **August 2026**, Romania faces a critical deadline for the identification of project feasibility regarding **Next Generation EU (NGEU)** funds. This milestone is significant as the IMF has previously highlighted Romania's struggles with low absorption rates of these funds (currently at 38%) due to administrative bottlenecks.

The successful identification of these projects is essential for the country to successfully absorb EU funding and achieve medium-term macroeconomic sustainability. The IMF emphasizes that overcoming these hurdles requires structural reforms in public investment management and the potential establishment of an independent agency to oversee State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs). This deadline serves as a key pressure point for the Romanian government to streamline governance and improve the efficiency of public spending.
