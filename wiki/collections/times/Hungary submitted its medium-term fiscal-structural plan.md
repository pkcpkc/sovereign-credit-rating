# Hungary submitted its medium-term fiscal-structural plan

## Event Details

In December 2024, Hungary submitted its medium-term fiscal-structural plan to the European Commission. This submission occurred within a broader context of economic recovery and structural adjustment for the country. 

Key elements surrounding this fiscal milestone include:
- **Fiscal Context:** The plan was developed amidst a period of high public debt (reaching 73.5% of GDP in 2024) and high debt-servicing costs, with implicit interest rates near 6% in 2025.
- **Economic Outlook:** The submission followed a period of slow recovery characterized by 0.5% GDP growth in 2024, with expectations for moderate growth through 2026.
- **Policy Objectives:** The plan addresses a budgetary framework that has faced criticism for a lack of transparency and multiannual spending ceilings. It is part of a larger effort to manage net expenditure growth, which was forecast at 6.1% for 2025.
- **Subsequent Actions:** Following the submission, the Council adopted a Recommendation endorsing Hungary's plan and correcting excessive deficits in February 2025, with a formal Council Recommendation on the plan endorsed by March 18, 2025.
