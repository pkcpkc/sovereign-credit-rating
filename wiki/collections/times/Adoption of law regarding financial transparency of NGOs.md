# Adoption of law regarding financial transparency of NGOs

## Event Details

On April 16, 2025, a new legal framework was adopted in Slovakia imposing heavy reporting and disclosure obligations on non-governmental organizations (NGOs). While framed as a transparency measure, stakeholders and civil society groups have characterized the law as an increased administrative burden designed to pressure the NGO sector. This development is part of a broader trend of legislative actions in Slovakia, including the frequent use of fast-track procedures to bypass stakeholder consultation.
