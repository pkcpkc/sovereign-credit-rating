# Parliament approval of Bill No. 125 reforming magisterial inquiries

## Event Details

In April 2025, the Maltese Parliament approved Bill No. 125, which introduced significant reforms to the system of magisterial inquiries in [[Malta]]. The legislation notably increased the threshold required to initiate citizen-prompted magisterial inquiries, a mechanism traditionally used to investigate matters of public interest and potential high-level misconduct.

This legislative change was met with strong criticism from various stakeholders, including civil society organizations and international monitors, who argued that the increased threshold limits accountability, particularly for high-level offences. The reform was cited in the 2025 [[Rule of Law Report]] by the [[European Commission]] as a weakness in institutional checks and balances, raising concerns about the independence of specialised tribunals and the capacity to deliver final judgments in corruption cases. The approval of this bill occurred amidst broader constitutional reforms regarding the justice sector, which were tabled in Parliament in April 2025.
