# Press release of Article IV consultation conclusion

## Event Details

The press release of the Article IV consultation conclusion occurred on **January 21, 2025**, following a series of systematic reviews of the Polish economy. The timeline leading to this conclusion began with the **start of IMF staff discussions in Warsaw on October 8, 2024**, which concluded on **October 17, 2024**.

Following the conclusion of discussions, the **IMF Staff Report was completed on December 16, 2024**. This report served as the basis for the **IMF Executive Board consideration on January 10, 2025**, ultimately resulting in the formal press release.

The consultation concluded that while Poland's economy is in a recovery phase with projected growth of 3.5% in 2025-2026, it faces significant fiscal challenges. Specifically, the report highlighted a widening fiscal deficit of 5.9% of GDP in 2024, triggering the EU's Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP). The IMF recommended a shift toward fiscal consolidation to stabilize debt at 60% of GDP and urged structural reforms to address labor supply and the climate transition.
