# Implementation of Criminal Procedure Law Amendments to Increase Efficiency

## Event Details

On January 1, 2025, Austria implemented specific amendments to its [[Criminal Procedure Law]] designed to enhance the efficiency of the justice system. This legislative change serves as a key component of the broader "Justice 3.0" digitalization initiative, which aims to achieve 90% digital case management within the courts by year-end.

The amendments are part of Austria's ongoing efforts to align its legal framework with European standards for judicial efficiency. High public perception of judicial independence (86%) is supported by these structural improvements, although the report notes that slow progress on other judicial reforms, such as the appointment of administrative court presidents, remains a vulnerability. The implementation coincides with the extension of compulsory online publication of judgments to Higher Regional Courts, signaling a continued push toward transparency and digital transformation in the Austrian legal sector.
