# IMF Staff Report Completion

## Event Details

On December 17, 2024, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) completed its staff report for the 2024 Article IV Consultation with [[Malta]]. This event marked the conclusion of the technical assessment phase following a mission that began on November 4, 2024, and policy discussions that concluded on November 15, 2024. The completion of the report preceded the final endorsement by the IMF Executive Board, which took place on January 15, 2025.

The staff report, issued under the leadership of Executive Director [[Riccardo Ercoli]] and Team Leader [[Kotaro Ishi]], evaluated Malta’s macroeconomic performance, fiscal sustainability, and financial sector health. Key findings highlighted Malta’s robust economic growth, driven by service exports in tourism and online gaming, and a substantial improvement in its external position. The report noted that inflation had fallen to approximately 2%, aligning with Euro Area averages, while public debt remained projected at around 50% of GDP, well below the EU’s 60% threshold.

The IMF staff identified significant downside risks, including geopolitical spillovers from the war in Ukraine and the Israel-Gaza conflict, as well as domestic challenges such as labor shortages and strain on public services due to high population density. Recommendations included continuing fiscal consolidation, gradually exiting broad energy subsidies to create fiscal space for innovation and green infrastructure, and strengthening macroprudential policies to address banks' substantial exposure to the real estate sector. The report also emphasized the importance of structural reforms, including enhancing digitalization, STEM education, and the efficiency of the justice system to maintain institutional strength and prevent trade-based money laundering.
