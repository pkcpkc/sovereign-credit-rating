# Press release on conclusion of Article IV Consultation

## Event Details

The IMF concluded its 2025 Article IV consultation with [[Romania]] on November 14, 2025. The timeline of the consultation and subsequent fiscal projections are as follows:

- **September 3, 2025:** The IMF mission officially began discussions with Romanian officials, including Prime Minister [[Ilie Bolojan]] and Finance Minister [[Alexandru Nazare]].
- **September 12, 2025:** The IMF mission discussions were concluded.
- **October 15, 2025:** The IMF Staff Report was completed, detailing Romania's economic performance and structural reform progress.
- **November 7, 2025:** The IMF Executive Board reviewed the report on Romania.
- **November 14, 2025:** The official press release was issued, highlighting a period of subdued growth (0.9% in 2024) and widening "twin deficits." It noted that while a mid-2025 fiscal reform package (including VAT hikes and excise duties) was a positive step, further adjustments are required to stabilize public debt at approximately 60% of GDP by 2030.
- **March 2026:** Expected removal of the natural gas price cap, contributing to elevated headline inflation.
- **August 2026:** Deadline for identifying the feasibility of [[Next Generation EU (NGEU)]] projects, addressing the current low absorption rate (38%).
- **January 1, 2027:** Projected start of additional fiscal adjustment measures to ensure medium-term macroeconomic sustainability.
- **January 1, 2027:** Implementation of [[ETS 2 (Emission Trading System)]] for buildings and road transport as part of the green transition.
