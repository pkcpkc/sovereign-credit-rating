# Conclusion of discussions with Belgian officials

## Event Details

The IMF concluded its formal discussions with Belgian authorities on February 4, 2025, marking the end of the consultation period that began on January 22, 2025. These discussions were part of the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Belgium**, which aimed to assess the country's economic resilience, fiscal sustainability, and structural challenges.

Key outcomes from these discussions included:
- **Fiscal Policy:** Agreement on the need for sustained fiscal consolidation to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP, specifically targeting a seven-year adjustment period under the new EU economic governance framework (EGF).
- **Labor Market:** Discussions highlighted the necessity of reviewing the 1996 wage law and automatic wage indexation to improve labor-market efficiency.
- **Sovereign Risk:** Assessment of Belgium's "Moderate" sovereign stress risk, supported by a high-quality debt management profile.
- **Productivity:** Identification of the need to address lagging Total Factor Productivity (TFP) through reduced regulatory barriers and improved insolvency regimes.
