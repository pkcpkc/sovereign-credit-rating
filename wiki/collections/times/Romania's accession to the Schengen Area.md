# Romania's accession to the Schengen Area

## Event Details

On **1 January 2025**, Romania officially acceded to the [[Schengen Area]], marking a significant milestone in the country's integration within the [[European Union]]. This accession, which followed a period of extensive political and administrative preparation, allows for the removal of systematic border checks at Romania's internal borders with other Schengen member states, facilitating the free movement of people and goods.

The accession is widely recognized as a critical strength for Romania's geopolitical and economic stability. According to the **2025 Country Report - Romania** issued by the [[European Commission]], entry into the Schengen Area is expected to substantially facilitate trade and attract foreign direct investment (FDI). By eliminating border delays and enhancing connectivity, the move aims to boost the competitiveness of key industrial sectors, such as the automotive industry, and improve the overall investment climate.

While the accession brings significant economic advantages, it occurs against a backdrop of macroeconomic challenges, including a high government deficit and fiscal consolidation efforts outlined in the Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP). Nevertheless, the integration into the Schengen zone remains a pivotal factor supporting investor confidence and long-term growth prospects, projected to recover to 1.4% in 2025 and 2.2% in 2026.
