# Forecasted GDP growth of 4.1%

## Event Details

The economic trajectory for Malta shows a period of sustained growth following a strong 2024 performance. In 2024, the real GDP grew by 6.0%, establishing a high baseline for the country's economic momentum. 

Looking forward, the economy is projected to maintain steady expansion with a forecasted GDP growth of 4.1% in 2025, followed by a slightly moderated growth of 4.0% in 2026. This growth occurs amidst a period of fiscal consolidation, where the general government deficit is expected to decrease from 3.7% in 2024 to 2.8% by 2026, while the debt-to-GDP ratio is anticipated to stabilize below 50%.

However, this growth is tempered by significant structural challenges highlighted in the [[2025 Country Report - Malta]]. These include:
- **Labor Market Constraints**: Despite high employment rates, severe shortages in ICT, construction, and healthcare persist.
- **Innovation Gaps**: Low R&D intensity (0.64% of GDP) and high administrative burdens for businesses.
- **Sustainability Hurdles**: High fossil fuel subsidies and a low renewable energy mix (16.7%).

Progress toward these economic goals is being tracked through the Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP), with 39% of milestones fulfilled as of mid-2025.
