# Virtual IMF discussions start

## Event Details

On December 4, 2024, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) initiated virtual discussions with Slovak officials as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. These discussions were aimed at assessing the Slovak Republic's economic performance, specifically focusing on growth projections, fiscal consolidation plans, and financial sector resilience. 

The consultations served as the foundational phase for evaluating the government's commitment to a 4-year fiscal consolidation plan (2025-2028) and addressing structural challenges such as labor productivity, demographic shifts, and debt sustainability. These virtual sessions eventually led to the conclusion of discussions in late January 2025 and the subsequent publication of the Staff Report in March 2025.
