# High Court of Cassation issues periodic report on legislative measures including Security Law

## Event Details

On 2025-06-23, the [[High Court of Cassation]] issued its periodic report on legislative measures, specifically highlighting the recently adopted [[Security Law]]. This report was published in the context of significant concern from international bodies, including the United Nations Special Rapporteurs and the Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights, regarding the law's potential impact on civic space, freedom of assembly, and fundamental freedoms. The Security Law, which entered into force in April 2025 after being converted from a Government Decree, was adopted by Parliament on 2025-06-04. The High Court's assessment contributed to the broader critique that the law's provisions may allow for arbitrary application, thereby narrowing civic space in Italy.
