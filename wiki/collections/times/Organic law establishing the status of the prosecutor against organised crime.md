# Organic law establishing the status of the prosecutor against organised crime

## Event Details

On April 29, 2025, an organic law was established to define and regulate the status of the prosecutor specifically tasked with combating organized crime in France. This legislative action is part of a broader overhaul of the French judicial framework, which includes:

*   **Anti-Corruption Strengthening:** The law serves as a pillar for the 2024-2029 national anti-corruption plan, which prioritizes organized drug crime and municipal integrity.
*   **Institutional Support:** This move complements the reorganization of the Anti-Corruption Agency (AFA) and increased human resources for the National Financial Prosecutor (PNF) and OCLCIFF.
*   **Contextual Reforms:** The measure follows the 2023 law reforming magistrate status and aligns with ongoing efforts to improve the seizure of criminal assets (Law Warsmann).
