# Entry into force of the Justice Laws in Romania

## Event Details

In **October 2022**, the Justice Laws entered into force in Romania. This legislative development was a central component of the country's efforts to align its judicial framework with European standards and address concerns raised by the **Venice Commission** and the **European Commission**.

By the time of the **2025 Rule of Law Report**, the completion of the process to take into account Venice Commission recommendations on these Justice Laws was cited as a significant strength regarding **policy credibility** and **institutional strength**. The European Commission highlighted that Romania had made significant progress in addressing judicial independence concerns through these legislative steps, including measures to strengthen safeguards for high-ranking prosecutors and the judicial police.

However, despite the formal entry into force and subsequent alignment with Venice Commission recommendations, challenges persist in the perception and practical application of judicial independence. The **High Court of Cassation and Justice** has continued to face scrutiny, particularly regarding case-law on the statute of limitations and the interpretation of *lex mitior*, which has led to the discontinuation of numerous corruption cases. The **Superior Council of the Magistracy** remains a key institution in overseeing the implementation of these judicial reforms, while the **Ministry of Justice** has demonstrated openness to ensuring judicial independence in related IT system reforms.
