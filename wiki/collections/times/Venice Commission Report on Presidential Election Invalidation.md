# Venice Commission Report on Presidential Election Invalidation

## Event Details

On 6 June 2025, the [[Venice Commission]] published its report regarding the invalidation of the Romanian Presidential Election. This advisory opinion from the [[Council of Europe]] addressed the significant political event wherein the [[Constitutional Court of Romania]] annulled the first round of the 2024 presidential election due to irregularities, necessitating a repeat election in 2025.

The report evaluated the legal framework and judicial handling of the election dispute, contributing to the broader assessment of [[Judicial Independence]] and [[Institutional Integrity]] in Romania. The European Commission’s 2025 Rule of Law Report highlighted this incident as a key marker of the country's political and judicial landscape, noting the tension between high-level judicial decisions on electoral validity and persistent challenges in legislative predictability. The event is contextualized within ongoing efforts to implement [[Venice Commission]] recommendations on Justice Laws and address systemic risks in the judicial system.
