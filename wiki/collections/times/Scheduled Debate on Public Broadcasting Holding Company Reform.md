# Scheduled Debate on Public Broadcasting Holding Company Reform

## Event Details

On 30 June 2025, a scheduled parliamentary debate took place regarding the proposed reform of public broadcasting governance in France. The core of this legislative initiative involves the creation of a new holding company, named **France Médias**, designed to oversee major public broadcasters including **France Télévisions**, **Radio France**, and the **Institut National de l'Audiovisuel (INA)**.

The reform aims to pool resources and share investments across these entities, proposing a structure with a common Chief Executive Officer for the subsidiaries. However, the proposal has sparked significant controversy and industrial action. Strikes have emerged in response to concerns regarding job security and the potential erosion of editorial independence. The debate highlights tensions between administrative efficiency gains and the preservation of journalistic autonomy, fitting within the broader context of **Media Ownership Transparency** efforts in France. This event is part of the ongoing implementation of the 2025 Rule of Law Report's observations on **Checks and Balances** and **Media Freedom**.
