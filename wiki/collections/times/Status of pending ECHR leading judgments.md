# Status of pending ECHR leading judgments

## Event Details

On **2025-01-01**, the status of pending leading judgments from the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR) was noted as a key component in the ongoing assessment of Poland's judicial and rule of law framework. This monitoring occurs alongside broader legislative reforms, including the separation of the Prosecutor General from the Minister of Justice and ongoing consultations with the Venice Commission regarding the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ) and the Constitutional Tribunal.
