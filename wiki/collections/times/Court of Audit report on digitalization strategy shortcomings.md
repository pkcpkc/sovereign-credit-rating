# Court of Audit report on digitalization strategy shortcomings

## Event Details

In December 2024, the [[Court of Audit]] issued a report highlighting significant shortcomings in Belgium's digitalization strategy for the justice system. The audit identified critical gaps in governance and fraud controls within the digitalization efforts, raising concerns about oversight mechanisms. In response to these findings, an action plan was submitted in September 2024 to address the identified deficiencies and improve the integrity of the digital transition within the judicial administration. This report is part of broader efforts to enhance [[Justice System Resources]] and efficiency, following continued issues with backlogs, particularly at the [[Court of Appeal of Brussels]].
