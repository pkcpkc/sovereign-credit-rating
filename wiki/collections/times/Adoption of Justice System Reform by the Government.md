# Adoption of Justice System Reform by the Government

## Event Details

In March 2024, the Italian government adopted a comprehensive justice system reform, marking a significant structural shift in the country's legal and institutional framework. This legislative package has since been implemented through specific implementing legislation, with the [[High Council for the Judiciary]] actively executing its new mandates.

Key components of the reform include the establishment of clear rules for magistrate appointments, performance appraisals, and judicial management transparency. Between September 2024 and December 2024, the High Council for the Judiciary approved several critical directives stemming from this reform:
- **September 25, 2024:** Approval of the directive on out-of-tenure appointments for magistrates.
- **November 13, 2024:** Approval of the directive regarding the appraisal of magistrates’ performance.
- **December 3, 2024:** Approval of the Code of Judicial Management.

The reform also addresses persistent challenges such as staff shortages across court levels and aims to enhance the efficiency of civil, criminal, administrative, and tax jurisdictions. While the reform has received stakeholder support for guaranteeing judicial impartiality, it exists alongside broader constitutional debates, including a draft reform to separate the careers of judges and prosecutors, which faced strong opposition from judicial associations and was subsequently approved by the Chamber of Deputies in January 2025. The adoption of this initial justice system reform is viewed by international observers, including the European Commission, as a strength contributing to institutional stability and policy predictability in Italy.
