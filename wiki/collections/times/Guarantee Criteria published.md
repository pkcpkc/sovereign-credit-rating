# Guarantee Criteria published

## Event Details

On **2016-10-21**, S&P Global Ratings published the **Guarantee Criteria**. This publication served as a foundational component of the broader framework for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness, preceding the major methodology overhauls in 2017. It established the standards for how guarantees—including those from sovereign entities or public-sector enterprises—impact credit assessments. 

This event is part of a chronological progression of methodology updates, following the *Principles Of Credit Ratings* (2011) and the *Monetary Authorities Rating Methodology* (2013), and leading into the comprehensive *Sovereign Rating Methodology (2017)*. The 2016 criteria laid the groundwork for assessing contingent liabilities and debt burdens that were later refined into the current five-pillar assessment system (Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary).
