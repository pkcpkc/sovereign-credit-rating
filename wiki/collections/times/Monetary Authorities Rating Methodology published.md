# Monetary Authorities Rating Methodology published

## Event Details

On **2013-09-11**, the **Monetary Authorities Rating Methodology** was published as part of a broader framework for evaluating sovereign creditworthiness. This methodology is a critical component of the global framework used to assess the ability and willingness of sovereign states to service financial obligations to nonofficial (commercial) creditors.

The methodology serves as a foundational piece of the sovereign rating architecture, which was further refined in the **2017 Sovereign Rating Methodology**. The broader framework evaluates sovereigns based on five core pillars: Institutional, Economic, External, Fiscal, and Monetary assessments. 

Key elements of the methodology include:
- **Monetary Assessment:** Evaluating exchange rate regimes and the credibility of monetary policy, specifically focusing on independence, price stability, and **Lender of Last Resort** capabilities.
- **Integration with Sovereign Frameworks:** The methodology works in tandem with assessments of **Net General Government Debt**, **Current Account Payments (CAP)**, and the **Banking Industry Country Risk Assessment (BICRA)** to quantify systemic risks and contingent liabilities.
- **Currency Dynamics:** It provides the basis for distinguishing between foreign-currency and local-currency ratings, ensuring that ratings reflect the nuances of a country's monetary policy environment.
