# Addition of TVN and Polsat to List of Strategic Companies Requiring Government Approval for Sale

## Event Details

On **2024-12-18**, the Polish Government officially added major media outlets **[[TVN]]** and **[[Polsat]]** to the list of strategic companies requiring government approval for any change in ownership or control. This policy decision was submitted via a parliamentary bill on **2024-11-29** and subsequently adopted, marking a significant intervention in media pluralism and corporate governance structures.

The move was part of broader legislative efforts to strengthen state oversight over critical infrastructure and media entities, occurring amidst ongoing tensions regarding media regulator independence and transparency of media ownership. While the government has made strides in reducing politically biased content in public service media and improving state advertising distribution, this specific regulation raises concerns about potential political influence over private media holdings. The decision intersects with wider rule of law discussions, including the independence of the **[[National Broadcasting Council]]** and the safety of journalists in Poland.
