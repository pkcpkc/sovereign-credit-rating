# New Government Programme presented

## Event Details

In **March 2025**, the Austrian government presented its new Government Programme, which outlined key commitments regarding judicial independence and prosecutorial autonomy. A central pledge within the programme was the establishment of an independent Federal Prosecution Office to mitigate risks of political influence.

As assessed in the **EU Commission 2025 Rule of Law Report** (published in July 2025), progress on this specific commitment has been limited. While the reform is in the drafting stage, the Minister of Justice retains the power to issue instructions to prosecutors in individual cases—a practice that continued despite an investigative commission's findings of past political influence. The report notes that the implementation of these prosecutorial independence reforms remains delayed.

Additionally, the Government Programme committed to **merit-based appointments** for high-level positions at independent authorities to counter politicization risks. This commitment is being monitored as part of the institutional checks and balances framework, alongside ongoing challenges in judicial appointment procedures for administrative court presidents and lobbying transparency.
