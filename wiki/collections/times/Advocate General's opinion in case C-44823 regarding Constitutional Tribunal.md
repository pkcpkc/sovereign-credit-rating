# Advocate General's opinion in case C-448/23 regarding Constitutional Tribunal

## Event Details

On **2025-03-11**, an Advocate General's opinion was issued regarding case **C-448/23** concerning the status and functions of the Polish Constitutional Tribunal. 

This event is situated within a broader context of judicial reform in Poland, where the government has faced significant scrutiny over the independence of the Constitutional Tribunal and the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ). The opinion coincides with a period of legislative activity aimed at addressing "neo-judges" and revising judicial appointments. Specifically, the Polish government has been consulting with the [[Venice Commission]] on reforms to these institutions while simultaneously working to separate the office of the Minister of Justice from the Prosecutor General.
