# Projected GDP growth surpassing 2.9%

## Event Details

In 2025, Poland is projected to experience GDP growth surpassing the 2.9% mark observed in 2024. This economic acceleration is primarily driven by robust private consumption and significant investment activities. 

While the country maintains a position as one of the fastest-growing economies in the EU, this growth occurs alongside a complex fiscal landscape. Poland is currently navigating an excessive deficit procedure (following a 6.6% GDP deficit in 2023) and is working toward a binding expenditure path to reduce the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2028. The expansion is further shaped by structural efforts to address labor shortages in STEM and construction, as well as initiatives to modernize the healthcare system and transition toward a circular economy.
