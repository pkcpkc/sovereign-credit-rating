# Expected entry into force of the draft law on the simplification of economic life

## Event Details

The entry into force of the [[Draft Law on the Simplification of Economic Life]] is scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2025 (2025-Q4). This legislative milestone is a key component of France's medium-term fiscal-structural plan, aimed at addressing complex administrative procedures and the heavy regulatory burden that hinder economic activity.

The law seeks to streamline permitting processes, particularly for renewable energy projects and energy-efficient building renovations, which have faced significant delays due to administrative barriers. By simplifying economic regulations, the government aims to boost investment, improve business competitiveness, and support the green transition. This reform is part of broader efforts to enhance institutional strength and policy predictability, although implementation challenges remain given the fragmented political landscape and high economic uncertainty following the 2024 general elections.
