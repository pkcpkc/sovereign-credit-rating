# Conclusion of IMF staff discussions with Slovak officials

## Event Details

On January 28, 2025, the IMF staff concluded their discussions with Slovak officials as part of the 2025 Article IV Consultation. This engagement served as a critical assessment of the Slovak Republic's economic trajectory following a period of sharp slowdown in 2022-23.

During these discussions, key themes emerged regarding the country's recovery phase, which saw growth reach 2.0% in 2024. The dialogue focused on several pillars:

*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** Officials discussed the government's ambitious 4-year fiscal plan (2025-2028) aimed at reducing the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP in 2025. The IMF highlighted the need for additional expenditure-based measures to meet long-term targets.
*   **Economic Outlook:** Projections were reviewed for a moderate growth rate of 1.8% in 2025, with expectations for inflation to stabilize toward the 2% target by early 2027.
*   **Financial Stability:** Discussions addressed the resilience of the banking sector against vulnerabilities in the residential and commercial real estate markets, particularly regarding high house prices and mortgage refixations.
*   **Structural Reforms:** The staff engaged with officials on addressing demographic challenges, including a shrinking labor force due to aging and migration. Recommendations included improvements in vocational training and migrant integration.
*   **Governance and Energy:** The talks touched upon the necessity of a new national anti-corruption strategy and the transition toward 2030 climate targets, specifically reducing fossil fuel subsidies.
