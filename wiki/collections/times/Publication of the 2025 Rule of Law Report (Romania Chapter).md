# Publication of the 2025 Rule of Law Report (Romania Chapter)

## Event Details

On **July 8, 2025**, the European Commission released the **2025 Rule of Law Report (Romania Chapter)**, providing a comprehensive assessment of the country's judicial independence, anti-corruption framework, and media landscape.

The report highlights a period of mixed progress for Romania:
- **Judicial Progress:** Significant strides were made in aligning Justice Laws with Venice Commission recommendations and improving human resources, with judge occupancy reaching 83% in early 2025. However, public perception of judicial independence saw a decline to 44% in 2025.
- **Anti-Corruption Challenges:** While the 2021-2025 Anti-Corruption Strategy remains on track, a "statute of limitations" crisis emerged following High Court of Cassation and Justice (HCCJ) rulings on the *lex mitior* principle, leading to the closure of numerous corruption cases.
- **Governance and Transparency:** In **April 2025**, a new Strategy for Open Government (2025-2030) was adopted to counter a narrowing civic space. Conversely, the report noted a lack of progress on lobbying rules for Members of Parliament and issues regarding transparency in media ownership.
- **Legal Developments:** The report also noted a significant ruling on **May 29, 2025**, where the Constitutional Court declared it unconstitutional to require officials to declare the assets of spouses and children, potentially creating a transparency gap in asset declaration.
