# Completion of IMF Staff Report

## Event Details

The **International Monetary Fund (IMF)** completed the preparation of its **Staff Report** for two major member countries in 2025, marking key milestones in their respective **Article IV Consultation** processes. These completions preceded the final Executive Board conclusions and signaled the end of intensive staff missions and data gathering phases.

### France: 2025 Article IV Consultation

On **June 25, 2025**, the IMF officially completed its Staff Report for France's 2025 Article IV Consultation. This milestone followed the conclusion of the IMF staff mission in Paris on May 22, 2025, and subsequent discussions with French authorities, including **Minister for the Economy, Finance, and Industrial and Digital Sovereignty** **Lombard**, **Minister for Public Accounts** **de Montchalín**, and **Banque de France** Governor **Villeroy de Galhau**.

The completion of the report highlighted several critical economic assessments:
*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** The report underscored the urgency of addressing France's high public debt, which reached 113.1% of GDP in 2024. It recommended a credible package of structural fiscal measures to bring the deficit below 3% of GDP by 2029, emphasizing rationalization of current spending over increasing the already high tax burden.
*   **Structural Reforms:** The IMF emphasized the need for structural reforms to boost productivity, particularly in leveraging France's strengths in **Green Transition** and **Digital Economy**, including **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** adoption.
*   **Financial Sector:** The 2025 **Financial Sector Assessment Program (FSAP)** confirmed the resilience of the French banking sector, with strong capital and liquidity buffers, though risks from **Commercial Real Estate** and corporate indebtedness require monitoring.
*   **Economic Outlook:** The report projected a slowdown in real GDP growth to 0.6% in 2025, driven by trade tensions and weak external demand, before recovering to 1% in 2026.

The Staff Report was subsequently discussed by the **IMF Executive Board** on July 11, 2025, leading to the conclusion of the Article IV Consultation. **Executive Director** **Arnaud Buisse** and **IMF Staff** members, including **Helge Berger** and **Manuela Goretti**, contributed to the final assessment.

### Slovakia: 2025 Article IV Consultation

On **March 3, 2025**, the IMF completed its Staff Report for Slovakia's 2025 Article IV Consultation. This completion followed staff discussions with Slovak officials in late 2024 and early 2025, involving **National Bank of Slovakia** Governor **Kažimír** and **Minister of Finance** **Kamenický**.

Key findings from the completed Staff Report included:
*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** The report endorsed Slovakia's ambitious fiscal consolidation plan, targeting a reduction in the headline deficit to 4.7% of GDP in 2025. It noted that further measures were needed to ensure long-term debt sustainability, particularly given the burden of aging-related costs.
*   **Growth and Inflation:** The Slovak economy was projected to grow by 1.8% in 2025, moderated by fiscal consolidation, with inflation expected to reach the 2% target by early 2027.
*   **Governance and Structural Reforms:** The report stressed the need for structural reforms to diversify the economy away from its heavy dependence on the automotive sector. It highlighted critical needs for strengthening **Governance**, reducing corruption, and enhancing judicial independence to ensure effective absorption of EU funds.
*   **Financial Stability:** The banking sector was deemed resilient, but vulnerabilities in the residential real estate market due to mortgage refixation were identified as a key risk.

The completed report was endorsed by the **IMF Executive Board** on March 24, 2025, concluding the consultation process for Slovakia.
