# Mandatory use of digital case management system (APP) in criminal courts and prosecutors' offices

## Event Details

On **January 1, 2025**, Italy implemented the mandatory use of the digital case management system (*APP*) across all criminal courts and prosecutors' offices. This measure was a key component of the comprehensive justice system reforms, with implementing legislative decrees having been adopted by the Government in March 2024 and tasks overseen by the [[High Council for the Judiciary]].

While the digitalisation initiative represented significant progress in modernising the Italian justice sector, the transition encountered operational challenges. Technical difficulties during the rollout led to temporary derogations for certain courts to ensure continuity of judicial proceedings. The implementation of the *APP* system aligns with broader EU Rule of Law objectives to enhance judicial efficiency and transparency, although it occurs against a backdrop of persistent administrative staff shortages and ongoing debates regarding judicial independence and the separation of careers between judges and prosecutors.
