# Labor Force Participation

Labor force participation refers to the percentage of the working-age population that is either employed or actively seeking employment. It serves as a critical indicator of an economy's productive capacity and human capital utilization.

## Current Context: Romania
Based on the IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation, Romania faces significant structural challenges regarding its labor market:

*   **Low Participation Rates:** Romania currently maintains one of the lowest labor force participation rates in the European Union, falling below 70%.
*   **Growth Drivers:** The IMF identifies improving labor force participation as a fundamental structural reform necessary to unlock economic growth.
*   **Key Focus Areas for Improvement:**
    *   **Female Participation:** Increasing the involvement of women in the workforce is highlighted as a primary lever for expansion.
    *   **Education:** Enhancing educational outcomes is identified as a prerequisite for improving the quality and quantity of labor participation.
