# Energy Subsidies

Energy subsidies refer to government financial support provided to reduce the cost of energy for consumers or producers. In the context of Malta's current economic landscape, these subsidies—specifically the **fixed-energy price model**—represent a significant fiscal burden.

## Current Status and Fiscal Impact
As of the 2025 IMF Article IV Consultation, energy subsidies are identified as a primary driver of Malta's fiscal deficit. The current model provides a large portion of the deficit, limiting the government's ability to invest in other critical areas.

## Strategic Policy Shift
The IMF and Maltese authorities have identified a strategic necessity to exit the fixed-energy price model. The goal of this transition includes:
*   **Fiscal Consolidation:** Reducing the fiscal deficit to meet the EU’s new fiscal framework (targeting 2.6% of GDP by 2027).
*   **Resource Reallocation:** Redirecting funds previously used for energy subsidies toward:
    *   **Research and Development (R&D)**
    *   **Public Services:** Specifically healthcare and education.
    *   **Green Investment:** Supporting the transition to a more sustainable economy.
*   **Climate Goals:** Aligning with Malta's target of a 19% GHG reduction by 2030, particularly within the buildings and transportation sectors.
