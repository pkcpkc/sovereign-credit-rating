# Circular Economy

The **Circular Economy** is an economic model aimed at minimizing waste and maximizing the lifecycle of resources by transitioning from a "take-make-dispose" linear system to one based on recycling, reusing, and refurbishing.

## Contextual Applications
In the context of European economic reports (e.g., Austria 2025), the Circular Economy is identified as a key pillar for achieving long-term sustainability and industrial resilience. It is closely linked to the following strategic goals:

*   **Decarbonisation:** Integrating circular principles is essential for reducing the carbon footprint of manufacturing and energy-intensive sectors.
*   **Innovation:** Transitioning to a circular model requires significant R&D investment to develop "breakthrough" technologies for material recovery and product longevity.
*   **Industrial Policy:** Addressing industrial decarbonisation—particularly in sectors contributing significantly to emissions—requires moving toward circular production cycles to mitigate environmental impact while maintaining competitiveness.
