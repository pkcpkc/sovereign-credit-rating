# Digitalization

Digitalization refers to the integration of digital technologies into all areas of economic and social life, fundamentally changing how businesses, governments, and individuals operate. In the context of macroeconomic stability and structural reform, it is a critical lever for enhancing efficiency and competitiveness.

## Economic Impact and Structural Reform
In the context of the Slovak Republic's economy, digitalization is identified as a key component of structural reform aimed at addressing several macroeconomic challenges:

*   **Productivity Growth:** Digitalization is a primary mechanism to counter the "secular decline" in labor productivity. By integrating advanced technologies, economies can offset the risks associated with automation and an aging workforce.
*   **Innovation and R&D:** To maintain economic growth, there is a strategic need to increase R&D spending and foster a digital ecosystem that encourages innovation.
*   **Economic Diversification:** Digital tools are essential for diversifying the economy beyond traditional sectors, helping to mitigate risks from geoeconomic fragmentation.
