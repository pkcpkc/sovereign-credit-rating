## Current Status and Regulatory Landscape

Recent developments highlight a tension between the constitutional guarantee of assembly and legislative measures that restrict its exercise. Key issues include:

*   **Disproportionate Measures:** Stakeholders have raised concerns regarding disproportionate measures taken against protesters, including potential judicial bans on demonstrations. These restrictions are viewed as potentially undermining the effective exercise of this right.
*   **Legislative Obstacles:** There are persistent legislative obstacles to the **Right to Demonstrate**, creating uncertainty for civil society organizations and individual citizens.
*   **Judicial Intervention:** The **Constitutional Court** has played a corrective role, notably annulling specific restrictive provisions related to subsidies for organizations and restricting recourse to urgent court orders challenging strikes.
