# Magisterial Inquiry

A **Magisterial Inquiry** is a formal judicial process in Malta used to investigate specific matters, often involving allegations of wrongdoing or institutional failings. It serves as a mechanism for oversight and fact-finding within the legal framework.

### Current Context and Developments
Recent legislative developments in Malta have highlighted tensions regarding the accessibility of this process:

*   **Legislative Reform (Bill 125):** A controversial legislative reform (Bill 125) was introduced which increased the threshold required for citizens to petition for a magisterial inquiry. 
*   **Civil Society Impact:** This increase in the threshold has drawn significant criticism from civil society organizations, who argue it may restrict the ability of the public to seek judicial oversight or investigations into perceived injustices.
*   **Institutional Role:** The process is tied to the broader framework of [[Judicial Independence]] and [[Institutional Checks]], serving as a tool for accountability within the Maltese justice system.
