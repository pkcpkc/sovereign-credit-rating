# Long-term Care

Long-term care refers to the services and support provided to individuals who are unable to perform activities of daily living due to chronic illness, disability, or age-related decline. 

## Current Status and Projections (Belgium)
Based on recent economic assessments, Belgium is facing significant structural pressures regarding long-term care:
*   **Fiscal Pressure:** The country is projected to have some of the highest long-term care spending in the European Union by the year 2070.
*   **Demographic Drivers:** These rising costs are primarily driven by an aging population, which also contributes to increased pension spending (projected to rise by 3.5% of GDP between 2022 and 2070).
*   **Strategic Priorities:** To ensure fiscal sustainability, there is a critical need to improve the efficiency of the current system. Key policy objectives include:
    *   Transitioning toward more cost-effective, community-based services.
    *   Addressing the high regulatory complexity and labor costs that impact service delivery.
    *   Mitigating the impact of aging-related costs on the overall [[Fiscal-Structural Plan]].
