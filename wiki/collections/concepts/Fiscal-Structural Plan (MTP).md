# Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP)

The **Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP)** is a strategic policy framework utilized by the Romanian government to manage the country's economic trajectory, aiming to balance fiscal responsibility with structural reforms. It serves as a roadmap for achieving long-term economic stability while addressing systemic weaknesses in the national economy.

## Core Objectives and Mechanisms
The MTP is designed to address several critical macroeconomic challenges identified in the **2025 Country Report - Romania**:

*   **Expenditure Control:** A primary goal of the MTP is to cap net expenditure growth. For instance, the plan aims to limit net expenditure growth to 5.1% in 2025 to curb the rising public deficit, which reached 9.3% of GDP in 2024.
*   **Debt Sustainability:** The plan monitors and manages government debt, which has been rising rapidly (reaching nearly 55% of GDP by the end of 2024).
*   **Structural Transition:** Beyond simple fiscal balancing, the MTP is intended to facilitate a transition from a low-cost labor model to an innovation-driven economy by addressing structural bottlenecks.

## Key Challenges and Context
The implementation of the MTP faces significant hurdles due to broader macroeconomic and structural conditions:
*   **Macroeconomic Imbalance:** High fiscal and current account deficits create a volatile environment for the execution of long-term plans.
*   **Implementation Delays:** Despite formal commitments to the MTP, there have been noted delays in executing critical components, specifically regarding tax reforms and pension adjustments.
*   **Productivity vs. Wages:** The plan must contend with nominal wages that have significantly outpaced productivity growth, threatening external cost competitiveness.
