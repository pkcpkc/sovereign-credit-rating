# Qualitative Overlay (QO)

The **Qualitative Overlay (QO)** is a critical component of the Fitch Ratings Sovereign Rating Criteria framework. It serves as a subjective adjustment mechanism used by analysts to refine the quantitative outputs generated by the **Sovereign Rating Model (SRM)**.

While the SRM provides a baseline score based on 18 quantitative variables, the QO allows for a more nuanced assessment of a sovereign's creditworthiness by accounting for qualitative factors that may not be fully captured by regression models.

### Key Functions and Mechanics
*   **Notch Adjustments:** Analysts are permitted to apply a notch adjustment of **+/- 2 per analytical pillar**, with a maximum cumulative adjustment of **+/- 3 notches** overall.
*   **Adjustment Drivers:** The QO is primarily used to address nuances such as:
    *   Geopolitical risk and political stability.
    *   Policy credibility and institutional strength.
    *   Financial sector health.
    *   Fiscal sustainability and policy flexibility.

### Integration with Rating Framework
The QO operates in tandem with the four analytical pillars of the sovereign credit assessment:
1.  **Structural Features** (Highest weight: 53.7%)
2.  **Public Finances** (19.1%)
3.  **External Finances** (17.3%)
4.  **Macroeconomic Performance** (9.8%)

By synthesizing these qualitative insights with the quantitative SRM baseline, Fitch Ratings produces a final **Issuer Default Rating (IDR)** that reflects both the hard data of the economy and the "soft" risks inherent in sovereign governance.
