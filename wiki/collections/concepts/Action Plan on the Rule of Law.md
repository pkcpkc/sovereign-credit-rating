# Action Plan on the Rule of Law

The **Action Plan on the Rule of Law** refers to the structured legislative and institutional framework designed to restore and maintain the integrity of a nation's legal system, specifically within the context of European Union standards. It serves as a roadmap for addressing systemic judicial irregularities, enhancing anti-corruption measures, and protecting democratic freedoms.

## Core Objectives and Implementation
Based on recent assessments (notably the 2025 Rule of Law Report for Poland), the Action Plan focuses on several critical pillars:

### 1. Judicial Reform and Independence
*   **Restructuring the Judiciary:** Implementing reforms to ensure [[Judicial Independence]] by addressing irregular appointments. This includes specific measures to identify and remove "neo-judges" (judges appointed via non-constitutional procedures).
*   **Institutional Oversight:** Consulting with the [[Venice Commission]] to reform the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ) and the Constitutional Tribunal.
*   **Prosecutorial Separation:** Establishing a clear separation between the office of the Minister of Justice and the Prosecutor General to prevent political interference in criminal investigations.

### 2. Anti-Corruption and Transparency
*   **Institutional Realignment:** Transitioning anti-corruption competencies to established law enforcement bodies (e.g., police, internal security agencies) while integrating with the European Public Prosecutor's Office (EPPO).
*   **Strategic Frameworks:** Developing a comprehensive national anti-corruption strategy to replace ad-hoc measures, focusing on the removal of "impunity clauses" for high-ranking executives.
*   **Asset and Procurement Integrity:** Strengthening [[Public Procurement]] oversight and ensuring transparent [[Asset Declarations]] for public officials.

### 3. Media Freedom and Civic Space
*   **Regulatory Alignment:** Implementing reforms to comply with the European Media Freedom Act (EMFA), focusing on [[Media Pluralism]] and the impartiality of national broadcasting councils.
*   **Protection of Journalists:** Addressing the threat of [[SLAPPs]] (Strategic Lawsuits Against Public Participation) and improving protections for whistleblowers.
*   **Civic Space:** Expanding the "narrowed" civic space to ensure that civil society can operate without obstruction.
