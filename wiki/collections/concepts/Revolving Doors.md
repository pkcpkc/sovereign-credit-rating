# Revolving Doors

The "revolving door" phenomenon refers to the movement of individuals between roles as legislators or regulators and the private sector, particularly in industries they previously oversaw or influenced. This movement can create significant conflicts of interest, as former public officials may use their insider knowledge and connections to benefit private entities, while private-sector interests may influence public policy during the official's tenure.

## Current Status in Austria (2025)
According to the **EU Commission 2025 Rule of Law Report**, the situation regarding revolving doors in Austria is characterized by the following:

*   **Limited Restrictions:** Current post-employment "revolving doors" restrictions are noted to be narrow in scope.
*   **Recent Developments:** A three-year cooling-off period was recently introduced specifically for some constitutional judges to mitigate influence.
*   **Lobbying Framework:** The broader [[Lobbying Framework]] remains limited in scope and lacks sufficient sanctions to effectively regulate these transitions.
*   **Anti-Corruption Context:** Addressing revolving doors is a key component of the **National Anti-Corruption Strategy 2023-2025**, aimed at strengthening the integrity of public institutions.
