# Digitalisation of Courts

The **Digitalisation of Courts** refers to the integration of information and communication technologies (ICT) into the judicial system to improve administrative efficiency, transparency, and the speed of legal proceedings.

## Current Status in Italy
As of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the progress of digitalization in the Italian justice system is heterogeneous across different legal branches:

*   **Successful Implementations:** The **tax, civil, and administrative justice** sectors are reported to be fully digitalized. These branches have successfully adopted digital workflows to manage cases and documentation.
*   **Challenges in Criminal Justice:** The criminal justice sector faces significant hurdles in its digital rollout. Specifically, the **APP platform** has encountered technical issues and is hampered by the use of obsolete equipment.

## Strategic Objectives & Impact
*   **Backlog Management:** Digitalization is a key component in Italy's positive trend of reducing case backlogs, contributing to clearance rates exceeding 100% in many jurisdictions.
*   **Public Procurement Integrity:** While digitalization is being used to mitigate infiltration in public procurement, the reliance on digital platforms remains a critical tool for ensuring the integrity of government contracts.
*   **Mediation:** New rules (effective January 2025) aim to leverage streamlined processes to encourage mediation, thereby reducing the total volume of legal proceedings.
