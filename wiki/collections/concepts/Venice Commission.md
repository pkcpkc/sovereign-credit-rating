# Venice Commission

The **Venice Commission** (formally the European Commission for Democracy through Law) is a consultative body of the Council of Europe. It serves as a primary advisory organ providing legal expertise on constitutional matters, human rights, and the rule of law.

## Role in Judicial Reform
The Commission plays a critical role in evaluating and providing guidance on the structural integrity of national judiciaries. In the context of contemporary EU policy, it is frequently consulted on:
- **Judicial Independence:** Assessing the autonomy of courts and the mechanisms for judicial appointments.
- **Constitutional Frameworks:** Providing opinions on the functioning of Constitutional Tribunals and the legality of judicial removals.
- **Legislative Oversight:** Reviewing draft laws to ensure compliance with democratic standards and the rule of law.

## Recent Context: Poland
The Commission is actively involved in monitoring and advising on the Polish judicial landscape. Key areas of engagement include:
- **NCJ and Constitutional Tribunal Reforms:** The Polish government consults the Venice Commission specifically regarding reforms for the National Council for the Judiciary (NCJ) and the Constitutional Tribunal.
- **Action Plan Implementation:** The Commission's guidance informs the progress of the [[Action Plan on the Rule of Law]], particularly in addressing "neo-judges" and rectifying irregular judicial appointments.
