# Carbon Removals

Carbon removals refer to the process of capturing and removing carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) from the atmosphere to mitigate climate change. Within the context of national economic and environmental strategies, such as those outlined in the **2025 Country Report - France**, carbon removals are a critical component of the broader **Energy Transition** and decarbonization goals.

## Context in France
France has demonstrated significant progress in its energy mix, achieving 95% low-carbon electricity. However, the transition to a net-zero economy requires addressing hard-to-abate sectors like transport and buildings. Carbon removals are essential to:
*   **Offset Residual Emissions:** Compensating for emissions that cannot be eliminated through efficiency or **Renewable Energy** alone.
*   **Meet Climate Targets:** Supporting the target for a 50% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions by 2030.
*   **Structural Transition:** Integrating removal technologies into the national economic framework to support long-term **Fiscal Sustainability** and industrial competitiveness.
