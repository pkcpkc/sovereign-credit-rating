# Lobbying Rules

Lobbying rules refer to the regulatory frameworks designed to oversee, disclose, and limit the influence of interest groups, corporations, and individuals on public policy and legislative processes. These rules are a critical component of [[Open Government]] and [[Anti-Corruption Strategy]], ensuring that democratic processes remain transparent and free from undue influence.

## Current Status in Romania
As of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the following status is noted regarding lobbying regulations in Romania:

*   **Legislative Stagnation:** There has been a documented lack of progress in introducing specific lobbying rules for Members of Parliament (MPs).
*   **Integrity Measures:** While some regulations regarding the acceptance of gifts were adopted in March 2025, these do not substitute for a comprehensive lobbying registry or set of rules governing direct interactions between lobbyists and legislators.
*   **Transparency Gaps:** The absence of clear lobbying regulations contributes to a lack of transparency in how political decisions are influenced, potentially undermining the integrity of the legislative process.

## Related Concepts
*   **[[Anti-Corruption Strategy]]**: Lobbying rules are a primary pillar of anti-corruption efforts to prevent "capture" of the state by private interests.
*   **[[Open Government]]**: Transparency in lobbying is essential for a functional open government and meaningful public consultation.
*   **[[Asset Declaration]]**: Often paired with lobbying rules to ensure that officials do not have undisclosed financial ties to interest groups.
