# SLAPP

**Strategic Lawsuits Against Public Participation (SLAPP)** refer to lawsuits intended to censor, intimidate, and silence critics by burdening them with the cost of a legal defense until they abandon their criticism or opposition.

## Context in Slovakia
As of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the environment for journalists in Slovakia is characterized by an increasingly hostile atmosphere. A significant concern identified by the European Commission is the rise of **SLAPP-like litigation** used as a tool to deter investigative reporting and public discourse.

## Key Characteristics & Impacts
- **Chilling Effect:** These lawsuits are designed to create a "chilling effect," where journalists and civil society actors self-censor to avoid the financial and emotional exhaustion of protracted legal battles.
- **Media Freedom Erosion:** The report highlights that the government has abandoned plans to reform defamation laws or improve physical safety for journalists, while simultaneously witnessing a deterioration in the environment for media pluralism.
- **Judicial Context:** The use of SLAPPs often intersects with broader concerns regarding [[Judicial Independence]] and the ability of the judiciary to provide a fair and impartial forum for those facing meritless litigation.
