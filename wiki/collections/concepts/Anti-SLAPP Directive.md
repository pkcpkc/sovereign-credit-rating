# Anti-SLAPP Directive

The **Anti-SLAPP Directive** is an EU legislative framework designed to protect individuals and organizations—particularly journalists, activists, and civil society groups—from "Strategic Lawsuits Against Public Participation" (SLAPPs). These are legal actions intended to censor, intimidate, or silence critics by burdening them with excessive legal costs and protracted litigation.

## Context and Implementation
As of mid-2025, the directive is a key component of European efforts to safeguard [[Media Pluralism]] and ensure that freedom of expression is not undermined by abusive litigation.

### Key Developments in Belgium
- **Transposition Status:** Work is ongoing to transpose the EU anti-SLAPP Directive into Belgian national law.
- **Complementary Measures:** Implementation is paired with other judicial reforms, including the decriminalization of defamation and the introduction of harsher penalties for crimes committed against journalists.
- **Objective:** The directive aims to provide a clear legal mechanism to dismiss frivolous lawsuits early in the proceedings, preventing "lawfare" from stifling public debate.
