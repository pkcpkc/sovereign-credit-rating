# Real Estate Exposure

Real Estate Exposure refers to the concentration of credit risk within a financial institution's loan portfolio tied to the property market. In certain economies, this exposure can represent a significant systemic risk if the property market undergoes a downturn or overvaluation.

## Contextual Analysis: Malta
In the context of the Maltese economy, real estate exposure is a critical concern for financial stability. The banking sector faces high concentration in this area, with property-related loans accounting for approximately 70% of total loan portfolios.

## Risk Management & Policy Recommendations
To mitigate the risks associated with high real estate exposure, the following measures have been identified:

*   **Macroprudential Policy:** The implementation of stricter oversight to manage sectoral risks.
*   **Sectoral Systemic Risk Buffer (sSyRB):** The IMF recommends raising the sSyRB to create a capital cushion against real estate volatility.
*   **Scope Expansion:** Recommendations include broadening the scope of these buffers to specifically encompass commercial real estate, not just residential.
*   **Economic Impact:** Addressing these exposures is vital for maintaining the stability of the banking sector amidst a transition toward a productivity-driven growth model.
