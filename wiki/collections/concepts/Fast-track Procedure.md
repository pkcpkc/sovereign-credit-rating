# Fast-track Procedure

A legislative mechanism used to accelerate the passage of laws by bypassing standard parliamentary stages and stakeholder consultations. 

## Context and Usage
In the context of the Slovakian legal landscape, the use of fast-track procedures has been identified as a significant concern regarding democratic oversight and transparency. 

### Key Observations:
- **Frequency of Use:** In 2024, a substantial portion of legislation (e.g., 28 out of 58 laws) was passed via fast-track procedures.
- **Impact on Governance:** The primary criticism of this method is that it circumvents meaningful stakeholder consultation, potentially undermining the quality of legislation and the integrity of the [[Rule of Law]].
- **Relationship to Judicial Independence:** The rapid passage of laws without sufficient scrutiny can contribute to a legislative environment that may impact [[Judicial Independence]] and the protection of civil liberties.
