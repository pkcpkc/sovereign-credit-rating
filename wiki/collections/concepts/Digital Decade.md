# Digital Decade

The **Digital Decade** refers to the European Union's strategic framework and initiative aimed at accelerating digital transformation across all sectors of the economy and society. It serves as a cornerstone for modernizing infrastructure, fostering innovation, and ensuring that the EU remains competitive in a globalized digital landscape.

## Context and Implementation
The Digital Decade is closely linked to the **Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP)**, which provides the funding mechanism for large-scale transitions. In specific national contexts, such as Malta and Romania, the initiative focuses on:

- **Digital Transformation**: Utilizing RRP grants to bolster digital infrastructure and services. In Romania, this is a critical priority to address "Digital Lag," where current adoption rates—particularly in AI (just over 3% in businesses)—remain significantly below EU averages.
- **Innovation and R&D**: Addressing "Innovation Deficits" by increasing **R&D Intensity**. For example, Romania currently faces a severe deficit with an R&D intensity of 0.52% of GDP, far below the 2% target for 2027. The initiative seeks to develop venture capital ecosystems and move economies from low-cost labor models to innovation-driven ones.
- **Skills Development**: Bridging education gaps to ensure the workforce can navigate a digital-first economy. This specifically targets high-demand sectors like ICT, addressing massive shortages of skilled workers and low tertiary education attainment.

## Key Objectives
- **Economic Growth**: Leveraging digital tools to improve productivity, address labor shortages in critical service sectors, and mitigate **Macroeconomic Imbalance**.
- **Sustainability**: Integrating digital technologies to optimize energy mix, waste management, and water exploitation monitoring. This includes supporting the **Circular Economy** and improving **Energy Affordability** by reducing fiscal burdens on electricity.
- **Regulatory Reform**: Streamlining administrative burdens, improving **Public Procurement** processes, and enhancing the business environment to facilitate digital entrepreneurship.
- **Social Integration**: Ensuring digital literacy is accessible to all, aiming to improve the 27.7% of the population currently possessing basic digital skills and fostering social fairness across rural and urban divides.
