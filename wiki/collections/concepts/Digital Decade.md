# Digital Decade

The **Digital Decade** refers to the European Union's strategic framework and initiative aimed at accelerating digital transformation across all sectors of the economy and society. It serves as a cornerstone for modernizing infrastructure, fostering innovation, and ensuring that the EU remains competitive in a globalized digital landscape.

## Context and Implementation
The Digital Decade is closely linked to the **Recovery and Resilience Plan (RRP)**, which provides the funding mechanism for large-scale transitions. In specific national contexts, such as Malta, the initiative focuses on:

- **Digital Transformation**: Utilizing RRP grants to bolster digital infrastructure and services.
- **Innovation and R&D**: Addressing "Innovation Deficits" by increasing **R&D Intensity** and developing the venture capital ecosystem.
- **Skills Development**: Bridging education gaps to ensure the workforce can navigate a digital-first economy, specifically targeting high-demand sectors like ICT.

## Key Objectives
- **Economic Growth**: Leveraging digital tools to improve productivity and address labor shortages in critical service sectors.
- **Sustainability**: Integrating digital technologies to optimize energy mix, waste management, and water exploitation monitoring.
- **Regulatory Reform**: Streamlining administrative burdens and improving the business environment to facilitate digital entrepreneurship.
