# Labor Productivity

Labor productivity refers to the amount of goods and services produced by a unit of labor input (e.g., per hour worked or per worker). It is a critical indicator of economic efficiency and long-term growth potential.

## Current Context and Challenges
In the context of the Slovak Republic, labor productivity is currently facing a "secular decline," posing a significant headwind to long-term economic growth. This trend is exacerbated by structural issues and demographic shifts, such as an aging population.

## Strategic Responses and Recommendations
To counter declining productivity and mitigate the risks associated with automation, several strategic interventions have been identified:

*   **Active Labor Market Policies (ALMPs):** Intensifying these policies to improve workforce participation and skill alignment.
*   **Research and Development (R&D):** Increasing R&D spending to foster innovation and technological advancement.
*   **Economic Integration:** Deepening integration into the European single market to enhance competitiveness.
*   **Digitalization:** Leveraging digital transformation as a tool to modernize production processes and offset demographic pressures.
