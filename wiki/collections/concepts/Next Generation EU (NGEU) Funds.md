# Next Generation EU (NGEU) Funds

The **Next Generation EU (NGEU)** funds represent a significant European Union recovery instrument designed to provide substantial financial support for economic recovery and structural transformation. 

### Role in Economic Growth
In the context of the Polish economy, the absorption of NGEU funds is a primary driver of short-term economic acceleration. Specifically:
- **Growth Drivers:** The funds are expected to contribute significantly to Poland's projected growth rates of approximately 3.5% in 2025-2026.
- **Medium-Term Impact:** While the funds provide a substantial boost to domestic demand in the immediate term, the IMF notes that medium-term growth is expected to moderate as the direct impact of these funds unwinds and demographic challenges, such as population aging, begin to take effect.

### Fiscal and Structural Context
The utilization of NGEU funds is intertwined with broader fiscal and structural goals:
- **Fiscal Positioning:** The influx and expenditure of these funds occur alongside a period of widening fiscal deficits (e.g., Poland's 5.9% deficit in 2024), necessitating a transition toward fiscal consolidation and the implementation of a **Medium-Term Fiscal Structural Plan (MTFSP)**.
- **Structural Objectives:** The funds are intended to support critical transitions, including:
    - **Climate Transition:** Supporting the goal of halving coal-fueled power generation by 2030.
    - **Innovation:** Fostering private equity and technological advancement.
    - **Labor Market:** Addressing labor supply shortages and facilitating the integration of refugees and women into the workforce.
