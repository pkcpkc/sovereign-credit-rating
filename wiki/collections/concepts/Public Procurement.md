# Public Procurement

Public procurement refers to the process by which government bodies and public authorities purchase goods, services, and works from the private sector. It serves as a critical lever for economic policy, public spending management, and the achievement of broader socio-economic objectives.

## Context in France (2025 Report)
In the context of the French economy, public procurement is increasingly intertwined with several strategic national priorities:

*   **Fiscal Sustainability:** As France faces a rising public debt-to-GDP ratio (projected at 116% in 2025), the management of public spending through procurement is essential for fiscal consolidation. The report emphasizes a need for permanent spending reviews to rationalize tax expenditures and ensure sustainable public finance.
*   **Energy Transition & Decarbonization:** Public procurement is a key vehicle for meeting the 2030 targets for greenhouse gas reduction. It plays a vital role in scaling up renewable energy infrastructure and transitioning the transport and building sectors toward low-carbon solutions.
*   **Administrative Simplification:** To address the "administrative complexity" hindering economic growth, France is moving toward a "simplification of economic life" law. This includes aiming to reduce bureaucracy in licensing and regulations, which directly impacts how businesses interact with public procurement processes.
*   **Social and Regional Impact:** Procurement policies are also linked to addressing regional disparities and social fairness, ensuring that public spending contributes to reducing poverty rates and improving infrastructure in underserved territories.

## Context in Romania (2025 Country Report)
In the Romanian context, public procurement is a central component of the country's broader economic and structural transformation efforts:

*   **Fiscal-Structural Alignment:** Public procurement must operate within the constraints of the Medium-Term Fiscal-Structural Plan (MTP). With a public deficit reaching 9.3% of GDP in 2024, procurement processes are critical to managing net expenditure growth and ensuring debt sustainability.
*   **Digitalization & Innovation:** As Romania seeks to address its "Digital Lag" and low R&D intensity (0.52% of GDP), public procurement is a primary mechanism for driving the adoption of AI and digital technologies in public services and infrastructure.
*   **Energy & Circular Economy:** Procurement serves as a tool to tackle high energy affordability and the low circular material use rate (1.3%). It is essential for procuring renewable energy technologies and establishing waste management systems to support the 2030 renewable targets.
*   **Infrastructure & Social Fairness:** Given the severe housing deprivation and underdeveloped transport networks, public procurement is the primary vehicle for funding the modernization of railway infrastructure and addressing rural-urban disparities.
