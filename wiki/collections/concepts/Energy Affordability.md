# Energy Affordability

Energy affordability refers to the economic accessibility of energy resources for households, businesses, and industries. It is a critical component of [[Social Fairness]] and economic competitiveness, determining the viability of both industrial growth and standard of living.

## Contextual Analysis: Romania (2025)
Recent data from the 2025 Country Report highlights specific challenges regarding energy affordability within the Romanian context:

*   **Fiscal Burden:** A primary driver of high costs is the fiscal structure, where electricity is taxed more heavily than gas. This disproportionately impacts consumers and creates a barrier to economic efficiency.
*   **Price Comparison:** Romania currently faces some of the highest electricity prices in the European Union, creating a significant hurdle for [[Digitalization]] and industrial modernization.
*   **Energy Transition Dynamics:** While renewables reached 48% of electricity generation in 2024, the transition toward a sustainable model is hampered by:
    *   Regulatory barriers.
    *   Grid limitations.
    *   The high cost of electricity impacting the pace of [[Decarbonization]].

## Related Concepts
*   **[[Fiscal Policy]]**: The mechanism through which taxation on energy directly influences affordability.
*   **[[Decarbonization]]**: The long-term goal of transitioning to renewable sources, which must be balanced with immediate cost accessibility.
*   **[[Macroeconomic Imbalance]]**: High energy costs contribute to inflationary pressures and impact the overall fiscal health of the nation.
