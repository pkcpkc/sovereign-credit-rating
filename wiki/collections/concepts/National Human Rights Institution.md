# National Human Rights Institution

The **National Human Rights Institution** is a key component of Malta's institutional framework for upholding the rule of law and protecting fundamental rights. As indicated in the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the establishment of such an institution is a specific policy recommendation aimed at strengthening constitutional safeguards and institutional checks and balances.

## Context and Development

In the context of Malta's 2025 rule of law assessment, limited progress was made in establishing a **National Human Rights Institution**. The draft legislation governing its creation and operational framework remains pending. This institutional gap is viewed by the European Commission as a weakness in the broader system of checks and balances, particularly concerning the protection of civil society and the formalization of public participation in the legislative process.
