# Whistleblowing

Whistleblowing refers to the act of reporting internal misconduct, illegal activities, or ethical violations within an organization or government body. It serves as a primary mechanism for identifying corruption and maintaining the [[Rule of Law]].

## Context in Italy (2025)
Based on the 2025 Rule of Law Report, whistleblowing is situated within a broader effort to improve [[Public Procurement Integrity]] and judicial accountability. While specific legislative updates for whistleblowers are part of the ongoing anti-corruption framework, the following related themes impact the effectiveness of whistleblowing in Italy:

*   **Anti-Corruption Framework:** Whistleblowing serves as a primary mechanism for identifying corruption, which remains a significant concern in Italy (where 82% of citizens consider corruption widespread).
*   **Judicial Independence:** The integrity of whistleblowing reports relies on a perceived [[Judicial Independence]], which has seen a rise in public trust (reaching 46% in 2025).
*   **Media Pluralism:** The ability of whistleblowers to bring information to the public is linked to [[Media Pluralism]]. However, journalists and potential sources face a "chilling effect" from legislative restrictions on judicial information and reports of spyware usage.
*   **Institutional Checks:** The move toward more robust [[Constitutional Reform]] and the regulation of lobbying are intended to create a more transparent environment where whistleblowing can occur without fear of retaliation.

## Context in Malta (2025)
In the Maltese context, whistleblowing is integrated into a broader landscape of justice reform and anti-corruption measures. Key factors influencing the environment for whistleblowing include:

*   **Judicial and Prosecutorial Integrity:** The effectiveness of reporting misconduct is tied to the perceived [[Judicial Independence]] (rated high by the public) and the recent completion of transferring serious prosecutions to the Office of the Attorney General.
*   **Anti-Corruption Progress:** While progress has been made in high-level investigations, the lack of a "robust track record of final judgments" and the under-resourced nature of the Permanent Commission Against Corruption (PCAC) present challenges for the ultimate resolution of whistleblower reports.
*   **Media Protection:** The enactment of anti-SLAPP laws in 2024 provides a layer of protection against strategic lawsuits, though the "very high risk" score for public service media independence remains a concern for the dissemination of whistleblower information.
*   **Transparency and Inquiry:** Legislative changes regarding the threshold for citizens to petition for magisterial inquiries (Bill 125) and the push for [[Digital Justice]] and [[Asset Declaration]] standards are critical components of the transparency framework that supports whistleblowing activities.
