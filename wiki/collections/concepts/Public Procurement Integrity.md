# Public Procurement Integrity

Public Procurement Integrity refers to the framework of rules, transparency measures, and oversight mechanisms designed to ensure that the acquisition of goods, works, and services by public entities is conducted fairly, efficiently, and without corruption.

## Current Status and Challenges (Italy)
As of 2025, public procurement remains a high-risk area for corruption within the Italian institutional framework. Despite ongoing efforts to strengthen integrity, several key challenges persist:

*   **Direct Allocation Risks:** A significant portion of public contracts continue to be awarded through direct allocation processes. In 2024, approximately 98% of contracts were allocated via this method, which inherently presents a higher risk of favoritism or illicit influence compared to open competitive bidding.
*   **Digital Mitigation:** To counter infiltration and improve transparency, the Italian government is increasingly utilizing digital platforms for procurement processes. These systems are intended to create an audit trail and reduce the opportunities for manual interference or opaque negotiations.
*   **Anti-Corruption Context:** Procurement integrity is a core component of the broader [[Anti-Corruption]] framework. While Italy maintains a stable corruption perception score (54/100), the high rate of direct procurement highlights the need for more robust oversight and a transition toward more transparent tender systems.
