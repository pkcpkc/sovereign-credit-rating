# Real GDP Growth

Real GDP (Gross Domestic Product) growth measures the percentage increase in the value of all finished goods and services produced within a country's borders over a specific period, adjusted for inflation. It serves as a primary indicator of economic health, productivity, and the standard of living.

## Current Context: Malta (2024-2026)
Based on the 2025 Country Report, Malta's economy is currently experiencing significant growth momentum:

- **2024 Performance**: Real GDP grew by **6.0%** in 2024.
- **2025 Forecast**: Growth is projected to continue at a rate of **4.1%**.
- **2026 Forecast**: Growth is expected to stabilize at approximately **4.0%**.

## Economic Drivers and Constraints
While Malta maintains robust growth figures, the following factors influence its long-term trajectory:

- **Fiscal Policy**: Growth is supported by a stabilizing fiscal position, with the general government deficit projected to fall from 3.7% (2024) to 2.8% (2026) and a debt-to-GDP ratio expected to remain below 50%.
- **Labor Market Dynamics**: High employment rates (83%) are contrasted by severe labor shortages in critical sectors like ICT, construction, and healthcare, which may act as a ceiling for future output.
- **Innovation and R&D**: A significant constraint on long-term potential is the low R&D intensity (0.64% of GDP), which ranks among the lowest in the EU.
- **Regulatory Environment**: High administrative burdens and Product Market Regulation (PMR) issues are noted as structural hurdles to optimal economic expansion.
- **Sustainability Pressures**: Economic growth is increasingly linked to climate resilience, requiring a transition away from high fossil fuel subsidies and addressing severe water scarcity (WEI+ of 34.1%).
