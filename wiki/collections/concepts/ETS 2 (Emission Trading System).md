# ETS 2 (Emission Trading System)

The **ETS 2 (Emission Trading System)** is a regulatory framework designed to price carbon emissions, specifically targeting sectors that were previously outside the scope of the primary EU Emissions Trading System. It serves as a cornerstone for the **[[Green Transition]]** by internalizing the environmental costs of carbon-intensive activities.

## Key Details and Implementation
*   **Scope:** The system is specifically targeted at emissions from **buildings** and **road transport**.
*   **Implementation Timeline:** In the context of Romania's economic outlook, the implementation of ETS 2 is projected to begin on **January 1, 2027**.
*   **Economic Objectives:** The system aims to incentivize private investment in **renewables** and energy efficiency by strengthening carbon pricing mechanisms.

## Contextual Significance
The adoption of ETS 2 is a critical component of Romania's shift toward a low-carbon economy. While it presents potential inflationary pressures in the short term—contributing to the economic headwinds observed in 2025—it is viewed by the IMF as a necessary structural move to ensure medium-term macroeconomic sustainability and meet climate goals.

## Related Concepts
*   **[[Green Transition]]**: The broader movement toward a low-carbon economy.
*   **[[Fiscal Consolidation]]**: Intersects with ETS 2 as part of the broader structural reforms and fiscal adjustments required for economic stability.
*   **Carbon Pricing**: The underlying mechanism of the ETS 2 to penalize emissions and reward green innovation.
