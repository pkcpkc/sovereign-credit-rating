# AML/CFT

Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) refers to the framework of laws, regulations, and procedures designed to prevent criminals from disguising the origins of illegally obtained funds and to stop the financing of terrorist activities.

### Context in Slovakia
In the context of the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Slovak Republic**, AML/CFT is a critical component of the country's broader financial stability and governance agenda. The IMF emphasizes the necessity of strengthening these frameworks to ensure the integrity of the financial system and to mitigate risks associated with illicit financial flows.

### Key Governance & Stability Links
- **Governance and Corruption:** Strengthening AML/CFT measures is inextricably linked to broader efforts to improve judicial independence and anti-corruption frameworks in Slovakia. The IMF notes that improving perceptions of corruption is vital for long-term economic stability.
- **Financial Sector Resilience:** While the Slovak banking system currently maintains high capital ratios and ample liquidity, robust AML/CFT protocols are essential to protect these buffers from being compromised by criminal activity.
- **Structural Reforms:** The IMF views the improvement of governance and anti-corruption efforts—which encompass AML/CFT—as a primary recommendation for addressing structural headwinds and fostering a more transparent economic environment.
