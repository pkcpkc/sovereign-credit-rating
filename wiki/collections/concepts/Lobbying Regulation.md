# Lobbying Regulation

Lobbying regulation refers to the legal framework and transparency mechanisms designed to govern the influence exerted by interest groups, corporations, and individuals on public policy and legislative processes. It is a critical component of [[Anti-Corruption]] and the maintenance of [[Judicial Independence]] and democratic integrity.

## Current Status in Italy
As of the 2025 Rule of Law Report, progress regarding lobbying transparency in Italy remains limited. Key observations include:

*   **Register Status:** While some individual ministries have established voluntary registers for lobbyists, there is currently no comprehensive national lobbying register.
*   **Political Office Holders:** Clear and binding rules governing the conduct of political office holders regarding lobbying interactions remain pending.
*   **Policy Context:** The lack of a unified regulatory framework is identified as a barrier to improving the transparency of the legislative process and mitigating potential conflicts of interest.

## Related Concepts
*   **[[Anti-Corruption]]**: Lobbying regulations serve as a primary tool to prevent illicit influence and corruption within government institutions.
*   **[[Public Procurement Integrity]]**: Transparency in lobbying is often linked to the integrity of public tenders and contract allocations.
*   **[[Constitutional Reform]]**: Ongoing debates regarding the separation of powers and the use of government urgency decrees impact the environment in which lobbying occurs.
