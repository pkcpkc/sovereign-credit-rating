# Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER)

The **Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER)** is a measure of the relative value of a country's currency against a basket of currencies of its major trading partners, adjusted for inflation (price levels). Unlike a nominal exchange rate, which only tracks the price of one currency against another, the REER provides a comprehensive view of a country's international competitiveness.

### Key Characteristics
- **Competitiveness Indicator:** It reflects how expensive or cheap a country's exports are compared to imports from trading partners. A depreciating REER typically indicates an increase in export competitiveness, while an appreciating REER may suggest a stronger domestic economy but potentially weaker export performance.
- **Inflation Adjustment:** By accounting for the difference in inflation rates between the home country and its trading partners, the REER isolates the impact of price changes from pure currency fluctuations.

### Contextual Application (Poland)
In the context of recent economic assessments, such as the **IMF 2025 Poland Country Report**, the REER serves as a critical metric for evaluating the balance between domestic price stability and international trade positioning. For Poland, monitoring the REER is essential for:
- Assessing the impact of **Inflation** and wage growth on the manufacturing sector.
- Evaluating the sustainability of the current **Monetary Policy** stance.
- Gauging the external pressures on the economy as it absorbs **Next Generation EU (NGEU) Funds** and manages a widening **Fiscal Deficit**.

### Contextual Application (Romania)
According to the **IMF 2025 Article IV Consultation - Romania**, the REER is a pivotal factor in determining long-term economic resilience and trade positioning. The IMF highlights the following regarding exchange rate dynamics:
- **Competitiveness Strategy:** There is a specific recommendation for a gradual increase in two-way exchange rate flexibility. This move is intended to improve the country's international competitiveness and enhance its resilience against external shocks.
- **Inflationary Interaction:** The REER is closely tied to the management of headline inflation, which remains elevated due to the removal of electricity price caps and VAT hikes.
- **Monetary Policy Alignment:** The REER serves as a guide for the National Bank of Romania (NBR) in balancing cautious interest rate stances with the need to moderate wage growth and stabilize the currency's real value.
