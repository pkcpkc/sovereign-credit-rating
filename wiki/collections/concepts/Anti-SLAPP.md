# Anti-SLAPP

Strategic Lawsuits Against Public Participation (SLAPP) are legal actions—typically involving defamation, libel, or other civil claims—intended to censor, intimidate, and silence critics by burdening them with the cost of a legal defense. 

## Legislative Context
In the context of [[Malta]], a specific law addressing cross-border strategic lawsuits was enacted in July 2024. This legislation is part of a broader effort to protect individuals and organizations, particularly those engaged in public interest activities, from being harassed by meritless litigation.

## Key Objectives
- **Protection of Media Freedom:** Safeguarding journalists and media outlets from being silenced by well-resourced entities.
- **Promotion of Public Participation:** Ensuring that citizens can engage in political and social discourse without fear of retaliatory legal action.
- **Judicial Efficiency:** Providing mechanisms to identify and dismiss "frivolous" lawsuits early in the legal process to prevent the clogging of the justice system.
