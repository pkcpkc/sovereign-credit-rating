# Federal Human Rights Institute

The **Federal Human Rights Institute** (Institut des droits de l'homme / Instituut voor de rechten van de mens) is a key independent body in Belgium responsible for promoting and protecting human rights. In the context of sovereign credit analysis and institutional governance, the Institute serves as a critical indicator of the strength of checks and balances, civic space quality, and compliance with international human rights obligations.

## Role in Sovereign Risk and Governance

The operational capacity and financial stability of the Federal Human Rights Institute are directly linked to **Civic Space** indicators used in sovereign risk assessments. A reduction in the Institute's resources or influence can signal a narrowing of civic space, potentially increasing political risk premiums.
