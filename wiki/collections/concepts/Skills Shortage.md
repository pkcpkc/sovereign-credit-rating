# Skills Shortage

Skills shortage refers to a condition where the demand for workers with specific qualifications, expertise, or technical abilities exceeds the available supply within a labor market. This phenomenon acts as a significant structural barrier to economic growth and industrial modernization.

## Contextual Analysis: Hungary
In the context of the **2025 Country Report - Hungary**, the skills shortage is identified as a critical structural vulnerability affecting the nation's economic trajectory. Key characteristics include:

*   **Sectoral Impact:** The shortage is most acute in **STEM** (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) fields and sectors related to the **Green Transition**. These gaps hinder the country's ability to innovate and compete in high-value industries.
*   **Educational Barriers:** A primary driver of this shortage is the low level of tertiary education attainment, which stands at 32.3%—one of the lowest figures in the European Union.
*   **Economic Consequences:** The lack of skilled labor directly contributes to low [[Labour Productivity]], limiting the potential for sustainable GDP growth and undermining the country's competitiveness in the face of rising unit labour costs.
*   **Social Implications:** The shortage is also reflected in the healthcare sector, where a lack of qualified professionals contributes to high out-of-pocket costs and systemic strain.
