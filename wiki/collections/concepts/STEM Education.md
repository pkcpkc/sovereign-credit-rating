# STEM Education

STEM Education refers to the integrated approach to teaching and learning in the fields of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. It is a critical driver for economic productivity, technological advancement, and the transition to a green and digital economy.

## Current Status and Challenges
Based on recent economic assessments (e.g., the 2025 Country Report for Belgium and Poland), STEM education faces several critical hurdles:

*   **Skills Mismatch:** There is a significant gap between the skills provided by the current educational system and the requirements of the labor market. This is evidenced by high job vacancy rates, particularly in the STEM and healthcare sectors. In Poland, 82% of SMEs report skills shortages as a primary barrier to growth.
*   **Educational Decline:** There is a documented long-term decline in basic skills (as measured by PISA/TIMSS). Specifically, underachievement in mathematics among 15-year-olds has shown a significant increase in recent years.
*   **Impact on Productivity:** The shortage of qualified STEM professionals is identified as a primary factor hampering productivity growth and hindering the ability of nations to meet modern industrial demands. Currently, labor productivity in some regions remains significantly below the EU average, exacerbated by a lack of technical literacy.

## Economic and Strategic Importance
*   **Innovation Leadership:** While high R&D intensity (e.g., 3.3% of GDP in Belgium) indicates a strong innovative foundation, the lack of a sufficient pipeline of STEM-educated workers can limit the dynamism of the business environment. In "emerging innovators," a stronger link between academic research and private industry is required to commercialize innovation.
*   **Labor Market Dynamics:** Addressing the STEM education gap is essential for reducing job vacancy rates and improving employment activity among low-skilled individuals and older workers. It is particularly vital in manufacturing, where a majority of firms report severe labor shortages.
*   **Digital & Green Transition:** STEM education is the backbone of achieving targets related to the [[Circular Economy]] and the [[Energy Transition]], as these fields require advanced technical literacy, engineering capabilities, and the ability to navigate decarbonization challenges in heavy industry and transport.
