# E-health

E-health refers to the application of information and communication technologies (ICT) to healthcare, aimed at improving public health, clinical outcomes, and the efficiency of healthcare delivery.

## Contextual Application: Austria
In the context of the 2025 Country Report for Austria, E-health is a critical component of the nation's digital and social infrastructure. Key aspects include:

- **Healthcare Expenditure:** Austria faces a projected increase in public healthcare expenditure, expected to reach 8.9% of GDP by 2070. E-health initiatives are vital to managing these rising costs associated with an ageing population.
- **Digital Maturity:** While Austria shows high performance in mobile-friendly public services and 5G coverage (96%), the integration of E-health services is part of a broader effort to enhance digital maturity and infrastructure.
- **Social Policy Integration:** E-health serves as a technological pillar to address the "care specialist" shortage and the growing demand for healthcare services in a demographic landscape characterized by a shrinking working-age population.
