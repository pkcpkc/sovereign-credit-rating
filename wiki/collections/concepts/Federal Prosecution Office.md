# Federal Prosecution Office

The **Federal Prosecution Office** in Austria is a key institutional component of the national justice system, currently undergoing significant structural reform to enhance judicial independence and integrity.

## Current Status and Reform Initiatives
As of 2025, the establishment of an independent Federal Prosecution Office is a primary objective of the Austrian government's commitment to rule of law standards. 

*   **Legislative Status:** The office is currently in the legislative drafting phase. The government has committed to establishing it as a **collegiate body** to ensure balanced decision-making and oversight.
*   **Independence Goals:** A core objective of the reform is to decouple the prosecution from direct executive interference. Currently, the Minister of Justice retains the right to instruct prosecutors in individual cases; the new structure aims to mitigate this to ensure a more autonomous prosecutorial function.
*   **Contextual Drivers:** The push for this reform follows an investigative commission's final report (July 2024) regarding prosecutorial influence, highlighting the need for structural changes to protect the integrity of legal proceedings.

## Related Contexts
*   **Judicial Independence:** The creation of the office is a cornerstone of broader efforts to improve the perception and reality of judicial independence in Austria.
*   **Anti-Corruption:** The office is expected to play a vital role in the implementation of the **National Anti-Corruption Strategy 2023-2025**, working alongside the Federal Bureau of Anti-Corruption (BAK).
*   **Rule of Law:** The transition toward an independent Federal Prosecution Office is a key metric in the EU Commission's assessment of Austria's adherence to European rule of law standards.
