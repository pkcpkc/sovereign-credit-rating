# Open Government

Open Government refers to a framework of governance that promotes transparency, accountability, and citizen participation in public decision-making processes. It seeks to bridge the gap between the state and civil society by ensuring that government actions are visible, understandable, and subject to public scrutiny.

## Current Context and Developments
Recent developments in Romania highlight the tension between the adoption of formal open government frameworks and the practical realities of civic space:

*   **Strategic Frameworks:** Romania adopted a new **Strategy for Open Government (2025-2030)** in April 2025. This initiative is specifically aimed at addressing a "narrowed" civic space and improving the quality of public engagement.
*   **Public Consultations:** While efforts have been made to train officials on best practices for public engagement, civil society organizations (CSOs) continue to report that many consultations remain "box-ticking" exercises characterized by tight deadlines and limited meaningful influence.
*   **Transparency Challenges:** The efficacy of open government is currently challenged by several systemic factors:
    *   **Legislative Unpredictability:** The frequent use of Government Emergency Ordinances (GEOs) can bypass traditional transparent legislative processes.
    *   **Lobbying and Integrity:** A lack of progress on [[Lobbying Rules]] for Members of Parliament remains a significant hurdle to transparent influence.
    *   **Asset Transparency:** Recent judicial rulings regarding the constitutionality of declaring assets of spouses and children create potential gaps in public oversight of official wealth.
