# Digital Justice

Digital Justice refers to the integration of digital technologies into the judicial system to improve the efficiency, accessibility, and transparency of legal processes. It encompasses the modernization of court proceedings, the digitization of public records, and the implementation of electronic systems for case management and evidence handling.

## Contextual Application: Malta
In the context of the **2025 Rule of Law Report - Malta**, Digital Justice is situated within a broader effort to address systemic inefficiencies in the justice sector. While the report highlights significant backlogs and high disposition times for civil, criminal, and administrative cases, the modernization of these systems via digital infrastructure is a key component of improving the **Judicial Efficiency** and the overall rule of law.

## Key Related Dimensions
*   **Judicial Efficiency:** Leveraging technology to reduce the judge-to-inhabitant ratio impact and clear substantial case backlogs.
*   **Transparency and Access:** Improving the accessibility of official documents and legal information to the public.
*   **Institutional Reform:** Supporting the transition of non-summary prosecutions and ensuring that legislative reforms (such as those involving **Magisterial Inquiries**) can be processed through modernized, transparent channels.
