# Excessive Deficit Procedure

The **Excessive Deficit Procedure (EDP)** is a fiscal surveillance mechanism used by the European Union to ensure that member states maintain sustainable public finances. It is a core component of the [[Stability and Growth Pact]], designed to prevent excessive government borrowing from destabilizing the Eurozone economy.

## Key Mechanisms and Context
The procedure is typically triggered when a member state's budget deficit exceeds a specific threshold (generally 3% of GDP) or when its public debt levels become unsustainable.

### Application in Poland (2023-2025)
Recent developments regarding the EDP include:
*   **Initiation:** The Council initiated an excessive deficit procedure for Poland in January 2023 due to a deficit reaching 6.6% of GDP.
*   **Fiscal Adjustment:** To address this, Poland is currently undergoing a fiscal adjustment process. This involves a binding expenditure path intended to reduce the deficit to below 3% of GDP by 2028.
*   **Compliance:** As of January 2025, the Council endorsed Poland’s medium-term fiscal-structural plan, marking a step toward compliance with EU fiscal rules.
