# Asset Declarations

Asset declarations are a fundamental transparency mechanism used to monitor the financial holdings, interests, and assets of public officials to prevent corruption, conflicts of interest, and illicit enrichment.

## Current Status in Hungary
Based on the 2025 Rule of Law Report, the framework for asset declarations in Hungary faces significant systemic challenges:

*   **Transparency Shortcomings:** There are significant deficiencies in the transparency and oversight of asset declarations specifically for individuals holding senior political functions.
*   **Anti-Corruption Context:** While the country has established some low-level codes of conduct, the lack of robust oversight on high-level asset disclosures contributes to the perception that high-level corruption remains largely unaddressed.
*   **Institutional Obstacles:** The effectiveness of monitoring these declarations is hindered by the limitations faced by the Integrity Authority, which struggles with accessing necessary state databases to verify reported assets.
